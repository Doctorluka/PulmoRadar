Na+/H+ Exchanger Isoform 1 Regulates Apoptosis Susceptibility in Pulmonary Arterial Smooth Muscle from the Sugen/Hypoxia model of Pulmonary Hypertension.

杂志American journal of physiology. Lung cellular and molecular physiology
IF: 4.0 · JCR: Q2 · 中科院2区 · 新锐2区
文章2026 Nov 10
10.1152/ajplung.00385.2025
作者Manuella R Andrade · Larissa A Shimoda
机构Department of Physiology, Johns Hopkins School of Medicine Baltimore, MD 21224.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 该研究首次将 NHE1 定义为 SuHx 肺高压模型中 PASMC 凋亡抵抗的必要且充分调控节点，而不仅是 pH 调节或骨架锚定蛋白。
  • 该研究闭合了 NHE1 抑制 p-p38 促凋亡信号这一机制链，并用 p38 抑制剂 SB203580 阻断 EIPA 促凋亡效应完成因果验证。
  • 该研究在原代 SuHx PASMC 中结合 NHE 活性、NHE1 表面表达、siRNA/过表达获得与缺失功能以及 NHE1 突变体，提供了较严格的体外功能验证。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Pulmonary hypertension (PH) is characterized by vascular remodeling driven in part by apoptosis-resistant pulmonary arterial smooth muscle cells (PASMCs). Na+/H+ exchanger isoform 1 (NHE1) regulates intracellular pH and plasma membrane cytoskeleton anchoring, but the role of NHE1 in PASMC apoptosis remains unclear. PASMCs were isolated from distal pulmonary arteries from control and Sugen/Hypoxia (SuHx) rats, a model of PH. NHE activity was measured via the ammonium pulse technique, protein via immunoblot, and apoptosis via Hoechst staining, Caspase-Glo® 3/7 Assay System and Annexin V/ propidium iodide immunofluorescence. NHE activity and NHE1 surface expression were increased in SuHx PASMCs compared to controls. Despite increased endoplasmic reticulum stress at baseline, SuHx PASMCs were resistant to apoptosis following hydrogen peroxide (H2O2) challenge. Inhibition of NHE activity with ethyl-isopropyl amiloride (EIPA) and NHE1 silencing with siRNA restored apoptosis in SuHx PASMCs. Conversely, NHE1 overexpression in control PASMCs conferred apoptosis resistance. Expression of mutant NHE1 constructs lacking ion translocation or ezrin binding also reduced H2O2-induced apoptosis. Mechanistically, H2O2 increased p38 phosphorylation (p-p38) in PASMCs from control, but not SuHx, rats, indicating impaired activation of this pro-apoptotic pathway. NHE1 suppression via EIPA or siRNA restored p-p38 in SuHx PASMCs, while overexpression of NHE1 (wild-type or mutants) suppressed p-p38 following apoptotic stimulation. Inhibition of p-p38 with SB203580 prevented the pro-apoptotic effect of EIPA, validating a role for p-p38 signaling in NHE1-mediated apoptosis resistance in SuHx PASMCs. These findings identify NHE1 as necessary and sufficient for PASMC apoptosis resistance in PH, by a mechanism involving suppression of p-p38.

  • 从 control 与 SuHx 大鼠 distal pulmonary artery 分离原代 PASMC。
  • ammonium pulse 测 NHE 活性，immunoblot 测 NHE1 蛋白/表面表达。
  • H2O2 刺激后用 Hoechst、Caspase-Glo® 3/7 与 Annexin V/PI 评估凋亡。
  • 采用 EIPA、NHE1 siRNA、NHE1 过表达及缺失离子转运或 ezrin 结合的突变体做缺失/获得功能。
  • 检测 p-p38，并用 SB203580 验证 p38 通路在 NHE1 介导凋亡抵抗中的作用。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • SuHx PASMC 的 NHE 活性与 NHE1 表面表达较 control 升高，且基线 ER stress 增加但仍对 H2O2 诱导凋亡抵抗。
  • EIPA 抑制 NHE 活性或 NHE1 siRNA 可恢复 SuHx PASMC 凋亡，而 control PASMC 过表达 NHE1 获得凋亡抵抗。
  • 缺失离子转运或 ezrin 结合的 NHE1 突变体同样降低 H2O2 诱导凋亡。
  • H2O2 在 control 但不在 SuHx PASMC 增加 p-p38；NHE1 抑制恢复 p-p38，NHE1 过表达（野生型或突变体）抑制 p-p38。
  • SB203580 抑制 p-p38 可阻止 EIPA 的促凋亡效应，支持 p-p38 是 NHE1 介导凋亡抵抗的下游节点。

  • 研究为离体原代 PASMC 的 SuHx 模型，摘要未提及 in vivo 干预或 human PH 组织验证。
  • NHE1 如何抑制 p-p38 的上游机制未在摘要中阐明，ER stress 与凋亡抵抗的关系也未展开。

ZEB1-Driven GLS1 Transcription Promotes Macrophage Glutamine Metabolism in PAH.

杂志Hypertension (Dallas, Tex. : 1979)
IF: 9.8 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2026 Nov 10
10.1161/HYPERTENSIONAHA.126.27213
作者Xing Chen* · Riken Chen* · Limei Liang* · Yan Deng
机构Department of Ultrasound, The First Affiliated Hospital of Guangxi Medical University, Nanning, China (X.C., L. Liang, L.X., L.P., L. Li, J.L., H.M., Y.D.).

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 本研究将 PAH 中 ZEB1 的高表达定位到募集而来的 disease-associated macrophages，从而把转录因子与特定巨噬细胞状态而非泛巨噬细胞活化联系起来。
  • 研究定义了 ZEB1–BRD4–H3K27ac–GLS 这一新的转录-表观遗传-代谢因果轴，说明 ZEB1 通过招募 BRD4 增强 GLS 启动子 H3K27ac 来驱动谷氨酰胺代谢重编程。
  • 通过髓系特异性 ZEB1 条件敲除、GLS 回补以及 BRD4/GLS 联合抑制和腺病毒 ZEB1 沉默，在 hypoxia 与 monocrotaline 模型中提供了体内因果与可逆性验证。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

BACKGROUND: The transcriptional mechanisms governing macrophage glutamine metabolism in pulmonary arterial hypertension remain incompletely understood. We investigated the role of ZEB1 (zinc finger E-box-binding homeobox 1)-mediated transcriptional regulation in macrophage metabolic reprogramming and the development of pulmonary arterial hypertension.

METHODS: Clinical samples and multiomics data sets were analyzed to characterize ZEB1 expression in pulmonary arterial hypertension. Myeloid-specific ZEB1 conditional knockout mice were subjected to hypoxia-induced pulmonary hypertension. Transcriptomic sequencing, metabolomic profiling, chromatin immunoprecipitation-quantitative polymerase chain reaction, dual-luciferase reporter assays, and coimmunoprecipitation were integrated to define ZEB1-dependent molecular, metabolic, and inflammatory programs in pulmonary and bone marrow-derived macrophages. Therapeutic potential was further evaluated using pharmacological interventions and adenoviral ZEB1 silencing in monocrotaline-induced pulmonary hypertension in rats.

RESULTS: ZEB1 was upregulated in pulmonary arterial hypertension and enriched in recruited disease-associated macrophages. Myeloid-specific ZEB1 deletion attenuated macrophage inflammation, pulmonary vascular remodeling, and right ventricular dysfunction. Mechanistically, ZEB1 bound the GLS (glutaminase) promoter and recruited BRD4 (bromodomain-containing protein 4), enhancing histone H3 lysine 27 acetylation at the GLS promoter and promoting GLS transcription and glutamine metabolic reprogramming. Restoration of GLS partially reversed the metabolic and inflammatory effects of ZEB1 deficiency. Pharmacological targeting of ZEB1, combined BRD4 and GLS inhibition, and adenoviral ZEB1 silencing ameliorated experimental pulmonary hypertension.

CONCLUSIONS: ZEB1 promotes macrophage glutamine metabolic reprogramming by recruiting BRD4 and enhancing histone H3 lysine 27 acetylation-dependent GLS transcription. This ZEB1-driven metabolic program promotes macrophage inflammation and pulmonary vascular remodeling, identifying the ZEB1-BRD4-GLS regulatory axis as a potential therapeutic target in pulmonary arterial hypertension.

  • 整合转录组、代谢组、ChIP-qPCR、双荧光素酶与 coIP 解析 ZEB1 依赖程序。
  • GLS 回补及药物干预/腺病毒 ZEB1 沉默在 monocrotaline 大鼠 PH 中评估。

  • GLS 回补部分逆转 ZEB1 缺失所致的代谢和炎症效应。
  • 靶向 ZEB1、联合抑制 BRD4 与 GLS、以及腺病毒沉默 ZEB1 均改善实验性 PAH。

Single-Cell and Spatial Transcriptomics Identified Fatty Acid-Binding Proteins Controlling Endothelial Glycolytic and Arterial Programming in Pulmonary Hypertension.

杂志Arteriosclerosis, thrombosis, and vascular biology
IF: 8.8 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2025 Jul
10.1161/ATVBAHA.124.321173
作者Bin Liu · Zhiyu Dai
机构Division of Pulmonary, Critical Care and Sleep (B.L., D.Y., S.L., K.R., X.X., Y.C., H.Z., A.T., K.S.K., Z.D.), College of Medicine-Phoenix, University of Arizona, Phoenix.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 首次将内皮 FABP4/FABP5 从既往仅作为 PAH 循环生物标志物的相关性线索，确立为驱动肺血管闭塞性重构的因果性节点，并在 Egln1Tie2Cre 小鼠中通过 Fabp4/Fabp5 双基因敲除实现表型逆转。
  • 在单细胞与空间转录组层面重新定义了 PH 内皮细胞的“远端动脉程序化（distal arterial programming）+ 糖酵解增强”这一此前未被明确界定的病理细胞状态，并把 FABP4/5 定位为该状态的调控枢纽。
  • 闭合了 FABP4/5→EC 糖酵解/ROS→HIF-2α→异常内皮增殖→动脉内皮细胞堆积与重构的机制链，并在 IPAH 患者肺血管内皮、血浆蛋白组与 PH 大鼠模型中完成跨物种、人组织层面的严格验证。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

BACKGROUND: Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) is a devastating disease characterized by obliterative vascular remodeling and persistent increase of vascular resistance, leading to right heart failure and premature death. Understanding the cellular and molecular mechanisms will help develop novel therapeutic approaches for patients with PAH. Recent studies showed that FABP (fatty acid-binding protein) 4 and FABP5 are expressed in endothelial cells (ECs) across multiple tissues, and circulating FABP4 level is elevated in patients with PAH. However, the role of endothelial FABP4/5 in the pathogenesis of PAH remains undetermined.

METHODS: FABP4/5 expression was examined in pulmonary arterial ECs and lung tissues from patients with idiopathic PAH and pulmonary hypertension (PH) rat models. Plasma proteome analysis was performed in human PAH samples. Echocardiography, hemodynamics, histology, and immunostaining were performed to evaluate the lung and heart PH phenotypes in Egln1Tie2Cre (CKO) mice and Egln1Tie2Cre/Fabp4/5-/- (TKO) mice. Bulk RNA sequencing (RNA-seq), single-cell RNA sequencing analysis, and spatial transcriptomic analysis were performed to understand the cellular and molecular mechanisms of endothelial FABP4/5-mediated PAH pathogenesis.

RESULTS: Both FABP4 and FABP5 were highly induced in ECs of CKO mice and pulmonary arterial ECs from patients with idiopathic PAH (IPAH) and in whole lungs of PH rats. Plasma levels of FABP4/5 were upregulated in patients with IPAH and directly correlated with severity of hemodynamics and biochemical parameters. Genetic deletion of both Fabp4 and Fabp5 in CKO mice caused a reduction of right ventricular systolic pressure and right ventricular hypertrophy, attenuated pulmonary vascular remodeling, and prevented the right heart failure secondary to PH. FABP4/5 deletion also normalized EC glycolysis and distal arterial programming, reduced reactive oxygen species and HIF (hypoxia-inducible factor)-2α expression, and decreased aberrant EC proliferation in CKO lungs.

CONCLUSIONS: PH causes aberrant expression of FABP4/5 in pulmonary ECs, which leads to enhanced EC glycolysis and distal arterial programming, contributing to the accumulation of arterial ECs and vascular remodeling and exacerbating the disease.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • 人源样本：IPAH 患者肺动脉内皮与肺组织 FABP4/5 表达检测，IPAH 血浆蛋白组分析与血流动力学/生化指标相关性分析。
  • 动物模型：Egln1Tie2Cre（CKO）与 Egln1Tie2Cre/Fabp4/5−/−（TKO）小鼠，PH 大鼠模型，行超声心动图、血流动力学、组织学与免疫染色表型评估。
  • 组学解析：bulk RNA-seq、single-cell RNA sequencing 与 spatial transcriptomics，解析内皮 FABP4/5 介导 PAH 的细胞与分子机制。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • FABP4 与 FABP5 在 CKO 小鼠肺内皮、IPAH 患者肺动脉内皮及 PH 大鼠全肺中均显著诱导。
  • IPAH 患者血浆 FABP4/5 升高，且与血流动力学严重程度及生化参数直接相关。
  • Fabp4/Fabp5 双基因缺失降低 CKO 小鼠右心室收缩压与右心室肥厚，减轻肺血管重构，并阻止继发于 PH 的右心衰竭。
  • FABP4/5 缺失使 EC 糖酵解与远端动脉程序化正常化，降低 ROS 与 HIF-2α 表达，减少异常内皮增殖。
  • 机制结论为：PH 诱导肺内皮 FABP4/5 异常表达，经增强 EC 糖酵解与远端动脉程序化促进动脉内皮细胞堆积与血管重构，加重疾病。

AI 解读 · 为什么入选

机制链完整（FABP4/5→内皮糖酵解/ROS→HIF-2α→异常增殖→重构）、采用内皮特异条件敲除叠加双基因缺失的严格 in vivo Rescue 验证、并有 IPAH 人组织与血浆蛋白组支持；单细胞与空间转录组提供了细胞状态与空间定位层面的新解读。

AI 解读 · 局限

  • 仅有摘要，缺少具体样本量、统计方法、单细胞/空间数据的细胞亚群定义与轨迹分析细节，机制链条的中间环节无法独立评估。
  • 提供的全文片段实为另一篇关于 FABP4 与胰岛素-eNOS/NO 通路的外文文献，并非本研究的正文，故不能据此补充证据。
  • 小鼠模型基于 Egln1 缺失，不能完全代表 IPAH 的遗传异质性；FABP4/5 下游糖酵解-HIF-2α 轴的直接分子互作尚未在摘要中阐明。

AI 解读 · 可借鉴之处

  • 可复用“单细胞 + 空间转录组定位候选分子 → 条件性内皮敲除 → 三基因杂交 Rescue（CKO vs TKO）”的因果验证路径，尤其适用于把分泌型/代谢型分子从相关性提升为驱动因素。
  • Fabp4/5 双基因缺失同时纠正 EC 糖酵解、ROS 与 HIF-2α 的设计提示：代谢-缺氧信号轴（glycolysis–ROS–HIF-2α）可作为肺血管重构研究的可干预中间表型读出，而非仅看形态学终点。
  • “远端动脉程序化”这一内皮状态概念可迁移至肺泡毛细血管与血管重塑研究，用于区分损伤后异常内皮亚群与正常毛细血管内皮，并可与成纤维细胞、免疫-基质 crosstalk 的空间邻域分析结合。

MEASUREMENT AND MAIN RESULTS: The single-cell atlas reveals marked changes in the PVOD endothelium compared to controls. EndMT and ectopic COL15A1+ systemic venous shunts and pulmonary vein endothelium are hallmarks of PVOD. Molecular analyses reveal activation of the integrated stress response (ISR)/ATF4, TGFβ signaling, MAPK signaling, and BRD4 are potential key players in the pathophysiology of PVOD. While PVOD and PAH share both pathological and some molecular mechanisms, ISR and TGFβ signaling are more prominent in PVOD than PAH.

CONCLUSIONS: These data indicate that endothelial dysfunction is a primary etiology in the development of PVOD with activation of multiple pathways with druggable targets.

AT A GLANCE COMMENTARY: Scientific Knowledge of the Subject: Pulmonary veno-occlusive disease (PVOD) is driven by progressive vasculopathy of the pulmonary veins and capillaries that results in significant pre-lung transplant mortality. Only 30% of PVOD cases can be explained by genetic mutations in EIF2AK4 , a critical protein translation factor associated with the integrated stress response. Thus, the majority of PVOD cases have no known molecular and cellular etiologies. What This Study Adds to the Field: This study generated a cell atlas of idiopathic PVOD in patients with the more common, non- EIF2AK4 mutation etiology. This cell atlas defines pathological cell state changes in the lung vasculature including aberrant populations in the endothelium of veins and capillaries. These data also provide key insight into the molecular underpinnings driving PVOD disease and identified both novel and common targetable pulmonary vascular disease pathways with known drugs that are currently under investigation or FDA approved.

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

UNLABELLED: Influenza infection increases cardiovascular risk in individuals with pre-existing atherosclerosis, yet the genome-wide transcriptional basis of this association remains undefined. We performed bulk RNA sequencing of aorta and lung tissues from high-fat diet-fed Apoe -/- mice infected with influenza A/PR/8/34 versus PBS-treated controls at day 15 post-infection, identifying 6,828 differentially expressed genes across both tissues (5,694 in the lung and 1,134 in the aorta; adjusted p < 0.05). In the lung, influenza sustained a CXCR3-driven chemokine storm, comprehensive Th1, Th17, and exhaustion T-cell activation programs, coordinated monocyte recruitment through Ccl2 , Ccl7 , Ccl8 , and Ccl12 , a broad acute phase response including Saa3 and A2m , alveolar identity collapse through loss of Nkx2-1 and surfactant proteins, and broad metabolic dysregulation involving suppression of fatty acid oxidation, mitochondrial function, and circadian metabolic regulators. In the aorta, influenza drove macrophage and DAMP activation through Trem1 , Fpr2 , and S100a8 , cytotoxic T-cell engagement, smooth muscle cell dedifferentiation through Myocd loss, convergent bioenergetic failure, and an ectopic lung-identity gene signature representing a previously undescribed cross-organ communication mechanism. Circadian clock and Hippo/YAP pathway suppression were shared across both tissues, identifying systemic vulnerabilities connecting pulmonary infection to vascular structural compromise. Consistent with the in vivo findings, influenza-infected Apoe -/- bone marrow-derived macrophages exhibited inflammasome activation, DAMP amplification, M1 polarization, and suppression of prostaglandin-mediated inflammatory resolution. Together these findings define a lung-to-aorta inflammatory and metabolic axis driving atherosclerotic plaque destabilization during the post-infection recovery period and identify CCR2/CXCR3 antagonism, NLRP3 inhibition, and YAP pathway activation as candidate therapeutic interventions.

GRAPHICAL ABSTRACT: Influenza infection drives atherosclerotic plaque destabilization.
