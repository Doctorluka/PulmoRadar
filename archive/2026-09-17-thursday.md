### 1. Sexually Dimorphic Role of Estrogen Receptor α in Preserving Right Ventricular Endothelial Integrity.

Arteriosclerosis, thrombosis, and vascular biology | 2026 Sep 10 | Jiajun Li, Tim Lahm
IF 2025：8.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Pharmacology and Molecular Medicine (J.L.), University of Colorado, Anschutz Medical Campus, Aurora.; Division oficine, Department of Medicine, National Jewish Health, Denver, CO (J.L., C.-S.C.W., I.P., T.L.).
- 领域：药理学与分子医学/内科方向，聚焦肺高血压中右心室内皮细胞与性别差异机制。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42717866/
- DOI：10.1161/ATVBAHA.126.325390

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Right ventricular (RV) adaptation to increased afterload determines survival in pulmonary hypertension (PH) and is sexually dimorphic and more preserved in females, mediated in part by protective ERα (estrogen receptor α) signaling in cardiomyocytes. However, effects of ERα on RV endothelial cells (RVECs), a critical mediator of RV adaptation, are unknown. We hypothesized that ERα exerts sexually dimorphic proangiogenic effects on RVECs in vitro and in vivo.
METHODS: We isolated RVECs from male and female rats with an ERαMut (ERα loss-of-function mutation) or wild-type controls and assessed angiogenesis end points. Male and female ERαMut or wild-type rats were treated with monocrotaline or underwent pulmonary arterial banding. Single-nucleus RNA sequencing was performed to investigate RVEC transcriptomic changes.
RESULTS: Compared with cells isolated from wild-type animals, RVECs from male and female ERαMut rats exhibited reduced ability to form pseudovascular networks, sprout, and migrate. RVECs from female ERαMut rats demonstrated increased apoptosis. Restoring migration rescued the impaired angiogenic phenotype in female ERαMut RVECs. Female monocrotaline-PH ERαMut rats exhibited increased RV hypertrophy and reduced RV capillary density before (10 days) and at the time of established PH (28 days). Female ERαMut pulmonary arterial banding rats also exhibited decreased RV capillary density. Capillary rarefaction was associated with decreased tip cell abundance and increased RVEC apoptosis, and a net loss of the endocardial RVEC subpopulation. Differentially expressed gene analysis and pathway analysis identified decreased expression of migration pathways and increased expression of apoptosis pathways in capillary and endocardial RVECs from female but not male monocrotaline-PH ERαMut rats.
CONCLUSIONS: ERα exhibits a sex-specific endothelial-intrinsic role that is essential for RV angiogenesis under both homeostatic and pathological conditions. This effect appears to stem from enhanced survival and migration capacity of capillary and endocardial RVECs. ERα may be a potential target for sex-specific RV-directed therapies in PH.

**What Is New?**
- 首次将ERα对右心室适应性的保护作用从既往已知的心肌细胞ERα信号，重新定位为RVEC的内皮细胞内在性（endothelial-intrinsic）机制。
- 发现ERα缺失选择性地损害雌性而非雄性RVEC的凋亡与迁移程序，从而把性别二态性从流行病学现象转化为一个可操作的性别特异因果节点。
- 通过ERαMut大鼠在monocrotaline-PH与pulmonary arterial banding两种后负荷模型中的一致表型，结合snRNA-seq，锁定capillary与endocardial RVEC亚群为ERα作用的关键细胞状态。
- 以恢复迁移（restoring migration）逆转雌性ERαMut RVEC成管缺陷，闭合了'ERα→迁移/存活→毛细血管密度→RV适应'的机制链。

**研究设计**
- 从ERαMut与wild-type雌雄大鼠分离RVEC，评估成管、出芽、迁移与凋亡等血管生成终点。
- ERαMut或wild-type雌雄大鼠分别接受monocrotaline或pulmonary arterial banding造模。
- 对RVEC进行single-nucleus RNA sequencing，比较转录组与通路变化。
- 在雌性ERαMut RVEC中进行迁移恢复实验，检验表型挽救。

**主要发现**
- 与wild-type相比，雌雄ERαMut大鼠来源的RVEC形成伪血管网络、出芽和迁移能力均下降。
- 雌性ERαMut RVEC凋亡增加；恢复迁移可挽救其受损的血管生成表型。
- 雌性monocrotaline-PH ERαMut大鼠在第10天（PH尚未确立）和第28天（PH已确立）均表现为RV肥厚加重与RV毛细血管密度降低；pulmonary arterial banding模型中也见RV毛细血管密度下降。
- 毛细血管稀疏伴随tip cell数量减少、RVEC凋亡增加以及endocardial RVEC亚群的净丢失。
- 差异表达与通路分析显示，雌性（而非雄性）monocrotaline-PH ERαMut大鼠的capillary与endocardial RVEC中迁移通路下调、凋亡通路上调。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整（ERα→内皮亚群存活/迁移→毛细血管密度→RV适应），性别二态性明确，采用ERαMut遗传模型加monocrotaline与pulmonary arterial banding双后负荷模型、snRNA-seq与rescue实验相互印证，直接切入肺高血压中决定预后的右心室适应这一核心问题。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无法评估样本量、统计方法、ERαMut具体突变位点及snRNA-seq的细胞数与聚类细节。
- 全部证据来自大鼠模型，缺乏人RV组织或人RVEC的验证。
- 分析聚焦RVEC自身，未解析内皮与心肌细胞、免疫细胞或成纤维细胞之间的crosstalk。

**可借鉴之处**
- ERαMut（loss-of-function）与wild-type、雌雄双向对照的设计，可作为验证任何性二态通路功能必要性的范式，比单纯性激素处理更能排除混杂。
- 先用体外成管/出芽/迁移/凋亡分型，再用rescue实验区分'迁移受限'与'存活下降'谁是限速环节，这一逻辑可直接迁移到其他内皮-基质研究。
- 在两种不同后负荷模型中平行做snRNA-seq，并按雌雄分层比较capillary与endocardial等内皮亚群的差异表达通路，是解析内皮亚群异质性与免疫-基质crosstalk的可复用设计。

### 2. Paracrine action of bone morphogenetic protein 3 in pulmonary arterial hypertension.

The European respiratory journal | 2026 Jul 30 | Aymen Halouani, Yassine Sassi
IF 2025：23.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：年内旗舰系统研究
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Fralin Biomedical Research Institute at Virginia Tech Carilion, Roanoke, VA, USA.
- 领域：根据Fralin Biomedical Research Institute at Virginia Tech Carilion affiliation与摘要，第一作者主要研究肺血管生物学与BMP信号，聚焦PAH中PASMC-内皮细胞旁分泌调控。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42532545/
- DOI：10.1183/13993003.02146-2025

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Despite advances in therapy, pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) remains progressive with poor survival. Although dysregulation of Bone Morphogenetic Protein (BMP) signaling is central to PAH, key modulators of this pathway remain incompletely defined.
OBJECTIVES: To identify the role of BMP3 in pulmonary vascular homeostasis and PAH.
METHODS: BMP3 expression was assessed in experimental pulmonary hypertension (PH) and in pulmonary vascular cells and plasma from PAH patients. In vitro studies were performed in human pulmonary artery smooth muscle cells (hPASMCs) and endothelial cells (hPAECs). Global and SMC-specific Bmp3-deficient mice were generated. Recombinant BMP3 and adeno-associated viruses (AAV) were used to overexpress BMP3 in animal models of PAH.
RESULTS: BMP3 is predominantly expressed in PASMCs and is consistently downregulated in experimental and human PAH. Secreted BMP3 from PAH-PASMCs and circulating BMP3 levels in PAH patients were significantly reduced. Functionally, PASMC-derived BMP3 inhibited the migration and proliferation of PAH-diseased PAEC. In both global and SMC-specific Bmp3-deficient mice, induction of PAH led to significantly exacerbated disease in middle-aged mice. A recombinant BMP3 prevented and reversed PAH in mice. Furthermore, lung-targeted overexpression of BMP3, via AAV1-BMP3, reversed pulmonary vascular remodeling and ameliorated cardiac function in both mice and rats. Mechanistically, BMP3 restored the balance between BMP/SMAD1,5,8 and TGF-β/SMAD2,3 signaling, and decreased the expression of cell cycle genes in PAECs.
CONCLUSIONS: BMP3 acts as a paracrine regulator of pulmonary vascular homeostasis and may contribute to the modulation of TGF-β/BMP signaling in PAH, highlighting its potential as a therapeutic target.

**What Is New?**
- 本研究将BMP3定义为PASMC优势表达且在实验性和人类PAH中一致下调的旁分泌调控节点，而不仅是BMP家族信号的伴随性改变。
- 通过全局与SMC特异性Bmp3缺失小鼠，证明PASMC来源BMP3对PAH易感性具有因果作用，且该效应在中老年小鼠中更显著。
- 重组BMP3和肺靶向AAV1-BMP3在mice与rats中既能预防也能逆转肺血管重构并改善心功能，机制上重新平衡BMP/SMAD1,5,8与TGF-β/SMAD2,3并下调PAEC cell cycle genes。

**研究设计**
- 人类PAH血浆与肺血管细胞BMP3表达检测+实验PH模型表达验证
- hPASMC与hPAEC体外功能实验（迁移、增殖）及分泌BMP3检测
- Global与SMC-specific Bmp3敲除小鼠PAH诱导实验
- Recombinant BMP3与AAV1-BMP3过表达在mice和rats中做预防/逆转
- 机制检测BMP/SMAD1,5,8与TGF-β/SMAD2,3平衡及PAEC cell cycle genes

**主要发现**
- BMP3主要表达于PASMCs，并在实验性和人类PAH中持续下调；PAH-PASMC分泌BMP3及患者循环BMP3均显著降低。
- PASMC来源BMP3可抑制PAH病变PAEC的迁移和增殖。
- Global和SMC-specific Bmp3缺失均加重中老年小鼠PAH诱导后的疾病表型。
- Recombinant BMP3可预防并逆转小鼠PAH；AAV1-BMP3肺靶向过表达在mice和rats中逆转肺血管重构并改善心功能。
- 机制上BMP3恢复BMP/SMAD1,5,8与TGF-β/SMAD2,3信号平衡，并降低PAEC cell cycle genes表达。

**为什么入选** 机制链较完整：从人/动物表达→细胞旁分泌功能→细胞特异性遗传缺失→重组蛋白与AAV增益功能→SMAD平衡与细胞周期机制；且覆盖mice与rats、预防与逆转，符合肺血管重构与BMP/TGF-β交叉调控的研究热点。

**局限**
- 仅为摘要，评估基于abstract_only，无法核对全文方法、统计与补充数据。
- 人类证据主要是表达/循环水平关联，摘要未显示BMP3在人类PAH中的直接因果干预。
- BMP3作用于PAEC的受体/直接下游靶点及AAV剂量、时程、性别/年龄分层等摘要未详述。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可借鉴PASMC→PAEC旁分泌研究范式：用PAH-PASMC条件培养基或共培养结合recombinant BMP3处理，检测PAEC迁移、增殖及SMAD平衡。

### 3. BRCC3 Regulation of ALK2 in Vascular Smooth Muscle Cells: Implication in Pulmonary Hypertension.

Circulation | 2024 Jul 09 | Hui Shen*, Ya Gao*, Kaizheng Gong
IF 2025：41.3 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：领域经典
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Cardiology, Affiliated Hospital of Yangzhou University, Yangzhou University, Institute of Cardiovascular Disease, Yangzhou Key Lab of Innovation Frontiers in Cardiovascular Disease, China (H.S., Y.G., D.G., M.T., Q. Yin, Z.L., X.L., Z.C., Y.Y., Z.Z., K.G.).
- 领域：扬州大学附属医院心血管病科/心血管病研究所团队，研究领域推断为心血管疾病、肺血管重塑及BMP/TGF-β信号机制。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38557054/
- DOI：10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.120.048191

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: An imbalance of antiproliferative BMP (bone morphogenetic protein) signaling and proliferative TGF-β (transforming growth factor-β) signaling is implicated in the development of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH). The posttranslational modification (eg, phosphorylation and ubiquitination) of TGF-β family receptors, including BMPR2 (bone morphogenetic protein type 2 receptor)/ALK2 (activin receptor-like kinase-2) and TGF-βR2/R1, and receptor-regulated Smads significantly affects their activity and thus regulates the target cell fate. BRCC3 modifies the activity and stability of its substrate proteins through K63-dependent deubiquitination. By modulating the posttranslational modifications of the BMP/TGF-β-PPARγ pathway, BRCC3 may play a role in pulmonary vascular remodeling, hence the pathogenesis of PAH.
METHODS: Bioinformatic analyses were used to explore the mechanism by which BRCC3 deubiquitinates ALK2. Cultured pulmonary artery smooth muscle cells (PASMCs), mouse models, and specimens from patients with idiopathic PAH were used to investigate the rebalance between BMP and TGF-β signaling in regulating ALK2 phosphorylation and ubiquitination in the context of pulmonary hypertension.
RESULTS: BRCC3 was significantly downregulated in PASMCs from patients with PAH and animals with experimental pulmonary hypertension. BRCC3, by de-ubiquitinating ALK2 at Lys-472 and Lys-475, activated receptor-regulated Smad1/5/9, which resulted in transcriptional activation of BMP-regulated PPARγ, p53, and Id1. Overexpression of BRCC3 also attenuated TGF-β signaling by downregulating TGF-β expression and inhibiting phosphorylation of Smad3. Experiments in vitro indicated that overexpression of BRCC3 or the de-ubiquitin-mimetic ALK2-K472/475R attenuated PASMC proliferation and migration and enhanced PASMC apoptosis. In SM22α-BRCC3-Tg mice, pulmonary hypertension was ameliorated because of activation of the ALK2-Smad1/5-PPARγ axis in PASMCs. In contrast, Brcc3-/- mice showed increased susceptibility of experimental pulmonary hypertension because of inhibition of the ALK2-Smad1/5 signaling.
CONCLUSIONS: These results suggest a pivotal role of BRCC3 in sustaining pulmonary vascular homeostasis by maintaining the integrity of the BMP signaling (ie, the ALK2-Smad1/5-PPARγ axis) while suppressing TGF-β signaling in PASMCs. Such rebalance of BMP/TGF-β pathways is translationally important for PAH alleviation.

**What Is New?**
- 首次在PAH患者PASMC及实验性肺高血压模型中明确BRCC3显著下调，并将BRCC3缺失与BMP/TGF-β信号失衡直接关联，补足了PAH中去泛素化酶调控BMP受体活性的认识空白。
- 鉴定出ALK2的K63去泛素化新位点Lys-472和Lys-475，证明BRCC3依赖该位点激活Smad1/5/9并上调PPARγ、p53、Id1，从而闭合了“受体翻译后修饰—下游转录—细胞命运”机制链。
- 通过SM22α-BRCC3-Tg小鼠和Brcc3-/-小鼠进行双向体内遗传验证，并结合人IPAH标本，证明PASMC中BRCC3-ALK2-Smad1/5-PPARγ轴既是保护节点也是实验性PH易感节点。

**研究设计**
- Bioinformatic analyses预测BRCC3去泛素化ALK2的机制。
- 培养PASMC、小鼠PH模型及人iPAH标本联合验证。
- 使用BRCC3 gain/loss of function、ALK2-K472/475R去泛素化拟似突变体、SM22α-BRCC3-Tg小鼠和Brcc3-/-小鼠。
- 检测ALK2泛素化/磷酸化、Smad1/5/9与Smad3、PPARγ/p53/Id1、PASMC增殖/迁移/凋亡及在体PH表型。

**主要发现**
- BRCC3在PAH患者PASMC和实验性PH动物中显著下调。
- BRCC3通过K63去泛素化ALK2的Lys-472和Lys-475，激活受体调控的Smad1/5/9，并转录激活BMP调控的PPARγ、p53和Id1。
- BRCC3过表达还下调TGF-β表达并抑制Smad3磷酸化，从而削弱TGF-β信号；过表达BRCC3或ALK2-K472/475R可抑制PASMC增殖和迁移、促进凋亡。
- SM22α-BRCC3-Tg小鼠因PASMC中ALK2-Smad1/5-PPARγ轴激活而PH减轻；Brcc3-/-小鼠因ALK2-Smad1/5信号受抑而更易发生实验性PH。
- 人IPAH标本与小鼠模型共同支持BRCC3通过维持BMP信号完整性并抑制TGF-β信号来“再平衡”BMP/TGF-β通路。

**为什么入选** 机制链较完整：从去泛素化酶BRCC3→ALK2 K63去泛素化位点→Smad1/5/9-PPARγ→PASMC表型→PH；并有双向遗传rescue/易感验证及人IPAH组织，发表于Circulation，基础机制与肺血管重塑高度相关。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要评估，未获取OA全文；实验细节、样本量、统计方法和具体rescue设计无法核验。
- 摘要未提供BRCC3如何选择性影响PPARγ/p53/Id1的机制细节，也未说明内皮、成纤维或免疫细胞等其他细胞类型的贡献。
- 摘要未报告BRCC3的上游调控机制及其在人类PAH中的直接因果证据（如遗传变异或药物干预），转化意义仍需全文验证。

**可借鉴之处**
- 位点特异性去泛素化拟似突变体（ALK2-K472/475R）可作为解析BMP受体翻译后修饰与信号偏向的工具；该“去泛素化酶—底物—下游转录轴”验证逻辑可迁移至其他肺血管重塑相关信号节点的因果验证。
- PASMC特异性的转基因过表达与基因敲除正反验证，结合人IPAH组织，适合建立从细胞表型（增殖、迁移、凋亡）到在体PH表型的完整因果链；若扩展至成纤维细胞、免疫-基质crosstalk或肺泡/肺血管微环境，可检验该轴是否具有细胞类型特异性。

### 1. Immunomodulation of the Innate Host Response by Mesenchymal-Derived Versican during Influenza A Virus Infection.

American journal of physiology. Lung cellular and molecular physiology | 2026 Sep 16 | Jourdan E Brune, Charles W Frevert
IF 2025：4.0 · JCR：Q2 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Comparative Medicine, University of Washington, Seattle, WA, USA.; Center for Lung Biology, University of Washington at South Lake Union, Seattle, WA, USA.
- 领域：第一作者隶属于 University of Washington 比较医学系与 Center for Lung Biology，研究方向为肺部感染中细胞外基质与固有免疫的相互作用，一句话概括为肺基质-免疫界面生物学。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42747347/
- DOI：10.1152/ajplung.00265.2025

**Abstract**

Viral and bacterial lung infections place a significant burden on public health. Versican, an extracellular matrix (ECM) chondroitin sulfate proteoglycan, coordinates the innate immune response in multiple experimental models. Versican's potential as an immunomodulatory molecule makes it a promising therapeutic target for controlling the host's immune response to lung infection. However, versican's contribution to lung inflammation, injury, and immune cell activity during influenza A virus (IAV) infection represents a critical knowledge gap. To address our central hypothesis that mesenchymal-derived versican is pro-inflammatory and enhances the innate immune response to IAV infection, we generated a tamoxifen-inducible mouse deficient in mesenchymal-derived versican (B6. Col1a2-CreERT+/-/Vcantm1.1Cwf, Col1a2/Vcan-/-). We report that mesenchymal-derived versican plays a critical role in neutrophil, monocyte, and dendritic cell migration into the lungs and airways early in IAV infection. Intriguingly, mesenchymal-derived versican deficiency had the most substantial negative impact on neutrophil emigration into the lungs. We found that neutrophils were less adhesive to the ECM of Col1a2/Vcan-/- mouse lung fibroblasts (mLFs), which had a significant decrease in versican compared to wild-type mLFs. Additionally, Col1a2/Vcan-/- mLFs treated with poly(I:C) in vitro have reduced cell-associated hyaluronan. These findings suggest that fibroblast-derived versican is necessary for adhesion to lung fibroblasts by neutrophils as they transit into the lung interstitium and airways from the pulmonary vasculature. Our findings demonstrate that mesenchymal-derived versican is a key regulator of the early host immune responses to IAV.

**What Is New?**
- 首次用可诱导的间充质特异性 versican 缺失小鼠（B6. Col1a2-CreERT+/-/Vcantm1.1Cwf）证明，间充质来源而非全部来源的 versican 是 IAV 感染早期中性粒细胞、单核细胞和树突状细胞向肺与气道募集的关键调控者。
- 将 versican 的促炎作用从相关性推进到一条闭合的细胞机制链：versican 缺失的肺成纤维细胞 ECM 使中性粒细胞黏附下降，从而解释细胞从肺血管向肺间质与气道迁移的受阻。
- 新发现成纤维细胞 versican 与 hyaluronan 功能耦联——Col1a2/Vcan-/- mLF 经 poly(I:C) 刺激后细胞相关 hyaluronan 减少，提示 versican 是固有免疫刺激下成纤维细胞基质重塑的上游节点。

**研究设计**
- 构建 tamoxifen 诱导的间充质特异性 versican 缺失小鼠 B6. Col1a2-CreERT+/-/Vcantm1.1Cwf（简写 Col1a2/Vcan-/-）。

Transcriptomic analyses identified inflammatory, metabolic, chemokine-associated, and smooth muscle cell-like programs in CD133 + cells from PH lungs. Lineage tracing showed that CD133 + cells contributed to endothelial and smooth muscle cell compartments during experimental PH. Genetic ablation of CD133 + cells attenuated hypoxia-induced PH and pulmonary vascular remodeling, whereas Cxcr4 deletion in CD133 + cells reduced PH severity.
CONCLUSIONS: CD133 + progenitor cells are functional contributors to pulmonary vascular remodeling, and CXCR4 signaling mediates their pathogenic activity. Targeting pathogenic CD133 + cell states or CXCL12/CXCR4 signaling may provide a strategy to limit vascular remodeling in PH.

### 3. Poly-Pharmacologic Disruption of the Proliferative-to-Mesenchymal Fate Branch Point Reverses EndMT and Pulmonary Hypertension.

Research square | 2026 Jul 23 | Dinesh Bharti, Ruslan Rafikov


- 来源：preprint
- 槽位：预印本
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Medicine, Division of Pulmonary, Critical Care, Sleep & Occupational Medicine, Indiana University, Indianapolis, Indiana 46202.
- 领域：印第安纳大学医学院内科呼吸、危重症、睡眠与职业病学系；由单位与摘要推断方向为肺动脉高压与肺血管重塑的转化/基础研究（具体课题组方向不详）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42539088/
- DOI：10.1038/s41587-019-0336-3

**Abstract**

Endothelial-to-mesenchymal transition (EndMT) drives the vascular remodeling in pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH), yet the regulators that commit endothelial cells to this fate and whether they are pharmacologically addressable remain poorly defined. We report TK22, a small molecule that selectively erases the EndMT-competent endothelial state and reverses experimental pulmonary hypertension. TK22 was developed by structure-based design as an ATP-competitive CDK5 inhibitor (IC50 181 nM). Unbiased profiling across 394 kinases, however, revealed that TK22 inhibits the interphase cell-cycle kinases CDK2/3/4/6 node as well as a pro-mesenchymal node of AMPK-related kinases (NUAK1 and SIK1/2/3) together with PDGFRβ and MLK3, placing its activity at the intersection of proliferative and EndMT control rather than at a single node. Owing to this dual-node action, TK22 produced a strikingly clean cellular phenotype. In primary murine lung endothelial cells, TK22 reversed TGF-β1-induced EndMT across morphological, proteomic, and functional readouts, normalizing α-SMA, SM22, and Calponin, restoring angiogenic tube formation, abolishing acquired smooth-muscle-like contractility, and returning TGF-β1-driven hyperproliferation to baseline without suppressing normal endothelial growth. Single-cell RNA sequencing resolved the basis of this precision: TGF-β1 redirected a cycling Mki67+ endothelial subpopulation into an EndMT-committed state (Tnnt2, Ptgs2, Serpine1) and induced an associated metabolic-stress program (Egln3, Pfkfb3, Pdk1, Bnip3). Cross-validated PHATE and Monocle3 trajectory inference defined a directional proliferative-to-mesenchymal fate transition, and kernel density estimation revealed a stable EndMT cell-state attractor. TK22 selectively eliminated the EndMT-competent and metabolic-stress subpopulations while leaving the remaining endothelial landscape intact. This population-level precision of TK22 is consistent with its dual engagement of the anti-cell-cycle and anti-mesenchymal kinase nodes. In the Su5416/hypoxia rat PH model, TK22 normalized right ventricular systolic pressure, Fulton index, and cardiac function. Together, these findings reframe pharmacologic EndMT control: inhibition at the proliferative-to-mesenchymal fate branch point, through coordinated suppression of cell-cycle and mesenchymal kinases, achieves high selectivity for erasing a pathological endothelial fate state. These findings propose TK22 as a mechanistically precise agent for EndMT-driven vascular disease and establish single-cell trajectory analysis as an essential readout of cellular target engagement and cell-state precision.

**What Is New?**
- 首次把 EndMT 的“增殖→间充质命运分叉点”定义为可药物干预的因果节点：TK22 虽按 ATP 竞争性 CDK5 抑制剂（IC50 181 nM）结构导向设计，但 394 激酶无偏谱显示其真实作用落在 CDK2/3/4/6 细胞周期节点与 NUAK1/SIK1-3 促间充质节点（另含 PDGFRβ、MLK3）的交汇处，从而把“多靶点”重构为分支点协同抑制的设计逻辑。
- 以 scRNA-seq 首次 pinpoint 出 TGF-β1 驱动下 cycling Mki67+ 内皮亚群向 EndMT 承诺态（Tnnt2、Ptgs2、Serpine1）的方向性命运转变，并伴随 Egln3/Pfkfb3/Pdk1/Bnip3 代谢应激程序，PHATE 与 Monocle3 轨迹推断加核密度估计进一步给出稳定的 EndMT 细胞状态 attractor。
- 在 Su5416/hypoxia 大鼠 PH 模型中证明 TK22 可逆转 RVSP、Fulton index 与心功能，并提出单细胞轨迹分析可作为细胞靶点占领与 cell-state precision 的必要读出，而不仅是 bulk 标志物变化。

**研究设计**
- 结构导向设计 ATP 竞争性 CDK5 抑制剂 TK22（IC50 181 nM）
- 394 激酶无偏谱分析确定实际靶点谱（CDK2/3/4/6、NUAK1、SIK1/2/3、PDGFRβ、MLK3）
- 原代小鼠肺内皮细胞 + TGF-β1 诱导 EndMT 模型，形态/蛋白组/功能多 readout
- scRNA-seq 加 PHATE 与 Monocle3 轨迹推断、核密度估计细胞状态 attractor
- Su5416/hypoxia 大鼠 PH 模型体内验证血流动力学与心功能

**主要发现**
- TK22 在 394 激酶谱中同时抑制间期细胞周期 CDK2/3/4/6 节点与 AMPK 相关促间充质激酶 NUAK1、SIK1/2/3，并作用于 PDGFRβ 与 MLK3。
- 原代小鼠肺内皮细胞中 TK22 逆转 TGF-β1 诱导的 EndMT：α-SMA、SM22、Calponin 正常化，血管成管能力恢复，获得性平滑肌样收缩性消失，TGF-β1 驱动的过度增殖回到基线且不抑制正常内皮生长。
- scRNA-seq 显示 TGF-β1 将 cycling Mki67+ 内皮亚群重定向为 EndMT 承诺态（Tnnt2、Ptgs2、Serpine1）并诱导代谢应激程序（Egln3、Pfkfb3、Pdk1、Bnip3）。
- TK22 选择性清除 EndMT-competent 与代谢应激亚群，而其余内皮细胞图谱保持完整。
- 在 Su5416/hypoxia 大鼠 PH 模型中，TK22 使右心室收缩压、Fulton index 与心功能正常化。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整且闭环：结构导向药物设计→394 激酶谱确定真实靶点→原代肺内皮 EndMT 的功能性 rescue→scRNA-seq 揭示命运分叉与稳定 attractor→体内大鼠 PH 模型表型逆转；核心概念创新在于用“双节点协同抑制”实现病理细胞状态的选择性清除，并引入单细胞轨迹作为 target engagement 读出。来源为 Research Square 预印本（尚未见同行评议全文）。

**局限**
- 仅能获取摘要，无全文与补充材料，激酶谱、蛋白组、scRNA-seq 的样本量、统计方法与独立验证细节无法评估。

**可借鉴之处**
