### 1. Maresin 1 promotes alveolar epithelial regeneration in LPS-induced acute lung injury via the Rac1-WASF2-Arp2/3 pathway.

International immunopharmacology | 2026 Nov 14 | Bowen Ji, Shengxing Zheng
IF 2025：4.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Anesthesia and Critical Care, The Second Affiliated Hospital and Yuying Children's Hospital of Wenzhou Medical University, Key Laboratory of Pediatric Anesthesiology, Ministry of Education, Precision Anesthesiology Key Laboratory of Zhejiang Province, Wenzhou, China.
- 领域：第一作者来自温州医科大学附属第二医院麻醉与危重症医学科及儿童麻醉重点实验室，研究领域可概括为麻醉与危重症中的急性肺损伤/ARDS 肺泡上皮修复。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/43033544/
- DOI：10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117666

**Abstract**

PURPOSE: Acute respiratory distress syndrome (ARDS) is marked by alveolar epithelial injury, and promoting alveolar regeneration is a promising therapeutic strategy. Maresin1 (MaR1), a specialized pro-resolving mediator, exhibits anti-inflammatory and pro-regenerative properties; however, its role in alveolar epithelial regeneration remains unclear. This study aimed to investigate whether MaR1 alleviates lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced acute lung injury (ALI) by promoting alveolar epithelial regeneration and to elucidate the involvement of the Rac1-WASF2-Arp2/3 pathway.
MATERIALS AND METHODS: An LPS-induced ALI mouse model was established, and primary alveolar type II (AT2) cells were isolated from rats. Lung injury was assessed by histopathology (H&E) and immunofluorescence for AT2 (pro-SFTPC), intermediate transition cells(Krt-8) and AT1 (PDPN) markers. AT2 cell proliferation and transdifferentiation were evaluated by flow cytometry (cell cycle analysis), qRT-PCR (SPC and AQP5), and alveolar organoid formation assays. Pharmacological inhibitors CK666 (Arp2/3 complex inhibitor) and NSC23766 (Rac1 inhibitor) were used to investigate the underlying mechanisms. Protein-level validation was performed by GTP-Rac1 pull-down and Western blotting.
RESULTS: MaR1 treatment significantly reduced pulmonary inflammation (IL-6, IL-1β, TNF-α) and promoted recovery of alveolar structure. MaR1 enhanced AT2 cell proliferation (increased KI67+cells and G1-to-S/G2 phase transition), reduced transitional Krt8+intermediate cells, and increased AT1 cell marker expression (PDPN, AQP5). MaR1 also improved alveolar organoid formation, size, and the proportion of PDPN+ organoids. Notably, CK666 and NSC23766 abolished MaR1-mediated AT2 proliferation, transdifferentiation, and alveolar structural repair. Mechanistically, MaR1 activated GTP-Rac1 without altering Rac1 gene expression, and upregulated WASF2 and Arp3 at both mRNA and protein levels, whereas Arp2 remained unchanged, indicating that MaR1 promotes alveolar regeneration via the Rac1-WASF2-Arp2/3 pathway.
CONCLUSION: Maresin 1 promotes alveolar epithelial regeneration in LPS-induced acute lung injury via the Rac1-WASF2-Arp2/3 pathway. These findings highlight epithelial regeneration as a critical determinant of recovery in ARDS and suggest that MaR1 represents a promising therapeutic strategy for acute lung injury.

**What Is New?**
- 此前 MaR1 主要被视为促炎症消退介质，本研究将其作用推进到肺泡上皮再生层面，证明 MaR1 可促进 AT2 细胞增殖、减少 Krt8+ 过渡态并推动向 AT1 标志物表达方向转分化。
- 本研究以 CK666 和 NSC23766 的药理阻断把 Rac1-WASF2-Arp2/3 通路确立为 MaR1 促 AT2 增殖、转分化及肺泡结构修复所必需的因果节点，而非仅伴随激活。
- 本研究将 LPS-ALI 小鼠体内的肺泡结构修复、原代 AT2 细胞功能与肺泡类器官形成和生长串联起来，为 MaR1 的促再生效应提供了跨模型的功能验证。

**研究设计**
- 建立 LPS 诱导的小鼠 ALI 模型，用 H&E 与免疫荧光评估肺损伤及 AT2（pro-SFTPC）、Krt8+ 过渡细胞和 AT1（PDPN）状态。
- 分离大鼠原代 AT2 细胞，采用流式细胞周期分析、qRT-PCR（SPC、AQP5）和肺泡类器官形成实验评估增殖与转分化。
- 使用 CK666（Arp2/3 复合物抑制剂）和 NSC23766（Rac1 抑制剂）进行药理阻断，验证通路必要性。
- 通过 GTP-Rac1 pull-down 与 Western blotting 检测 Rac1 活化及 WASF2、Arp3、Arp2 蛋白水平。

**主要发现**
- 在 LPS-ALI 小鼠中，MaR1 降低 IL-6、IL-1β、TNF-α，并促进肺泡结构恢复。
- MaR1 增加 KI67+ AT2 细胞和 G1-to-S/G2 期转变，提示 AT2 增殖增强。
- MaR1 减少 Krt8+ 过渡细胞，并提高 AT1 标志物 PDPN、AQP5 表达。
- MaR1 改善肺泡类器官形成、大小及 PDPN+ 类器官比例。
- CK666 或 NSC23766 取消 MaR1 的促增殖、转分化和结构修复效应；MaR1 激活 GTP-Rac1 而不改变 Rac1 基因表达，并上调 WASF2 与 Arp3，Arp2 不变。

**为什么入选** 机制链较清晰：MaR1→GTP-Rac1→WASF2/Arp2/3→AT2 增殖/转分化→肺泡结构修复；同时具备 LPS-ALI 体内模型、原代 AT2、肺泡类器官和两种药理阻断的 rescue 逻辑，发表于 International Immunopharmacology，适合作为肺泡上皮再生研究的可复用范式。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无 OA 全文，无法评估样本量、剂量-时程、盲法与统计细节。
- 小鼠 ALI 模型与大鼠原代 AT2/类器官存在物种不匹配，且缺乏遗传学 rescue（如 Rac1 或 WASF2 敲低/敲除）进一步确认因果。
- 未说明 MaR1 如何上游激活 Rac1，也未明确 WASF2-Arp2/3 与 AT2 转分化之间的直接分子连接。

**可借鉴之处**
- AT2 再生研究可采用“增殖（KI67/细胞周期）—过渡态（Krt8+）—终末标志（PDPN/AQP5）—类器官”的连续读出，避免只用单一标志物判断修复。
- 以 alveolar organoid 叠加通路抑制剂和 GTP-Rac1 pull-down 做因果验证，比单纯比较表达水平更能区分驱动因素与伴随现象。
- 该模型适合进一步加入谱系追踪或 scRNA-seq，解析 MaR1 是直接作用于 AT2，还是通过免疫-成纤维细胞微环境间接促进肺泡修复。

### 2. YAP/TAZ activity in PDGFRα-expressing alveolar fibroblasts modulates AT2 proliferation through Wnt4.

Cell reports | 2025 May 27 | Jane Y Song, Jason R Rock
IF 2025：7.5 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：三年内旗舰系统研究
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Immunology Discovery, Genentech, Inc., 1 DNA Way, South San Francisco, CA 94080, USA.
- 领域：第一作者来自 Genentech, Inc. Department of Immunology Discovery，主要研究领域可推断为肺免疫学/基质-上皮互作。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40333185/
- DOI：10.1016/j.celrep.2025.115645

**Abstract**

The Hippo pathway, mediated by its transcriptional effectors Yes-associated protein 1 (YAP) and WW-domain-containing transcription regulator 1 (TAZ), is crucial in maintaining lung homeostasis and facilitating injury repair. While its roles in epithelial cells are well established, its regulatory effects on lung fibroblasts remain less understood. We engineered a mouse model for the inducible knockdown of YAP/TAZ and showed that fibroblast-specific knockdown enhances PDGFRα+ alveolar fibroblasts' support for alveolar-epithelial-stem-cell-derived organoids in vitro. Single-cell profiling revealed changes in fibroblast subpopulations, including the emergence of a Wnt4+ enriched subpopulation. Epigenomic analyses revealed shifts in transcription factor motif enrichment in both fibroblasts and epithelial cells due to fibroblast YAP/TAZ suppression. Further computational and in vivo analyses confirmed increased Wnt signaling and Wnt4 expression in PDGFRα-lineage+ fibroblasts, which enhanced SPC+ alveolar type 2 (AT2) cell proliferation. These findings highlight a mechanistic role of YAP/TAZ in PDGFRα+ alveolar fibroblasts in supporting AT2 cell maintenance and proliferation via Wnt4 secretion.

**What Is New?**
- 本研究通过可诱导的成纤维细胞特异性 YAP/TAZ 敲低小鼠，将 Hippo/YAP-TAZ 的肺内作用从已较明确的上皮细胞拓展到 PDGFRα+ 肺泡成纤维细胞，证明后者是支持 AT2 维持与增殖的调控节点。
- 单细胞图谱在 YAP/TAZ 抑制后识别出 Wnt4+ 富集的成纤维细胞亚群，并伴随成纤维细胞与上皮细胞转录因子 motif 富集改变，提示基质遗传扰动可重塑上皮转录程序。
- 研究进一步在体内将机制闭合为 PDGFRα-lineage+ 成纤维细胞中 Wnt4/Wnt 信号升高并促进 SPC+ AT2 增殖，而不仅是体外 organoid 支持现象。

**研究设计**
- 可诱导成纤维细胞特异性 YAP/TAZ 敲低小鼠
- PDGFRα+ 肺泡成纤维细胞与肺泡上皮干细胞来源 organoids 共培养
- scRNA-seq 分析成纤维细胞亚群变化
- 表观基因组/转录因子 motif 富集分析
- 计算预测与体内验证 Wnt4/Wnt 信号及 SPC+ AT2 增殖

**主要发现**
- 成纤维细胞特异性 YAP/TAZ 敲低增强 PDGFRα+ 肺泡成纤维细胞对肺泡上皮干细胞来源 organoids 的支持。
- scRNA-seq 显示成纤维细胞亚群组成改变，出现 Wnt4+ 富集亚群。
- 表观基因组分析显示成纤维细胞和上皮细胞的转录因子 motif 富集均发生改变。
- 计算与体内分析证实 PDGFRα-lineage+ 成纤维细胞中 Wnt 信号和 Wnt4 表达增加，并增强 SPC+ AT2 增殖。
- 综上，YAP/TAZ 在 PDGFRα+ 肺泡成纤维细胞中通过 Wnt4 分泌调节 AT2 维持与增殖。

**为什么入选** 机制链从成纤维细胞 YAP/TAZ 到 Wnt4 分泌再到 AT2 增殖；结合可诱导遗传敲低、organoid 功能读出、单细胞与表观基因组筛选及体内验证，补足肺 Hippo 通路研究中的基质细胞视角；发表于 Cell Reports。

**局限**
- 仅提供摘要，无 OA 全文，无法评估实验细节、样本量、统计与对照。
- Wnt4 对 AT2 增殖的必要性/充分性及 YAP/TAZ 调控 Wnt4 的直接分子机制在摘要中未完全展开。
- 缺少人肺组织或原代人细胞验证（摘要未提及）。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可借鉴成纤维细胞特异性可诱导敲低联合肺泡上皮干细胞来源 organoid 共培养，作为评估基质细胞支持上皮干细胞能力的功能读出。
- scRNA-seq 与表观基因组/转录因子 motif 富集联合，可把基质细胞的遗传扰动映射到上皮细胞状态变化，适合研究 fibroblast–AT2 crosstalk。
- PDGFRα-lineage 标记与 Wnt4+ 亚群思路可用于肺泡修复、纤维化或血管重塑中的基质异质性研究；本文未涉及肺血管。

### 3. Distinct Mesenchymal Lineages and Niches Promote Epithelial Self-Renewal and Myofibrogenesis in the Lung.

Cell | 2017 Sep 07 | Jarod A Zepp, Edward E Morrisey
IF 2025：45.5 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：领域经典
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Medicine, Division of Pediatric Cardiology, Children's Hospital of Philadelphia, Philadelphia, PA 19104, USA; Penn Center for Pulmonary Biology, University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, PA 19104, USA; Penn Cardiovascular Institute, University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, PA 19104, USA.
- 领域：第一作者主要研究领域：依据 Children's Hospital of Philadelphia 小儿心内科及肺生物学/心血管研究所 affiliations，推测为肺与心血管发育及间充质细胞生物学（具体方向不详）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28886382/
- DOI：10.1016/j.cell.2017.07.034

**Abstract**

The lung is an architecturally complex organ comprising a heterogeneous mixture of various epithelial and mesenchymal lineages. We use single-cell RNA sequencing and signaling lineage reporters to generate a spatial and transcriptional map of the lung mesenchyme. We find that each mesenchymal lineage has a distinct spatial address and transcriptional profile leading to unique niche regulatory functions. The mesenchymal alveolar niche cell is Wnt responsive, expresses Pdgfrα, and is critical for alveolar epithelial cell growth and self-renewal. In contrast, the Axin2+ myofibrogenic progenitor cell preferentially generates pathologically deleterious myofibroblasts after injury. Analysis of the secretome and receptome of the alveolar niche reveals functional pathways that mediate growth and self-renewal of alveolar type 2 progenitor cells, including IL-6/Stat3, Bmp, and Fgf signaling. These studies define the cellular and molecular framework of lung mesenchymal niches and reveal the functional importance of developmental pathways in promoting self-renewal versus a pathological response to tissue injury.

**What Is New?**
- 通过 scRNA-seq 与信号谱系报告基因，作者首次将肺间充质拆分为具有不同空间地址和转录谱的谱系，并显示每个谱系承担不同的 niche 调控功能。
- 作者新定义 Wnt 响应性、表达 Pdgfrα 的 mesenchymal alveolar niche cell 是支持肺泡上皮细胞生长与自我更新的关键 niche，而 Axin2+ myofibrogenic progenitor 是损伤后病理性 myofibroblast 的优先来源。
- 通过分泌组-受体组分析，作者把肺泡 niche 对 AT2 祖细胞生长/自我更新的支持归结到 IL-6/Stat3、Bmp 和 Fgf 通路，并将发育相关通路置于“自我更新 vs 损伤后肌纤维化”的功能分叉上。

**研究设计**
- 单细胞 RNA 测序（scRNA-seq）联合信号谱系报告基因。
- 构建肺间充质的空间地址与转录谱图谱。
- 分泌组与受体组分析候选 niche-上皮信号通路。
- 评估肺泡上皮细胞生长/自我更新及损伤后 myofibroblast 生成。

**主要发现**
- 肺间充质由多个谱系组成，每个谱系具有 distinct spatial address 和 transcriptional profile，并对应 unique niche regulatory functions。
- mesenchymal alveolar niche cell 为 Wnt responsive、表达 Pdgfrα，对 alveolar epithelial cell growth 和 self-renewal 至关重要。
- Axin2+ myofibrogenic progenitor cell 在损伤后优先生成 pathologically deleterious myofibroblasts。
- alveolar niche 的 secretome/receptome 揭示 IL-6/Stat3、Bmp、Fgf 等通路介导 alveolar type 2 progenitor cell 的生长与自我更新。
- 研究提出发育相关通路在促进自我更新与驱动损伤后病理反应之间具有功能重要性。

**为什么入选** 以单细胞图谱+谱系报告基因拆解肺间充质异质性，机制链连接 niche→AT2 自我更新与损伤→Axin2+ 祖细胞→myofibroblast，并用 secretome/receptome 给出可检验通路；Cell 发表，概念对肺泡再生和纤维化均关键。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要，无 OA 全文；样本量、统计方法、体内遗传学验证及 rescue 细节无法评估。
- 摘要未提供人类组织或人类疾病验证信息，转化相关性未知。
- 各信号通路对 AT2 自我更新的必要性/充分性及细胞来源特异性在摘要中未展开。

**可借鉴之处**
- 用 signaling lineage reporter + scRNA-seq 将间充质异质性映射到空间 niche，可用于研究 fibroblast 亚群与上皮/免疫细胞 crosstalk。
- 分泌组-受体组配对分析提供从 niche 细胞到上皮祖细胞的配体-受体候选通路筛选框架，适合肺泡生物学中验证 AT2 自我更新信号。
- 将稳态自我更新与损伤后 myofibrogenesis 作为两个可分离的 lineage/niche 程序来设计比较实验，可借鉴到肺纤维化及肺血管 remodeling 的间充质祖细胞研究。

### 1. Perivascular stromal cells derived from CD34+CD45+ cells promote pulmonary vascular barrier repair in a rat model of phosgene-induced acute lung injury via the VEGF-PI3K/AKT/eNOS signaling pathway.

International immunopharmacology | 2026 Nov 15 | Yu Dun, Jie Shen
IF 2025：4.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Center of Emergency and Critical Medicine, Jinshan Hospital of Fudan University, Shanghai 201508, China; Research Center for Chemical Injury, Emergency and Critical Medicine of Fudan University, Shanghai 201508, China; Key Laboratory of Chemical Injury, Emergency and Critical Medicine of Shanghai Municipal Health Commission, Shanghai 201508, China.
- 领域：第一作者单位聚焦急诊与危重症医学及化学性肺损伤，研究方向可概括为 phosgene/化学伤相关急性肺损伤与细胞治疗。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42772040/
- DOI：10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117455

**Abstract**

Phosgene inhalation causes acute lung injury (ALI) with high mortality and no effective antidote, and stem cell therapy is limited by poor cell survival and low engraftment efficiency. Here, we identified a rare endogenous CD34+CD45+ cell fraction that reside in bone marrow, peripheral blood, and bronchoalveolar lavage fluid (∼1% baseline), exhibits a "decrease-then-recovery" dynamic after phosgene injury, and displays MSC-like properties with trilineage (osteogenic, adipogenic, and chondrogenic) potential in vitro. Intravenous administration of expanded CD34+CD45+-derived stromal cells (CD34/CD45-DSCs) significantly alleviated pulmonary vascular leakage and inflammatory in a rat model of phosgene-induced ALI (P-ALI). In vivo bioluminescence imaging further revealed that CD34/CD45-DSCs exhibited prolonged pulmonary retention compared with BMSCs (11.3 days vs. 48 h), proliferated, engrafted around the pulmonary vasculature, and acquired a perivascular stromal cell (PVSC)-like phenotype expressing late-stage mesenchymal progenitor, pericyte, and fibroblast markers. In a bleomycin-induced pulmonary fibrosis model, CD34/CD45-DSCs did not exacerbate fibrotic changes at day 28. Mechanistically, VEGF knockdown or neutralization abolished the barrier-protective effect, which was rescued by an AKT agonist and blocked by an inhibitor. Moreover, CD34/CD45-DSCs upregulated tight junction proteins ZO-1 and Occludin in injured endothelial cells via VEGF-PI3K/AKT/eNOS signaling. Collectively, our study defines the origin, spatiotemporal dynamics, perivasucular engraftment, PVSC-like fate acquisition, and VEGF-PI3K/AKT/eNOS-dependent mechanism of CD34/CD45-DSCs, supporting their potential as a relatively safe, long-engrafting, and mechanistically transparent cell-based strategy for ALI.

**What Is New?**
- 本研究定义了一群存在于骨髓、外周血和 BALF 中、约占基线 1% 且损伤后呈“先降后回升”动态的 CD34+CD45+ 细胞，并证明其可扩增为具三系分化潜能的 MSC 样基质细胞。
- 与既往 BMSC 肺内滞留短相比，CD34/CD45-DSCs 在 phosgene 大鼠肺内滞留延长至 11.3 天（BMSC 48 小时），并在肺血管周围定植、增殖并获得 PVSC 样表型。
- 本研究把屏障保护效应闭合到 VEGF-PI3K/AKT/eNOS 轴：VEGF 敲低/中和取消保护，AKT agonist 可 rescue、inhibitor 可阻断，并连接至内皮 ZO-1/Occludin 上调。

**研究设计**
- 大鼠 phosgene 吸入性 ALI 模型
- 分离骨髓、外周血和 BALF 中 CD34+CD45+ 细胞并扩增为 CD34/CD45-DSCs，静脉给药
- 活体生物发光成像比较肺内滞留、增殖与血管周定植
- bleomycin 肺纤维化模型第 28 天评估纤维化变化
- VEGF knockdown/neutralization、AKT agonist rescue、AKT inhibitor block
- 检测内皮 ZO-1/Occludin 与 VEGF-PI3K/AKT/eNOS 信号

**主要发现**
- CD34+CD45+ 细胞在骨髓、外周血和 BALF 中约占 1%，phosgene 损伤后先下降后恢复，体外具 osteogenic、adipogenic 和 chondrogenic 三系分化潜能。
- CD34/CD45-DSCs 静脉移植显著减轻 P-ALI 大鼠肺血管渗漏和炎症。
- 生物发光显示其肺内滞留 11.3 天，长于 BMSC 的 48 小时；细胞增殖并定植于肺血管周围，获得 late-stage mesenchymal progenitor、pericyte 和 fibroblast marker 的 PVSC 样表型。
- 在 bleomycin 肺纤维化模型中，CD34/CD45-DSCs 未在第 28 天加重纤维化。
- VEGF 敲低或中和消除屏障保护，AKT agonist 恢复、AKT inhibitor 阻断；CD34/CD45-DSCs 经 VEGF-PI3K/AKT/eNOS 上调损伤内皮细胞 ZO-1 和 Occludin。

**为什么入选** 细胞来源、体内时空动态、血管周定植/命运、机制 rescue 与第二模型安全性形成较完整链条；使用 phosgene 化学性 ALI 这一相对少用的模型，并以活体成像和信号阻断验证因果，适合基础研究周报选读。

**局限**

- RORγ 扰动可增强 PD-L1 阻断的长期肿瘤控制效果。
- 对转移建立的影响呈情境与时机依赖，说明该轴的可干预性不是单向的。

**为什么入选** 具备完整机制链（人源 cNLR/RORγ+ 髓系表型 → 骨髓祖细胞 → 组织空间组织 → 肿瘤轨迹）、药物干预与免疫治疗联用的 rescue 式验证、以及时序分层设计；同时提示传统外周血中性粒细胞比例作为替代终点的失效，对髓系—免疫研究方向有方法学警示价值。

### 3. Targeting alpha-ketoglutarate starvation within the tumor-immune microenvironment rescues T cell effector function in cancer immunotherapy.

**Abstract**

Given its status as the leading cause of global cancer mortality, there is a critical need to enhance immunotherapy strategies for lung cancer to overcome widespread resistance to current treatments. Immunotherapy recruits the patient's immune system to attack and destroy cancer cells. While highly effective in a subset of patients, chronic exposure of immune cells to cancer cells leads to a progressive loss of effector T cell function and progression of T cell exhaustion; now considered two well-established hallmarks of immunotherapy resistance. The tumor-immune microenvironment (TIME)-driven differentiation into the exhausted T cell phenotype is marked by a loss of metabolic plasticity and epigenome-wide changes in distribution of repressive histone marks such as histone H3 subunit methylation at lysine 27 (H3K27me 3 ). Here, we studied how these processes are connected to metabolite availability within the TIME. In vitro , chronic antigen stimulation and hypoxia led to rapid loss of effector function, associated with a suppression of alpha-ketoglutarate (aKG) intracellular concentrations in T cells. Supplementation of cell-permeable aKG led to a rescue of effector cytokine production and to improved cytotoxic capacity despite chronic antigen stimulation and hypoxia. Increased intracellular aKG levels restored the activity of H3K27 demethylases UTX and JMJD3, consistent with epigenome-wide decreased H3K27me 3 . In vivo , dietary supplementation with commercially available calcium-aKG (Ca-aKG) supplements led to an elevation of serum and intratumoral aKG levels associated with a significant increase of immunotherapy effectiveness in mouse models of NSCLC. Clinical samples from patients with NSCLC contained significantly decreased serum aKG levels in older NSCLC patients (>65 years of age). Analysis identified a novel association between serum aKG levels and poor immunotherapy response. Thus, our work demonstrates that intratumoral metabolite "starvation" can be therapeutically exploited to improve immunotherapy outcomes in non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC).

**What Is New?**
- 本研究发现 TIME 中的 aKG 可用性下降是连接慢性抗原刺激/缺氧与 T 细胞效应功能丧失的代谢检查点，而不仅是已知 exhaustion 的伴随代谢缺陷。
- 机制上，aKG 补充通过恢复 H3K27 去甲基化酶 UTX/JMJD3 活性并降低 H3K27me3，闭合了“代谢物—表观遗传—T 细胞效应功能”的 rescue 链条。
- 在体内，市售 Ca-aKG 饮食补充可提升血清和瘤内 aKG 并增强 mouse NSCLC 免疫治疗疗效，且人 NSCLC 血清 aKG 降低与高龄及较差免疫治疗反应相关，提供了跨物种验证线索。

**研究设计**
- 体外：慢性抗原刺激+缺氧处理 T 细胞，检测 intracellular aKG、效应细胞因子和细胞毒能力；cell-permeable aKG 补充进行 rescue。
- 表观：检测 UTX/JMJD3 活性与 H3K27me3 分布，连接 aKG 水平与抑制性组蛋白修饰。
- 体内：mouse NSCLC 模型中 dietary Ca-aKG 补充，检测 serum/intratumoral aKG 与 immunotherapy 疗效。
- 人源：NSCLC 临床血清样本分析 aKG 水平与年龄及 immunotherapy response 的关联。

**主要发现**
- 慢性抗原刺激和缺氧导致 T 细胞效应功能快速丧失，并伴随 intracellular aKG 浓度受抑制。
- Cell-permeable aKG 补充可恢复 effector cytokine production 和 cytotoxic capacity，即使存在慢性抗原刺激和缺氧。
- 升高 intracellular aKG 可恢复 H3K27 demethylases UTX 和 JMJD3 活性，并与 epigenome-wide H3K27me3 降低一致。
- Dietary Ca-aKG 补充提升 serum 和 intratumoral aKG 水平，并显著增强 mouse NSCLC 模型的 immunotherapy effectiveness。
- 人 NSCLC 样本中，>65 岁患者 serum aKG 显著降低；分析发现 serum aKG 水平与较差 immunotherapy response 的新关联。

**为什么入选** 机制链清晰：TIME 代谢物 aKG 饥饿—H3K27me3/UTX/JMJD3 表观调控—T 细胞效应功能 rescue；同时具备体外 rescue、mouse NSCLC 体内 dietary 补充和人血清关联三层证据，直接面向 lung cancer immunotherapy resistance。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要，无 OA 全文；无法评估样本量、统计方法、对照设置及 aKG 在体内是否依赖 T 细胞内在机制。
- bioRxiv 预印本，尚未经同行评议。
- 人源数据为相关性分析，不能证明 serum aKG 对 immunotherapy response 的因果或预测价值。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可复用“代谢物 rescue + 表观标志物（H3K27me3、UTX/JMJD3）+ 效应功能 readout”的实验链，验证其他 TIME 代谢限制是否同样可逆。
- 慢性抗原刺激叠加缺氧的体外 T 细胞模型，可与 mouse NSCLC 模型及人血清样本形成“体外—体内—人关联”的分层验证设计。
- 若关注肺泡/成纤维细胞/免疫-基质 crosstalk，可将 metabolite availability 作为微环境变量，检测其对免疫细胞表观状态和效应功能的影响。
