### 1. Alveolar epithelial depletion of βPAK-interacting exchange factor drives spontaneous pulmonary fibrosis by impairing stemness.

Signal transduction and targeted therapy | 2026 Sep 10 | Han-Byeol Kim, Woogil Song, Sun-Ok Kim, Jin-Hee Park, Junyeol Han, Ok-Jun Lee, Nak-Kyun Soung, Bumhee Yang

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42716949/
- DOI：10.1016/j.stem.2023.10.001

**Abstract**

Aberrant repair/regeneration of alveolar epithelial stem cells is a key driver of pulmonary fibrosis (PF). While βPAK-interacting exchange factor (βPIX) is connected to stem cell signaling, its role in pulmonary fibrosis (PF) remains unknown. Here, we generated conditional βPIX knockout mice to investigate its contribution to PF pathogenesis. Selectively depleting βPIX in alveolar type 2 (AT2) cells led to progressive PF in mice, recapitulating the key features observed in human idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF), where AT2 cells exhibit significantly reduced βPIX levels. Single-nucleus RNA sequencing of the lungs of βPIX-deficient mice revealed the emergence of progenitor cells in a transitional stem cell state with senescent attributes, along with active, collagen-producing myofibroblasts. Mechanistically, the loss of βPIX impaired AT2 cell stemness by downregulating Wnt/β-catenin signaling. AT2 cells from IPF patients also presented reduced levels of β-catenin and Axin2, confirming the downregulation of Wnt/β-catenin signaling. Treatment with a glycogen synthase kinase-3β inhibitor reactivated Wnt/β-catenin signaling and attenuated fibrosis in βPIX-deficient mice. Our results establish a direct causal link between βPIX deficiency and PF, highlighting βPIX and its downstream pathway as promising therapeutic targets for this devastating disease.

**一句话结论** 该研究通过AT2细胞特异性敲除βPIX（ARHGEF6）小鼠证明：肺泡上皮干细胞βPIX缺失可通过下调Wnt/β-catenin信号损伤AT2干性，导致自发进展性肺纤维化，并伴 transitional progenitor 细胞与产胶原 myofibroblast 扩增；IPF患者AT2中βPIX、β-catenin与Axin2同样降低，GSK-3β抑制剂可再激活Wnt通路并减轻纤维化。

**研究设计**
- 条件性βPIX敲除小鼠（AT2细胞特异性敲除），观察自发肺纤维化表型
- 人IPF肺组织AT2细胞中βPIX及Wnt/β-catenin组分（β-catenin、Axin2）表达检测
- βPIX缺陷小鼠肺组织 single-nucleus RNA sequencing（snRNA-seq）解析细胞状态
- 机制验证：βPIX缺失对AT2 stemness 与 Wnt/β-catenin 信号的调控
- 干预实验：GSK-3β inhibitor 处理βPIX缺陷小鼠，评估Wnt通路再激活与纤维化程度

**主要发现**
- AT2细胞特异性敲除βPIX即可引起小鼠进行性肺纤维化，重现人IPF关键特征（含AT2中βPIX显著降低）
- snRNA-seq显示βPIX缺陷肺内出现处于 transitional stem cell state 且带衰老特征的progenitor细胞，同时存在活跃的产胶原 myofibroblast
- 机制上βPIX缺失通过下调 Wnt/β-catenin 信号损害AT2细胞 stemness
- IPF患者AT2细胞中β-catenin与Axin2降低，提示人源样本同样存在Wnt/β-catenin下调
- GSK-3β inhibitor 再激活Wnt/β-catenin信号并可减轻βPIX缺陷小鼠的纤维化

**为什么入选** 具备完整因果链：上皮（AT2）特异基因缺失 → Wnt/β-catenin 下调 → 干性丧失与 transitional/衰老 progenitor 出现 → myofibroblast 扩增与纤维化；并配有snRNA-seq、人IPF组织验证与药理学rescue（GSK-3β inhibitor），机制闭合度高，是本周期肺纤维化上皮-间质轴的代表性工作。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，缺少具体样本量、时间点、剂量与统计学细节，无法评估效应量
- 研究终点集中在肺纤维化与AT2干性，未涉及肺血管重塑、血流动力学或右心指标，也无内皮/PASMC直接数据
- RNA velocity 或 lineage tracing 是否确证 transitional progenitor 向 myofibroblast 的分化路径在摘要中未明确

**关联** 对Group 3 PH与血管重塑课题而言，本文的价值在于提供一条与肺纤维化高度同源的机制模板：Wnt/β-catenin 既是AT2干性维持因子，也是PASMC增殖、内皮-间质转化与血管重塑的核心通路。βPIX作为Rho GTPase交换因子（ARHGEF6），调控细胞骨架与迁移/收缩表型，理论上可能同时作用于AT2、内皮与PASMC；若在SU5416/hypoxia或monocrotaline模型中检测βPIX及Wnt/β-catenin组分（含WIF1、Axin2、β-catenin），可检验上皮来源信号是否也参与远端肺血管肌化。

### 2. Knockdown of CDC20 attenuates pulmonary fibrosis by repressing fibroblast activation via the TSC1-mediated mTOR pathway.

Cellular signalling | 2026 Sep 08 | Yatao Guo, Mengchan Zhu, Ruyi Li, Jia Li, Jiming Si, Yuanhua Liu, Jingjing Mei, Yan Kang

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42710704/
- DOI：10.1016/j.cellsig.2026.112886

**Abstract**

Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) is a fatal disease of the fibrous lungs that is closely associated with fibroblast activation. Cell division cycle protein 20 homolog (CDC20) regulates cell cycle progression, yet its role in lung fibrosis remains unclear. This study aimed to explore the function of CDC20 in IPF and investigate the potential mechanism of CDC20 to influence the progression of pulmonary fibrosis. Here, we found that the expression levels of CDC20 were upregulated in the lung tissue of mice with bleomycin (BLM)-induced pulmonary fibrosis, as well as in activated fibroblasts. Knockdown of CDC20 suppressed the activation of MRC-5 triggered by TGF-β1 via enhancing autophagy. Nevertheless, chloroquine (CQ)-mediated inhibition of autophagy abolished this regulatory effect on fibroblast activation. CDC20 knockdown also inhibited TGF-β1-induced migration and contraction of fibroblasts. CDC20 silencing had a protective effect against BLM-induced lung injury in mice, and CDC20 knockdown inhibited fibrosis in lung tissue and promoted autophagy. The interacting protein TSC1 with CDC20 was screened by IP/LC-MS, and Co-IP analysis showed that TSC1 interacted with CDC20, and CDC20 promoted the ubiquitination degradation of TSC1. Mechanistically, CDC20 boosted the ubiquitination and degradation of TSC1, and the reduction of TSC1 promoted the activation of the mTOR pathway to inhibit fibroblast autophagy, thereby facilitating fibroblast activation. Overall, CDC20 exacerbated pulmonary fibrosis by inhibiting fibroblast autophagy via blocking the mTOR signaling pathway, which suggested that targeting CDC20 might hold putative therapeutic effect in IPF.

**一句话结论** 该研究在 bleomycin (BLM) 诱导的小鼠肺纤维化模型及 TGF-β1 激活的 MRC-5 成纤维细胞中发现 CDC20 表达上调；CDC20 通过促进 TSC1 的泛素化降解、激活 mTOR 通路、抑制成纤维细胞自噬，从而促进成纤维细胞活化与肺纤维化。敲低 CDC20（或自噬抑制剂 CQ 反向验证）可减轻纤维化，提示 CDC20 是 IPF 的潜在干预靶点。

**研究设计**
- 疾病模型：bleomycin (BLM) 诱导的小鼠肺纤维化 + TGF-β1 刺激的人成纤维细胞系 MRC-5
- 表达验证：BLM 小鼠肺组织及活化成纤维细胞中 CDC20 表达水平检测
- 功能实验：CDC20 knockdown 对成纤维细胞活化、迁移、收缩能力的影响
- 机制筛选：IP/LC-MS 筛选 CDC20 互作蛋白，Co-IP 验证互作，评估泛素化降解
- 通路/自噬干预：TGF-β1 + chloroquine (CQ) 抑制自噬，观察是否废除 CDC20 敲低效应；mTOR 通路检测
- 体内验证：CDC20 silencing 对 BLM 诱导肺损伤/肺纤维化的保护作用及自噬水平

**主要发现**
- CDC20 在 BLM 诱导的小鼠肺纤维化肺组织及活化成纤维细胞中表达上调。
- 敲低 CDC20 通过增强自噬抑制 TGF-β1 诱导的 MRC-5 活化和成纤维细胞迁移、收缩；CQ 抑制自噬可废除该效应。
- IP/LC-MS 与 Co-IP 显示 CDC20 与 TSC1 互作，CDC20 促进 TSC1 的泛素化降解。
- 机制链：CDC20 → TSC1 泛素化降解 → TSC1 减少 → mTOR 通路激活 → 成纤维细胞自噬受抑 → 成纤维细胞活化增强 → 肺纤维化加重。
- 体内：CDC20 silencing 减轻 BLM 诱导的肺损伤和肺组织纤维化，并促进自噬。

**为什么入选** 机制链相对完整：以 CDC20–TSC1 互作为核心，串起“泛素化降解—mTOR—自噬—成纤维细胞活化”这一可验证的轴，并用 CQ 阻断自噬做了反向（rescue-like）验证；同时具备 BLM 体内模型 + 人成纤维细胞体外模型，属于与成纤维细胞/肌成纤维细胞活化、肺结构重塑直接相关的靶点类研究，可与本周 Group 3 PH 血管重塑及成纤维细胞方向互为参照。

**局限**
- 仅基于 PubMed 摘要，未见 OA 全文，无法评估样本量、统计方法、抗体/knockdown 效率、BLM 模型时点（预防性 vs 治疗性给药）等关键细节。
- 机制验证以 MRC-5 细胞系和 Co-IP/泛素化实验为主，缺少 TSC1 回复实验（rescue）、自噬通量（flux）实验和遗传学体内敲除/条件性模型证据，CDC20–TSC1 直接泛素化修饰位点未在摘要中体现。
- 研究对象为 IPF/肺纤维化成纤维细胞，不含内皮细胞、平滑肌细胞、单细胞/空间转录组或谱系追踪数据，向 Group 3 PH 血管重塑外推需谨慎。

**关联** 对你的 Group 3 PH / 血管重塑课题，这篇工作的价值主要在“成纤维细胞—肌成纤维细胞活化”的通用调控节点：CDC20 作为 APC/C 共激活因子，通过 TSC1 泛素化降解解除对 mTOR 的抑制，并压制自噬，这一“泛素化—mTOR—自噬”逻辑在肺纤维化、以及 Group 3 PH 中常见的 perivascular 成纤维细胞/肌成纤维细胞增生与外膜重塑中可能同样适用，值得在自身 scRNA-seq 或 spatial transcriptomics 数据中检查 Cdkn/Cdc20 模块、TSC1-mTOR 通路与肌成纤维细胞（myofibroblast）标签的共表达关系；此外，自噬受抑常伴随 IL-6 等炎症因子分泌增强，可能参与中性粒细胞募集与炎症性血管重塑。

### 3. ZD-4009, a novel B-cell lymphoma 9 inhibitor, is associated with ameliorated pulmonary fibrosis and reduced hypoxia-inducible factor 1α-mediated glycolysis and transforming growth factor-β/SMAD signaling.

Molecular pharmacology | 2026 Jul 24 | Anqi Li, Yuanyuan Zhu, Fan Yang, Yunlu Li, Yuxuan Dong, Cuiting Liu, Daizhou Zhang, Wei Lu

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42721930/
- DOI：10.1016/j.molpha.2026.100144

**Abstract**

Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) is a progressive and fatal interstitial lung disease with limited therapeutic options, highlighting the need for new molecular targets and effective antifibrotic agents. In this study, we investigated the role of B-cell lymphoma 9 (BCL9) in IPF and evaluated the antifibrotic potential of ZD-4009, a novel small-molecule inhibitor targeting BCL9. BCL9 was upregulated in fibrotic lung tissues, suggesting a possible association between BCL9 and pulmonary fibrosis progression. ZD-4009 showed high-affinity binding to β-catenin (pKD = 7.36 ± 0.01, KD = 44.72 nM). In vitro, ZD-4009 inhibited fibroblast activation, suppressed extracellular matrix deposition, and reduced the expression of fibrosis-associated markers, including collagen type I alpha 1 chain (COL1A1) and alpha-smooth muscle actin (α-SMA). Mechanistically, ZD-4009 suppressed hypoxia-inducible factor 1α-associated glycolytic reprogramming and inhibited transforming growth factor-β (TGF-β)/SMAD family member (SMAD) signaling, suggesting that its antifibrotic effects are associated with coordinated regulation of metabolic and profibrotic pathways. In vivo, ZD-4009 treatment was associated with alleviated bleomycin (BLM)-induced pulmonary fibrosis, as reflected by improved survival from 55% in the BLM group to 82% in the BLM + ZD-4009 group, reduced relative collagen content from 61% to 48%, and ameliorated micro-computed tomography-assessed fibrotic changes. In the tested model, ZD-4009 showed favorable antifibrotic effects compared with nintedanib, whereas liposome-encapsulated ZD-4009 reduced treatment-related toxicity. Together, these findings nominate ZD-4009 as a promising antifibrotic candidate and highlight BCL9 as a potential therapeutic target for IPF. SIGNIFICANCE STATEMENT: ZD-4009 is a potent and selective B-cell lymphoma 9 inhibitor that suppresses fibroblast proliferation and extracellular matrix deposition in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis. It shows superior antifibrotic efficacy compared with current therapies, positioning ZD-4009 as a promising therapeutic candidate for treating this progressive and untreatable disease.

**一句话结论** 该研究报道新型BCL9抑制剂ZD-4009在博来霉素诱导的肺纤维化模型中通过抑制WNT/β-catenin、HIF-1α相关糖酵解和TGF-β/SMAD信号，减轻成纤维细胞活化和ECM沉积。ZD-4009改善生存率，效果优于nintedanib，提示BCL9是IPF的潜在治疗靶点。

**研究设计**
- 体外：成纤维细胞活化、ECM沉积及纤维化标志物检测
- 体内：bleomycin诱导的小鼠肺纤维化模型
- 药物：ZD-4009（BCL9小分子抑制剂）及其脂质体封装
- 对照：nintedanib
- 机制：HIF-1α糖酵解和TGF-β/SMAD信号

**主要发现**
- BCL9在纤维化肺组织中上调，提示其与IPF进展相关
- ZD-4009与β-catenin高亲和力结合（KD=44.72 nM）
- ZD-4009抑制成纤维细胞活化、ECM沉积及COL1A1、α-SMA表达
- 机制上，ZD-4009抑制HIF-1α相关糖酵解重编程和TGF-β/SMAD信号
- 体内：ZD-4009改善BLM模型生存率（55%→82%）、减少胶原含量（61%→48%）和mCT纤维化改变，效果优于nintedanib；脂质体封装降低毒性

**为什么入选** 该研究提供了一条WNT/β-catenin（BCL9）-HIF-1α糖酵解-TGF-β/SMAD的机制链，在bleomycin肺纤维化模型中验证了成纤维细胞活化和ECM沉积的表型，并进行了与nintedanib的头对头比较及脂质体减毒，对纤维化肺病及血管重塑研究有参考价值。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无全文数据
- 未在Group 3 PH或血管重塑模型中验证
- BCL9上调的人体组织来源和样本量未明确

**关联** 读者关注Group 3 PH、成纤维细胞/肌成纤维细胞、内皮、WNT信号和单细胞等。本文虽聚焦IPF而非PH，但BCL9/β-catenin、TGF-β/SMAD和HIF-1α/糖酵解通路在肺血管重塑和成纤维细胞活化中同样重要，ZD-4009可作为工具化合物用于测试这些通路在PAH/Group 3模型中的作用。

未来可结合scRNA-seq或lineage tracing解析BCL9在成纤维细胞亚群中的表达，并探索其在SU5416/hypoxia或MCT模型中的抗重塑效果。

### 4. DPEP1 promotes pulmonary fibrosis by regulating arachidonic acid metabolism.

Journal of thoracic disease | 2026 Aug 31 | Guiyu Zhou, Jie He, Jiaqing Jiang, Zhenglin Chen, Yaju Deng, Yu Li

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42724184/
- DOI：10.1126/science.adp0240

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Although dipeptidase 1 (DPEP1) is known to be involved in endothelial function and metabolic regulation, its contribution to idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) has not been established. This study aimed to investigate the role of DPEP1 in the pathogenesis of IPF and to explore its potential mechanism involving arachidonic acid (ARA) metabolism and endothelial inflammatory responses.
METHODS: Public transcriptomic datasets from the Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) database, including bulk RNA sequencing and single-cell RNA sequencing datasets, were analyzed to evaluate DPEP1 expression and cellular localization in IPF. Correlations between DPEP1 expression and pulmonary function parameters were assessed. Serum samples from IPF patients and healthy controls were collected to validate DPEP1 and ARA levels. A bleomycin (BLM)-induced pulmonary fibrosis mouse model was established to investigate the in vivo role of DPEP1. Histological staining, enzyme-linked immunosorbent assay (ELISA), reverse transcription quantitative polymerase chain reaction (RT-qPCR), immunohistochemistry, immunofluorescence, and Western blotting were performed to evaluate fibrosis, inflammatory responses, and signaling pathway alterations. The therapeutic effects of the DPEP1 inhibitor cilastatin sodium were also examined.
RESULTS: DPEP1 expression was significantly elevated in lung tissues and pulmonary vascular endothelial cells of IPF patients and negatively correlated with lung function indices, including forced expiratory volume in the first second percent predicted (FEV1%), diffusing capacity of the lungs for carbon monoxide percent predicted (DLCO%), and forced vital capacity percent predicted (FVC%). Single-cell RNA sequencing identified endothelial cells as the primary source of DPEP1 expression in IPF lungs. Functional enrichment analysis suggested that DPEP1 was closely associated with fatty acid metabolism, particularly ARA metabolism. Serum ARA levels were significantly decreased in IPF patients and negatively correlated with DPEP1 expression. In the BLM-induced mouse model, DPEP1 expression was markedly increased, whereas ARA levels were reduced. Pharmacological inhibition of DPEP1 alleviated pulmonary fibrosis, decreased inflammatory cytokine expression, restored ARA, PGE2, and cAMP levels, and suppressed activation of the TGF-β1/Smad3 signaling pathway.
CONCLUSIONS: DPEP1 promotes pulmonary fibrosis by regulating ARA metabolism and endothelial inflammatory responses. Targeting DPEP1 may represent a promising therapeutic strategy for the treatment of IPF.

**一句话结论** 该研究基于公共转录组/scRNA-seq数据、IPF患者血清与bleomycin小鼠模型，提出内皮细胞来源的DPEP1通过调控arachidonic acid（ARA）代谢与内皮炎症反应促进肺纤维化；药物抑制DPEP1（cilastatin sodium）可恢复ARA/PGE2/cAMP水平并抑制TGF-β1/Smad3活化，从而减轻纤维化。

**研究设计**
- 公共数据库分析：GEO bulk RNA-seq + scRNA-seq，评估DPEP1在IPF肺组织中的表达与细胞定位
- 临床样本验证：IPF患者与健康对照血清，检测DPEP1与ARA水平并做相关性及肺功能（FEV1%、DLCO%、FVC%）相关分析
- 体内模型：bleomycin诱导小鼠肺纤维化模型，观察DPEP1表达与ARA变化
- 药理学干预：DPEP1抑制剂cilastatin sodium处理，评估纤维化、炎症因子与信号通路
- 方法学：组织学染色、ELISA、RT-qPCR、IHC、IF、Western blot

**主要发现**
- IPF患者肺组织及肺血管内皮细胞中DPEP1表达显著升高，且与FEV1%、DLCO%、FVC%呈负相关
- scRNA-seq提示内皮细胞是IPF肺中DPEP1的主要来源，功能富集指向脂肪酸代谢尤其是ARA代谢
- IPF患者血清ARA显著降低，并与DPEP1表达呈负相关；bleomycin小鼠肺内DPEP1升高、ARA降低
- cilastatin sodium抑制DPEP1后可减轻肺纤维化、降低炎症因子表达、恢复ARA/PGE2/cAMP水平
- 机制上伴随TGF-β1/Smad3信号活化的抑制

**为什么入选** 该文把“内皮细胞—脂质代谢（ARA）—炎症—TGF-β1/Smad3—纤维化”串成一条较完整的机制链，并用scRNA-seq定位到内皮细胞这一读者关注的细胞类型；同时具备人肺组织/血清验证、bleomycin动物模型与药理学rescue（cilastatin sodium）三个层次，属于内皮-代谢轴驱动肺重塑的典型范式。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，缺少具体样本量、统计细节、细胞特异性机制实验（如内皮特异性敲除/过表达）信息
- DPEP1-ARA-PGE2/cAMP与TGF-β1/Smad3之间的因果方向与直接分子互作未在摘要中阐明
- 研究为IPF（Group 1/间质性肺病）背景，结论向Group 3 PH及肺动脉血管重塑的外推需谨慎

**关联** 对Group 3 PH与血管重塑研究而言，本文的价值在于再次提示“肺血管内皮细胞并非被动受损者，而是代谢-炎症信号的主动放大器”。DPEP1作为内皮富集的代谢酶，其底物ARA代谢产物（PGE2/cAMP）与TGF-β1/Smad3轴的联动，与Group 3 PH中内皮功能障碍、内皮-间充质转化及远端肺动脉肌化/重塑过程高度可类比；cilastatin sodium这类已上市药物的再定位也可能为肺血管重塑干预提供思路。

### 5. Isoforskolin targets ADCY7 to restore mitochondrial function in COPD-associated macrophages.

International immunopharmacology | 2026 Sep 08 | Zengrui Wang, Rongrong Wu, Shuai Jun Li, Xiaorong Su, Yue Yang, Le Wang, Jiefu Tang, Zhuang Luo

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42710396/
- DOI：10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117398

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Mitochondrial dysfunction in alveolar macrophages (AMs) drives chronic airway inflammation in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). Adenylyl cyclase 7 (ADCY7) is a key enzyme in cAMP signaling, but its role in macrophage mitochondrial dysfunction and immune regulation in COPD remains unclear. Isoforskolin (ISOF), a bioactive natural product from Coleus forskohlii, has known anti-inflammatory properties, yet whether it improves macrophage mitochondrial function via ADCY7 is unknown.
OBJECTIVE: To investigate whether ISOF acts through ADCY7 to restore mitochondrial function and correct the dysfunctional phenotype of a COPD-enriched ADCY7+CCL4+ macrophage subpopulation.
METHODS: ADCY7 expression was measured in AMs from COPD patients. Single-cell and bulk transcriptomics (GSE171541, GSE57148) were used to identify ADCY7+ macrophage subpopulations.
