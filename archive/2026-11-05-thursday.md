### 1. Targeting ALDOA Lactylation Reverses Vascular Remodeling in Pulmonary Hypertension by Disrupting Actin Polymerization and YAP Activation.

Circulation | 2026 Nov 03 | Xiaoxun Huang*, Jincheng Zhang*, Qingye Zeng*, Deping Kong
IF 2025：41.3 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Cardiology (X.H., J.Z., Q.Z., X.Z., Q.L.), Shanghai Jiao Tong University, China.
- 领域：心血管内科方向，结合单位与摘要推断其关注肺血管重塑和肺动脉高压的代谢/表观机制。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42974708/
- DOI：10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.125.078300

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Maladaptive vascular remodeling is a hallmark of pulmonary hypertension (PH), and therapeutic strategies directly targeting the pathological activity of smooth muscle cells remain limited. Metabolic and epigenetic dysregulation are established drivers of PH, and lysine lactylation has emerged as a novel posttranslational modification implicated in diverse diseases; however, its direct contribution to PH progression remains undefined.
METHODS: Integrated lactylomic and proteomic profiling was performed in hypoxic human pulmonary arterial smooth muscle cells. The functional role of ALDOA (aldolase A) lactylation at lysine 42 (K42) was assessed using site-specific mutant virus and smooth muscle cell-specific inducible ALDOA knockout mice in 2 established PH models: SU5416+hypoxia in mice and monocrotaline in rats.
RESULTS: We identified ALDOA as a prominent lactylation target in hypoxic pulmonary arterial smooth muscle cells, with K42 lactylation consistently elevated in primary pulmonary arterial smooth muscle cells derived from PH rodent models and human PH tissues. Mechanistically, AARS1 (alanyl-tRNA synthetase 1)-mediated ALDOA K42 lactylation enhanced its binding to γ-actin, independent of glycolytic activity. This lactylation-enhanced interaction promoted F-actin polymerization and activated YAP (Yes-associated protein) signaling via LATS1 (large tumor suppressor kinase 1) inhibition, thereby driving pulmonary arterial smooth muscle cell proliferation and phenotypic switching. Moreover, YAP/TEAD4 (TEA domain transcription factor 4)-mediated transcriptional upregulation of AARS1 established a positive feedback loop reinforcing ALDOA K42 lactylation under hypoxia. In vivo, ALDOA inducible knockout or reconstitution with the lactylation-deficient K42R (lysine 42 mutant to arginine) mutant attenuated experimental PH progression, with suppressed F-actin/YAP activation and reduced vascular remodeling. Furthermore, competitive inhibition of lactylation through β‑alanine reduced ALDOA K42 lactylation and ameliorated vascular remodeling in ALDOA-overexpressing rodent PH models. Translationally, pharmacological disruption of the ALDOA/γ-actin interaction by raltegravir suppressed cytoskeletal reorganization and YAP signaling, mitigating PH progression in both mouse and rat models.
CONCLUSIONS: AARS1-mediated ALDOA K42 lactylation drives pulmonary vascular remodeling through a mechanism independent of ALDOA's catalytic activity, promoting F-actin polymerization and YAP activation. Precision targeting of this modification selectively inhibits pathological signaling while preserving metabolic homeostasis, highlighting a translational potential for precision intervention in PH.

**What Is New?**
- 本研究在缺氧人肺动脉平滑肌细胞中通过乳酰化组学将 ALDOA K42 乳酰化定义为 PH 相关的新修饰节点，并在 PH 动物原代 PASMCs 与人 PH 组织中验证其升高。
- 研究闭合了 AARS1–ALDOA K42 乳酰化–γ-actin/F-actin–LATS1/YAP 的机制链，且该作用不依赖 ALDOA 的糖酵解催化活性。
- 通过在两种 PH 模型中采用 SMC 特异性诱导性 ALDOA 敲除、K42R 重建及 ALDOA/γ-actin 相互作用干扰策略，研究提供了遗传与药理双重体内验证，将靶点从相关性推进到可干预的因果节点。

**研究设计**
- 缺氧人肺动脉平滑肌细胞乳酰化组与蛋白质组整合分析
- ALDOA K42R 位点特异性突变病毒与 SMC 特异性诱导性 ALDOA 敲除小鼠
- SU5416+hypoxia 小鼠与 monocrotaline 大鼠两种 PH 模型
- ALDOA 敲除/K42R 重建、β-alanine 抑制乳酰化、raltegravir 阻断 ALDOA/γ-actin 在体验证
- PH  rodent 原代 PASMCs 与人 PH 组织验证 K42 乳酰化

**主要发现**
- ALDOA 是缺氧 PASMCs 中显著的乳酰化靶点，K42 乳酰化在 PH  rodent 原代 PASMCs 与人 PH 组织中一致升高。
- AARS1 介导 ALDOA K42 乳酰化，增强 ALDOA 与 γ-actin 结合，且不依赖 ALDOA 的糖酵解活性。
- 该乳酰化增强的互作促进 F-actin 聚合，并通过抑制 LATS1 激活 YAP，驱动 PASMC 增殖与表型转换。
- YAP/TEAD4 转录上调 AARS1，形成缺氧下强化 ALDOA K42 乳酰化的正反馈环。
- 体内 ALDOA 诱导性敲除或 K42R 重建减轻实验性 PH，伴随 F-actin/YAP 抑制和血管重塑减少；β-alanine 与 raltegravir 在 ALDOA 过表达 rodent PH 模型中改善重塑。

**为什么入选** 该研究建立了从代谢/表观修饰 writer AARS1 到非催化性 ALDOA K42 乳酰化、γ-actin/F-actin 细胞骨架重塑、LATS1/YAP 激活及转录正反馈的较完整机制链，并在人 PH 组织、两种 rodent PH 模型及遗传/药理 rescue 中验证，符合 Circulation 对机制深度与转化潜力的关注。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无 OA 全文；无法评估样本量、统计方法、模型细节及药物特异性/脱靶效应。
- 人 PH 组织中的 K42 乳酰化验证在摘要层面主要为相关性，未说明与 PH 亚型、严重度或治疗状态的关系。
- raltegravir 与 β-alanine 的长期体内安全性、代谢稳态影响及 ALDOA/γ-actin 干扰的选择性需全文数据支持。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可借鉴“乳酰化组+蛋白质组”在缺氧原代 PASMCs 中提名 PTM 靶点的策略，并用 K-to-R 位点突变体与 SMC 特异性诱导性敲除/重建区分催化活性与非催化相互作用功能。
- 机制验证可沿“PTM 增强非糖酵解蛋白互作→细胞骨架聚合→YAP/LATS1 读出→writer 转录反馈环”的链路设计，适合推广到其他血管壁细胞的表型转换研究。
- raltegravir 阻断 ALDOA/γ-actin 互作与 β-alanine 竞争抑制乳酰化可作为工具性干预思路，供肺血管重塑、成纤维细胞或免疫-间质 crosstalk 研究借鉴，但不构成临床建议。

### 2. Targeting ZIP12-mediated Zn2+ signaling as a treatment for pulmonary hypertension.

Science translational medicine | 2026 Oct 21 | Lan Zhao, Martin R Wilkins
IF 2025：15.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：三年内旗舰系统研究
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：National Heart and Lung Institute, Faculty of Medicine, Imperial College London, London, UK.
- 领域：据 Imperial College London National Heart and Lung Institute 与摘要推断，主要方向为肺血管生物学/PAH 的 Zn2+ 信号与靶向治疗；具体课题组信息不详。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42905856/
- DOI：10.1126/scitranslmed.aee0680

**Abstract**

ZIP12, a membrane-bound zinc importer, is up-regulated in the remodeled pulmonary arterioles of patients with pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH). Genetic deletion of its encoding gene, Slc39a12, ameliorates the disease in rodent models. Here, we show that ZIP12 overexpression in human pulmonary artery smooth muscle cells (PASMCs) increased intracellular labile Zn2+ and promoted proliferation. ZIP12 siRNA knockdown attenuated pulmonary hypertension in rodent models. We developed a humanized anti-ZIP12 monoclonal antibody, APL-9796, that normalized intracellular Zn2+ concentration, suppressed proliferation in fibroblasts derived from patients with PAH and PDGF-stimulated PASMCs, and reduced PDGF-induced cytoskeletal remodeling in PASMCs and contractility in PASMCs and engineered pulmonary artery tissues. APL-9796 showed dose-dependent efficacy in humanized Slc39a12 rats, improving cardiopulmonary parameters after induction of pulmonary hypertension and was well tolerated in phase 1 clinical studies. Transcriptomic profiling revealed modulation of several molecular pathways, including those involving AMPK, PPAR, and PI3K-Akt. ZIP12-driven labile Zn2+ signaling underpins the pathogenesis of PAH. Targeting ZIP12 with APL-9796 offers a promising therapeutic strategy.

**What Is New?**
- 把 ZIP12 从 PAH 中“表达上调/遗传缺失可缓解”的相关性推进为可直接驱动人 PASMCs 内 labile Zn2+ 升高和增殖的因果节点。
- 开发人源化抗 ZIP12 单克隆抗体 APL-9796，并在 PAH 患者来源 fibroblasts、PDGF 刺激的 PASMCs、工程化肺动脉组织及 humanized Slc39a12 大鼠中完成从细胞到在体的药理学验证。
- 在 humanized Slc39a12 大鼠中证明 APL-9796 剂量依赖性改善肺动脉高压诱导后的心肺参数，且 phase 1 临床研究显示耐受良好，形成机制—药物—人源化在体—早期临床的闭环。

**研究设计**
- 在人 PASMCs 中过表达 ZIP12，检测 intracellular labile Zn2+ 与增殖。
- 用 ZIP12 siRNA 在 rodent PH models 中做靶点敲低验证。
- 开发 anti-ZIP12 mAb APL-9796，并在 PAH 患者来源 fibroblasts、PDGF 刺激 PASMCs 和工程化肺动脉组织中检测 Zn2+、增殖、细胞骨架重塑与收缩性。
- 在 humanized Slc39a12 rats 中评估 APL-9796 剂量依赖性疗效及 cardiopulmonary parameters。
- 转录组分析 AMPK、PPAR、PI3K-Akt 等通路，并引用 phase 1 耐受性数据。

**主要发现**
- ZIP12 过表达升高人 PASMCs 的 intracellular labile Zn2+ 并促进增殖。
- ZIP12 siRNA 敲低在 rodent models 中减轻 pulmonary hypertension。
- APL-9796 使细胞内 Zn2+ 浓度正常化，抑制 PAH 患者来源 fibroblasts 和 PDGF 刺激 PASMCs 的增殖，并减轻 PASMCs 的 PDGF 诱导细胞骨架重塑及 PASMCs/工程化肺动脉组织的收缩性。
- 在 humanized Slc39a12 rats 中，APL-9796 剂量依赖性改善 PH 诱导后的 cardiopulmonary parameters，并在 phase 1 研究中耐受良好。
- Transcriptomic profiling 显示 AMPK、PPAR 和 PI3K-Akt 等相关分子通路被调节。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整（ZIP12→labile Zn2+→PASMC 增殖/收缩与 fibroblast 增殖），并有人源细胞、患者来源 fibroblast、工程化组织、humanized 大鼠和 phase 1 耐受性数据；靶向膜锌转运蛋白的治疗概念适合转化研究读者，且发表于 Science Translational Medicine。

**局限**
- 仅基于 PubMed abstract，无 OA 全文，无法评估实验细节、样本量、统计方法、抗体特异性和 Zn2+ 检测方法。
- rodent/humanized rat 模型不能完全代表人类 PAH 的异质性和长期安全性；phase 1 数据仅提示耐受性，未提供疗效结论。
- transcriptomic pathway modulation 为关联性结果，ZIP12 下游因果网络仍需全文或后续研究验证。

**可借鉴之处**
- humanized Slc39a12 大鼠可作为抗体类药物从靶点验证走向在体疗效评估的桥梁，尤其适合膜转运蛋白靶点。
- 患者来源 fibroblasts、PDGF 刺激 PASMCs 与工程化肺动脉组织收缩性读数可组合为 PAH 药物的人源相关药效学面板，兼顾增殖、细胞骨架重塑和血管收缩。
- 从过表达/siRNA 敲低到单抗阻断、再到 transcriptomic pathway profiling 的验证链，可用于判断 Zn2+ 等金属离子信号是驱动节点还是伴随现象。

### 3. Toll-like Receptor 3 Is a Therapeutic Target for Pulmonary Hypertension.

American journal of respiratory and critical care medicine | 2019 Jan 15 | Daniela Farkas, Laszlo Farkas
IF 2025：21.7 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：领域经典
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：1 Division of Pulmonary Disease and Critical Care Medicine, Department of Internal Medicine, Virginia Commonwealth University, Richmond, Virginia.
- 领域：根据 Virginia Commonwealth University 呼吸与危重症医学部隶属关系，第一作者主要处于肺血管疾病/肺动脉高压基础与转化研究方向，具体领域摘要未详。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30211629/
- DOI：10.1164/rccm.201707-1370OC

**Abstract**

RATIONALE: Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) is characterized by vascular cell proliferation and endothelial cell apoptosis. TLR3 (Toll-like receptor 3) is a receptor for double-stranded RNA and has been recently implicated in vascular protection.
OBJECTIVES: To study the expression and role of TLR3 in PAH and to determine whether a TLR3 agonist reduces pulmonary hypertension in preclinical models.
METHODS: Lung tissue and endothelial cells from patients with PAH were investigated by polymerase chain reaction, immunofluorescence, and apoptosis assays. TLR3-/- and TLR3+/+ mice were exposed to chronic hypoxia and SU5416. Chronic hypoxia or chronic hypoxia/SU5416 rats were treated with the TLR3 agonist polyinosinic/polycytidylic acid (Poly[I:C]).
MEASUREMENTS AND MAIN RESULTS: TLR3 expression was reduced in PAH patient lung tissue and endothelial cells, and TLR3-/- mice exhibited more severe pulmonary hypertension following exposure to chronic hypoxia/SU5416. TLR3 knockdown promoted double-stranded RNA signaling via other intracellular RNA receptors in endothelial cells. This was associated with greater susceptibility to apoptosis, a known driver of pulmonary vascular remodeling. Poly(I:C) increased TLR3 expression via IL-10 in rat endothelial cells. In vivo, high-dose Poly(I:C) reduced pulmonary hypertension in both rat models in proof-of-principle experiments. In addition, Poly(I:C) also reduced right ventricular failure in established pulmonary hypertension.
CONCLUSIONS: Our work identifies a novel role for TLR3 in PAH based on the findings that reduced expression of TLR3 contributes to endothelial apoptosis and pulmonary vascular remodeling.

**What Is New?**
- 该研究将 TLR3 定位为 PAH 中此前未被认识的内皮保护性受体节点：患者肺组织和内皮细胞中 TLR3 降低，而 TLR3-/- 小鼠在慢性缺氧/SU5416 下肺高血压更重。
- 机制上，TLR3 敲低使内皮细胞转向其他胞内 dsRNA 受体信号并增加凋亡易感性，从而把 TLR3 缺失与肺血管重塑的驱动环节连接起来。
- 在两种大鼠 PAH 模型中，高剂量 Poly(I:C) 在体外经 IL-10 上调内皮 TLR3，并在体内减轻已建立肺高血压及右心衰竭，提供了新的临床前 in vivo 验证。

**研究设计**
- 人 PAH 肺组织与内皮细胞：PCR、免疫荧光、凋亡 assay。
- TLR3-/- 与 TLR3+/+ 小鼠暴露于慢性缺氧和 SU5416，比较肺高血压严重程度。
- 慢性缺氧或慢性缺氧/SU5416 大鼠接受 TLR3 激动剂 Poly(I:C) 治疗，作为 proof-of-principle 体内实验。
- 体外 TLR3 knockdown 与 Poly(I:C)/IL-10 机制实验，评估 dsRNA 受体信号和内皮凋亡。

**主要发现**
- PAH 患者肺组织和内皮细胞中 TLR3 表达降低。
- TLR3-/- 小鼠在慢性缺氧/SU5416 后出现更严重的肺高血压。
- TLR3 knockdown 促进内皮细胞经其他胞内 RNA 受体传递 dsRNA 信号，并增加凋亡易感性。
- Poly(I:C) 在大鼠内皮细胞中经 IL-10 上调 TLR3 表达。
- 高剂量 Poly(I:C) 在两种大鼠模型中减轻肺高血压，并在已建立肺高血压中减轻右心衰竭。

**为什么入选** 机制链较完整：人 PAH 组织表达下降→TLR3 loss-of-function 加重体内肺高血压→内皮 dsRNA 受体信号与凋亡→IL-10 依赖的 TLR3 上调→Poly(I:C) rescue 及右心衰竭改善；同时包含人组织、遗传小鼠和两种大鼠模型，发表于 AJRCCM。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要，缺少样本量、剂量、统计方法及完整实验细节。
- Poly(I:C) 为 TLR3 激动剂但高剂量可能激活其他 dsRNA 感受通路，摘要未提供充分特异性对照。
- IL-10 介导 TLR3 上调主要来自大鼠内皮细胞体外实验，体内细胞特异性与完整因果链尚未在摘要中阐明。

**可借鉴之处**
- TLR3-/- 与 TLR3+/+ 小鼠结合慢性缺氧/SU5416 的 loss-of-function 体内设计，可用于验证其他内皮保护节点是否真正因果参与肺血管重塑。
- 内皮细胞中 dsRNA 受体信号重编程与凋亡易感性的读出，可迁移到肺泡/肺血管内皮损伤及免疫-基质 crosstalk 模型，用于区分内皮凋亡与增殖性重塑。
- 在已建立疾病阶段进行 agonist rescue 并同时观察右心衰竭，是可复用的治疗性靶点验证思路，尤其适合肺血管 remodeling 与右心功能耦联的研究。

### 1. Meteorin-like ameliorates acute bacterial lung infection by enhancing neutrophil function.

American journal of respiratory cell and molecular biology | 2026 Nov 01 | Hao Ding, Ju Cao
IF 2025：6.4 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Laboratory Medicine, The First Affiliated Hospital of Chongqing Medical University, Chongqing, China.
- 领域：重庆医科大学附属第一医院检验医学科，主要研究领域推断为临床检验医学与感染免疫，聚焦细菌性肺炎宿主免疫。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42153329/
- DOI：10.1093/ajrcmb/aanag068

**Abstract**

RATIONALE: Bacterial pneumonia is a common acute respiratory infection. The role of Meteorin-like (METRNL) in bacterial pneumonia is unknown.
OBJECTIVES: To investigate the clinical and functional role of METRNL in bacterial pneumonia.
METHODS: METRNL levels were examined in the animals and patients with bacterial pneumonia. Multiple genetic and pharmacologic approaches were used to investigate METRNL-mediated host immune responses during bacterial pneumonia.
MEASUREMENTS AND MAIN RESULTS: METRNL production was dramatically suppressed in response to acute bacterial lung infection. METRNL loss increased mortality and bacterial burden during Pseudomonas aeruginosa and Staphylococcus aureus pneumonia, but had no effects on Aspergillus fumigatus and influenza virus pneumonia. Conversely, METRNL overexpression resulted in decreased mortality and bacterial burden from bacterial pneumonia. Furthermore, therapeutic administration of recombinant METRNL protein improved mortality and bacterial clearance in a neutrophil-dependent manner after bacterial pneumonia. METRNL enhanced bacterial phagocytosis and subsequent killing capacity of neutrophils, and conditional knockout of KIT receptor tyrosine kinase in neutrophils abolished METRNL-mediated protection against bacterial pneumonia. Furthermore, the increased antibacterial functions of neutrophils elicited by the METRNL-KIT axis were mediated through adenosine monophosphate-activated protein kinase (AMPK) signaling pathway. The augmented antibacterial effects of METRNL on neutrophils were also confirmed in humans, and circulating METRNL levels were reduced in patients with bacterial pneumonia, which might serve as a new biomarker for patient stratification and therapeutic guidance.
CONCLUSIONS: This study suggests that a potential theranostic approach involving METRNL-guided patient stratification and targeted therapy using METRNL rescue therapy may help improve the management of patients with bacterial pneumonia.

**What Is New?**
- 首次将 METRNL 定义为急性细菌性肺炎中受感染迅速抑制的内源性宿主防御调节因子，并证明其缺失加重、过表达或补充可减轻细菌负荷与死亡。
- 新闭合了 METRNL 保护作用的因果链：其效应依赖中性粒细胞，中性粒细胞条件性敲除 KIT 可取消保护，抗菌功能增强由 AMPK 信号介导。
- 在动物模型之外获得人源验证：患者循环 METRNL 降低，人中性粒细胞抗菌增强，重组 METRNL 治疗性给药可改善细菌清除，提示其作为分层标志与 rescue 靶点的转化价值。

**研究设计**
- 检测动物模型和细菌性肺炎患者中 METRNL 水平。
- 采用遗传学和药理学多种手段进行 METRNL 缺失、过表达及重组蛋白治疗实验。
- 比较 Pseudomonas aeruginosa、Staphylococcus aureus、Aspergillus fumigatus 和 influenza virus 肺炎中的病原体特异性效应。
- 使用中性粒细胞条件性 KIT 敲除验证 METRNL–KIT 轴，并检测 AMPK 信号。
- 在人中性粒细胞和患者循环样本中验证抗菌功能与 METRNL 水平。

**主要发现**
- 急性细菌性肺感染后 METRNL 产生显著受抑，患者循环 METRNL 水平降低。
- METRNL 缺失增加 Pseudomonas aeruginosa 和 Staphylococcus aureus 肺炎的死亡率和细菌负荷，但对 Aspergillus fumigatus 和 influenza virus 肺炎无明显影响。
- METRNL 过表达降低细菌性肺炎死亡率和细菌负荷；治疗性给予重组 METRNL 以中性粒细胞依赖方式改善死亡率和细菌清除。

- 「外周免疫挑战—白质微环境—发育性髓鞘」这条轴可作为研究呼吸道感染远期中枢后果的模板：以限定发育时间窗的感染暴露 + 分阶段（1、2、6 个月）行为学追踪刻画表型演变。
- 用遗传性阻断同一细胞过程来复现表型的「反向 rescue/模拟」逻辑，比单纯相关性观察更能支撑因果链，值得在 fibroblast、immune-stromal crosstalk 等课题中借鉴。
- 细胞数恢复但髓鞘结构（髓鞘化轴突密度、鞘厚度）未恢复的分离现象提示：评估修复类干预时须以超微结构/髓鞘形态为终点，而不能只看细胞计数。

### 3. Spatiotemporal control of STING activation and saRNA delivery decouples humoral and cellular immunity.

bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology | 2026 Sep 18 | David J Peeler, John S Tregoning


- 来源：biorxiv
- 槽位：预印本
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Materials, Department of Bioengineering and Institute for Biomedical Engineering, Imperial College London, SW7 2AZ, UK.; Department of Infectious Disease, Imperial College London, SW7 2AZ, UK.; Department of Physiology, Anatomy and Genetics, Department of Engineering Science, Kavli Institute for Nanoscience Discovery, University of Oxford, OX1 3QU, UK.
- 领域：据Imperial College London材料/生物工程与牛津生理、解剖、遗传及工程科学等交叉单位及摘要推断，主要关注纳米材料/递送系统与saRNA疫苗免疫学的交叉。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42779658/
- DOI：10.1002/mabi.202200424

**Abstract**

Self-amplifying mRNA (saRNA) vaccine formulations link the location and timing of antigen expression to innate immune stimulation by both RNA cargo and carrier. We hypothesized that targeting a polymeric STING agonist prodrug (polySTING) to antigen-presenting cells (APCs) independently of saRNA delivery could tune vaccines responses without limiting saRNA expression. By varying the time and location of STING signaling relative to saRNA delivered by muscle-restricted (polyplex, PP) and lymph node-draining (lipid nanoparticle, LNP) saRNA formulations, we studied how spatiotemporal colocalization of type I interferon (IFN-I) and antigen expression impacts innate and adaptive vaccine responses. Co-administering PP + polySTING decreased antigen-specific adaptive type 1 responses by mismatching early lymph node IFN-I and later arriving antigen; this could be reversed by delaying polySTING delivery timing. Conversely, synchronizing early LNP saRNA delivery and polySTING IFN-I in the lymph node boosted saRNA type 1 T cell responses without impacting humoral immunity, which re-directed antigen-intrinsic immune skewing and enhanced protection against pulmonary Acinetobacter baumannii challenge. Collectively, these findings define formulation-specific innate immune responses to saRNA vaccines and establish APC-targeted adjuvants as a strategy to tune saRNA vaccine immunogenicity.

**What Is New?**
- 提出将APC靶向的STING激动剂前药与saRNA递送解耦，证明IFN-I信号与抗原表达的空间/时间配比是可独立调控疫苗免疫偏向的机制变量。
- 发现肌肉限制性polyplex saRNA与polySTING共给会因淋巴结早期IFN-I与后到抗原错配而削弱抗原特异性1型应答，且延迟polySTING给药可逆转这一缺陷，从而把给药时序确立为因果性调控点。
- 在淋巴结引流的LNP saRNA体系中，同步早期IFN-I可选择性增强1型T细胞应答而不影响体液免疫，并增强对肺部Acinetobacter baumannii攻击的保护，实现细胞免疫与体液免疫的解耦。

**研究设计**
- 使用saRNA疫苗平台，比较肌肉限制性polyplex（PP）与淋巴结引流脂质纳米颗粒（LNP）两种递送路径。
- 应用APC靶向的polymeric STING agonist prodrug polySTING，与saRNA分开给药以独立调节STING/IFN-I信号。
- 改变polySTING相对saRNA的时间与位置，评估IFN-I与抗原共定位对先天及适应性疫苗应答的影响。
- 读取抗原特异性1型T细胞应答、体液免疫及肺部Acinetobacter baumannii攻击保护。

**主要发现**
- saRNA递送路径决定抗原表达与先天免疫刺激的时空关系：肌肉限制性PP与淋巴结引流LNP呈现不同免疫后果。
- PP + polySTING共给降低抗原特异性1型适应性应答，机制归因于淋巴结早期IFN-I与较晚到达抗原的空间/时间错配。
- 调整polySTING给药时间可逆转PP体系中的削弱效应，说明时序而非单纯STING信号强度决定结局。
- LNP saRNA与polySTING在淋巴结同步可增强saRNA诱导的1型T细胞应答，同时不改变体液免疫，并重定向抗原内在的免疫偏向。
- 该同步策略提高对肺部Acinetobacter baumannii攻击的保护。

**为什么入选** 机制链清晰：把STING/IFN-I信号与saRNA抗原表达的时空配比作为因果变量，用递送路径和给药时序做双向调控（削弱可逆、增强选择性），并在肺部Acinetobacter baumannii攻击中验证保护；对疫苗佐剂与递送研究有概念价值。

**局限**
- 仅见摘要；未提供polySTING化学结构、APC靶向验证、给药剂量、动物模型细节、统计及安全性/毒性数据。
- 摘要未展开具体APC亚群、STING信号细胞来源、IFN-I下游通路及抗原特异性T细胞/抗体质量的精细表型。
- 为bioRxiv预印本，且提供的全文片段与本文无关，无法进行全文核验；结论需同行评议与独立重复。

**可借鉴之处**
- 将佐剂与抗原递送分开、只调佐剂时序/定位，是解析IFN-I与抗原共定位因果作用的可复用设计；延迟给药rescue尤其适合验证时间窗。
- APC靶向STING前药可作为可调模块，与肌肉限制或淋巴结引流载体组合，用于需要Th1/CTL偏向而保留抗体应答的saRNA疫苗基础研究。
- 用两种递送路径制造空间变量、结合肺部感染攻击读出，可借鉴到肺组织免疫微环境中先天-适应性免疫时序研究。
