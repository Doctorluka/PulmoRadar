### 1. Current smoking and COPD are associated with differentiation-dependent secretory and inflammatory programs in airway basal cells.

Respiratory research | 2026 Aug 29 | Felix Ritzmann*, Michelle Brand*, Christoph Beisswenger
IF 2025：5.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Internal Medicine V-Pulmonology, Allergology and Respiratory Critical Care Medicine, Saarland University, Homburg, 66421, Germany.; Microbial Natural Products (MINS), Helmholtz-Institute for Pharmaceutical Research Saarland (HIPS), Helmholtz Centre for Infection Research (HZI), Saarland University Campus, Saarbrücken, 66123, Germany.
- 领域：第一作者隶属 Saarland University 呼吸与重症医学科及 Helmholtz-Institute for Pharmaceutical Research Saarland，主要研究气道上皮细胞（basal cell）在吸烟与 COPD 中的表观遗传与分化重塑。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42698073/
- DOI：10.1186/s12931-020-01486-x

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Persistent airway epithelial abnormalities contribute to chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), but it remains unclear whether smoking- and COPD-associated epithelial remodeling is retained in airway basal cells and transmitted during differentiation. We determined whether current smoking and COPD are associated with methylation-linked regulatory programs in airway basal cells that shape epithelial differentiation in patient-derived bronchial organoids.
METHODS: We integrated DNA methylation profiling and bulk transcriptomics in patient-derived airway basal cells and matched three-dimensional bronchial organoids. Methylation-defined gene sets were mapped to organoid epithelial cell states using single-cell RNA-seq and contextualized with publicly available airway epithelial datasets.
RESULTS: In this exploratory cohort, current smoking was associated with a predominant shift toward promoter hypomethylation in airway basal cells and matched organoids. Hypomethylated promoters were enriched for genes preferentially expressed in secretory epithelial cells, including BPIFB1, BPIFA2, MSMB and GALNT6. These genes showed little smoking-associated expression difference in basal-cell culture but were upregulated after organoid differentiation, indicating a differentiation-dependent epithelial memory of smoking. In COPD-derived basal cells, promoter methylation changes involved reduced xenobiotic metabolism and enhanced immune- and infection-related programs. Consistently, COPD-derived organoids showed reduced expression of detoxification-associated pathways and increased lysosomal, endocytic and host-defense programs.
CONCLUSIONS: Current smoking and COPD are associated with persistent methylation-linked regulatory alterations in airway basal cells that become functionally apparent during epithelial differentiation. These findings support a model in which airway basal-cell memory contributes to secretory, inflammatory and host-defense remodeling in chronic airway disease.

**What Is New?**
- 将 current smoking 与人 airway basal cells 及其配对 bronchial organoids 的启动子低甲基化直接关联，并首次证明对应基因（BPIFB1、BPIFA2、MSMB、GALNT6）在 basal cell 培养中几乎无表达差异、仅在 organoid 分化后才上调，即 smoking 的“分化依赖性上皮记忆”而非即时转录反应。
- 在 COPD 来源 basal cells 中定义了一条以 xenobiotic metabolism 程序减弱、immune/infection 相关程序增强为特征的甲基化调控轴，并在配对 organoids 中一致地读出 detoxification 通路下降与 lysosomal、endocytic、host-defense 程序升高，把表观改变与分化后功能表型闭合起来。
- 通过“同一患者 basal cells + 3D bronchial organoids + scRNA-seq 细胞状态映射”的严格人源配对设计，把 methylation-defined gene sets 定位到具体上皮细胞状态，从而将 basal cell 确立为可跨分化传递的调控记忆载体这一机制节点。

**研究设计**
- 探索性人源队列：患者来源 airway basal cells 与配对 3D bronchial organoids
- 整合 DNA methylation profiling 与 bulk transcriptomics
- methylation-defined gene sets 经 scRNA-seq 映射至 organoid 上皮细胞状态
- 与公开气道上皮数据集进行 contextualization 比较
- 按 current smoking 与 COPD 分层，比较分化前（basal cell 培养）与分化后（organoid）读出

**主要发现**
- current smoking 相关改变以启动子 hypomethylation 为主，且在 basal cells 与配对 organoids 中表现一致。
- 低甲基化启动子富集在分泌型上皮细胞高表达基因上，包括 BPIFB1、BPIFA2、MSMB、GALNT6。
- 这些基因在 basal cell 培养中吸烟相关表达差异很小，但 organoid 分化后显著上调，构成分化依赖性记忆的证据。
- COPD 来源 basal cells 的启动子甲基化改变涉及 xenobiotic metabolism 减弱及 immune/infection 相关程序增强。
- COPD 来源 organoids 相应表现为 detoxification 相关通路表达下降，lysosomal、endocytic 与 host-defense 程序升高。

**为什么入选** 入选理由是表观（methylation）—转录组—单细胞细胞状态—人源类器官的多层闭环：以同一患者 basal cells 与配对 bronchial organoids 为材料，用分化作为功能读出，把吸烟/COPD 相关的调控改变从“相关”推进到“跨分化可传递”的机制模型；模型体系与通讯作者团队长期使用的 3D 气道类器官平台一致，机制链清晰（甲基化改变 → 分泌/解毒/宿主防御程序 → 分化后显现）。

**局限**
- 本次仅有摘要可评估；所提供的 full-text snippet 实为同一课题组 2020 年 Sprott 等关于 flagellin 与 bronchospheres 的另一篇文章，并非本文全文，故方法细节、样本量与统计均无法核实。
- 研究为探索性队列的关联性观察，缺少甲基化编辑、去甲基化干预或 CRISPR 等因果验证，也未报告基因 rescue 实验。
- 体系仅含上皮（basal cell 与 organoid），未纳入成纤维细胞或免疫细胞共培养及体内模型，因此上皮—基质/免疫 crosstalk 的作用未被评估。

**可借鉴之处**
- 配对设计可直接复用：同一患者来源 basal cells 体外扩增后分为“未分化”与“3D organoid 分化”两臂，用分化前后对比来区分即时表达变化与表观遗传记忆，适合气道/肺泡上皮的暴露—重塑类课题。
- 用 DNA methylation-defined gene sets 映射到 scRNA-seq 上皮细胞状态，是把表观组数据与单细胞图谱对齐、并赋予细胞类型归属的低成本分析范式。
- 对 fibroblast 与 immune-stromal crosstalk 方向：该体系提示 basal cell 内在程序可能需微环境输入才完全显形，可在 organoid 中加入成纤维细胞或免疫细胞共培养，检验记忆程序是否依赖基质/免疫信号。

### 1. ADAMTS14 is a Novel Modulator of Fibroblast Mechanoactivation in Pulmonary Fibrosis.

American journal of respiratory and critical care medicine | 2026 Jul 28 | Ingo Ganzleben, Benjamin D Medoff
IF 2025：21.7 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Division of Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine, Massachusetts General Hospital, Boston, USA.; Center for Immunology and Inflammatory Diseases, Massachusetts General Hospital, Boston, USA.; Harvard Medical School, Boston, USA.
- 领域：肺纤维化中成纤维细胞 mechanoactivation 与免疫-基质相互作用的基础/转化研究（Massachusetts General Hospital 呼吸与危重症医学科及免疫与炎症中心）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42518202/
- DOI：10.1093/ajrccm/aamag396

**Abstract**

RATIONALE: Yes-associated protein (YAP)-mediated fibroblast mechanoactivation is an important driver of fibrosis in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF).
OBJECTIVE: To characterize the role of ADAM with Thrombospondin motifs 14 (ADAMTS14) in YAP-mediated fibroblast mechanoactivation and pulmonary fibrosis.
METHODS: We disrupted ADAMTS14 expression in primary human lung fibroblasts (HLFs) and demonstrated its role in YAP nuclear translocation and fibroblast activation. We confirmed the in vivo relevance of ADAMTS14 in an IPF patient cohort using transcriptomic studies. ADAMTS14-deficient fibroblasts were further characterized in mechanistic studies combining advanced microscopy, unbiased proteomics, and co-immunoprecipitation with functional mechanobiology assays to delineate substrate-matrix interactions.
RESULTS: An unbiased siRNA screen identified ADAMTS14 as a regulator of YAP-mediated fibroblast activation and pro-fibrotic activity. Transcriptomic analyses of patient samples with fibrotic lung disease identified an ADAMTS14-expressing fibroblast population characterized by excessive collagen matrix synthesis and located within fibroblastic foci of IPF patients. Disruption of ADAMTS14 expression in IPF patient-derived HLFs reduced pro-fibrotic gene expression and attenuated the response to TGFβ. Mechanistically, we identified collagen V as a novel functionally relevant ADAMTS14 substrate essential for matrix stability. ADAMTS14-deficient fibroblasts produced an unstable extracellular matrix, leading to disorganized focal adhesions, impaired force transmission, and reduced focal adhesion-FAK-AKT signaling.
CONCLUSION: We identify a novel ADAMTS14-collagen V-focal adhesion axis as a potential driver of fibroblast activation in IPF, linking extracellular proteolytic matrix remodeling to focal adhesion dynamics and YAP-mediated mechanoactivation. This feed-forward circuit provides a new mechanistic framework for pulmonary fibrosis and identifies potential novel therapeutic targets.

**What Is New?**
- 通过 unbiased siRNA screen 首次将 ADAMTS14 定义为 YAP 介导成纤维细胞 mechanoactivation 的上游正向调控节点，而非既往已知的 YAP 通路组分。
- 在 IPF 患者肺组织中鉴定出一个新的 ADAMTS14-expressing 成纤维细胞亚群，其定位于 fibroblastic foci 并伴过度胶原基质合成，把分子功能与人类疾病中的特定细胞状态对应起来。
- 首次把 collagen V 定义为 ADAMTS14 的功能性相关底物，并闭合了 ADAMTS14–collagen V–基质稳定性–focal adhesion–FAK/AKT–YAP 这一前馈机制链。
- 在患者来源 IPF HLFs 中证明敲低 ADAMTS14 可降低促纤维化基因表达并减弱对 TGFβ 的反应，提供了人源细胞层面的功能验证。

**研究设计**
- 在 primary human lung fibroblasts（HLFs）及 IPF 患者来源 HLFs 中干扰 ADAMTS14 表达，检测 YAP 核转位与成纤维细胞活化。
- 用 unbiased siRNA screen 筛选调控 YAP 依赖性成纤维细胞活化与促纤维化活性的因子。
- 在 IPF 患者队列中通过转录组学分析验证 ADAMTS14 的体内相关性并定位其表达细胞群。
- 结合 advanced microscopy、unbiased proteomics、co-immunoprecipitation 与 functional mechanobiology assays 解析 ADAMTS14 的底物-基质相互作用。

**主要发现**
- unbiased siRNA screen 将 ADAMTS14 鉴定为 YAP 介导成纤维细胞活化与促纤维化活性的调控因子。
- 纤维化肺病患者样本转录组分析发现一个 ADAMTS14-expressing 成纤维细胞群，其特征为过度胶原基质合成，并位于 IPF 患者的 fibroblastic foci 内。
- 在 IPF 患者来源 HLFs 中干扰 ADAMTS14 可降低促纤维化基因表达并减弱对 TGFβ 的应答。
- collagen V 被鉴定为 ADAMTS14 的新功能性底物，对基质稳定性必需。
- ADAMTS14 缺失的成纤维细胞产生不稳定的细胞外基质，导致 focal adhesion 结构紊乱、力传递受损以及 focal adhesion–FAK–AKT 信号减弱。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整且闭合（蛋白酶–底物–ECM 稳定性–focal adhesion–FAK/AKT–YAP 前馈环路），兼具 unbiased siRNA screen、患者来源原代细胞功能验证、IPF 患者队列转录组细胞群定位与人组织 fibroblastic foci 证据，并发表于呼吸领域顶刊 AJRCCM。

**局限**
- 仅依据摘要撰写，无 OA 全文，无法核实实验细节、统计与补充数据。
- ADAMTS14 表达上调的上游驱动因素、其蛋白酶活性是否为必需（催化失活突变体等）以及 in vivo 遗传或药物干预的 rescue 数据在摘要中未体现。
- 人体证据主要为转录组相关性与组织定位，尚缺直接的人体因果性验证。

**可借鉴之处**
- unbiased siRNA 筛选 + 原代 HLFs/患者来源 HLFs 敲低 + 机制解析的流程，可作为寻找 YAP mechanoactivation 上游蛋白酶调控节点的通用模板。
- 把 unbiased proteomics、co-IP 与 functional mechanobiology assays（基质稳定性、focal adhesion 组织、力传递）组合起来判定底物与因果顺序，可迁移到其他基质蛋白酶–ECM 硬度–力学信号的研究。
- 用患者转录组把某个蛋白酶富集到 fibroblastic foci 内的特定成纤维细胞亚群，这一「细胞状态注释」逻辑可用于免疫-基质 crosstalk、肺泡修复或肺血管重构中的成纤维细胞/肌成纤维细胞亚群定位。

### 2. Aberrant expression of the PREX2-MAGI2 axis modulates myofibroblast differentiation in pulmonary fibrosis.

American journal of physiology. Lung cellular and molecular physiology | 2026 Aug 22 | Yozo Sato, Masahiro Seike
IF 2025：4.0 · JCR：Q2 · 中科院2025：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Pulmonary Medicine and Oncology, Graduate School of Medicine, Nippon Medical School, Tokyo 113-8603, Japan.
- 领域：据单位为日本医科大学大学院医学研究科呼吸器・腫瘍内科及摘要内容推断，主要研究领域为呼吸系统疾病（含 IPF 等间质性肺病）的基础研究，具体方向不详。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42631393/
- DOI：10.1152/ajplung.00168.2025

**Abstract**

Myofibroblast differentiation is a pivotal event in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) and is driven by TGF-β1-dependent PTEN destabilization; however, the intermediate regulators remain incompletely defined. We tested three lung fibroblast cell lines (one normal and two IPF-derived) and identified the phosphatidylinositol 3,4,5-trisphosphate-dependent Rac exchanger 2 (PREX2)-membrane-associated guanylate kinase inverted 2 (MAGI2) as a critical regulator of myofibroblast differentiation. Loss-of-function analysis of MAGI2 in normal human lung fibroblasts revealed a shift toward a myofibroblast phenotype characterized by abundant α-SMA expression. In vitro experiments revealed increased PREX2 expression and decreased MAGI2 expression in IPF-derived lung fibroblasts compared with those in normal lung fibroblasts. Consistent with this finding, in vivo experiments using bleomycin-induced murine pulmonary fibrosis revealed PREX2 upregulation in contrast to MAGI2 downregulation with the progression of lesions. Notably, PREX2 depletion in IPF-derived cells enhanced MAGI2 expression, mitigating TGF-β1-induced α-SMA expression. Conversely, PREX2 overexpression promoted myofibroblast differentiation, as evidenced by MAGI2 downregulation followed by marked α-SMA expression. The PREX2-targeting suppressive miR-338-3p upregulated MAGI2 expression in vitro and ameliorated bleomycin-induced murine pulmonary fibrosis in vivo. Immunohistochemical staining of PREX2 on lung samples obtained from patients with IPF delineated that PREX2 was exclusively expressed in the fibroblastic foci of IPF-affected lungs. Fluorescence immunostaining revealed PREX2 colocalization with α-SMA in myofibroblasts within fibroblastic foci, corroborating our hypothesis. These findings suggest that MAGI2 acts as a negative regulator of myofibroblast differentiation by stabilizing PTEN, whereas PREX2 is aberrantly expressed in the fibrotic milieu, negatively modulating MAGI2, thereby accelerating pulmonary fibrosis.

**What Is New?**
- 首次将 PREX2–MAGI2 轴定义为 TGF-β1 依赖的 PTEN 失稳与肌成纤维细胞分化之间的中间调控节点，并新定义 MAGI2 为稳定 PTEN、抑制肌成纤维细胞分化的负调控因子。
- 通过 IPF 患者肺组织的 IHC 与荧光免疫共定位，新确立 PREX2 在 IPF 肺中几乎仅局限于 fibroblastic foci、并与肌成纤维细胞标志 α-SMA 共定位的细胞状态定位证据。
- 以 PREX2 敲低/过表达的双向功能验证叠加 miR-338-3p 体内干预，新闭合了“PREX2↑→MAGI2↓→PTEN 失稳→α-SMA↑”这一可被 miRNA 挽救的因果链。

**研究设计**
- 体外：3 株人肺成纤维细胞系（1 株正常、2 株 IPF 来源）比较 PREX2/MAGI2 表达，并进行 MAGI2 功能缺失、PREX2 敲低与过表达、TGF-β1 刺激及 miR-338-3p 干预。
- 体内：bleomycin 诱导小鼠肺纤维化模型，观察病变进展中 PREX2/MAGI2 变化并给予 miR-338-3p 治疗。
- 人组织：IPF 患者肺标本 IHC 检测 PREX2 分布，并以荧光免疫染色评估其与 α-SMA 的共定位。

**主要发现**
- IPF 来源肺成纤维细胞相较正常肺成纤维细胞呈现 PREX2 表达升高、MAGI2 表达降低，bleomycin 小鼠肺内随病变进展呈相同方向的变化。
- 在正常人气道/肺成纤维细胞中敲低 MAGI2 即可推动细胞向肌成纤维细胞表型转化，表现为 α-SMA 大量表达。
- 在 IPF 来源细胞中敲低 PREX2 可上调 MAGI2 并减轻 TGF-β1 诱导的 α-SMA 表达；反之 PREX2 过表达下调 MAGI2 并显著促进 α-SMA 表达。
- miR-338-3p 靶向抑制 PREX2，体外上调 MAGI2 表达，并在体内改善 bleomycin 诱导的小鼠肺纤维化。
- IPF 患者肺组织中 PREX2 仅表达于 fibroblastic foci，且与其中的 α-SMA 阳性肌成纤维细胞共定位。

**为什么入选** 机制链相对完整（TGF-β1→PREX2↑→MAGI2↓→PTEN 失稳→α-SMA↑），兼具双向功能验证、miRNA 体内 rescue、bleomycin 小鼠模型与 IPF 人组织定位证据，聚焦肺成纤维细胞/肌成纤维细胞分化这一纤维化的核心环节。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无 OA 全文，方法学细节、样本量、统计与独立重复等信息无法核实。
- MAGI2“稳定 PTEN”在摘要中主要为推断性结论，缺少 PTEN 蛋白半衰期、泛素化或直接相互作用等直接生化证据。
- miR-338-3p 对 PREX2 的直接靶向关系（如 3'UTR 报告基因验证）与体内给药方式在摘要中未详述。

**可借鉴之处**
- 配对使用正常来源与 IPF 来源成纤维细胞系、并以功能获得/缺失双向解析负调控因子的策略，可迁移到其他纤维化调控节点的筛选与验证。
- 以 miRNA mimic/antagomir 在 bleomycin 模型中做体内 rescue，是判断候选轴“可干预性”的实用设计范式。
- 用 α-SMA 与目标蛋白共染的免疫荧光在 fibroblastic foci 上做细胞状态定位，可借鉴于肺泡区或肺血管重塑相关成纤维细胞/肌成纤维细胞研究。

### 3. Gammaherpesvirus infection impairs lung fibrosis resolution through PD-L1+ fibroblasts and PD-1+ CD8+ T cell interaction.

Mucosal immunology | 2026 Jul 22 | Laura Kempen, Bénédicte Machiels
IF 2025：7.6 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者单位：Laboratory of Immunology and Vaccinology, Faculty of Veterinary Medicine, FARAH, ULiège, Liège 4000, Belgium; Laboratory of Immunophysiology, GIGA Institute, Liege University, Liège, Belgium.; Cardiovascular Research Institute Maastricht, Experimental Vascular Pathology, Department of Pathology, Maastricht University Medical Centre+, Maastricht, the Netherlands.
- 通讯作者单位：Laboratory of Immunology and Vaccinology, Faculty of Veterinary Medicine, FARAH, ULiège, Liège 4000, Belgium; Walloon Excellence in Life Sciences and Biotechnology (WELBIO) Department, WEL Research Institute; Wavre, Belgium. Electronic address: bmachiels@uliege.be.
- 第一作者领域：病毒–免疫互作与肺部炎症/纤维化消退机制，结合免疫生理学与实验血管病理背景。
- 通讯作者领域：γ疱疹病毒（MuHV-4/EBV）黏膜免疫与疫苗研究，关注病毒感染驱动的免疫–基质互作与组织修复障碍。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42486432/
- DOI：10.1016/j.mucimm.2026.100388

**Abstract**

Pulmonary fibrosis is a complex disease with poorly understood, multifactorial triggers. Gammaherpesviruses (γHVs), including Epstein-Barr virus in humans and Murid herpesvirus 4 (MuHV-4) in mice, have been linked to pulmonary fibrosis exacerbation, although the underlying mechanisms remain unclear. This study explores how γHV infection modulates bleomycin (bleo)-induced lung fibrosis in mice, using flow cytometry and multiplex spectral immunofluorescence (MSI) to analyze cell dynamics, localization, and intercellular crosstalk. Despite the substantial contribution of monocytes (MOs) to the alveolar macrophage (AM) pool following MuHV-4 infection, these cells do not exhibit overt profibrotic properties upon bleo treatment. In contrast, MuHV-4 infection led to the accumulation of activated, IFNγ-producing memory CD8+ T cells in the lungs of bleo-treated mice, with MSI revealing their enrichment and colocalization with fibroblasts. Notably, fibroblasts showed increased IFNγ-induced PD-L1 expression, while T cells upregulated PD-1, co-localizing in fibrosis-rich regions. In vivo neutralization of the PD-L1/PD-1 axis after bleo treatment significantly improved the resolution of fibrosis in MuHV-4-infected mice, highlighting the key pathogenic role of this interaction in sequelae persistence. Compared to fibroblasts cocultured with T cells from naïve mice, those cocultured with MuHV-4-induced T cells exhibited significantly increased collagen expression, while this effect was abrogated by PD-1 blockade. Similar results were observed in cocultures of human fibroblasts and CD8+ T cells isolated from EBV-infected patients, underscoring the translational potential of these findings. Collectively, our findings identify interactions between PD-1+ CD8+ T cells and PD-L1+ fibroblasts as central drivers of persistent lung fibrosis associated with γHV infection.

**What Is New?**
- 本研究把 PD-L1+ 成纤维细胞与 PD-1+ CD8+ T 细胞的互作确立为γHV 相关纤维化持续的中心致病节点，而非既往假设的单核细胞/巨噬细胞驱动的促纤维化机制。
- 研究闭合了一条新机制回路：MuHV-4 诱导的 IFNγ 产生型记忆 CD8+ T 细胞上调成纤维细胞 PD-L1，并与 PD-1+ T 细胞在纤维化富集区共定位，体内中和 PD-L1/PD-1 轴即可显著改善纤维化消退，提供了因果性 rescue 证据。
- 研究首次在 EBV 感染者来源的 CD8+ T 细胞与人成纤维细胞共培养中重现 PD-1 阻断可逆的胶原上调，把小鼠 MuHV-4 模型的结论延伸至人源体系。

**研究设计**
- 小鼠模型：bleomycin 诱导肺纤维化 + Murid herpesvirus 4（MuHV-4）感染的双打击设计，评估纤维化消退。
- flow cytometry 分析单核细胞（MOs）、肺泡巨噬细胞（AMs）与 T 细胞亚群动态。
- multiplex spectral immunofluorescence（MSI）解析细胞空间富集与共定位。
- 体内 PD-L1/PD-1 中和处理，检验对纤维化消退的因果作用。
- 体外共培养：MuHV-4 诱导的小鼠 T 细胞 ± PD-1 阻断与成纤维细胞共培养，测定胶原表达；并以 EBV 感染者 CD8+ T 细胞 + 人成纤维细胞做平行验证。

**主要发现**
- MuHV-4 感染后单核细胞对肺泡巨噬细胞池贡献显著，但这些细胞在 bleomycin 处理后未表现明显促纤维化表型。

- 与 naïve T 细胞相比，MuHV-4 诱导的 T 细胞使共培养成纤维细胞胶原表达显著升高，PD-1 阻断可消除此效应；EBV 感染者 CD8+ T 细胞与人成纤维细胞共培养呈相似结果。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整（γHV 感染 → IFNγ+ 记忆 CD8+ T 细胞 → 成纤维细胞 PD-L1 → PD-1 抑制信号 → 消退受阻），同时具备体内中和 rescue、空间共定位证据与 EBV 患者来源细胞的人源验证，直接落在免疫–基质 crosstalk 与肺泡/肺纤维化修复领域。

**局限**
- 仅获取到摘要，无 OA 全文，细胞亚群定义、时间窗、抗体剂量与统计学细节无法核验。

- 可借鉴“人肺单细胞/组织筛选候选基因 → 上皮特异性条件敲除 → 原代 AT2 细胞表型与多组学整合 → 人细胞 CRISPR 缺失和回补 rescue”的因果验证链，用于其他肺泡上皮损伤或修复研究。
- AT2 细胞 spheroid colony formation 可作为上皮再生/应激耐受读出；线粒体功能可组合 mtDNA 拷贝数、TOMM20/ATP5A、mitochondrial ROS、membrane potential、OCR/ECAR 进行分层评估。
- 对肺泡生物学及免疫-间质 crosstalk 的提示是，上皮线粒体应激可能改变 AT2 存活和修复信号，后续可用共培养或条件培养基实验检验其对成纤维细胞及免疫细胞的影响。
