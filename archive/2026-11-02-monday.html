Myeloid integrin α9β1 deficiency protects alveolar bone by remodeling the immune microenvironment of periodontitis.

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Periodontitis is a chronic inflammatory disease initiated by microbial infection that can lead to periodontal tissue damage and alveolar bone resorption. Macrophages contribute to the regulation of periodontal inflammation and local tissue homeostasis, but the role of integrin α9β1 in these processes remains unclear. Here, we combined analysis of human gingival single-cell transcriptomic data, a mouse model of myeloid cell-specific conditional Itga9 deletion, in vitro experiments with bone marrow-derived macrophages (BMMs), and conditioned-medium experiments in gingival fibroblasts to investigate how α9β1 affects the immune microenvironment and bone destruction in periodontitis. ITGA9 was highly expressed in human gingival macrophages and was further upregulated in periodontitis. Itga9 deletion altered the basal transcriptional state of BMMs and their response to Porphyromonas gingivalis (Pg), with reduced TNF production, downregulation of inflammation-related gene sets, and lower AKT and S6 phosphorylation following Pg stimulation. In vivo, myeloid Itga9 deletion reduced periodontal tissue damage and alveolar bone loss and was accompanied by changes in local immune cell composition and fibroblast-associated communication networks. Gene sets related to skeletal development, ossification, and extracellular matrix (ECM)-receptor interactions were positively enriched in fibroblasts. Further in vitro experiments showed that conditioned medium from Pg-stimulated KO BMMs was less inhibitory to gingival fibroblast mineralization than that from Pg-stimulated Het BMMs, as reflected by higher expression of mineralization-related genes, greater alkaline phosphatase (ALP) activity, and increased mineralized nodule formation. These findings suggest that myeloid integrin α9β1 contributes to the regulation of immune and stromal cell functions in periodontitis and that targeting this receptor may help reduce inflammation-associated bone destruction and support the mineralization potential of gingival fibroblasts.

MUC5B Overproduction Disrupts Club Cell Mediated Alveolar Repair in COPD.

杂志American journal of respiratory cell and molecular biology
IF: 6.4 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院2区 · 新锐2区
文章2026 Oct 28
10.1093/ajrcmb/aanag218
作者Yan Hu · Christopher M Evans
机构Center for Perinatal Research, Nationwide Children's Hospital, Columbus, OH, United States.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 将小气道 club cell 的 MUC5B 过度产生从黏液清除障碍标志提升为限制其祖细胞功能、连接 SAD 与肺泡修复失败的候选因果节点。
  • 利用 club cell 特异性 Muc5b 过表达与条件性敲除，在体内证明 MUC5B 足以损害 club cell→ATII 分化并加重肺气肿中的肺功能下降，而早期敲除可恢复 club cell 来源的肺泡再生。
  • 在人肺气肿组织和 PPE 小鼠模型中一致定位小气道 club cell 的 MUC5B 过表达，为“小气道病变驱动肺泡修复障碍”提供了跨物种验证。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

RATIONALE: Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) is characterized by small airway disease (SAD) and emphysema, but mechanisms linking these pathologies remain unclear. SAD arises early and is the primary site of airflow limitation, marked by epithelial injury, mucus obstruction, and bronchiole loss. Excessive production of the mucin MUC5B by bronchiolar club cells is a key feature of SAD and is associated with impaired mucociliary clearance and inflammation. Club cells also function as progenitors for alveolar type II (ATII) cells, yet how their regenerative capacity is altered in COPD is unknown.

OBJECTIVES: To determine whether MUC5B overproduction in small airway club cells alters their capacity to regenerate ATII cells and contributes to emphysema progression.

METHODS: We assessed MUC5B expression in human emphysematous lungs and a porcine pancreatic elastase (PPE)-induced mouse model of emphysema. Genetic approaches were used to overexpress or conditionally delete Muc5b in club cells to evaluate effects on club-to-ATII differentiation, alveolar structure, and lung function in health and emphysema.

RESULTS: MUC5B is overexpressed in small airway club cells in human emphysematous lungs and animal models. Muc5b overexpression impairs differentiation of club cells into ATII cells and worsened lung function decline in emphysema. Importantly, conditional deletion of Muc5b during early disease stages restored club cell-derived alveolar regeneration.

CONCLUSIONS: MUC5B overproduction links SAD to defective alveolar repair by limiting club cell progenitor function. Targeting MUC5B may restore epithelial regeneration and slow early COPD progression.

03 · 领域经典 仅摘要

ISM1 protects lung homeostasis via cell-surface GRP78-mediated alveolar macrophage apoptosis.

杂志Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America
IF: 9.4 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2022 Jan 25
10.1073/pnas.2019161119
作者Terence Y W Lam · Ruowen Ge
机构Department of Biological Sciences, Faculty of Science, National University of Singapore, Singapore 117543, Singapore.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 首次确定 ISM1 是健康肺内维持稳态的驻留抗炎蛋白，通过选择性诱导 csGRP78^high 肺泡巨噬细胞凋亡来控制肺泡巨噬细胞数量，从而把巨噬细胞凋亡调控与肺稳态直接连接起来。
  • 定义了一个此前未被明确区分的促炎肺泡巨噬细胞状态——csGRP78^high MMP-12^+ AM，并证明该状态在 Ism1^-/- 小鼠、香烟烟雾诱导 COPD 小鼠及人 COPD 肺中扩增，使其成为 ISM1 凋亡信号的优先靶细胞。
  • 通过气道内递送重组 ISM1 在 Ism1^-/- 与香烟烟雾诱导 COPD 小鼠中清除 csGRP78^high AM、阻断肺气肿发展并保留肺功能，提供了因果性体内 rescue 与转化前验证。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Alveolar macrophages (AMs) are critical for lung immune defense and homeostasis. They are orchestrators of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), with their number significantly increased and functions altered in COPD. However, it is unclear how AM number and function are controlled in a healthy lung and if changes in AMs without environmental assault are sufficient to trigger lung inflammation and COPD. We report here that absence of isthmin 1 (ISM1) in mice (Ism1-/- ) leads to increase in both AM number and functional heterogeneity, with enduring lung inflammation, progressive emphysema, and significant lung function decline, phenotypes similar to human COPD. We reveal that ISM1 is a lung resident anti-inflammatory protein that selectively triggers the apoptosis of AMs that harbor high levels of its receptor cell-surface GRP78 (csGRP78). csGRP78 is present at a heterogeneous level in the AMs of a healthy lung, but csGRP78high AMs are expanded in Ism1-/- mice, cigarette smoke (CS)-induced COPD mice, and human COPD lung, making these cells the prime targets of ISM1-mediated apoptosis. We show that csGRP78high AMs mostly express MMP-12, hence proinflammatory. Intratracheal delivery of recombinant ISM1 (rISM1) depleted csGRP78high AMs in both Ism1-/- and CS-induced COPD mice, blocked emphysema development, and preserved lung function. Consistently, ISM1 expression in human lungs positively correlates with AM apoptosis, suggesting similar function of ISM1-csGRP78 in human lungs. Our findings reveal that AM apoptosis regulation is an important physiological mechanism for maintaining lung homeostasis and demonstrate the potential of pulmonary-delivered rISM1 to target csGRP78 as a therapeutic strategy for COPD.

01 · 近一月高质量 仅摘要

Adenoviral-mediated SIRT1 expression restores lung architecture and reduces fibrotic progression in experimental models.

杂志American journal of physiology. Lung cellular and molecular physiology
IF: 4.0 · JCR: Q2 · 中科院2区 · 新锐2区
文章2026 Nov 01
10.1152/ajplung.00111.2026
作者Liliana Faridi Saavedra-Salazar · Jesús García-Bañuelos
机构Programa de Doctorado en Ciencias en Biología Molecular en Medicina, Centro Universitario de Ciencias de la Salud, Universidad de Guadalajara, Guadalajara, Jalisco, México.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 相较于既往主要依赖 SRT1720 等药理激活 SIRT1，本研究将 Ad-SIRT1 过表达作为基因递送策略，并在 BLM 肺纤维化模型中验证其抗纤维化作用。
  • 在 BLM 诱导的已建立肺纤维化中，于损伤后第 15 天经气管给予 Ad-SIRT1，第 21 天观察到胶原沉积、α-SMA 及 p53/H3K9 乙酰化降低，提示延迟基因干预可逆转已形成的纤维化表型。
  • 机制上将核内 SIRT1 过表达与 p53 和 histone H3K9 去乙酰化、肺泡上皮细胞存活恢复相连接，并用 SRT1720 与 EX527 进行药理双向验证该去乙酰化轴。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Pulmonary fibrosis (PF) involves remodeling of lung architecture, and limited therapeutic options are available. Sirtuin 1 (SIRT1), an epigenetic regulator, has emerged as a promising antifibrotic target due to its role in apoptosis, inflammation, and chromatin remodeling. We evaluated the effects of a recombinant adenovirus expressing human SIRT1 (Ad-SIRT1) both in vitro and in vivo. Human alveolar epithelial cells (A549) were transduced with Ad-SIRT1 or Ad-green fluorescent protein (Ad-GFP) and subjected to bleomycin (BLM)-induced injury. Transduction efficiency, cell viability (MTT assay), SIRT1 localization (immunofluorescence), and expression of acetylated p53 and histone H3K9 (Western blot) were assessed. Pharmacological modulation of SIRT1 was also tested using the activator SRT1720 and the inhibitor EX527. For the in vivo experiments, mice with bleomycin-induced PF received intratracheal Ad-SIRT1 on day 15 after injury. Histological, immunohistochemical, and molecular analyses were performed on day 21. Ad-SIRT1-transduced A549 cells showed increased nuclear SIRT1 overexpression that restored viability of bleomycin-injured cells and reduced p53 and H3K9 acetylation, consistent with a protective deacetylating effect. These changes mirrored those obtained with SRT1720 and were reversed by EX527 treatment. In vivo, Ad-SIRT1 attenuated collagen deposition, reduced α-smooth muscle actin (α-SMA) expression, and decreased p53 and H3K9 acetylation. The histological scores were significantly improved compared with those of the fibrotic control group. Adenoviral SIRT1 gene delivery exerts protective effects on the alveolar epithelium in vitro and reverses established PF in vivo through epigenetic regulation of key profibrotic pathways. This dual approach highlights the translational potential of SIRT1 as a therapeutic target for PF.NEW & NOTEWORTHY This study demonstrates that by epigenetically modifying p53 and histone H3K9 acetylation, adenoviral Sirtuin 1 (SIRT1) gene delivery reverses established pulmonary fibrosis and restores epithelial cell viability. Unlike previous techniques that relied on pharmacological activation, our results support the translational potential of SIRT1 as a therapeutic intervention for pulmonary fibrosis by establishing its overexpression as a gene-based antifibrotic strategy.

  • 体外：A549 细胞转导 Ad-SIRT1 或 Ad-GFP，给予 BLM 损伤，检测转导效率、MTT 活力、SIRT1 免疫荧光定位、acetyl-p53 与 acetyl-H3K9 Western blot。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Osteoarthritis (OA) and systemic sclerosis (SSc) are diseases with distinct etiologies but similar disease phenotypes, most prominently fibrosis, a process governed by the pathological activation of fibroblasts. Mechanisms underpinning fibrosis in these diseases are widely studied, though have yet to be directly compared. We employed single-cell RNA-seq and high-resolution spatial transcriptomics to describe the functional plasticity and the range of fibroblast activation states in both diseases. We identified disease-enriched fibroblast subsets conserved in both diseases, including fibrogenic myofibroblast-like cells, chemokine- and cytokine-expressing inflammatory fibroblasts, and lining-like fibroblasts. These subsets exhibited convergent fibrotic programs also observed in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis, as well as overlapping osteochondral transcriptional programs. Xenium 5K spatial transcriptomics revealed that conserved, disease-associated fibroblasts reside in highly similar anatomic niches in OA synovium and SSc skin. These consisted of a fibrotic ECM-rich niche harboring myofibroblast-like cells and an inflammatory niche containing inflammatory fibroblasts adjacent to infiltrating immune cells. Disease-associated transcriptional remodeling converged on shared functional pathways and common putative upstream molecular regulators. These findings identify conserved cellular states, spatial niches, and regulatory programs across OA and SSc, suggesting that fibrotic disease is underpinned by shared pathogenic stromal states and candidate targets for therapeutic strategies are applicable across both diseases.
