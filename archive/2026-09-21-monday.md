### 1. Generational changes in lung function in adults from several world regions: results from the Burden of Obstructive Lung Disease study.

Thorax | 2026 Sep 18 | Jixuan Ma
IF 2025：9.0 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Occupational & Environmental Health, School of Public Health, Tongji Medical College,Huazhong University of Science and Technology, Wuhan, Hubei, China j.ma@imperial.ac.uk.; National Heart and Lung Institute, Imperial College London, London, UK.
- 领域：呼吸系统流行病学与职业/环境暴露相关肺功能研究，单位为中国华中科技大学同济医学院公共卫生学院职业与环境健康系及英国 Imperial College London National Heart and Lung Institute。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42760129/
- DOI：10.1136/thorax-2026-225173

**Abstract**

This study estimated birth cohort effects on lung function using BOLD (Burden of Obstructive Lung Disease) data from 28 569 adults born between 1902 and 1976 across 41 sites in 34 countries. Forced vital capacity (FVC), forced expiratory volume in 1 s (FEV1) and the FEV1/FVC increased across successive birth cohorts in both high-income countries (HICs) and low- and middle-income countries (LMICs), with mean values rising steadily with later birth year. In fully adjusted models, FVC increased by 21.4 mL, FEV1 by 24.6 mL and FEV1/FVC by 0.25% per birth year. These positive associations were consistent across sex, smoking status and country-income subgroups (HICs and LMICs).

**What Is New?**
- 首次以 BOLD 统一标准化肺功能方案在 34 国 41 个中心量化出生队列效应，给出每出生年 FVC +21.4 mL、FEV1 +24.6 mL、FEV1/FVC +0.25% 的具体代际增益幅度。
- 代际增益不仅见于肺容积（FVC、FEV1），也见于 FEV1/FVC 比值本身，提示改善并非单纯"肺更大"，气道阻塞比例亦随出生队列下降，这是既往单一国家或单一指标研究未能同时确立的。
- 该代际增益在 HICs 与 LMICs、男女、吸烟与非吸烟亚组中方向完全一致，说明这一队列效应不能仅由吸烟模式或国家收入水平解释。

**研究设计**
- 数据来源：BOLD（Burden of Obstructive Lung Disease）跨国多中心调查。
- 样本：28 569 名成人，出生年份 1902–1976，来自 34 国 41 个中心。
- 暴露/自变量：出生年份（出生队列）。
- 结局：FVC、FEV1、FEV1/FVC。
- 统计：完全校正模型估计每出生年的肺功能变化，并按性别、吸烟状态、HICs/LMICs 分层。

**主要发现**
- FVC、FEV1 与 FEV1/FVC 均随出生队列递进而升高，平均值随出生年份稳步上升。
- 完全校正模型中，每推迟一个出生年，FVC 增加 21.4 mL、FEV1 增加 24.6 mL、FEV1/FVC 增加 0.25%。
- 上述正相关在 HICs 与 LMICs 两个国家收入亚组中均存在。
- 该正相关在女性与男性、吸烟者与非吸烟者中同样一致。

**为什么入选** ['样本量大（28 569 人）且覆盖 34 国 41 个中心，采用统一肺功能测量方案，跨国可比性强；', '以出生队列为切入点，把肺功能的长期时间趋势从单国观察提升为跨收入层级的定量比较；', '三个肺功能指标（容积指标与比值指标）方向一致，结论稳健；发表于 Thorax。']

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无 OA 全文，方法学细节（如年龄–时期–队列如何分离、校正变量清单、肺功能质控与参考方程）无法核实。
- 横断面重复调查本质上无法完全区分年龄效应、时期效应与出生队列效应，文中仅报告出生年份的校正关联。
- BOLD 各中心为现场抽样，未必代表各国全国人群；存活偏倚与各中心人群构成差异可能影响代际趋势估计。
- 摘要未提供任何机制或细胞层面数据，无法说明代际改善的生物学来源。

**可借鉴之处**
- 多中心、跨收入层级、统一肺功能测量与统一质控的横向设计，可作为环境暴露（职业、空气污染）与肺功能关联研究的外部参照基准，用于校正出生队列带来的时间趋势混杂。
- 分析策略上对出生年份做连续建模并按性别、吸烟状态、国家收入分层验证方向一致性，是处理 age–period–cohort 混杂时可借鉴的稳健性检验思路；若读者关注肺泡发育或肺血管重塑，本研究的队列趋势提示任何"暴露—肺功能"关联分析都需先扣除代际基线漂移。

### 2. Interrogation of functional variants in COPD GWAS loci by massively parallel reporter assays.

American journal of respiratory cell and molecular biology | 2026 Sep 15 | Lu Gong, Anny Xiaobo Zhou
IF 2025：6.4 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：年内旗舰系统研究
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者单位：Channing Division of Network Medicine, Brigham and Women's Hospital.; Harvard Medical School, Boston, MA 02115, USA.
- 通讯作者单位：Division of Pulmonary and Critical Care, Department of Medicine.; Department of Genome Science, University of Virginia, Charlottesville, VA 22903.
- 第一作者领域：据机构推断，第一作者主要研究领域为 COPD 遗传学与网络医学，聚焦 GWAS 位点功能变异及统计/功能基因组学（Channing Division of Network Medicine, Brigham and Women's Hospital；Harvard Medical School）。
- 通讯作者领域：据机构推断，通讯作者主要研究领域为呼吸与危重症医学结合基因组科学，聚焦 COPD 功能基因组学与变异到基因的机制解析（University of Virginia）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42745457/
- DOI：10.1093/ajrcmb/aanag179

**Abstract**

RATIONALE: Genome-wide association study (GWAS) loci often contain many linked variants, making it difficult to determine which variant is functionally relevant. Massively parallel reporter assays (MPRA) allow experimental testing of candidate variants to identify those with regulatory activity. Prior chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) MPRA studies have largely focused on individual loci, whereas broader multi-locus, multi-cell-type interrogation remains limited.
OBJECTIVES: We aim to identify functional variants in five COPD GWAS loci across three lung-relevant cell types.
METHODS: We screened 1120 variants using MPRA in epithelial (16HBE), fibroblast (MRC5), and endothelial (HUVEC) cells followed by reporter assay validation. Public Hi-C, ChIP-seq and ATAC-seq datasets were analyzed to evaluate chromatin context near candidate variants. We further performed CRISPR interference (CRISPRi) targeting variant-containing regions and measured gene expression by RT-qPCR in primary normal human bronchial epithelial (NHBE) cells using two gRNAs per variant. Co-immunoprecipitation was performed to test interaction between selected candidate genes.
MEASUREMENTS AND MAIN RESULTS: In MPRA, we identified 25 variants with allele-specific effects (∼2% of tested variants). Enrichment of H3K27Ac and open chromatin near rs35421223 was detected in 16HBE cells. CRISPRi identified two SNP-gene pairs, RUVBL1 and RAB7A regulated by rs35421223 in both the 16HBE cell line and primary NHBE cells. We detected interaction between RUVBL1 and the known COPD gene product FAM13A.
CONCLUSIONS: Screening COPD loci across three cell types identified functional regulatory variants and linked them to candidate target genes for future mechanistic studies.

**What Is New?**
- 将 COPD GWAS 功能变异筛查从以往单一位点研究扩展为五个位点 × 三种肺相关细胞类型（16HBE 上皮、MRC5 成纤维、HUVEC 内皮）的并行 MPRA 解析，首次给出跨位点、跨细胞类型的调控变异图谱。
- 通过 CRISPRi 在 16HBE 细胞系与 primary NHBE 两种体系中同时将 rs35421223 因果性地连接到 RUVBL1 与 RAB7A 两个靶基因，把以往仅有的报告基因活性关联推进为“变异—染色质—调控—靶基因”的机制闭环。
- 发现新鉴定的候选靶基因 RUVBL1 与已知 COPD 基因产物 FAM13A 存在蛋白互作，提示 GWAS 位点靶基因与既有 COPD 分子网络之间存在此前未被定义的连接节点。

**研究设计**
- 5 个 COPD GWAS 位点，共 1120 个候选变异
- MPRA 在 16HBE（上皮）、MRC5（成纤维）、HUVEC（内皮）三种细胞类型中平行筛查，并作 reporter assay 验证
- 公共 Hi-C、ChIP-seq、ATAC-seq 数据评估候选变异附近染色质语境
- CRISPRi 靶向含变异区域（每个变异 2 条 gRNA），在 16HBE 与 primary NHBE 中用 RT-qPCR 检测基因表达
- Co-IP 检测候选基因与已知 COPD 基因产物 FAM13A 的互作

**主要发现**
- MPRA 在 1120 个变异中鉴定出 25 个具有等位基因特异性效应的变异，约占受测变异的 2%。
- 在 16HBE 细胞中检测到 rs35421223 附近 H3K27Ac 富集与开放染色质信号。
- CRISPRi 鉴定出两个 SNP–基因对：rs35421223 调控 RUVBL1 与 RAB7A，且在 16HBE 细胞系与 primary NHBE 细胞中均成立。
- Co-IP 检测到 RUVBL1 与已知 COPD 基因产物 FAM13A 之间的相互作用。
- 结论认为跨三种细胞类型筛查 COPD 位点可识别功能性调控变异，并将其与候选靶基因相连，供后续机制研究。

**为什么入选** 机制链较完整（GWAS 变异→染色质语境→MPRA 调控活性→CRISPRi 因果验证→靶基因—FAM13A 蛋白互作），且覆盖上皮、成纤维、内皮三种肺相关细胞类型并在人原代 NHBE 中做了验证；属于呼吸细胞与分子生物学专业期刊的功能基因组学工作，对从 GWAS 关联走向因果机制的思路有参考价值。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要、无 OA 全文，MPRA 文库设计与等位基因效应阈值、CRISPRi 敲低效率、Co-IP 为内源还是过表达等关键方法细节均无法评估。
- MPRA 所用为 16HBE、MRC5、HUVEC 细胞系，其中 HUVEC 并非肺来源内皮，与 COPD 患者肺组织及原代成纤维/内皮仍存在差距。
- 1120 个变异中仅约 2% 显示等位基因特异性效应，且仅对 rs35421223 做了 CRISPRi 因果验证，其余位点尚缺体内或人组织水平的功能与表型验证。

**可借鉴之处**
- 多细胞类型（上皮/成纤维/内皮）平行 MPRA 加正交验证的流程，可复用于其他肺部 GWAS 位点或纤维化、肺血管重塑相关变异的优先级排序，避免单一细胞类型带来的假阴性/假阳性。
- “CRISPRi 每个变异 2 条 gRNA + RT-qPCR，在细胞系与 primary NHBE 双重验证 SNP–基因对”是可复用的因果验证范式，配合公共 Hi-C/ChIP-seq/ATAC-seq 染色质语境注释可提高“变异到基因”的置信度。
- 用 Co-IP 将候选靶基因与已知疾病基因（FAM13A）相连的“靶基因—疾病网络”思路，可为免疫–基质 crosstalk、肺泡上皮调控研究提供切入方式。

### 3. Reversal of emphysema by restoration of pulmonary endothelial cells.

The Journal of experimental medicine | 2021 Aug 02 | Shu Hisata*, Alexandra C Racanelli*, Augustine M K Choi
IF 2025：12.6 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：领域经典
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Division of Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine, Department of Medicine, Weill Cornell Medicine, New York, NY.; Division of Pulmonary Medicine, Department of Medicine, Jichi Medical University, Shimotsuke, Japan.; New York Presbyterian Hospital/Weill Cornell Medical Center, New York, NY.
- 领域：肺与危重症医学方向，聚焦肺内皮细胞生物学、COPD/emphysema 微血管功能障碍与再生干预。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34287647/

**Abstract**

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) is marked by airway inflammation and airspace enlargement (emphysema) leading to airflow obstruction and eventual respiratory failure. Microvasculature dysfunction is associated with COPD/emphysema. However, it is not known if abnormal endothelium drives COPD/emphysema pathology and/or if correcting endothelial dysfunction has therapeutic potential. Here, we show the centrality of endothelial cells to the pathogenesis of COPD/emphysema in human tissue and using an elastase-induced murine model of emphysema. Airspace disease showed significant endothelial cell loss, and transcriptional profiling suggested an apoptotic, angiogenic, and inflammatory state. This alveolar destruction was rescued by intravenous delivery of healthy lung endothelial cells. Leucine-rich α-2-glycoprotein-1 (LRG1) was a driver of emphysema, and deletion of Lrg1 from endothelial cells rescued vascular rarefaction and alveolar regression. Hence, targeting endothelial cell biology through regenerative methods and/or inhibition of the LRG1 pathway may represent strategies of immense potential for the treatment of COPD/emphysema.

**What Is New?**
- 将肺内皮细胞从 COPD/emphysema 的"伴随受损者"重新定位为驱动病理的中心因果细胞，并在人肺组织与 elastase 小鼠模型中同时确立这一关系。
- 新定义内皮来源的 LRG1 为致病调节节点：内皮细胞特异性删除 Lrg1 即可同时逆转血管稀疏（vascular rarefaction）与肺泡扩张，而非仅在表达层面相关。
- 给出"纠正内皮功能障碍"的再生性 in vivo 治疗验证——静脉输注健康肺内皮细胞可挽救 elastase 诱导的肺泡结构破坏，把内皮生物学从描述性关联推进为可操作的干预靶点。

**研究设计**
- 人肺组织观察性分析：肺气肿区域内皮细胞丢失与转录组状态刻画。
- elastase 诱导小鼠肺气肿模型，评估内皮丢失与肺泡扩张的时序关系。
- 静脉输注健康肺内皮细胞进行 rescue 实验。
- 内皮细胞特异性 Lrg1 缺失（条件性敲除）验证血管稀疏与肺泡回归。

**主要发现**
- 肺气肿肺组织的空气腔病变区显示显著的内皮细胞丢失，提示微血管功能障碍与疾病进程相关。
- 内皮转录组谱呈现凋亡、血管生成与炎症并存的复合状态。
- 静脉递送健康肺内皮细胞可挽救 elastase 模型中的肺泡结构破坏。
- LRG1 被鉴定为肺气肿的驱动因子，内皮来源 Lrg1 缺失可挽救血管稀疏与肺泡回归。
- 据此提出再生性内皮替代与 LRG1 通路抑制两条潜在干预策略。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整（内皮丢失 → LRG1 驱动 → 内皮特异性基因删除 rescue → 细胞治疗 rescue），同时具备人组织证据与小鼠 in vivo 因果验证，且涉及肺血管重塑与再生医学交叉点。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无 OA 全文，无法评估具体实验细节、样本量与统计学强度。
- 人源证据限于组织层面的描述性关联，未见人体验证 LRG1 因果性的干预数据。

**可借鉴之处**
- 静脉递送健康内皮细胞作为"细胞再生 rescue"范式，可用于检验其他肺血管稀疏/肺泡简化模型中的内皮依赖性因果链。
- 内皮特异性条件性敲除（如 Lrg1-flox × endothelial Cre）叠加 elastase 模型的因果验证逻辑，可复用于肺泡-血管互作与免疫-基质 crosstalk 研究。

### 1. Pirfenidone exerts anti-inflammatory effects by inhibiting CXCL6 to alleviate idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis.

International immunopharmacology | 2026 Sep 18 | Guangchun Sun, Zhijun Jie#, Feng Qian#, Qing Liang#
IF 2025：4.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Pharmacy, Shanghai Fifth People's Hospital, Fudan University, Shanghai 200240, China.
- 领域：第一作者隶属复旦大学附属上海市第五人民医院药剂科，推测关注肺纤维化药物治疗的药理机制与临床药学，具体方向不详。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42759355/
- DOI：10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117430

**Abstract**

Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) poses a health challenge with the destruction of lung architecture. It is featured by limited effective therapeutic options and a complex pathological mechanism. The molecular mechanism underlying the antifibrotic agent pirfenidone (PFD) remains to be fully determined. This study investigated the anti-inflammatory effects of pirfenidone in the treatment of IPF and explored its regulation of the C-X-C motif chemokine ligand 6 (CXCL6), a potent neutrophil chemoattractant. Using the murine model, pirfenidone has been identified to limit lung inflammation and neutrophil infiltration. Mice with pirfenidone administration expressed less CXCL6 than controls. Exogenous CXCL6 administration counteracted the anti-pulmonary fibrosis effect of pirfenidone in mice. Mechanistically, pirfenidone inhibited phosphorylation of NF-κB/p65, p38 MAPK, and Akt in IL-1β-stimulated macrophages, suggesting that pirfenidone modulated the inflammatory signaling pathways. Clinical analysis indicated that CXCL6 was upregulated in patients and decreased after pirfenidone treatment. Each unit increase in plasma CXCL6 concentration was linked to a 24% higher risk of death. This study identified the anti-inflammatory effects of pirfenidone and elucidated its novel mechanism by inhibiting CXCL6 secretion and neutrophil migration. Moreover, the identification of CXCL6 as a critical mediator of IPF may highlight a potential therapeutic target.

**What Is New?**
- 首次把趋化因子 CXCL6 确立为吡非尼酮抗 IPF 疗效所必需的下游效应节点——外源补充 CXCL6 可抵消其抗纤维化作用，构成此前未知的『药物—趋化因子』因果环节。
- 在机制上把吡非尼酮的抗炎作用收敛为对 IL-1β 刺激巨噬细胞中 NF-κB/p65、p38 MAPK 与 Akt 磷酸化的抑制，从而闭合『药物抑制巨噬细胞炎症信号→CXCL6 生成减少→中性粒细胞迁移与浸润下降→纤维化减轻』这一完整链条。
- 在人体层面新增了 CXCL6 的验证维度：IPF 患者 CXCL6 升高、吡非尼酮治疗后下降，且血浆 CXCL6 每升高 1 个单位对应死亡风险增加 24%。

**研究设计**
- 摘要层面研究设计：小鼠 IPF 模型给予吡非尼酮干预，观察肺部炎症与中性粒细胞浸润。
- 设置外源 CXCL6 补充（逆转）实验，检验 CXCL6 是否介导吡非尼酮的抗纤维化效应。
- 体外以 IL-1β 刺激巨噬细胞，检测 NF-κB/p65、p38 MAPK、Akt 磷酸化变化。
- 临床样本检测 IPF 患者 CXCL6 表达及吡非尼酮治疗后的变化，并与死亡风险做关联分析。

**主要发现**
- 吡非尼酮可限制小鼠肺内炎症反应与中性粒细胞浸润。
- 吡非尼酮给药小鼠的 CXCL6 表达低于对照组；外源给予 CXCL6 可抵消吡非尼酮的抗肺纤维化效果。
- 机制上，吡非尼酮抑制 IL-1β 刺激巨噬细胞中 NF-κB/p65、p38 MAPK 与 Akt 的磷酸化，提示其作用于炎症信号通路。
- 临床分析显示 CXCL6 在 IPF 患者中上调，经吡非尼酮治疗后下降。
- 血浆 CXCL6 浓度每升高 1 个单位，与死亡风险升高 24% 相关。

We validate this observation in-vivo using a genetic model of spontaneous lung fibrosis and in-vitro with mixed alveolar organoid cultures of various homeostatic and injury associated fibroblasts wherein we identify a mesenchymal-epithelial BMP signaling axis as a key driver of the AT2 cell injury repair response. Finally, we present supporting evidence from human disease, reinforcing the relevance of this fibroblast subset in pathological settings. These findings extend critical observations made prior to the single-cell era and contribute to our evolving understanding of fibroblast heterogeneity as a key feature of lung repair.

### 3. Cell-Specific Modulation of the Aryl Hydrocarbon Receptor by Kynurenine in Pulmonary Fibrosis Requires Microenvironmental Crosstalk.

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) is a progressive, chronic lung disease with limited therapeutic options. Tryptophan metabolism is significantly dysregulated during lung fibrogenesis, with the metabolite kynurenine (kyn) accumulating in lung tissue and driving pathology via the aryl hydrocarbon receptor (AHR). This study evaluates the cell-specific contributions of kyn-mediated AHR signaling across different pulmonary cell types to clarify its role in disease progression.
METHODS: Using a murine model of bleomycin-induced pulmonary fibrosis, lung tryptophan metabolites were profiled via liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry. The functional and transcriptomic impacts of kyn administration and AHR modulation were subsequently characterized across three distinct cellular compartments: CD103+ dendritic cells (DCs), fibroblasts, and alveolar epithelial cells (AECs).
RESULTS: Kyn levels were elevated in fibrotic lungs, and exogenous kyn selectively exacerbated collagen deposition during the fibrogenic phase rather than altering acute injury. In vitro monocultures of primary lung fibroblasts and AECs revealed negligible functional responses to kyn or AHR inhibition regarding myofibroblast differentiation, migration, or epithelial barrier disruption. Intriguingly, primary tissue-resident CD103+ DCs exhibited a hyperinflammatory, non-canonical AHR signaling profile in vivo. While ex vivo monoculture rapidly reverted these DCs to an anti-inflammatory, canonical AHR state, directly co-culturing DCs with fibrotic primary lung fibroblasts successfully restored the pathogenic, non-canonical signaling phenotype characterized by augmented IL-6 production and suppressed canonical targets.
CONCLUSIONS: Pathogenic AHR signaling in pulmonary fibrosis is highly cell-context dependent and driven by complex cell-cell interactions. Reductionist monocultures fail to replicate tissue-level dendritic cell phenotypes, highlighting the necessity of co-culture models and providing a cautionary note for the systemic clinical use of AHR-targeted therapeutics.

**What Is New?**
- 把 kyn–AHR 的致病作用从笼统的“通路激活”收窄为时相与细胞类型双重限定：外源 kyn 只在 bleomycin 模型的成纤维期加重 collagen 沉积而不影响急性损伤期，且原代 fibroblast 与 AEC 单培养对 kyn 或 AHR 抑制在 myofibroblast 分化、迁移、上皮屏障破坏上几乎无功能应答。
- 首次在体内原位的 CD103+ DC 中鉴定出一种高炎症、非经典（non-canonical）AHR 信号状态，区别于既往以 canonical AHR–IL-10/耐受性 DC 为主轴的抗炎认识。
- 通过 ex vivo 单培养迅速逆转为抗炎经典 AHR 状态、而仅与纤维化肺成纤维细胞直接共培养才能恢复 IL-6 升高与 canonical 靶基因受抑，闭合了“致病性 AHR 输出由细胞间 crosstalk 决定而非细胞自主”的机制环。

**研究设计**

- 三个细胞区室平行解析：CD103+ DC、原代肺 fibroblast、AEC

**主要发现**
- 纤维化肺内 kyn 水平升高；外源 kyn 选择性加重纤维化期 collagen 沉积，而不改变急性损伤。

- DC 离体单培养后迅速回到抗炎、经典 AHR 状态。
- DC 与纤维化肺原代 fibroblast 共培养可恢复致病性非经典表型，表现为 IL-6 产生增强与 canonical 靶基因受抑。
