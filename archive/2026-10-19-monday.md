### 1. Stem cells inhibit cigarette smoke extract-induced dendritic cells activation and alleviate chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

International immunopharmacology | 2026 Oct 18 | Ronghao Zhang, Xiaoyu Wang#, Yue Zhang#
IF 2025：4.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 领域：上海中医药大学中药学院/实验中心，研究方向从摘要与单位推断为中药药理学、干细胞与COPD炎症免疫调节。

**Abstract**

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) is a common lung disease; however, these is a need for new therapeutic drugs. Herein, we prepared cigarette smoke extract (CSE)-induced dendritic cells (DCs) activation, and investigated the effects of bone marrow-derived mesenchymal stem cells (BMSCs) on DCs. We found that BMSCs inhibited CSE induced DCs maturation. In addition, in COPD mice, BMSCs and umbilical cord blood-derived mesenchymal stem cells (UCBSCs) decreased percentage of DCs and macrophages in lungs. Furthermore, BMSCs and UCBSCs inhibited the mRNA level of inflammatory cytokines, such as TNFα, IL-6, IL-1β and iNOS via qRT-PCR. Nr4a family members were upregulated in COPD mice, and BMSC and UCBSC treatment inhibited Nr4a1, Nr4a2 and Nr4a3 expression via qRT-PCR, immunohistochemistry and Western blot. In addition, BMSCs and UCBSCs inhibited inflammation in colon of mice with CODP and regulated the gut microbiota using 16S rRNA gene sequencing. Collectively, our findings reveal that Nr4a family members are involved in the BMSCs-and UCBSCs-amelioration of COPD.

- 在COPD小鼠中并列验证BMSCs与UCBSCs两种来源MSC，均能降低肺内DCs和巨噬细胞比例并抑制TNFα/IL-6/IL-1β/iNOS炎症程序，提供了跨来源的体内证据。

**研究设计**
- 体外：CSE诱导DCs活化，给予BMSCs干预，评估DCs成熟。

**主要发现**

### 2. Fibroblast growth factor 10 reverses cigarette smoke- and elastase-induced emphysema and pulmonary hypertension in mice.

The European respiratory journal | 2023 Nov | Stefan Hadzic, Norbert Weissmann
IF 2025：23.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：三年内旗舰系统研究
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Excellence Cluster Cardio-Pulmonary Institute (CPI), Universities of Giessen and Marburg Lung Center (UGMLC), Member of the German Center for Lung Research (DZL), Justus-Liebig-University, Giessen, Germany.
- 领域：第一作者主要研究领域：依托CPI/UGMLC与DZL开展COPD、肺气肿及肺血管重塑的机制与转化研究（由摘要和单位推断）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37884305/
- DOI：10.1038/s41586-020-2882-8

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: COPD is an incurable disease and a leading cause of death worldwide. In mice, fibroblast growth factor (FGF)10 is essential for lung morphogenesis, and in humans, polymorphisms in the human FGF10 gene correlate with an increased susceptibility to develop COPD.
METHODS: We analysed FGF10 signalling in human lung sections and isolated cells from healthy donor, smoker and COPD lungs. The development of emphysema and PH was investigated in Fgf10+/- and Fgfr2b+/- (FGF receptor 2b) mice upon chronic exposure to cigarette smoke. In addition, we overexpressed FGF10 in mice following elastase- or cigarette smoke-induced emphysema and pulmonary hypertension (PH).
RESULTS: We found impaired FGF10 expression in human lung alveolar walls and in primary interstitial COPD lung fibroblasts. In contrast, FGF10 expression was increased in large pulmonary vessels in COPD lungs. Consequently, we identified impaired FGF10 signalling in alveolar walls as an integral part of the pathomechanism that leads to emphysema and PH development: mice with impaired FGF10 signalling (Fgf10+/- and Fgfr2b+/- ) spontaneously developed lung emphysema, PH and other typical pathomechanistic features that generally arise in response to cigarette smoke exposure.
CONCLUSION: In a therapeutic approach, FGF10 overexpression successfully restored lung alveolar and vascular structure in mice with established cigarette smoke- and elastase-induced emphysema and PH. FGF10 treatment triggered an initial increase in the number of alveolar type 2 cells that gradually returned to the basal level when the FGF10-mediated repair process progressed. Therefore, the application of recombinant FGF10 or stimulation of the downstream signalling cascade might represent a novel therapeutic strategy in the future.

**What Is New?**
- 新发现COPD人肺中FGF10信号呈腔室特异性改变：肺泡壁及原代间质肺成纤维细胞中FGF10表达受损，而肺大血管中FGF10表达升高。
- 通过Fgf10+/-与Fgfr2b+/-小鼠，将FGF10–FGFR2b信号减弱从人群遗传关联推进为足以自发引起肺气肿、PH及吸烟相关病理特征的因果节点。
- 在已建立的香烟烟雾或elastase诱导肺气肿和PH小鼠中，FGF10过表达可恢复肺泡与血管结构，并伴随alveolar type 2 cells先扩增后回落，提供了严格的in vivo rescue验证。

**研究设计**
- 分析人肺切片及健康供者、吸烟者和COPD肺分离细胞中的FGF10信号。
- Fgf10+/-和Fgfr2b+/-小鼠接受慢性香烟烟雾暴露，评估肺气肿与PH。
- 在elastase或香烟烟雾诱导的已建立肺气肿/PH小鼠中过表达FGF10。
- 检测肺泡结构、肺血管/PH表型及alveolar type 2 cells动态。

**主要发现**

- Fgf10+/-与Fgfr2b+/-小鼠自发出现肺气肿、PH及通常在香烟烟雾暴露后出现的典型病理机制特征。
- FGF10过表达可恢复香烟烟雾或elastase诱导的已建立肺气肿和PH小鼠的肺泡与血管结构。
- FGF10治疗早期增加alveolar type 2 cells数量，随修复进展逐渐回落至基础水平。

**为什么入选** 机制链较完整：人肺腔室特异性表达→遗传性FGF10/FGFR2b信号减弱因果模型→已建立疾病模型的FGF10过表达rescue；同时覆盖肺气肿与PH，兼具肺泡和肺血管维度，发表于ERJ。

### 3. Single-cell Wnt signaling niches maintain stemness of alveolar type 2 cells.

Science (New York, N.Y.) | 2018 Mar 09 | Ahmad N Nabhan, Mark A Krasnow#, Tushar J Desai#
IF 2025：47.3 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：领域经典
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Biochemistry, Stanford University School of Medicine, Stanford, CA 94305-5307, USA.; Howard Hughes Medical Institute, Stanford University School of Medicine, Stanford, CA 94305-5307, USA.
- 领域：第一作者主要研究领域：肺泡上皮干细胞与Wnt信号微环境（依据Stanford Biochemistry/HHMI单位与摘要推断）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29420258/
- DOI：10.1126/science.aam6603

**Abstract**

Alveoli, the lung's respiratory units, are tiny sacs where oxygen enters the bloodstream. They are lined by flat alveolar type 1 (AT1) cells, which mediate gas exchange, and AT2 cells, which secrete surfactant. Rare AT2s also function as alveolar stem cells. We show that AT2 lung stem cells display active Wnt signaling, and many of them are near single, Wnt-expressing fibroblasts. Blocking Wnt secretion depletes these stem cells. Daughter cells leaving the Wnt niche transdifferentiate into AT1s: Maintaining Wnt signaling prevents transdifferentiation, whereas abrogating Wnt signaling promotes it. Injury induces AT2 autocrine Wnts, recruiting "bulk" AT2s as progenitors. Thus, individual AT2 stem cells reside in single-cell fibroblast niches providing juxtacrine Wnts that maintain them, whereas injury induces autocrine Wnts that transiently expand the progenitor pool. This simple niche maintains the gas exchange surface and is coopted in cancer.

**What Is New?**
- 提出肺泡AT2干细胞并非均匀群体，而是具有活跃Wnt信号、并常紧邻单个Wnt表达成纤维细胞的微环境依赖状态。
- 确立成纤维细胞来源的juxtacrine Wnt是维持AT2干性的因果性调控节点：阻断Wnt分泌导致干细胞耗竭，维持Wnt阻止向AT1转分化，消除Wnt则促进转分化。
- 区分稳态与损伤两种Wnt逻辑——稳态依赖单细胞成纤维细胞niche，损伤诱导AT2自分泌Wnt短暂招募“bulk” AT2作为祖细胞，并提示该程序在癌症中被劫持。

**研究设计**
- 单细胞尺度分析AT2干细胞Wnt信号状态及其与Wnt表达成纤维细胞的空间邻近关系
- 阻断Wnt分泌以检验niche对AT2干细胞维持的必要性
- 追踪离开niche的daughter cell向AT1转分化，并用Wnt信号维持/消除进行双向干预
- 损伤背景下检测AT2自分泌Wnt及“bulk” AT2祖细胞招募

**主要发现**
- 稀有AT2干细胞显示活跃Wnt信号，且许多细胞邻近单个Wnt表达成纤维细胞。
- 阻断Wnt分泌会耗竭这些AT2干细胞。
- 离开Wnt niche的daughter cells向AT1转分化；维持Wnt信号可阻止转分化，消除Wnt信号则促进转分化。
- 损伤诱导AT2自分泌Wnt，从而把“bulk” AT2招募为祖细胞。
- 该单细胞成纤维细胞niche维持气体交换表面，并在癌症中被coopt。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整：从单细胞niche定位到Wnt配体来源、干细胞维持、AT1转分化命运决定，再到损伤后祖细胞扩增与癌症coopt；包含Wnt分泌阻断和信号双向干预的功能验证，概念上把“干细胞微环境”具体化为可操作的成纤维细胞-Wnt轴。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要，未提供全文方法、样本量、统计与模型细节。
- 摘要未明确具体Wnt配体/受体、成纤维细胞marker、损伤模型及癌症模型细节。
- 摘要未提供人肺组织或人体内验证信息。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可借鉴“配体分泌阻断＋信号维持/消除”的双向功能操作来验证微环境信号的因果性，而不只依赖相关性表达数据。
- 将niche分析下沉到单细胞尺度（单个成纤维细胞与邻近AT2的空间关系），适合肺泡上皮、免疫-基质crosstalk及肺血管重塑研究中的微环境拆解。
- 稳态niche维持与损伤后自分泌扩增的两套Wnt逻辑，可作为研究组织修复中“干细胞池扩增 vs. 干性维持”分离机制的框架。

### 1. FABP5 attenuates pulmonary fibrosis by regulating lipid metabolism in type II alveolar epithelial cells.

International immunopharmacology | 2026 Oct 15 | Hang Yin, Jin Su#, Penghui Yang#
IF 2025：4.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：State Key Laboratory of Respiratory Disease, the Second Affiliated Hospital of Guangzhou Medical University, Guangzhou, Guangdong, China; Ezhou Central Hospital, Ezhou, Hubei, China.
- 领域：据单位与摘要推断，第一作者主要关注呼吸疾病、肺纤维化及肺泡上皮细胞损伤机制。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42537352/
- DOI：10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117197

**Abstract**

Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) is a chronic, progressive disease resulting from alveolar epithelial cell injury. Senescence and dysfunction of alveolar epithelial type II (AT2) cells are key drivers of IPF pathogenesis. Given that metabolism underpins cellular homeostasis and AT2 cell function critically depends on lipid metabolism, we investigated the role of fatty acid binding protein 5 (FABP5), a key regulator of intracellular fatty acid trafficking. Using single-cell RNA-sequencing data, we assessed FABP5 expression across primary cellular subpopulations of IPF lung tissue, and validated its expression in IPF patient lungs and bleomycin-induced pulmonary fibrosis (PF) models by western blotting, quantitative PCR and immunofluorescence. We generated FABP5-overexpressing cell lines and human lung precision-cut lung slices (PCLS) via lentiviral transduction, and examined the effects of FABP5 on cell proliferation, apoptosis, mitochondrial function, and lipid droplet formation using staining-based assays. To evaluate in vivo effects, we overexpressed FABP5 in mouse lung tissue via adeno-associated virus (AAV) delivery and evaluated fibrosis by micro-CT and histopathological staining. We found that FABP5 expression was significantly downregulated in AT2 cells from IPF patients and bleomycin-induced models. Overexpression of FABP5 attenuated pathological fibroblast activation and enhanced mitochondrial bioenergetics, thereby protecting alveolar epithelial cells from injury. Furthermore, FABP5 mitigated bleomycin-induced lung fibrosis by increasing global lipid and medium/long-chain fatty acid levels. Our findings identify FABP5 as a critical regulator of pulmonary fibrosis. Downregulation of FABP5 in AT2 cells contributes to disease progression, whereas restoration of FABP5 expression attenuates fibrogenesis by suppressing pathological fibroblast activation, preserving mitochondrial function in AT2 cells, and restoring lipid homeostasis through elevation of medium- and long- chain fatty acids. These results nominate FABP5 as a promising therapeutic target for this devastating disease.

**What Is New?**
- 本研究把 FABP5 定义为 AT2 细胞内在的脂肪酸转运调控节点，并发现它在人 IPF 与 bleomycin 肺纤维化中显著下调，从而将 AT2 脂质代谢失衡与纤维化进展直接联系起来。
- 机制上，恢复 FABP5 可同时维持 AT2 的 mitochondrial bioenergetics 并抑制病理性 fibroblast activation，提出一条由上皮脂质代谢状态驱动成纤维细胞活化的通路。
- 在体内通过 AAV 过表达 FABP5 可减轻 bleomycin 诱导的纤维化，并伴随整体脂质及 medium/long-chain fatty acid 水平升高，提供了超出体外表型的在体干预证据。

**研究设计**
- 基于 IPF 肺组织 scRNA-seq 数据评估 FABP5 在主要细胞亚群中的表达，并在 IPF 患者肺与 bleomycin 诱导 PF 模型中用 WB、qPCR 和 immunofluorescence 验证。
- 通过 lentiviral transduction 构建 FABP5 过表达细胞系和人肺 PCLS，检测增殖、凋亡、mitochondrial function 与 lipid droplet formation。
- 用 AAV 在小鼠肺内过表达 FABP5，结合 micro-CT 与组织病理学染色评估 bleomycin 诱导的肺纤维化，并检测脂质及脂肪酸水平。

**主要发现**
- FABP5 在 IPF 患者及 bleomycin 诱导模型的 AT2 细胞中显著下调。
- 过表达 FABP5 可减弱病理性 fibroblast activation，并增强 mitochondrial bioenergetics，从而保护肺泡上皮细胞免受损伤。
- 在体内 AAV 介导的 FABP5 过表达可减轻 bleomycin 诱导的肺纤维化。
- FABP5 的保护作用伴随整体脂质以及 medium/long-chain fatty acid 水平升高，提示其通过恢复脂质稳态发挥作用。
- 作者据此将 FABP5 列为肺纤维化的关键调控因子和潜在干预靶点。

**为什么入选** 该研究形成较完整的机制链：AT2 脂质代谢调控因子 FABP5 下调 → mitochondrial bioenergetics 受损与 fibroblast activation → bleomycin 肺纤维化；并整合人 IPF 组织、scRNA-seq、人 PCLS 与 AAV 在体过表达 rescue，发表于 International Immunopharmacology。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，缺少样本量、统计方法、AT2 特异性机制细节及脂质组学数据，无法充分评估因果强度与脱靶效应。
- 摘要主要报告 FABP5 过表达/恢复实验，未说明 FABP5 缺失或 AT2 特异性敲低/敲除模型的结果。
- 人类证据以表达验证和离体 PCLS 为主，长期在体安全性与翻译价值尚未评估。

**可借鉴之处**
- AT2 脂质代谢—线粒体功能—成纤维细胞活化轴可作为肺泡生物学与 immune-stromal crosstalk 研究的可检验框架，适合在共培养或条件培养基体系中拆分上皮到成纤维细胞的信号。
- 人 PCLS 的 lentiviral 过表达与小鼠 AAV 肺内递送组合，为候选代谢调节因子提供从人组织到在体 rescue 的验证路径。
- 先用 scRNA-seq 定位细胞亚群表达，再用 qPCR、IF 和 WB 在人 IPF 组织与 bleomycin 模型中交叉验证的流程，可复用于其他 AT2 损伤或肺泡修复相关靶点。

**Abstract**

Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) has emerged as a powerful tool for studying individual cell populations in complex tissues during injury and repair. However, it involves extensive sample manipulation, limiting its application in rare and difficult-to-dissociate cell populations. Single-nucleus RNA sequencing (snRNA-seq) bypasses several of these challenges, but its performance in lung disease models is not well characterized. In a longitudinal murine model of bleomycin-induced lung injury, we performed whole lung snRNA-seq to systematically map 46 cell populations and states with minimal tissue processing. Our approach captured the emergence of Krt8+/Cdkn1a+ transitional alveolar cells and an expansion of macrophage populations during injury. Additionally, we identified sizeable populations of rare cell types, such as pericytes, and cells sensitive to digestion protocols, including a population of activated fibroblasts. These results indicate that snRNA-seq recapitulates the dynamic changes associated with injury as previously reported using single-cell methods and outperforms these approaches in the representation of cell types that are sensitive to processing, thereby highlighting its utility for high resolution analysis of heterogenous tissues such as lung.

**What Is New?**
- 在博来霉素肺损伤纵向小鼠模型中，以最小组织处理的全肺 snRNA-seq 系统绘制了 46 个细胞群/状态，提供了肺损伤动态的核转录组基准图谱。
- 该研究在 snRNA-seq 数据中重现了既往 scRNA-seq 报道的 Krt8+/Cdkn1a+ 过渡型肺泡细胞出现和 macrophage 扩增，系统评估了 snRNA-seq 对肺损伤动态的还原能力。
- 相较 scRNA-seq，snRNA-seq 更好捕获对解离敏感的稀有细胞和消化敏感群体，包括 pericyte 和 activated fibroblast，明确了其在异质肺组织中的方法学优势。

**研究设计**
- 小鼠博来霉素肺损伤纵向模型
- 全肺 snRNA-seq，最小组织处理
- 系统注释 46 个细胞群/状态，并与既往 scRNA-seq 报道的损伤动态比较
- 重点评估稀有细胞和对消化敏感细胞类型的捕获表现

**主要发现**
- 全肺 snRNA-seq 在纵向损伤模型中注释到 46 个细胞群/状态。
- 损伤期出现 Krt8+/Cdkn1a+ 过渡型肺泡细胞，并见 macrophage 群体扩增。
- 捕获到数量可观的稀有细胞类型（如 pericyte）及对消化敏感的 activated fibroblast。
- snRNA-seq 重现了既往单细胞方法报道的损伤相关动态变化，并在处理敏感细胞类型的代表性上优于 scRNA-seq。

**为什么入选** 以纵向博来霉素模型联合全肺 snRNA-seq 构建 46 群图谱，核心价值在方法学基准：既复现 Krt8+/Cdkn1a+ 过渡型肺泡细胞和 macrophage 扩增，又突出 pericyte、activated fibroblast 等易丢失群体的捕获优势；对肺泡生物学、基质和血管周细胞研究有参考价值，但摘要未提供因果机制或人体验证。
