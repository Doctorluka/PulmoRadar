Prior Venous Thromboembolism Defines Distinct Genetic and Molecular Phenotypes in Chronic Thromboembolic Pulmonary Hypertension.

杂志Arteriosclerosis, thrombosis, and vascular biology
IF: 8.8 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2026 Oct 15
10.1161/ATVBAHA.126.325037
作者Zhu Zhang* · Mengjie Duo* · Xiaofan Ji* · Bingzhang Zou* · Chen Wang
机构National Center for Respiratory Medicine, State Key Laboratory of Respiratory Health and Multimorbidity, National Clinical Research Center for Respiratory Diseases, Institute of Respiratory Medicine, Chinese Academy of Medical Sciences & Peking Union Medical College, Department of Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine, Center of Respiratory Medicine, China-Japan Friendship Hospital, Beijing (Z. Zhang, M.D., X.J., B.Z., Haobo Li, K.L., Huiwen Li, Yuhan Li, Yishan Li, X.L., J. Liu, Y.Z., S.Z., W.X., Z. Zhai, C.W.).

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 本研究按既往 VTE 病史将 CTEPH 划分为病变分布、手术比例和遗传架构均不同的生物学亚型，而非仅视为 PE 的远期并发症。
  • 在 CTEPH-VTE(+) 中定位 ABO 与 FGG 凝血相关易感位点并显示与 PE 的高遗传相关，而 VTE(-) 缺乏一致基因组信号，明确了亚型特异的遗传结构。
  • 通过多组学汇聚将 CTSS/cathepsin S 提名为 CTEPH-VTE(+) 的关键分子节点，并在人血浆、PEA 组织和原代内皮细胞中验证其经 ERK 促进增殖、可被 LY3000328 抑制，形成从遗传提名到功能干预的闭环。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

BACKGROUND: Chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension (CTEPH) has traditionally been considered a long-term complication of pulmonary embolism. However, a substantial proportion of patients have no history of venous thromboembolism (VTE), suggesting underlying biological heterogeneity.

METHODS: We analyzed clinical and imaging characteristics of 712 patients with CTEPH stratified by prior VTE history. Whole-genome sequencing was performed in all patients and 2952 healthy controls to assess common variants. Bulk RNA sequencing of pulmonary endarterectomy tissues and plasma protein quantification were integrated to identify and validate subgroup-specific molecular signatures, with further validation in tissue and primary endothelial cells.

RESULTS: Patients with CTEPH and prior VTE (n=576, 80.9%) exhibited a distinct thrombotic phenotype with more proximal disease distribution (92.5% versus 66.2%) and greater likelihood of undergoing pulmonary endarterectomy (22.6% versus 3.7%). Genome-wide association study in CTEPH-VTE(+) patients versus controls identified coagulation-related loci at ABO (rs676996, P=1.1×10-9) and FGG (rs7681423, P=2.5×10-8), with high genetic correlation with pulmonary embolism (rg=0.52, P=0.028). In contrast, no consistent genomic signal was identified in patients without prior VTE. Integrative multiomics (transcriptome-wide association study, MAGMA, and bulk RNA sequencing differential expression analysis) convergently prioritized CTSS (cathepsin S) as a key molecular candidate in CTEPH-VTE(+). CTSS was consistently elevated in plasma, pulmonary endarterectomy tissue, and primary endothelial cells from CTEPH-VTE(+) patients and promoted endothelial proliferation via ERK signaling, which was attenuated by the selective CTSS inhibitor LY3000328.

CONCLUSIONS: Stratification by prior VTE history reveals biologically distinct CTEPH phenotypes with divergent genetic architectures and identifies cathepsin S as a genetically nominated therapeutic target for thrombosis-associated pulmonary vascular remodeling.

  • 712 例 CTEPH 按既往 VTE 病史分层，比较临床与影像特征。
  • 全部患者及 2952 名健康对照行全基因组测序，评估常见变异并做 GWAS。
  • 整合 PEA 组织 bulk RNA-seq 与血浆蛋白定量，结合 TWAS、MAGMA 和差异表达汇聚候选分子。
  • 在 PEA 组织与原代内皮细胞中验证 CTSS，并用选择性 CTSS 抑制剂 LY3000328 干预 ERK/增殖。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • CTEPH-VTE(+) 对健康对照的 GWAS 检出 ABO（rs676996，P=1.1×10-9）和 FGG（rs7681423，P=2.5×10-8）凝血相关位点，并与 PE 呈高遗传相关（rg=0.52，P=0.028）。
  • CTEPH-VTE(-) 患者未发现一致的基因组信号，提示两类 CTEPH 的遗传架构不同。
  • 多组学汇聚优先提名 CTSS；CTSS 在 CTEPH-VTE(+) 患者血浆、PEA 组织和原代内皮细胞中一致升高。
  • CTSS 经 ERK 信号促进内皮增殖，该效应可被选择性 CTSS 抑制剂 LY3000328 减弱。

  • 摘要未呈现 CTSS 的体内遗传因果验证或动物模型结果，因果方向主要依赖人组织关联与原代内皮细胞抑制剂实验。
  • VTE(-) 亚组的遗传与分子异质性仍需更大队列和独立人群验证。

Hypusine Signaling Promotes Pulmonary Vascular Remodeling in Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension.

杂志American journal of respiratory and critical care medicine
IF: 21.7 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2024 Jun 01
10.1164/rccm.202305-0909OC
作者Sarah-Eve Lemay · Olivier Boucherat
机构Pulmonary Hypertension Research Group, Québec Heart and Lung Institute Research Centre, Quebec City, Quebec, Canada; and.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 首次将 spermidine 依赖的 hypusination/eIF5A 轴定义为 PAH 中 PASMC 过度增殖与凋亡抵抗的新调控节点，而不仅是既往已知的 polyamine 代谢改变。
  • 通过 SMC 靶向 Dhps 单等位基因失活及 DHPS 药物抑制，在两种已建立 PAH 的大鼠模型中证明抑制 hypusine 信号可减轻肺血管重塑并改善血流动力学与心功能。
  • 以 mass spectrometry 将 hypusine 信号与 oxidative phosphorylation 相关蛋白的广泛翻译输出相连，提出其通过支持生物能量需求维持 PASMC 存活与增殖的机制链。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Rationale: The ubiquitous polyamine spermidine is essential for cell survival and proliferation. One important function of spermidine is to serve as a substrate for hypusination, a posttranslational modification process that occurs exclusively on eukaryotic translation factor 5A (eIF5A) and ensures efficient translation of various gene products. Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) is a life-threatening disease characterized by progressive obliteration of the small pulmonary arteries (PAs) caused by excessive proliferation of PA smooth muscle cells (PASMCs) and suppressed apoptosis. Objectives: To characterize the role of hypusine signaling in PAH. Methods: Molecular, genetic, and pharmacological approaches were used both in vitro and in vivo to investigate the role of hypusine signaling in pulmonary vascular remodeling. Measurements and Main Results: Hypusine forming enzymes-deoxyhypusine synthase (DHPS) and deoxyhypusine hydroxylase (DOHH)-and hypusinated eukaryotic translation factor 5A are overexpressed in distal PAs and isolated PASMCs from PAH patients and animal models. In vitro, inhibition of DHPS using N1-guanyl-1,7-diaminoheptane or shRNA resulted in a decrease in PAH-PASMC resistance to apoptosis and proliferation. In vivo, inactivation of one allele of Dhps targeted to smooth muscle cells alleviates PAH in mice, and its pharmacological inhibition significantly decreases pulmonary vascular remodeling and improves hemodynamics and cardiac function in two rat models of established PAH. With mass spectrometry, hypusine signaling is shown to promote the expression of a broad array of proteins involved in oxidative phosphorylation, thus supporting the bioenergetic requirements of cell survival and proliferation. Conclusions: These findings support inhibiting hypusine signaling as a potential treatment for PAH.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • 分子、遗传与药理学方法结合体外和体内实验。
  • 检测 PAH 患者及动物模型远端肺动脉与分离 PASMC 中 DHPS、DOHH 和 hypusinated eIF5A 表达。
  • 体外用 N1-guanyl-1,7-diaminoheptane 或 shRNA 抑制 DHPS，评估 PAH-PASMC 凋亡抵抗与增殖。
  • 体内采用 SMC 靶向 Dhps 单等位基因失活小鼠及两种已建立 PAH 大鼠模型的药物抑制。
  • 用 mass spectrometry 分析 hypusine 信号相关蛋白表达谱。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • DHPS、DOHH 及 hypusinated eIF5A 在 PAH 患者和动物模型的远端肺动脉及分离 PASMC 中过表达。
  • 体外抑制 DHPS 可降低 PAH-PASMC 的凋亡抵抗和增殖。
  • SMC 靶向 Dhps 单等位基因失活可减轻小鼠 PAH。
  • DHPS 药物抑制在两种已建立 PAH 大鼠模型中减轻肺血管重塑，并改善血流动力学和心功能。
  • Mass spectrometry 显示 hypusine 信号促进 oxidative phosphorylation 相关蛋白表达，以支持细胞存活和增殖的生物能量需求。

AI 解读 · 为什么入选

该研究把 spermidine-hypusination-eIF5A 翻译调控轴与 PAH PASMC 存活/增殖机制相连，并用 SMC 特异性遗传失活和两种已建立 PAH 大鼠模型的药理干预形成较强因果链，兼具人组织表达证据与可操作的治疗靶点验证。

AI 解读 · 局限

  • 仅基于摘要，无法评估全文中的具体模型细节、统计效力、药物特异性和脱靶效应。
  • 人组织证据主要为表达相关性，尚缺乏人体干预验证。
  • 机制主要聚焦 PASMC，对其他肺血管细胞及免疫-基质 crosstalk 的贡献未在摘要中展开。

  • 可借鉴其“人肺组织/分离 PASMC 表达验证 + shRNA/药物体外功能 + SMC 特异性遗传失活 + 两种大鼠已建立 PAH 模型药理验证”的递进式因果链设计。

Niacin Attenuates Pulmonary Hypertension Through H-PGDS in Macrophages.

杂志Circulation research
IF: 18.0 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2020 Oct 23
10.1161/CIRCRESAHA.120.316784
作者Daile Jia* · Peiyuan Bai* · Naifu Wan* · Jiao Liu* · Yujun Shen
机构Pharmacology and Tianjin Key Laboratory of Inflammatory Biology, Key Laboratory of Immune Microenvironment and Disease (Ministry of Education), School of Basic Medical Sciences, Tianjin Medical University, Tianjin, China (D.J., J.L., G.C., Y.Y., J.Z., Y. Shen).

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 首次将常用降脂药 niacin 确立为对 hypoxia/SU5416 与 monocrotaline 诱导 PH 具有明确保护作用的干预手段；该保护作用在小鼠与大鼠两类物种、三种模型中均可重现，而不只是相关性观察。
  • 通过 H-PGDS 与 L-PGDS 全身敲除的对比，将 niacin 的获益从“肺内 PGD2 升高”这一表型，明确落实为 H-PGDS 依赖的因果节点：敲除 H-PGDS 不仅加重 PH，也取消了 niacin 的保护作用。
  • 用 macrophage-specific H-PGDS 敲除在体内证明肺内 PGD2 主要来源于浸润巨噬细胞，且这一来源是 niacin 疗效所必需；同时在 idiopathic PAH 患者肺组织中验证了该表达定位，使“药物—巨噬细胞代谢酶—血管重塑”机制链形成闭环。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

RATIONALE: Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) is characterized by progressive pulmonary vascular remodeling, accompanied by varying degrees of perivascular inflammation. Niacin, a commonly used lipid-lowering drug, possesses vasodilating and proresolution effects by promoting the release of prostaglandin D2 (PGD2). However, whether or not niacin confers protection against PAH pathogenesis is still unknown.

OBJECTIVE: This study aimed to determine whether or not niacin attenuates the development of PAH and, if so, to elucidate the molecular mechanisms underlying its effects.

METHODS AND RESULTS: Vascular endothelial growth factor receptor inhibitor SU5416 and hypoxic exposure were used to induce pulmonary hypertension (PH) in rodents. We found that niacin attenuated the development of this hypoxia/SU5416-induced PH in mice and suppressed progression of monocrotaline-induced and hypoxia/SU5416-induced PH in rats through the reduction of pulmonary artery remodeling. Niacin boosted PGD2 generation in lung tissue, mainly through H-PGDS (hematopoietic PGD2 synthases). Deletion of H-PGDS, but not lipocalin-type PGDS, exacerbated the hypoxia/SU5416-induced PH in mice and abolished the protective effects of niacin against PAH. Moreover, H-PGDS was expressed dominantly in infiltrated macrophages in lungs of PH mice and patients with idiopathic PAH. Macrophage-specific deletion of H-PGDS markedly decreased PGD2 generation in lungs, aggravated hypoxia/SU5416-induced PH in mice, and attenuated the therapeutic effect of niacin on PAH.

CONCLUSIONS: Niacin treatment ameliorates the progression of PAH through the suppression of vascular remodeling by stimulating H-PGDS-derived PGD2 release from macrophages.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • SU5416 联合 hypoxia 诱导小鼠与大鼠 PH，monocrotaline 诱导大鼠 PH。
  • niacin 给药后评估 PH 进展与肺血管重塑程度。
  • 检测肺组织 PGD2 生成；进行 H-PGDS 与 L-PGDS 全身基因敲除对照。
  • 采用 macrophage-specific H-PGDS 敲除，并在此背景下重新评估 niacin 疗效。
  • 定位 PH 小鼠肺与 idiopathic PAH 患者肺中 H-PGDS 的表达细胞类型。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • Niacin 减轻 hypoxia/SU5416 诱导的小鼠 PH，并抑制大鼠 monocrotaline 与 hypoxia/SU5416 模型的 PH 进展，伴随肺血管重塑减轻。
  • Niacin 主要经 H-PGDS 提升肺组织 PGD2 生成，而非经 lipocalin-type PGDS。
  • H-PGDS 缺失（而非 L-PGDS 缺失）加重 hypoxia/SU5416 诱导的 PH，并废除 niacin 的抗 PAH 作用。
  • H-PGDS 在 PH 小鼠肺及 idiopathic PAH 患者肺中主要表达于浸润的巨噬细胞。
  • Macrophage-specific H-PGDS 缺失显著降低肺内 PGD2、加重 hypoxia/SU5416 诱导的 PH，并削弱 niacin 的治疗效应。

AI 解读 · 为什么入选

机制链完整且逐级验证：由药物（niacin）到代谢产物（PGD2）、到合成酶（H-PGDS，并与 L-PGDS 作阴性对照）、再到来源细胞（巨噬细胞，细胞特异性敲除），最后回到表型（肺血管重塑与 PH）；兼有跨物种、多模型重复以及人 IPAH 组织的表达对应，发表于 Circulation Research。

AI 解读 · 局限

  • 仅有摘要，血流动力学具体指标、给药剂量与时机、样本量及统计方法均不详。
  • 人源证据限于 H-PGDS 表达定位，缺乏人体干预或功能验证。
  • 不能被排除 niacin 的其他药理通路（其他前列腺素或烟酸受体相关效应）对结果的贡献，摘要未提供相应排除实验。

AI 解读 · 可借鉴之处

  • “药物干预 + 全身敲除 + 细胞特异性敲除”的三层遗传学验证，可作为判定代谢酶与来源细胞是否为因果节点的可复用设计模板，避免只停留在表达相关。
  • niacin→H-PGDS→PGD2 的读出逻辑可直接迁移到 perivascular 炎症与 immune-stromal crosstalk 研究，例如检验巨噬细胞代谢状态如何改变成纤维细胞或血管壁细胞的活化阈值。
  • 跨物种、跨三种诱导模型的重复疗效本身构成稳健性证据：在单一模型中得到的“抗重塑”结论，宜用第二种诱导方式与第二物种复核后再下机制结论。

Jet-injected combinational adjuvants potentiate naked mRNA vaccines for protection against respiratory viral infections in mice.

杂志The Journal of clinical investigation
IF: 13.3 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2026 Oct 20
10.1172/JCI204021
作者Nan Qiao · Satoshi Uchida
机构Department of Advanced Nanomedical Engineering, Institute of Science Tokyo, Tokyo, Japan.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 本研究在 N1-methyl-pseudouridine 修饰的裸 mRNA 皮肤 jet-injection 体系中重新筛选临床相关佐剂，发现多数传统佐剂无效甚至抑制，并锁定 aluminum phosphate 与 CpG oligonucleotide 这一新型组合。
  • 该佐剂化裸 mRNA 疫苗在小鼠中达到与 LNP 疫苗相当的 IgG2a 和细胞免疫水平，同时局部与全身反应原性更低，建立了以更低反应原性换取 LNP 级免疫原性的新配方边界。
  • 机制上把 type I interferon 与 interleukin-6 信号定义为免疫增强的关键节点，并在 SARS-CoV-2 与 influenza A virus 攻毒中把该配方与近乎完全清除病毒连接起来。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

The use of lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) to enhance delivery and immunogenicity of mRNA vaccines raises safety concerns due to strong reactogenicity. Developing naked mRNA vaccines, which can be delivered through liquid jet-injection to the skin, may offer a safer alternative, but their immunogenicity has been limited. To address this challenge, we screened clinically relevant adjuvants to enhance the efficacy of N1-methyl-pseudouridine-modified naked mRNA jet-injection vaccines in mouse models. Although many adjuvants showed little or inhibitory effects on inducing humoral or cellular immunity-despite their known ability to potentiate conventional non-mRNA vaccine platforms-we identified a combination of aluminum phosphate and CpG oligonucleotide that substantially enhanced both humoral and cellular immune responses with increased IgG2a/IgG1 ratio.   • 体外：PMN 杀灭肺炎链球菌实验 ± I 型 IFN；检测 IFIT1 与 ROS。
  • 对照比较 PMN 上 IFNAR 表达及 IFNα/IFNβ 水平，排除上游 IFN 产生差异。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • IAV 感染产生的 I 型 IFN 抑制 PMN 杀灭肺炎链球菌；体外 IFN 处理即可抑制杀菌，体内阻断 IFNAR1 可恢复 IAV 感染小鼠的 PMN 功能。
  • CD73 是中性粒细胞响应 I 型 IFN 所必需：WT 与 CD73KO 的 PMN IFNAR 表达及共感染时 IFNα/IFNβ 水平相当，但 CD73KO PMN 的 IFIT1 显著降低、对 IFN 介导的抗菌抑制更不敏感。
  • IFN 与 CD73 促进 PMN 在 IAV 刺激下产生 ROS，而 ROS 升高与杀灭肺炎链球菌能力下降相关。
  • 共感染 CD73KO 小鼠较 WT 对照更有效清除菌血症并显著提高生存。
  • 总体结论：CD73 部分通过使 PMN 对 I 型 IFN 介导的抗菌功能抑制更敏感，从而损害宿主对 IAV/肺炎链球菌共感染的防御。

AI 解读 · 为什么入选

机制链清晰（IAV→I 型 IFN/CD73→PMN ROS→杀菌下降→菌血症/生存），同时具备 IFNAR1 阻断与 CD73KO 的体内外 rescue、以及模拟定植向致病转化的共感染模型；但当前仅为 bioRxiv 预印本。

AI 解读 · 局限

  • 仅有摘要，无 OA 全文；实验细节、统计与独立重复情况无法评估。
  • 摘要未说明 CD73KO 是否为中性粒细胞特异性删除，亦未提供人体样本或临床相关性验证。
  • bioRxiv 预印本尚未经同行评议；CD73 如何具体放大 IFN 下游信号（除 ROS 外）仍未完全阐明。

AI 解读 · 可借鉴之处

  • 可借鉴“定植→致病转化”的共感染小鼠模型来研究病毒-细菌序贯感染中的髓系细胞功能抑制，尤其是把上游 IFN 信号与下游效应细胞杀菌能力分开测量。
  • 可复用“受体表达/配体产生不变，但下游 ISG 响应（如 IFIT1）下降”的排除性设计，用来定位响应性缺陷发生在信号整合层而非 IFN 产生或受体丰度层。
  • ROS 作为连接 IFN/CD73 与抗菌功能下降的可测效应节点，适合在 PMN 体外杀伤实验中与体内生存/菌血症清除联合验证；对肺泡免疫-间质 crosstalk 研究，可借鉴以髓系效应细胞为核心、IFN 为上游节点的拆解思路。

A Time-Resolved Single-Cell Atlas Reveals Infection-Status, Age-, and Sex-Dependent Immune Responses Drive Viral Disease Severity.

杂志bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology
文章2026 Aug 11
10.64898/2026.08.10.744019
作者Lujia Chen · Lokesh Sharma
机构未提供

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 该研究构建了覆盖年龄、性别与四个感染阶段的16种条件的时间分辨小鼠流感 scRNA-seq 图谱，将年龄和性别对 antiviral immunity 的影响从群体水平推进到细胞状态分辨率。
  • 研究首次将“细胞自身感染状态”定义为决定转录状态的主要驱动因素，发现感染 myeloid cells 出现 interferon-stimulated、inflammatory、complement 与 oxidative stress 相关基因的广泛上调。
  • 研究提出并验证了衰老相关转录模式的部分保守性：老年 COVID-19 患者 BAL immune cells 较年轻患者呈现更高的 antiviral 与 pro-inflammatory 基因表达，同时在小鼠中锁定老年雄性晚期持续 myeloid inflammation 作为死亡率升高的可能机制。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

The majority of mortality during viral infections occurs in older males; however, underlying mechanisms by which age and sex shape antiviral immunity and pathological inflammatory responses remain incompletely understood. Here, we performed time-resolved single-cell RNA sequencing across 16 conditions spanning age, sex, and four stages of influenza infection in mice, generating a high-resolution atlas. Aged mice demonstrate delayed antiviral and inflammatory responses in multiple myeloid cells, impairing viral clearance, which delays recovery. Similarly, endothelial cells from aging mice show prolonged inflammatory and antiviral gene signatures. Altered gene signatures in immune and endothelial cells result in a shift in endothelial-immune interactions in the aged lung. Further, the infection status of the cell is a major driver of transcriptional state, with infected myeloid cells exhibiting broad upregulation of genes, including interferon-stimulated, inflammatory, complement, and oxidative stress-related genes. To assess whether these age-associated transcriptional patterns are conserved in humans, we examined BAL cells obtained from healthy individuals and COVID-19 patients, and found that immune cells from aged COVID-19 patients had elevated antiviral and pro-inflammatory gene expression compared to cells from young patients. Our analyses of sex differences identified that multiple myeloid cell types in aged male mice, but not in young male mice, show persistent inflammatory responses at later stages of infection, a likely mechanism contributing to elevated mortality in older males. These data reveal how infection status of the cell, age, and sex interact to drive persistent inflammation and impaired resolution, providing a foundational resource for designing age- and sex-specific therapeutic strategies.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • 小鼠流感病毒感染模型，按年龄、性别与四个感染阶段组成16种条件。
  • 时间分辨 scRNA-seq 构建高分辨率肺组织免疫与非免疫细胞图谱。
  • 推断单个细胞的感染状态，比较感染与未感染 myeloid cells 的转录程序。
  • 分析 endothelial cells 的 antiviral/inflammatory 签名及 endothelial-immune interactions。
  • 用健康人与 COVID-19 患者 BAL cells 进行年龄相关转录模式的跨物种验证。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • 老年小鼠多个 myeloid cell 亚群的 antiviral 与 inflammatory 反应延迟，伴随 viral clearance 受损和恢复延迟。
  • 老年小鼠 endothelial cells 呈现延长的 inflammatory 与 antiviral gene signatures，并导致衰老肺中 endothelial-immune interaction 格局改变。
  • 感染状态是转录状态的主要驱动因素，感染 myeloid cells 广泛上调 interferon-stimulated、inflammatory、complement 与 oxidative stress 相关基因。
  • 老年 COVID-19 患者 BAL immune cells 较年轻患者显示更高的 antiviral 与 pro-inflammatory gene expression，提示部分衰老相关模式在人类中保守。
  • 老年雄性小鼠而非年轻雄性小鼠在感染后期多个 myeloid cell types 持续炎症，可能解释老年男性死亡率升高。

AI 解读 · 为什么入选

研究以时间分辨单细胞图谱串联“感染状态—年龄—性别”三条变量，提出衰老相关髓系反应延迟与持续炎症导致清除受损和修复障碍的机制链，并用人 BAL 样本提供初步跨物种验证，兼具资源性与机制假说价值。
