### 1. NAD+ metabolic restoration resolves the inflammation-immunocompetence trade-off in lung graft-versus-host disease.

Science advances | 2026 Oct 23 | Jing Li, Mingzhou Ye
IF 2025：11.7 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed

- DOI：10.1126/sciadv.aef3338

**Abstract**

Chronic lung graft-versus-host disease (GVHD) after allogeneic hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (allo-HSCT) remains difficult to treat, and broad immunosuppression increases infection risk. Single-cell profiling of bronchoalveolar lavage from bronchiolitis obliterans lesions revealed collapse of an epithelial nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD+) metabolic axis with pathogenic immune-epithelial remodeling, nominating NAD+ restoration as an entry point. Because free NAD+ is poorly cell-permeable and rapidly consumed, we engineered CaNAD+-PGB-LiP (CNAPL), a high-payload NAD+ nanomedicine enabling direct intracellular replenishment. In murine allo-HSCT GVHD, CNAPL improved clinical scores and survival, reduced vascular and epithelial injury, and curtailed hyperinflammation and effector overactivation across GVHD target organs. Unlike immunosuppression, CNAPL preserved phagocytosis, NK/NKT cell (natural killer/natural killer T cell) cytotoxicity, and T cell memory. In posttransplant influenza or Klebsiella infection, CNAPL maintained pathogen control without rekindling GVHD, decoupling inflammation control from infection susceptibility. This metabolism-centered, host-directed strategy restores immune-tissue homeostasis and provides a practical translational path for durable lung GVHD control with preserved antimicrobial defense.

**What Is New?**
- 在支气管闭塞性病变 BAL 的单细胞图谱中，将慢性肺 GVHD 的焦点从单纯免疫攻击推进到“上皮 NAD+ 代谢轴塌陷—致病性免疫-上皮重塑”这一可干预节点。
- 针对游离 NAD+ 细胞透过性差和快速消耗的问题，构建高载荷纳米药物 CNAPL 以实现直接细胞内 NAD+ 补充，并在小鼠 allo-HSCT GVHD 中显示可改善临床评分与生存、减轻血管和上皮损伤。
- 以移植后流感或 Klebsiella 感染攻击证明 CNAPL 在控制 GVHD 炎症的同时保留病原控制，从而把“抗炎”与“免疫抑制导致的感染易感”解耦。

**研究设计**
- 对支气管闭塞性病变 BAL 进行单细胞 profiling，识别上皮 NAD+ 代谢轴塌陷及免疫-上皮重塑。
- 工程化高载荷 NAD+ 纳米药物 CNAPL，设计为细胞内递送与补充。
- 小鼠 allo-HSCT GVHD 模型评估临床评分、生存、血管/上皮损伤和多器官炎症。
- 检测吞噬、NK/NKT 细胞毒性和 T 细胞记忆等免疫功能。
- 移植后流感或 Klebsiella 感染攻击，评估病原控制与 GVHD 是否复燃。

**主要发现**
- 单细胞 profiling 显示支气管闭塞性病变中上皮 NAD+ 代谢轴塌陷，并与致病性免疫-上皮重塑同时出现。
- CNAPL 作为高载荷 NAD+ 纳米药物，可绕过游离 NAD+ 的细胞透过性限制，实现直接细胞内补充。
- 在小鼠 allo-HSCT GVHD 中，CNAPL 改善临床评分和生存，减轻血管与上皮损伤，并抑制多靶器官的过度炎症和效应细胞过度活化。
- 与广谱免疫抑制不同，CNAPL 保留吞噬作用、NK/NKT 细胞毒性和 T 细胞记忆。
- 移植后流感或 Klebsiella 感染模型中，CNAPL 维持病原控制且不重新点燃 GVHD，显示炎症控制与感染易感性可被解耦。

**为什么入选** 研究构成从人源 BAL 单细胞发现代谢节点，到工程化纳米药物实现通路 rescue，再到小鼠 allo-HSCT GVHD 多器官表型和移植后感染攻击的较完整机制链；核心概念是宿主导向的代谢修复而非广谱免疫抑制，直接回应肺 GVHD 治疗中的炎症-免疫能力权衡。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，未提供 CNAPL 的粒径、载药量、释放、体内分布、毒性和 NAD+ 代谢通量等表征数据。
- 无法核验上皮 NAD+ 轴的具体基因/细胞亚群、因果 rescue 设计、对照设置与统计细节。
- 所给全文片段为流感病毒感染小鼠 protocol，并非本文正文，因此按摘要评估。

**可借鉴之处**
- BAL scRNA-seq 定位代谢轴塌陷，再用纳米递送做通路 rescue，是“人源病变样本发现—小鼠模型因果验证”的可复用闭环，可迁移到肺泡上皮损伤、成纤维细胞活化和免疫-间质 crosstalk 研究。
- 在 GVHD 多靶器官中同时读取组织损伤、炎症程序和功能免疫（吞噬、NK/NKT 杀伤、T 细胞记忆），并把移植后感染作为反向验证，可用于区分宿主导向抗炎与广谱免疫抑制。
- 上皮 NAD+ 代谢可能连接肺泡修复、血管重塑和免疫微环境，但具体上皮亚群、下游代谢流和纳米药物分布仍需全文或补充材料确认。

### 2. Regulation of epithelial transitional states in murine and human pulmonary fibrosis.

The Journal of clinical investigation | 2023 Nov 15 | Fa Wang, Rachel L Zemans
IF 2025：13.3 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：三年内旗舰系统研究
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Division of Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine, Department of Internal Medicine, University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, Michigan, USA.
- 领域：肺与危重症医学；研究肺泡上皮过渡态与肺纤维化机制。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37768734/
- DOI：10.1242/dev.199512

**Abstract**

Idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) is a progressive scarring disease arising from impaired regeneration of the alveolar epithelium after injury. During regeneration, type 2 alveolar epithelial cells (AEC2s) assume a transitional state that upregulates multiple keratins and ultimately differentiate into AEC1s. In IPF, transitional AECs accumulate with ineffectual AEC1 differentiation. However, whether and how transitional cells cause fibrosis, whether keratins regulate transitional cell accumulation and fibrosis, and why transitional AECs and fibrosis resolve in mouse models but accumulate in IPF are unclear. Here, we show that human keratin 8 (KRT8) genetic variants were associated with IPF. Krt8-/- mice were protected from fibrosis and accumulation of the transitional state. Keratin 8 (K8) regulated the expression of macrophage chemokines and macrophage recruitment. Profibrotic macrophages and myofibroblasts promoted the accumulation of transitional AECs, establishing a K8-dependent positive feedback loop driving fibrogenesis. Finally, rare murine transitional AECs were highly senescent and basaloid and may not differentiate into AEC1s, recapitulating the aberrant basaloid state in human IPF. We conclude that transitional AECs induced and were maintained by fibrosis in a K8-dependent manner; in mice, most transitional cells and fibrosis resolved, whereas in human IPF, transitional AECs evolved into an aberrant basaloid state that persisted with progressive fibrosis.

**What Is New?**
- 人 KRT8 遗传变异与 IPF 相关，且 Krt8-/- 小鼠在纤维化模型中受到保护并减少过渡态 AEC 积累，首次把 K8 定义为该过渡态和纤维化的遗传-功能调控节点。
- K8 调控巨噬细胞趋化因子表达与巨噬细胞募集，而 profibrotic macrophages 和 myofibroblasts 又促进过渡态 AEC 积累，从而闭合了一条 K8 依赖的上皮-免疫-基质正反馈促纤维化机制链。
- 少数小鼠过渡态 AEC 呈高度衰老和 basaloid 表型、可能不分化为 AEC1， recapitulate 人 IPF 的 aberrant basaloid 状态，为小鼠纤维化可消退而人 IPF 持续进展提供了新的细胞状态解释。

**研究设计**
- 人 KRT8 遗传变异与 IPF 的关联分析
- Krt8-/- 小鼠肺纤维化模型中的纤维化和过渡态 AEC 积累评估
- K8 对巨噬细胞趋化因子表达及巨噬细胞募集的调控分析
- profibrotic macrophages 与 myofibroblasts 促进过渡态 AEC 积累的互作/反馈分析
- 小鼠与 human IPF 过渡态 AEC 的衰老和 basaloid 特征比较

**主要发现**
- 人 KRT8 遗传变异与 IPF 相关，提示 K8 是候选易感/调控节点。
- Krt8-/- 小鼠在纤维化模型中受到保护，过渡态 AEC 积累减少。
- K8 调控巨噬细胞趋化因子表达和巨噬细胞募集。
- Profibrotic macrophages 和 myofibroblasts 促进过渡态 AEC 积累，形成 K8 依赖的正反馈促纤维化环路。
- 少数小鼠过渡态 AEC 呈高度衰老和 basaloid 表型、可能不分化为 AEC1，类似人 IPF 的 aberrant basaloid 状态；小鼠中多数过渡态细胞和纤维化可消退，而人 IPF 中该状态持续并伴随进展性纤维化。

**为什么入选** 以人遗传关联切入，结合 Krt8 基因敲除小鼠的保护表型，串起 K8→巨噬细胞趋化/募集→profibrotic macrophages/myofibroblasts→过渡态 AEC 的正反馈机制链；并用小鼠可逆与人 IPF 持续状态的对比，提出 aberrant basaloid 持续化解释。发表于 JCI，机制链和物种比较较完整。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无法评估具体模型、样本量、统计效应量及 K8 细胞特异性因果实验。
- 人 KRT8 变异关联与小鼠敲除保护不能单独证明人类 IPF 中的因果方向，正反馈环路的上下游次序需全文实验支持。
- “过渡态 AEC 是否直接导致纤维化”与“被纤维化诱导/维持”的边界在摘要中仍未完全展开，衰老/basaloid 亚群的命运需进一步验证。

**可借鉴之处**
- “人遗传变异关联 + 基因敲除小鼠保护表型”的组合可用于验证上皮候选调控节点；进一步用上皮特异性删除可区分 AEC2 内在效应与全局效应。
- 将 keratin 高表达、衰老和 basaloid 标志物组合用于定义过渡态亚群，有助于区分可逆的再生中间态与 IPF 样持续异常状态。
- K8—巨噬细胞趋化—肌成纤维细胞—过渡态 AEC 正反馈提示，上皮-免疫-基质三元互作可作为肺泡修复/纤维化研究的机制框架，并用趋化因子 rescue 或巨噬细胞干预检验环路方向。

### 3. β1 Integrin regulates adult lung alveolar epithelial cell inflammation.

JCI insight | 2020 Jan 30 | Erin J Plosa, Roy Zent
IF 2025：8.0 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：领域经典
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者单位：Division of Neonatology, Department of Pediatrics.
- 通讯作者单位：Nashville Veterans Affairs Medical Center, Nashville, Tennessee, USA.; Division of Nephrology and Hypertension, Department of Medicine.; Department of Cell and Developmental Biology, Vanderbilt University Medical Center, Nashville, Tennessee, USA.
- 第一作者领域：新生儿学，关注肺发育与肺泡上皮生物学（Division of Neonatology, Department of Pediatrics）。
- 通讯作者领域：肾脏与高血压、细胞外基质/整合素信号及上皮稳态（Vanderbilt 肾脏与高血压、细胞与发育生物学；Nashville VA）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31873073/
- DOI：10.1164/rccm.201207-1206OC

**Abstract**

Integrins, the extracellular matrix receptors that facilitate cell adhesion and migration, are necessary for organ morphogenesis; however, their role in maintaining adult tissue homeostasis is poorly understood. To define the functional importance of β1 integrin in adult mouse lung, we deleted it after completion of development in type 2 alveolar epithelial cells (AECs). Aged β1 integrin-deficient mice exhibited chronic obstructive pulmonary disease-like (COPD-like) pathology characterized by emphysema, lymphoid aggregates, and increased macrophage infiltration. These histopathological abnormalities were preceded by β1 integrin-deficient AEC dysfunction such as excessive ROS production and upregulation of NF-κB-dependent chemokines, including CCL2. Genetic deletion of the CCL2 receptor, Ccr2, in mice with β1 integrin-deficient type 2 AECs impaired recruitment of monocyte-derived macrophages and resulted in accelerated inflammation and severe premature emphysematous destruction. The lungs exhibited reduced AEC efferocytosis and excessive numbers of inflamed type 2 AECs, demonstrating the requirement for recruited monocytes/macrophages in limiting lung injury and remodeling in the setting of a chronically inflamed epithelium. These studies support a critical role for β1 integrin in alveolar homeostasis in the adult lung.

**What Is New?**
- 该研究在发育完成后对 type 2 AEC 特异性删除 β1 integrin，将 β1 integrin 从已知的器官形态发生因子明确为维持成年肺泡稳态、限制 COPD 样重塑的上皮内在关键节点。
- 研究把 β1 integrin 缺失后的上皮功能障碍定位到 ROS 过多和 NF-κB 依赖的 CCL2 等趋化因子上调，提出“基质黏附受体–氧化应激–上皮趋化因子”启动炎症的新因果链。
- 通过 Ccr2 遗传删除，研究证明 CCL2–CCR2 轴招募的单核细胞来源巨噬细胞在慢性上皮炎症中承担限制损伤和肺气肿的保护功能，而非单纯促炎。

**研究设计**
- 成年小鼠 type 2 AEC 特异性、发育完成后诱导删除 β1 integrin
- 老化观察与肺组织病理：emphysema、lymphoid aggregates、macrophage infiltration
- 检测 AEC ROS 与 NF-κB 依赖 chemokines（包括 CCL2）
- Ccr2 遗传删除进行上位验证，评估 monocyte-derived macrophage 募集
- 检测 AEC efferocytosis 与 inflamed type 2 AEC 数量

**主要发现**
- 老年 β1 integrin 缺失小鼠出现 COPD 样病理：肺气肿、淋巴样聚集和巨噬细胞浸润增加。
- 这些病理改变之前已出现 type 2 AEC 功能障碍：ROS 过多及 NF-κB 依赖的 CCL2 等趋化因子上调。
- 在 β1 integrin 缺失的 type 2 AEC 背景下删除 Ccr2，减少 monocyte-derived macrophage 募集，却加重炎症并导致更早、更严重的肺气肿破坏。
- 突变肺中 AEC efferocytosis 降低，并积累大量 inflamed type 2 AEC。
- 结果支持招募的单核/巨噬细胞在慢性上皮炎症中限制肺损伤和重塑，β1 integrin 是成年肺泡稳态的关键调节因子。

**为什么入选** 机制链较完整：成体上皮特异性基因删除→ROS/NF-κB–CCL2→CCR2 依赖的单核细胞来源巨噬细胞募集→efferocytosis 与肺气肿重塑；包含遗传上位验证和 COPD 样 in vivo 表型。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无 OA 全文；实验细节、样本量、统计方法和具体分子验证强度无法评估。
- 证据主要来自小鼠遗传模型，摘要未提供人肺组织或人遗传数据。
- CCL2–CCR2 之外的上皮–免疫介质以及 β1 integrin 下游直接信号节点在摘要中未展开。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可借鉴“发育完成后诱导删除”的策略，将黏附/基质受体在器官维持中的作用与发育缺陷区分开，并用老化队列观察迟发 COPD 样表型。
- CCL2–Ccr2 遗传上位实验提示，评价髓系募集时应同时检测单核细胞来源巨噬细胞数量、AEC efferocytosis 和上皮炎症状态，以判断其保护或损伤方向。
- 该上皮–趋化因子–单核/巨噬细胞轴可作为研究肺泡上皮与免疫/间质 crosstalk 的模板，尤其适用于肺气肿性重塑和慢性上皮炎症模型。

### 1. Nerandomilast's Antifibrotic Effects Are Modulated by the tRF-AKAP12-PKA Axis in Pulmonary Fibrosis.

American journal of respiratory cell and molecular biology | 2026 Oct 07 | Zhen Chen, Xiaowei Nie
IF 2025：6.4 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Pharmacology, Joint Laboratory of Guangdong-Hong Kong Universities for Vascular Homeostasis and Diseases, SUSTech Homeostatic Medicine Institute, School of Medicine, Southern University of Science and Technology, Shenzhen 518055, China.
- 领域：第一作者主要研究领域为药理学与血管稳态/疾病，聚焦肺纤维化中tRNA衍生小RNA与成纤维细胞活化机制。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42841745/
- DOI：10.1093/ajrcmb/aanag188

**Abstract**

RATIONALE: Pulmonary fibrosis (PF) involves persistent fibroblast activation, extracellular matrix deposition, and tissue stiffening. The role of oxidatively modified tRNA-derived small RNAs (tsRNAs) in linking fibroblast activation to mechanotransduction remains unclear.
METHODS: 5o8G tRF-1-AspGTC (tRF) expression was analyzed in lung tissues from patients with PF and experimental mouse models. Gain- and loss-of-function approaches were used to define its role in fibroblast activation and PF progression. Mechanistic studies examined the tRF-AKAP12-PKA-YAP pathway, and therapeutic relevance was evaluated by assessing the antifibrotic efficacy of Nerandomilast under AKAP12-deficient or AKAP12-restored conditions.
RESULTS: tRF was markedly upregulated in fibrotic lung tissues from PF patients and experimental mice. Myofibroblast-specific inhibition of this tRF significantly attenuated PF in mice. Consistently, tRF accumulation promoted fibroblast activation, cytoskeletal remodeling, and profibrotic phenotypes in human pulmonary fibroblasts (HPFs). Mechanistically, tRF directly targeted and suppressed AKAP12 expression, thereby disrupting the AKAP12-PKA signaling complex. Loss of AKAP12 disrupted AKAP12-dependent PKA compartmentalization, altered PKA substrate phosphorylation, and promoted cytoskeletal reorganization and YAP-dependent mechanotransduction. Importantly, AKAP12 deficiency impaired Nerandomilast-mediated restoration of PKA-YAP signaling and limited its antifibrotic efficacy, whereas AKAP12 restoration re-established this signaling axis and synergistically enhanced Nerandomilast therapeutic effects in PF mice.
CONCLUSION: tRF promotes PF by disrupting AKAP12-dependent spatial PKA signaling and activating YAP-dependent mechanotransduction. Targeting the tRF-AKAP12 axis, particularly through AKAP12 restoration, may improve Nerandomilast-based therapy.

**What Is New?**
- 本研究将氧化修饰的tsRNA 5o8G tRF-1-AspGTC定义为肺纤维化中上调并足以促进成纤维细胞活化的新型促纤维化节点，而不仅是相关性标志物。
- 在肌成纤维细胞特异性抑制该tRF可显著减轻小鼠肺纤维化，提供了细胞类型限定的体内因果验证。
- 机制上闭合了tRF→AKAP12→PKA区室化→YAP机械转导通路，并将AKAP12缺失/恢复与Nerandomilast抗纤维化疗效直接耦合。

**研究设计**
- 在人PF肺组织与实验小鼠模型中检测5o8G tRF-1-AspGTC表达。
- 采用tRF gain- and loss-of-function（含肌成纤维细胞特异性抑制）评估成纤维细胞活化与PF进展。
- 机制解析tRF-AKAP12-PKA-YAP轴及PKA区室化/底物磷酸化改变。
- 在AKAP12缺失或恢复条件下评价Nerandomilast的抗纤维化疗效。

**主要发现**
- 5o8G tRF-1-AspGTC在PF患者肺组织和实验小鼠纤维化肺组织中显著上调。
- 肌成纤维细胞特异性抑制该tRF可显著减轻小鼠PF；在HPFs中tRF累积促进成纤维细胞活化、细胞骨架重塑和促纤维化表型。
- tRF直接靶向并抑制AKAP12，破坏AKAP12-PKA信号复合物，导致PKA区室化异常和PKA底物磷酸化改变。

**Abstract**

Redox homeostasis requires both inducible antioxidant defense and constitutive machinery that continuously regenerates cellular reducing capacity. How these systems are altered across lung cell populations in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) remains unclear. We integrated donor-level single-nucleus lung transcriptomes with smoke-exposure models, independent cohorts, and large-scale pharmacologic perturbation data. Both redox programs were reduced in established COPD, but constitutive redox support showed the greater loss, concentrated in fibroblasts and lymphatic endothelium. In fibroblasts, PGC-1α target-program output showed the most negative effect among 515 regulator programs despite preserved PPARGC1A expression. Smoke exposure activated inducible antioxidant defense across experimental models, whereas this adaptive program was reduced in established COPD. Pharmacologic perturbations further separated the two arms of redox homeostasis, with redox-directed interventions producing larger transcriptional responses in inducible defense and a distinct set of compounds increasing constitutive support together with PGC-1α target output and movement away from an independently defined COPD fibroblast state. These findings identify a cell-selective loss of constitutive redox support in COPD and implicate reduced PGC-1α target-program output in the fibroblast redox phenotype.
