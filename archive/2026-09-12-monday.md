### 1. Isoforskolin targets ADCY7 to restore mitochondrial function in COPD-associated macrophages.

International immunopharmacology | 2026 Sep 08 | Zengrui Wang, Zhuang Luo
IF 2025：4.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Kunming Medical University, Kunming, Yunnan, China. Electronic address: 20240467@kmmu.edu.cn.
- 领域：昆明医科大学（20240467@kmmu.edu.cn），具体课题组方向不详，从论文内容推断涉及 COPD 巨噬细胞免疫代谢与天然产物药理学。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42710396/
- DOI：10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117398

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Mitochondrial dysfunction in alveolar macrophages (AMs) drives chronic airway inflammation in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). Adenylyl cyclase 7 (ADCY7) is a key enzyme in cAMP signaling, but its role in macrophage mitochondrial dysfunction and immune regulation in COPD remains unclear. Isoforskolin (ISOF), a bioactive natural product from Coleus forskohlii, has known anti-inflammatory properties, yet whether it improves macrophage mitochondrial function via ADCY7 is unknown.
OBJECTIVE: To investigate whether ISOF acts through ADCY7 to restore mitochondrial function and correct the dysfunctional phenotype of a COPD-enriched ADCY7+CCL4+ macrophage subpopulation.
METHODS: ADCY7 expression was measured in AMs from COPD patients. Single-cell and bulk transcriptomics (GSE171541, GSE57148) were used to identify ADCY7+ macrophage subpopulations. Network pharmacology, molecular docking and molecular dynamics simulations predicted the ISOF-ADCY7 interaction. A cigarette smoke extract (CSE)-induced THP-1-derived macrophage model and a cigarette smoke plus lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced mouse COPD model were employed, combined with ADCY7 silencing and rescue experiments. Mitochondrial function was assessed by Seahorse analysis, transmission electron microscopy, mtROS, mtDNA copy number, ATP, JC-1 and calcium assays.
RESULTS: ADCY7 expression was significantly downregulated in AMs from COPD patients and negatively correlated with lung function. Single-cell analysis identified a COPD-enriched ADCY7+CCL4+ macrophage subpopulation exhibiting a transitional inflammatory-repair phenotype. Molecular docking and dynamics simulations predicted stable binding of ISOF to ADCY7 (binding energy -5.9 kcal/mol). In vitro and in vivo experiments confirmed that ISOF upregulated ADCY7 expression. In vivo, ISOF improved pulmonary function, reduced collagen deposition and inflammatory cytokines (TNF-α, IL-1β, IL-6), and increased cAMP and ATP levels in mouse AMs. In vitro, ISOF reversed CSE-induced mitochondrial ultrastructural damage, calcium overload, mtROS overproduction, loss of mitochondrial membrane potential and respiratory chain deficiency.
CONCLUSION: ISOF alleviates COPD by modulating ADCY7-dependent pathways to improve macrophage mitochondrial function and reverse the functional imbalance of the ADCY7+CCL4+ macrophage subpopulation.</font> These findings position ISOF as a promising immunometabolic and mitochondrial-targeted candidate for COPD therapy.

**What Is New?**
- 新细胞状态：在 COPD 患者 AMs 中首次界定出一个 COPD 富集的 ADCY7+CCL4+ 巨噬细胞亚群，其呈 transitional inflammatory-repair 表型，且 ADCY7 表达下调并与肺功能负相关。
- 新调节节点与因果链：通过 ADCY7 沉默与 rescue 实验，将 ADCY7–cAMP 轴确立为巨噬细胞线粒体功能障碍的可干预因果节点，而非仅是相关性标志。
- 新机制闭合与体内验证：结合网络药理学、分子对接与分子动力学预测 ISOF 稳定结合 ADCY7（binding energy −5.9 kcal/mol），并在 CSE 体外模型与 cigarette smoke + LPS 小鼠 COPD 模型中验证其经 ADCY7 改善线粒体功能与肺功能。

**研究设计**
- 人源样本：检测 COPD 患者肺泡巨噬细胞 ADCY7 表达并与肺功能做相关性分析
- 生物信息学：单细胞与 bulk 转录组（GSE171541、GSE57148）鉴定 ADCY7+ 巨噬细胞亚群
- 计算预测：网络药理学 + 分子对接 + 分子动力学模拟预测 ISOF–ADCY7 结合
- 体外模型：CSE 诱导 THP-1 来源巨噬细胞，联合 ADCY7 沉默与 rescue
- 体内模型：cigarette smoke + LPS 诱导小鼠 COPD 模型，评估肺功能、胶原沉积与炎症因子
- 线粒体表型：Seahorse、TEM、mtROS、mtDNA copy number、ATP、JC-1、钙测定

**主要发现**
- COPD 患者 AMs 中 ADCY7 表达显著下调，且与肺功能呈负相关。
- 单细胞分析识别出 COPD 富集的 ADCY7+CCL4+ 巨噬细胞亚群，具 transitional inflammatory-repair 表型。
- 分子对接与动力学模拟预测 ISOF 与 ADCY7 稳定结合，结合能 −5.9 kcal/mol。
- 体内 ISOF 上调 ADCY7，改善肺功能，减少胶原沉积及 TNF-α、IL-1β、IL-6，并提升小鼠 AMs 的 cAMP 与 ATP 水平。
- 体外 ISOF 逆转 CSE 诱导的线粒体超微结构损伤、钙超载、mtROS 过量产生、线粒体膜电位丢失与呼吸链缺陷。

**为什么入选** 该研究以 COPD 患者 AMs 为起点，将单细胞定义的 ADCY7+CCL4+ 巨噬细胞亚群、ADCY7–cAMP 信号节点与天然产物 ISOF 的线粒体保护效应串联成完整机制链，并同时提供计算预测、体外沉默/rescue 与体内 COPD 模型验证，兼具免疫代谢与线粒体靶向的双重机制价值。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要评估，未获取 OA 全文，实验细节、样本量与统计方法无法核实。
- ADCY7 下游如何具体连接 cAMP 与线粒体呼吸链/钙稳态的分子机制在摘要中未展开，因果链中段仍待补全。
- 体外使用 THP-1 来源巨噬细胞而非原代肺泡巨噬细胞，且 docking 结合能（−5.9 kcal/mol）较弱，ISOF–ADCY7 直接结合尚需结构生物学或结合实验确证。

- 「单细胞+bulk 转录组定位疾病富集亚群 → 锁定亚群标志分子 → 天然产物/小分子干预 → 沉默+rescue 验证因果」这一研究链可直接迁移到肺泡巨噬细胞、成纤维细胞或免疫-基质 crosstalk 的靶点发现中。

### 2. Resveratrol attenuates LPS/CSE-induced macrophage ferroptosis through activation of the Nrf2-ferroportin axis.

International immunopharmacology | 2026 Sep 11 | Zhuozhe Li, Zhihong Chen#, Zhilong Jiang#
IF 2025：4.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine, Zhongshan Hospital, Fudan University, Shanghai, China.
- 领域：第一作者单位为 Zhongshan Hospital, Fudan University 呼吸与危重症医学科；据摘要推断其研究领域为 COPD/AECOPD 的免疫炎症与巨噬细胞铁死亡机制。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42727368/
- DOI：10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117380

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Resveratrol (Res) has therapeutic effects in a mouse model with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). However, the role of ferroportin (FPN) in the Res-mediated therapeutic effects remains unclear.
PURPOSE: We investigated the role and signaling pathways of FPN in macrophages and a mouse model with acute exacerbation of COPD (AECOPD) after Res treatment.
STUDY DESIGN/METHODS: Res was intraperitoneal (i.p.) injected into AECOPD-like mouse model or added into cultured macrophages prior to LPS/CSE treatment. Macrophages with FPN knockdown and over-expression were established by infection of the cells with lentiviral vector encoding FPN shRNA and cDNA.
RESULTS: Res i.p. suppressed pulmonary infiltration of neutrophils and emphysema in the LPS/CSE-treated mice, in association with upregulated expression of FPN. LPS, CSE and their mixture increased the expression of FPN, TNF-α and IL-6 in macrophages, with increased hemosiderin deposits, reactive oxygen species (ROS) and malondialdehyde (MDA), but the effects were reversed by pre-treatment with Res. In addition, Res activated nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2 (Nrf2) and NAD-dependent protein deacetylase sirtuin-1 (SIRT1), that was reversed by their inhibitors Brusatol and EX527, and subsequently abolished Res-induced upregulation of FPN in the treated cells. Knock-down of FPN partially reduced Res-mediated suppression of macrophage ferroptosis, whereas over-expression of FPN enhanced the effects. Adoptive transfer of FPN over-expressing bone marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs) reduced lung inflammation and injury, in association with reduced lung neutrophil infiltrates, and expression of TNF-α, IL-6 in the treated mice.
CONCLUSION: Res suppressed AECOPD partially through activation of Nrf2/FPN signaling and subsequent suppression of macrophage ferroptosis. FPN would be a potential therapeutic target in the treatment of AECOPD.

**What Is New?**
- 首次将 FPN 定义为 Res 改善 AECOPD 样小鼠肺损伤的巨噬细胞内在关键节点，而不仅是铁输出相关标志物。
- 把 Res 的保护机制闭合为 Nrf2/SIRT1 依赖的 FPN 上调—巨噬细胞铁死亡抑制轴，并用 Brusatol 与 EX527 阻断验证上游依赖性。
- 通过 FPN 敲低/过表达及 FPN 过表达 BMDMs 过继转移，提供了巨噬细胞 FPN 因果驱动肺部炎症和损伤的 in vivo 验证。

**研究设计**
- AECOPD-like mouse model induced by LPS/CSE and treated with Res i.p.
- Cultured macrophages stimulated with LPS/CSE with or without Res pretreatment
- Lentiviral FPN knockdown and overexpression in macrophages
- Nrf2 inhibitor Brusatol and SIRT1 inhibitor EX527 to test upstream dependence
- Adoptive transfer of FPN-overexpressing BMDMs into mice
- Readouts: lung neutrophil infiltration, emphysema, TNF-α/IL-6, FPN, Nrf2, SIRT1, hemosiderin, ROS, MDA, ferroptosis suppression

**主要发现**
- Res i.p. 抑制 LPS/CSE 处理小鼠的肺内 neutrophil infiltration 和 emphysema，并伴随 FPN 表达上调。
- LPS、CSE 及其混合物增加巨噬细胞 FPN、TNF-α、IL-6 表达以及 hemosiderin deposits、ROS 和 MDA，Res 预处理可逆转这些效应。
- Res 激活 Nrf2 和 SIRT1；Brusatol 或 EX527 可逆转激活并取消 Res 诱导的 FPN 上调。
- FPN knockdown 部分削弱 Res 对巨噬细胞 ferroptosis 的抑制，而 FPN overexpression 增强该效应。
- Adoptive transfer of FPN-overexpressing BMDMs 降低小鼠肺部炎症和损伤，并减少 lung neutrophil infiltrates、TNF-α 和 IL-6 表达。

**为什么入选** 机制链较完整：Res→Nrf2/SIRT1→FPN→巨噬细胞 ferroptosis 抑制；采用 FPN 敲低/过表达与 BMDMs 过继转移进行因果验证，并覆盖 AECOPD 样 in vivo 模型；契合肺部免疫-铁死亡与 COPD 急性加重机制研究。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要，未获取 OA 全文；实验细节、统计学及 ferroptosis 特异性标志物（如 GPX4/ACSL4/脂质过氧化）未在摘要中充分呈现。
- 摘要中 FPN 在 LPS/CSE 刺激下升高而 Res 又可上调 FPN，不同刺激背景下的调控差异未完全阐明。
- 证据主要来自小鼠模型与体外巨噬细胞，摘要未报告人肺组织或人样本验证，向人类 AECOPD 外推需谨慎。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可复用的 lentiviral FPN shRNA/cDNA 巨噬细胞 loss-/gain-of-function 联合 LPS/CSE 刺激体系，适合解析铁代谢/铁死亡在肺泡巨噬细胞中的作用。
- BMDMs 过继转移可作为连接体外巨噬细胞机制与 in vivo 肺损伤表型的桥梁；若关注 fibroblast/immune-stromal crosstalk，可进一步检测 FPN/铁死亡巨噬细胞对成纤维细胞或肺血管 remodeling 的旁分泌影响。
- Nrf2/SIRT1 药理学抑制（Brusatol/EX527）配合 FPN rescue 的设计，可用于判断上游通路是否必需；在肺泡生物学或肺血管 remodeling 研究中可借鉴铁死亡 readouts（ROS、MDA、hemosiderin）。

### 1. Mechanosensitive phosphorylation of NFATC4 at S213/S217 drives fibroblast-to-myofibroblast transition and fibrosis.

The Journal of clinical investigation | 2026 Sep 01 | Safwen Kadri, Herbert B Schiller
IF 2025：13.3 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Research Unit for Precision Regenerative Medicine, Helmholtz Munich, Comprehensive Pneumology Center, Member of the German Center for Lung Research (DZL), Munich, Germany.
- 领域：精准再生医学方向，聚焦肺成纤维细胞机械转导与磷酸化蛋白组学（Helmholtz Munich, Comprehensive Pneumology Center, DZL）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42677834/
- DOI：10.1093/nar/gky1106

**Abstract**

Mechanosensitive feedback between tissue stiffness and cellular contractile forces instructs cell identity. To characterize phosphorylation-mediated mechanosensing, we charted the global phosphoproteome dynamics of primary human lung fibroblasts on fibronectin-coated polydimethylsiloxane substrates of defined stiffness. We identified a key signaling threshold at 2-8 kPa, above which cells activated cytoskeletal remodeling, ECM secretion, and transition to a CTHRC1+/ACTA2+ myofibroblast state, accompanied by stiffness-dependent phosphorylation of the transcription factor NFATC4 at S213/S217. In micro-CT staged pulmonary fibrosis tissues, NFATC4 expression increased progressively, colocalizing with CTHRC1 and ACTA2 in myofibroblasts. Transcription factor regulon inference from a multicohort pulmonary fibrosis atlas confirmed elevated NFATC4 activity in disease fibroblasts, revealing a core 119-gene NFATC4-dependent fibrotic program with CTHRC1 as a top target. Phosphomimetic S213D/S217D mutants drove myofibroblast differentiation on soft substrates independently of TGFB, while phospho-dead S213A/S217A mutants blocked differentiation even on stiff matrix with TGFB, establishing the phospho-switch as both necessary and sufficient. Stiff matrix and TGFB converged on this JNK- and calcineurin-dependent switch to amplify the fibrotic response. This positions NFATC4 S213/S217 as a mechanosensitive checkpoint for CTHRC1+ myofibroblast fate and a candidate therapeutic target in multiorgan fibrosis.

**What Is New?**
- 在 fibronectin 包被、硬度明确的 PDMS 基质上绘制原代人肺成纤维细胞全局磷酸化蛋白组动态，首次定位出一个位于 2-8 kPa 之间的机械信号阈值，跨越该阈值即触发细胞骨架重塑、ECM 分泌与 CTHRC1+/ACTA2+ 肌成纤维细胞状态转变。
- 新定义一个机械敏感磷酸化开关——NFATC4 S213/S217：phosphomimetic S213D/S217D 突变体在软基质上不依赖 TGFB 即可驱动肌成纤维细胞分化，而 phospho-dead S213A/S217A 突变体在硬基质加 TGFB 条件下仍阻断分化，从而确立该位点既必要又充分。
- 通过 micro-CT 分期的人肺纤维化组织与多队列肺纤维化 atlas 的 regulon 推断相互印证，闭合出一条 NFATC4 依赖的 119 基因纤维化程序并以 CTHRC1 为首要靶基因的机制链。

**研究设计**
- 原代人肺成纤维细胞接种于 fibronectin 包被的 PDMS 基质（含 2-8 kPa 等定义硬度），进行全局磷酸化蛋白组动态分析
- micro-CT 分期的肺纤维化人组织，检测 NFATC4 表达并与 CTHRC1、ACTA2 共定位
- 多队列肺纤维化单细胞/转录组 atlas 的转录因子 regulon 活性推断（含 119 基因 NFATC4 依赖程序）
- NFATC4 S213D/S217D 与 S213A/S217A 突变体在软/硬基质 ± TGFB 条件下的功能获得与功能缺失实验
- JNK 与 calcineurin 通路干预，验证与硬基质、TGFB 信号的汇聚

**主要发现**
- 硬度超过 2-8 kPa 阈值后，人肺成纤维细胞激活细胞骨架重塑与 ECM 分泌，并转入 CTHRC1+/ACTA2+ 肌成纤维细胞状态，伴随 NFATC4 S213/S217 的硬度依赖性磷酸化。
- 在 micro-CT 分期的肺纤维化组织中 NFATC4 表达随分期递增，并与 CTHRC1、ACTA2 在肌成纤维细胞中共定位。
- 多队列纤维化 atlas 的 regulon 推断显示疾病成纤维细胞中 NFATC4 活性升高，并定义出一个含 119 个基因的 NFATC4 依赖促纤维化程序，CTHRC1 位居其靶基因之首。
- S213D/S217D 在软基质上不依赖 TGFB 即可诱导肌成纤维细胞分化；S213A/S217A 在硬基质加 TGFB 条件下仍阻断分化，证明该磷酸化位点是充分且必要的开关。
- 硬基质与 TGFB 通过 JNK 与 calcineurin 依赖的途径汇聚于该磷酸化开关，从而放大纤维化反应。

**为什么入选** ['机制链完整：硬度梯度磷酸化蛋白组 → 位点级 phospho-switch → 下游 119 基因程序与 CTHRC1 靶点 → 人肺组织分期验证。', '采用 phosphomimetic/phospho-dead 配对突变体在软硬基质 ± TGFB 的正交条件下完成必要性-充分性判定，因果证据强度高。', '同时具备人原代细胞、定义硬度材料、micro-CT 分期人组织与公共 atlas 多重验证，发表于 JCI，对纤维化与机械生物学领域有直接参考价值。']

**局限**
- 本次仅获取到摘要，所附全文片段实为 PRIDE 数据库（Nucleic Acids Research 2019）无关文献，无法核对样本量、统计方法、磷酸化位点鉴定与质谱可信度。
- 摘要未提及体内功能验证（如成纤维细胞特异性 Nfatc4 缺失或 S213/S217 位点突变小鼠模型），磷酸化开关的体内因果性尚不明确。
- 文中提出的"multiorgan fibrosis 候选治疗靶点"目前仅基于细胞与组织关联数据，缺乏干预性体内或人体验证。

**可借鉴之处**
- fibronectin-coated PDMS 硬度梯度（含 2-8 kPa 区间）叠加全局磷酸化蛋白组，是可复用的机械转导阈值筛选平台，可用于寻找其他力学响应细胞状态（如肺泡上皮、血管周成纤维细胞）的磷酸化节点。
- phosphomimetic/phospho-dead 配对突变体做双向功能获得-缺失判定，是验证"磷酸化开关"因果性的简洁范式，可移植到其他 mechanosensitive 转录因子的研究。
- 以公共纤维化 scRNA-seq 图谱做 regulon 推断、再回到人组织做 CTHRC1/ACTA2 共定位验证的两步逻辑，可用于免疫-基质 crosstalk 或肺血管重塑中致病性成纤维细胞亚群的甄别。

### 2. Critical role for the TGF-β1/mTORC1 signaling axis in defining the transcriptional identity of CTHRC1+ pathologic fibroblasts.

Science advances | 2026 Sep 04 | Jo-Anne Am Wilson, Rachel C Chambers
IF 2025：11.7 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Centre for Inflammation and Tissue Repair, UCL Respiratory, University College London, London WC1E 6JF, UK.
- 领域：UCL Centre for Inflammation and Tissue Repair（UCL Respiratory）方向，聚焦肺纤维化中成纤维细胞与细胞外基质/组织修复机制。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42685210/
- DOI：10.1126/sciadv.adx9868

**Abstract**

Fibrosis, marked by excess extracellular matrix (ECM) deposition, is the end stage of many diseases. Single-cell studies have highlighted the emergence of disease-specific fibroblast populations, including a high collagen-synthesizing CTHRC1+ subpopulation. The profibrotic cytokine TGF-β1 promotes fibrogenesis via cooperation between Smad and mTORC1/4E-BP1 signaling axes. Using CRISPR-Cas9 gene editing, we report that more than one-third of TGF-β1-regulated matrisome genes are under mTORC1 control. Mapping the transcriptome of TGF-β1-stimulated fibroblasts revealed similarity to CTHRC1+ fibroblasts identified in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF). This overlap is lost when mTORC1 is disabled. Using the selective mTORC1 inhibitor RMC-5552, we confirm a causal role for mTORC1 in promoting the acquisition of the collagen-high, CTHRC1+ phenotype in response to TGF-β1 stimulation in fibroblasts derived from patients with either IPF or lung adenocarcinoma. We conclude that mTORC1 plays a key role in shaping the transcriptional identity of these fibroblasts, with implications for therapeutic inhibition of mTORC1 in fibrosis and cancer.

**What Is New?**
- 通过 CRISPR-Cas9 基因编辑证明 TGF-β1 调控的 matrisome 基因中超过三分之一受 mTORC1 控制，从而把 mTORC1 从协同通路提升为 TGF-β1 促纤维化转录程序的关键效应节点。

### 3. Adenine nucleotide translocase 2 (ANT2) deficiency reprograms ferroptosis in alveolar progenitor cells to promote emphysema.

bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology | 2026 Jul 13 | Ugonna Mbaekwe, Corrine R Kliment


- 来源：biorxiv
- 依据：abstract_only

**Abstract**

Stem cell dysfunction and loss of renewal capacity are primary characteristics of tissue aging and decremental regeneration in response to injury. Alveolar type 2 cells (AT2) are key progenitor cells responsible for lung repair and are thought to be dysfunctional in diseases such as chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). AT2 cells are highly metabolic and rely on mitochondria, but how mitochondrial mechanisms influence their maintenance and cell fate is unclear. This gap is critical as no current therapies target lung repair or mitochondrial function in COPD. Here, we report that adenine nucleotide translocase 2 (ANT2), a key ATP/ADP transporter, is reduced in AT2 cells from COPD lungs, and that ANT2 loss impairs bioenergetics (ATP). We also identify, for the first time, ferroptotic susceptibility as a consequence of ANT2 loss in AT2 cells, leading to impaired self-renewal and progenitor capacity in alveolar organoids. Together, loss of ANT2 and the associated cellular dysfunction resulted in worsened lung damage or emphysema due to cigarette smoke in mice. Therapeutic restoration of ANT2 expression resulted in renewed AT2 stem cell function and prevention of emphysema by reducing oxidative stress and ferroptosis. These findings highlight the importance of ANT2 in metabolic regulation, plasticity, and cell resiliency of AT2 cells in the lung and that ANT2 is a potential target for lung repair.

**What Is New?**
- 首次将线粒体 ATP/ADP 转运体 ANT2 定义为 AT2 祖细胞维持的代谢检查点：COPD 患者肺 AT2 中 ANT2 降低，并直接导致 ATP 生物能量学受损。
- 首次把 ANT2 缺失与 ferroptosis 易感性确立为因果链条，说明 AT2 自我更新与祖细胞能力下降并非单纯增殖缺陷，而是特定死亡通路的重编程。
- 通过在小鼠中回补 ANT2 表达即减轻 oxidative stress 与 ferroptosis、恢复 AT2 干细胞功能并预防 cigarette smoke 诱导的 emphysema，给出体内 rescue 级验证而非仅相关性描述。

**研究设计**
- 以人 COPD 肺 AT2 中 ANT2 表达降低为起点建立相关性。
- 在 AT2 中评估 ANT2 缺失对 bioenergetics（ATP）与 ferroptosis 易感性的影响。
- 用 alveolar organoid 检测自我更新与祖细胞能力。
- 在小鼠 cigarette smoke 模型中检验 ANT2 缺失加重的肺损伤/emphysema。
- 治疗性回补 ANT2 表达，观察 oxidative stress、ferroptosis 与 emphysema 的逆转。

**主要发现**
- COPD 患者肺 AT2 细胞中 ANT2 表达降低，提示其与人类疾病状态相关。
- ANT2 缺失损害 AT2 的 ATP 生成与生物能量学功能。
- ANT2 缺失使 AT2 获得 ferroptosis 易感性，并在 alveolar organoid 中表现为自我更新与祖细胞能力下降。
- ANT2 缺失叠加 cigarette smoke 导致小鼠肺损伤/emphysema 加重。
- 恢复 ANT2 表达可降低 oxidative stress 与 ferroptosis，恢复 AT2 干细胞功能并预防 emphysema。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整且闭合（ANT2 → ATP/生物能量学 → ferroptosis → AT2 自我更新 → emphysema），同时具备人 COPD 组织证据、alveolar organoid 功能读出、体内 cigarette smoke 模型以及表达回补 rescue，将 ferroptosis 从一种死亡方式提升为肺泡祖细胞衰竭的可干预节点。
