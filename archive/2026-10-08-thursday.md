### 1. Sotatercept in Pediatric Pulmonary Hypertension: Experience from the Pediatric Pulmonary Hypertension Network.

Chest | 2026 Oct 05 | Paul J Critser, Usha S Krishnan
IF 2025：9.6 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed

- 第一作者单位：The Heart Institute, Cincinnati Children's Hospital Medical Center, University of Cincinnati College of Medicine, Cincinnati, Ohio.
- 通讯作者单位：Division of Pediatric Cardiology, Department of Pediatrics, Columbia University Irving Medical Center, New York, New York. Electronic address: usk1@cumc.columbia.edu.
- 第一作者领域：第一作者单位为 Cincinnati Children's Hospital Medical Center 心脏研究所，主要研究领域为儿童肺高血压与先天性心脏病的临床研究。
- 通讯作者领域：通讯作者单位为 Columbia University Irving Medical Center 儿科心脏病学，主要研究领域为 pediatric pulmonary hypertension 的临床与多中心注册研究。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42833549/
- DOI：10.1016/j.chest.2026.09.096

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Sotatercept, a ligand trap for TGF-β family members, has been shown to improve exercise capacity and decrease pulmonary vascular resistance (PVR) in adults with pulmonary arterial hypertension. Limited data exist describing the use of sotatercept in children with pulmonary hypertension (PH).
RESEARCH QUESTION: What is the safety and efficacy of sotatercept as add-on therapy in children with PH.
STUDY DESIGN AND METHODS: This is a multi-center, retrospective study of children and young adults ≤ 21 years of age with pediatric PH treated by programs in the Pediatric PH Network and institutional registries. Demographic data and clinical characteristics before and after sotatercept initiation were collected.
RESULTS: Sixty-six subjects received sotatercept as add-on therapy for a median duration of 323 days (Interquartile range 239, 500). Forty (60%) were female with median age 15.3 years (range 3.0 - 21.0) at initiation of sotatercept, and 61 with Group 1 (92%) PH. WHO functional class improved in 51% and did not worsen in any subjects (p < 0.0001). Subjects experienced significant improvement in six-minute walk distance (median 31 m, p=0.0003, n=40), NT-proBNP (median decrease 378 pg/ml, p< 0.0001, n=26), mean pulmonary artery pressure (median decrease 17 mmHg, p<0.0001, n=21), and PVR (median decrease 4.4 WUi, p=0.0011, n=21). Parenteral prostacyclin was weaned or discontinued in 13 of 38 patients. Adverse events occurred in 33 (50%) subjects with bleeding in 15 (23%). No patients had escalation of PH therapy, were hospitalized for clinical worsening, underwent lung transplant, or died.
INTERPRETATION: Pediatric PH patients treated with sotatercept as add-on therapy demonstrated improvement in functional class and other PH disease indicators and was generally well tolerated.

**What Is New?**
- 首次在儿童及 ≤21 岁人群中系统报告 sotatercept（ActRIIA-Fc 配体陷阱）作为 add-on 治疗的多中心安全性/疗效数据，把该药在成人 PAH 中的证据外推边界推到了 pediatric PH 这一此前几乎空白的领域。
- 明确 TGF-β 超家族配体通路在儿童肺血管病变中同样是一个可干预的因果节点：中位 323 天内 WHO functional class 改善者达 51% 且无人恶化，同时伴 NT-proBNP、mPAP 与 PVR 的显著下降。
- 首次报告 13/38 例患儿在加用 sotatercept 后成功减停 parenteral prostacyclin，为儿科 PH 中「加药后降阶梯」这一此前缺乏数据的治疗路径提供了初步可行性证据。

**研究设计**
- 多中心、回顾性队列研究（Pediatric Pulmonary Hypertension Network 及机构 registry）
- 纳入 ≤21 岁 pediatric PH 患儿，sotatercept 作为 add-on 治疗
- 采集用药前后人口学与临床指标，自身前后对比，无平行对照组
- n=66；中位用药 323 天（IQR 239–500）；60% 女性，中位年龄 15.3 岁；Group 1 PH 占 92%

**主要发现**
- WHO functional class：51% 改善、0% 恶化（p<0.0001）
- 6MWD 中位增加 31 m（p=0.0003，n=40）；NT-proBNP 中位下降 378 pg/ml（p<0.0001，n=26）
- mPAP 中位下降 17 mmHg（p<0.0001，n=21）；PVR 中位下降 4.4 WU·i（p=0.0011，n=21）
- 38 例使用 parenteral prostacyclin 者中 13 例成功减量或停用
- 安全性：AE 50%（33/66），其中 bleeding 23%（15/66）；无 PH 治疗升级、无临床恶化住院、无肺移植或死亡

**为什么入选** ['机制靶点明确且可迁移：sotatercept 为 TGF-β 家族（ActRIIA）配体陷阱，直接对应 BMP/TGF-β 失衡这一肺血管重构核心通路，成人已证实有效而儿科完全缺数据', '含「降阶梯」终点（parenteral prostacyclin 减停），在儿科 PH 研究中少见，比单纯的指标改善更有决策价值', '多中心网络化 registry 设计，样本量（n=66）在 pediatric PH 这一罕见病人群中已属可观，发表于 Chest，方法学可被同类研究复用']

**局限**
- 本次评估仅基于摘要，无 OA 全文，故无法核验纳入标准、失访、统计方法与安全性事件定义细节
- 单臂、回顾性、自身前后对照，无法排除自然病程、背景治疗调整与回归均值的影响，亦无成人或未用药儿科对照
- 中位随访仅 323 天，长期安全性（尤其儿科出血风险、生长发育与骨代谢）及停药后反弹均未知

**可借鉴之处**
- ActRIIA 配体陷阱可作为一类可复用的「通路探针」思路：在动物或细胞模型中用同类配体陷阱干预，检验 TGF-β/BMP（如 BMPR2 通路）信号失衡对 pulmonary vascular remodeling、平滑肌与 fibroblast 表型的因果贡献，而非仅观察表达变化。
- 该研究以 PVR、mPAP、NT-proBNP、6MWD、functional class 组成「血流动力学—生物标志物—功能」三层读数框架，提示基础研究设计 rescue 实验时可对应设置分层终点，避免只用单一分子或单一形态学指标判定干预成功。
- 儿科人群中 bleeding 发生率 23%、整体 AE 50% 与成人数据的差异，提示发育阶段可能改变 TGF-β 超家族信号对血管与止血系统的效应，值得在发育期肺血管/肺泡模型中进行年龄分层验证，并与免疫—间质 crosstalk 的现有模型对照。

### 2. Pulmonary Artery Dilation on Chest CT Across the Spectrum of Chronic Lung Disease.

Chest | 2026 Sep 24 | Tyler J Couch
IF 2025：9.6 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：年内旗舰系统研究
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Division of Allergy, Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine, Vanderbilt University Medical Center, Nashville, TN, USA. Electronic address: tylerjcouch@gmail.com.
- 领域：Tyler J. Couch，Vanderbilt University Medical Center 呼吸与危重症医学科；据单位与摘要推断，主要关注慢性肺病相关 pulmonary hypertension 的 CT 影像表型与血流动力学关联。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42785424/
- DOI：10.1016/j.chest.2026.08.053

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Pulmonary artery (PA) dilation on CT has been associated with pulmonary hypertension (PH) in subjects without lung disease using outdated PH criteria. The relationship between PA size and PH in chronic lung disease is unclear.
RESEARCH QUESTION: Is PA size associated with PH in subjects with chronic lung disease, and does association differ by disease etiology?
METHODS: PA diameter and PA/aorta ratio (PA/A) were measured on CT for all group 3 PH subjects and comparators in the PVDOMICS Cohort. Subjects were categorized into obstructive, interstitial, or non-parenchymal subgroups. Mean PA pressure (mPAP) was measured on right heart catheterization. Associations and diagnostic performance were assessed using multivariable linear and logistic regression and receiver operating curve analysis.
RESULTS: Among 252 subjects (81 obstructive, 125 interstitial, 46 non-parenchymal), PA size associated with PH (Q3 vs Q1 OR 4.30 [2.32-8.00] for diameter, 5.66 [2.86-11.18] for PA/A) and mPAP (ρ = 0.516 for diameter, 0.494 for PA/A; p < 0.001) and possessed moderate ability to detect PH (AUC 0.794 for diameter, 0.765 for PA/A). Association between PA/A and mPAP was strongest in non-parenchymal subjects (ρ = 0.750) and weakest in interstitial subjects (ρ = 0.373). PA/A diagnostic performance was lower in interstitial subjects (AUC 0.704) than in obstructive (AUC 0.865) and non-parenchymal (AUC 0.822) subjects. PA dilation between Q1 and Q3 mPAP was lower in interstitial (3.9 [95% CI 2.3-5.4] mm) compared to obstructive (4.9 [2.4-7.4] mm) and non-parenchymal (4.8 [1.8-7.8] mm) subjects.

**What Is New?**
- 在 PVDOMICS group 3 PH 队列中首次按 obstructive、interstitial、non-parenchymal 病因分层，量化 CT 上 PA diameter 与 PA/A 对 PH 的关联强度差异。
- 明确 PA/A 与 mPAP 的相关性在 non-parenchymal 亚组最强（ρ=0.750）、在 interstitial 亚组最弱（ρ=0.373），提示 CT 肺动脉扩张对 PH 的提示价值受基础肺病类型修饰。
- 以 right heart catheterization 和当前 PH 标准为参照，给出 PA diameter AUC 0.794、PA/A AUC 0.765 的诊断表现，并显示 interstitial 亚组 PA/A AUC 降至 0.704。
- 结果表明由 Q1 到 Q3 mPAP 的 PA 扩张幅度在 interstitial 亚组（3.9 mm）小于 obstructive（4.9 mm）和 non-parenchymal（4.8 mm）亚组，说明不同病因下血管扩张反应不同。

**研究设计**
- 基于 PVDOMICS Cohort，纳入 group 3 PH 受试者与 comparators，共 252 例。
- 按 obstructive、interstitial、non-parenchymal 分为三个亚组。
- 在 CT 上测量 PA diameter 与 PA/A ratio，在 right heart catheterization 上测量 mPAP。
- 使用多变量线性回归、logistic 回归和 ROC 分析评估关联与诊断表现。

**主要发现**
- 252 例中包括 81 例 obstructive、125 例 interstitial、46 例 non-parenchymal 受试者。
- PA 大小与 PH 相关：PA diameter Q3 vs Q1 OR 4.30（2.32-8.00），PA/A OR 5.66（2.86-11.18）。
- PA 大小与 mPAP 相关：PA diameter ρ=0.516，PA/A ρ=0.494，p<0.001。
- PA diameter 检测 PH 的 AUC 为 0.794，PA/A 为 0.765。
- PA/A 与 mPAP 的关联在 non-parenchymal 亚组最强（ρ=0.750），在 interstitial 亚组最弱（ρ=0.373）；PA/A 诊断表现也在 interstitial 亚组最低（AUC 0.704）。

**为什么入选** 该研究使用现代 PH 定义和 right heart catheterization 金标准，在 PVDOMICS 队列中按慢性肺病病因分层，揭示 CT 肺动脉扩张指标在不同 group 3 PH 亚型中的诊断异质性，对肺血管重塑与间质性、阻塞性肺病交叉领域有参考价值。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要，无 OA 全文，无法评估 CT 测量方法、排除标准、混杂校正和亚组分析细节。
- 研究对象为 PVDOMICS 队列中的 group 3 PH 与 comparators，结果外推至其他 PH 组或一般人群需谨慎。
- 研究主要评估关联与诊断表现，不能推断 PA 扩张与 PH 之间的因果关系或时间顺序。

**可借鉴之处**
- 按 chronic lung disease 病因分层比较影像指标与血流动力学终点，可作为将 alveolar、interstitial 或 pulmonary vascular remodeling 表型与临床 PH 表型关联的分析模板。
- 使用 right heart catheterization 测得的 mPAP 而非旧 PH 标准定义终点，有助于在机制研究或动物模型中验证 PA 扩张相关候选通路。
- PA/A 等简单、可重复的 CT 指标可用于队列分层，但 interstitial 肺病中诊断信号被稀释，提示机制研究需区分 parenchymal 病变与 non-parenchymal 肺血管受累。

### 3. SOX17 Deficiency Mediates Pulmonary Hypertension: At the Crossroads of Sex, Metabolism, and Genetics.

American journal of respiratory and critical care medicine | 2023 Apr 15 | Shreya Sangam, Ankit A Desai
IF 2025：21.7 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：领域经典
- 依据：full_text_oa
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Medicine and.; Department of Clinical Translational Sciences.
- 领域：第一作者 Shreya Sangam 隶属 Indiana University 医学系与 University of Arizona 临床转化科学系，研究方向推测为肺动脉高压的转化医学与肺血管生物学（具体领域不详）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36913491/
- DOI：10.1164/rccm.202203-0450OC

**Abstract**

Rationale: Genetic studies suggest that SOX17 (SRY-related HMG-box 17) deficiency increases pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) risk. Objectives: On the basis of pathological roles of estrogen and HIF2α (hypoxia-inducible factor 2α) signaling in pulmonary artery endothelial cells (PAECs), we hypothesized that SOX17 is a target of estrogen signaling that promotes mitochondrial function and attenuates PAH development via HIF2α inhibition. Methods: We used metabolic (Seahorse) and promoter luciferase assays in PAECs together with the chronic hypoxia murine model to test the hypothesis. Measurements and Main Results: Sox17 expression was reduced in PAH tissues (rodent models and from patients). Chronic hypoxic pulmonary hypertension was exacerbated by mice with conditional Tie2-Sox17 (Sox17EC-/-) deletion and attenuated by transgenic Tie2-Sox17 overexpression (Sox17Tg). On the basis of untargeted proteomics, metabolism was the top pathway altered by SOX17 deficiency in PAECs. Mechanistically, we found that HIF2α concentrations were increased in the lungs of Sox17EC-/- and reduced in those from Sox17Tg mice. Increased SOX17 promoted oxidative phosphorylation and mitochondrial function in PAECs, which were partly attenuated by HIF2α overexpression. Rat lungs in males displayed higher Sox17 expression versus females, suggesting repression by estrogen signaling. Supporting 16α-hydroxyestrone (16αOHE; a pathologic estrogen metabolite)-mediated repression of SOX17 promoter activity, Sox17Tg mice attenuated 16αOHE-mediated exacerbations of chronic hypoxic pulmonary hypertension. Finally, in adjusted analyses in patients with PAH, we report novel associations between a SOX17 risk variant, rs10103692, and reduced plasma citrate concentrations (n = 1,326). Conclusions: Cumulatively, SOX17 promotes mitochondrial bioenergetics and attenuates PAH, in part, via inhibition of HIF2α. 16αOHE mediates PAH development via downregulation of SOX17, linking sexual dimorphism and SOX17 genetics in PAH.

**What Is New?**
- 首次以内皮特异性双向遗传学确立 SOX17 是肺动脉高压的因果性抑制节点：Tie2-Sox17 条件敲除（Sox17EC-/-）加重、Tie2-Sox17 过表达（Sox17Tg）减轻慢性缺氧性肺高血压，而非仅停留于表达相关性。
- 新定义 SOX17–HIF2α 作为闭合机制节点：SOX17 缺失升高肺内 HIF2α，SOX17 促进 PAEC 氧化磷酸化与线粒体功能，该促代谢效应可被 HIF2α 过表达部分逆转。
- 新连接性别二态、代谢与人类遗传：病理雌激素代谢物 16αOHE 抑制 SOX17 启动子活性，Sox17Tg 可抵消其加重作用，并在 1,326 例 PAH 患者中把 SOX17 风险变异 rs10103692 与血浆柠檬酸降低相关联。

**研究设计**
- 人及啮齿类 PAH 组织与 PAEC 中检测 Sox17/SOX17 表达
- PAEC 体外 Seahorse 代谢分析与 SOX17 启动子 luciferase 报告实验
- 内皮特异性 Sox17 条件敲除（Sox17EC-/-）与转基因过表达（Sox17Tg）小鼠 + 慢性缺氧模型
- untargeted proteomics 筛选 SOX17 缺失后改变的通路并检测肺内 HIF2α
- 16αOHE 干预与 Sox17Tg 抵消实验；PAH 患者队列中 SOX17 变异 rs10103692 与血浆柠檬酸的关联分析（n=1,326）

**主要发现**
- PAH 状态下 SOX17/Sox17 表达下降，见于啮齿类模型与患者组织。
- 内皮特异性 Sox17 缺失加重、Sox17 过表达减轻慢性缺氧性肺动脉高压。
- SOX17 缺失后 PAEC 中改变最显著的通路为代谢；SOX17 增强氧化磷酸化与线粒体功能，HIF2α 过表达可部分削弱之。
- Sox17EC-/- 小鼠肺内 HIF2α 升高，Sox17Tg 小鼠肺内 HIF2α 降低，提示 SOX17 经抑制 HIF2α 发挥作用。
- 雄性大鼠肺内 Sox17 高于雌性，16αOHE 抑制 SOX17 启动子活性并加重缺氧性肺高血压，Sox17Tg 可减弱该加重；PAH 患者中 rs10103692 与血浆柠檬酸降低相关。

**为什么入选** 机制链自上游（雌激素代谢物 16αOHE 抑制 SOX17）经核心节点（SOX17–HIF2α）到下游表型（内皮线粒体生物能量与 PAH 严重度）较为完整；具备内皮特异性双向 in vivo 遗传学验证与回复实验，且有人肺组织及大样本患者遗传-代谢关联佐证，发表于 AJRCCM 肺血管病栏目。

**局限**
- 所提供全文片段以摘要、作者单位与刊头元数据为主，缺少方法学细节（如缺氧时长、Sox17Tg 表达量、HIF2α 回复的定量程度），机制推论以摘要为准。
- 核心因果证据主要来自小鼠/大鼠模型与培养 PAEC，人体部分为遗传-代谢关联，尚非人体因果验证。
- SOX17 抑制 HIF2α 的直接分子方式（转录抑制或蛋白层面互作）以及 SOX17 促线粒体功能的具体效应分子，在提供文本中未明确。

**可借鉴之处**
- 内皮特异性 Tie2 驱动的条件敲除加转基因过表达「双向验证」范式，配合慢性缺氧小鼠模型，可直接借用于检验其他候选基因在肺血管重构中的因果性而非伴随性。
- Seahorse 代谢表型 + untargeted proteomics 通路筛选 + 启动子 luciferase 的组合，是解析「转录因子—线粒体代谢」轴的可复用设计；用 HIF2α 过表达做上下游定位与部分回复实验，思路可迁移到其他内皮代谢调控研究。
- 将基因变异与循环代谢物在人群队列中做关联（rs10103692 与 plasma citrate），为把基础机制推向人类证据提供模板；关注免疫-基质 crosstalk 或成纤维细胞者可进一步设想内皮代谢改变经旁分泌重塑基质微环境的可能。

### 1. Monocyte-derived galectin-1hi cells provide innate immune help in the generation of functional memory CD8+ T cells.

Nature immunology | 2026 Oct | Kihong Lim#, Minsoo Kim#
IF 2025：27.7 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Microbiology and Immunology, University of Rochester, Rochester, NY, USA. kihong_lim@urmc.rochester.edu.
- 领域：第一作者Kihong Lim，罗切斯特大学微生物学与免疫学系，研究肺内髓系细胞（单核/巨噬来源亚群）与组织驻留记忆CD8+ T细胞的相互作用。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42675114/
- DOI：10.1186/s12864-018-4772-0

**Abstract**

After viral infections, tissue-resident memory T cells (TRM cells) are generated and reactivated rapidly upon re-exposure to previously encountered viral pathogens, providing immediate immune effector functions to limit infection at the site of viral entry. Here we found that a subset of newly recruited CCR2+ monocytes differentiated into memory-stage CCR2-tdTomato+ cells and persisted in the lung for more than 4 months after infection with the influenza virus. Selective depletion of the memory-stage CCR2-tdTomato+ cells reduced significantly the formation of lung CD8+ TRM cells and compromised secondary heterosubtypic immune protection. Memory-stage CCR2-tdTomato+ cells colocalized with lung CD8+ TRM cells and secreted galectin-1, which activated CD8+ T cells directly and enhanced transforming growth factor-β sensing. Intranasal administration of recombinant galectin-1 as an adjuvant for the influenza vaccine induced superior memory CD8+ T cell responses. Thus, the presence of a unique memory-like monocyte-derived subset provided crucial signals to establish and maintain functional CD8+ TRM cells in the lung.

**What Is New?**
- 鉴定出一个此前未被描述的记忆样单核来源CCR2-tdTomato+细胞亚群，其在流感病毒感染后肺部驻留超过4个月，把"记忆"属性从适应性T细胞拓展到固有髓系细胞。
- 通过选择性清除该亚群证明它是肺CD8+ TRM细胞生成与异亚型（heterosubtypic）二次保护的因果必需节点，而非感染后的伴随现象。
- 闭合了一条固有—适应性免疫互作机制链：该细胞与肺CD8+ TRM共定位并分泌galectin-1，直接激活CD8+ T细胞并增强其TGF-β感知；重组galectin-1作为鼻内疫苗佐剂可显著提升记忆CD8+ T细胞应答。

**研究设计**
- 小鼠流感病毒感染模型，追踪感染后肺内单核细胞来源亚群。

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Influenza-associated pulmonary aspergillosis (IAPA) is a severe complication of influenza infection associated with prolonged intensive care unit stay and increased mortality. Although impaired antifungal immunity has been implicated in IAPA pathogenesis, the cell type-specific immune mechanisms driving susceptibility remain incompletely understood. We aimed to characterize the pulmonary immune landscape during IAPA using single-cell transcriptomics and functional neutrophil assays.
METHODS: Male C57BL/6 mice were assigned to naïve control, influenza A/PR/8/34 (H1N1) infection, A. fumigatus (ATCC 42202) infection, or IAPA groups. Lung CD45 + immune cells underwent single-cell RNA sequencing with downstream clustering and CellChat ligand-receptor interaction analysis. Differential gene expression analyses were performed across myeloid, lymphoid, and neutrophil populations. Functional neutrophil responses were evaluated using flow cytometry, myeloperoxidase activity assays, and FLARE (fluorescent Aspergillus reporter) conidia to assess fungal conidia uptake and killing. Cross-species validation was performed using gene set enrichment analysis compared with published human IAPA transcriptomic datasets.
RESULTS: IAPA broadly remodeled the pulmonary immune landscape across myeloid, lymphoid, and neutrophil compartments. Myeloid cells showed coordinated suppression of fungal pattern recognition receptors, lysosomal biogenesis programs, and inflammatory signaling. Lymphoid populations exhibited transcriptional signatures of T cell exhaustion and Th17 suppression. Within the neutrophil compartment, we identified two transcriptionally and functionally distinct populations, conventional and inflammatory neutrophils, with divergent antifungal effector capacities. Inflammatory neutrophils showed selective killing defects, while both subsets exhibited impaired phagocytic uptake during IAPA. Murine transcriptomic findings demonstrated strong concordance with immune dysfunction signatures identified in human IAPA.
CONCLUSION: IAPA susceptibility arises from coordinated transcriptional dysfunction spanning innate and adaptive immune compartments. Distinct neutrophil subset dysfunction, impaired fungal recognition pathways, and T-cell exhaustion signatures collectively contribute to defective fungal clearance, providing mechanistic insight into IAPA susceptibility and potential therapeutic targets.

**What Is New?**
- 本研究以肺 CD45+ scRNA-seq 系统刻画 IAPA 免疫景观，提出 IAPA 易感性并非单一细胞类型缺陷，而是跨越 innate 与 adaptive 区室的协同转录功能障碍。
- 在 neutrophil 区室内新定义了两个转录与功能均不同的亚群——conventional 与 inflammatory neutrophil，其中 inflammatory 亚群表现出选择性的真菌杀伤缺陷，而两亚群均存在吞噬摄取受损。
- 通过 GSEA 将小鼠转录组与人 IAPA 数据集做跨物种比对，显示免疫功能障碍签名高度一致，为小鼠机制发现提供了人体相关性层面的验证。

**研究设计**
- 雄性 C57BL/6 小鼠分四组：naïve control、influenza A/PR/8/34 (H1N1)、A. fumigatus (ATCC 42202)、IAPA。
- 肺 CD45+ 免疫细胞行 scRNA-seq，下游聚类并用 CellChat 做配体-受体互作分析。
- 在 myeloid、lymphoid、neutrophil 群体中分别做差异表达分析。
- Neutrophil 功能评估：flow cytometry、myeloperoxidase (MPO) 活性检测、FLARE (fluorescent Aspergillus reporter) conidia 摄取与杀伤实验。
- 跨物种验证：GSEA 比对已发表人 IAPA 转录组数据集。

**主要发现**
- IAPA 广泛重塑肺 myeloid、lymphoid 与 neutrophil 三大免疫区室，而非局限于单一细胞类型。
- Myeloid 细胞出现真菌 pattern recognition receptor、lysosomal biogenesis 程序与炎症信号的协同抑制。
- Lymphoid 群体呈现 T cell exhaustion 转录特征与 Th17 抑制。
- Neutrophil 区室中鉴定出 conventional 与 inflammatory 两群，inflammatory neutrophil 存在选择性杀伤缺陷，而两亚群在 IAPA 中均表现为吞噬摄取受损。
- 小鼠转录组的人 IAPA 一致性分析显示，免疫功能障碍签名在跨物种间高度吻合。

**为什么入选** 以 scRNA-seq 加 CellChat 构建 IAPA 肺免疫全景，并用 FLARE conidia、MPO 与 flow cytometry 将 neutrophil 亚群状态落到功能表型，再以 GSEA 对接人 IAPA 数据形成模型-人体闭环；机制链覆盖 pattern recognition、溶酶体、炎症信号、T cell exhaustion 与 neutrophil 亚群杀伤/吞噬，属于感染合并症肺部免疫的基础研究，目前为 bioRxiv preprint。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要、无 OA 全文，细胞数、聚类分辨率、亚群 marker、统计方法与具体验证细节均无法评估。
- 小鼠模型仅使用单一 influenza 株（A/PR/8/34 H1N1）、单一 A. fumigatus 株（ATCC 42202）与雄性 C57BL/6，向其他毒株、性别与人体外推需谨慎。
- 跨物种验证停留在 GSEA 转录组一致性层面，未直接使用人源样本做功能验证。

**可借鉴之处**
- 四组对照（naïve / influenza / Aspergillus / IAPA）结合肺 CD45+ scRNA-seq 与 CellChat 的设计，可用于拆解感染合并症中 immune-stromal crosstalk；若在分选或分析中保留基质/成纤维细胞，可进一步观察肺泡壁与间质重塑相关信号。
- 把 scRNA-seq 推断的细胞状态直接绑定到功能读出（FLARE conidia 摄取与杀伤、flow cytometry、MPO 活性）的范式，可推广到其他 neutrophil 或 macrophage 亚群的功能分层研究，避免停留于转录组相关性。
- 用 GSEA 将小鼠签章映射到已发表人转录组，是低成本提升模型转化相关性的可行策略，同样适用于肺血管重塑或肺泡生物学课题中的人-鼠数据对照。

### 3. Multi-Omic Profiling Defines the Renal Mechanisms of Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Syndrome in Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension.

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic (CKM) syndrome integrates cardiac, renal, and metabolic abnormalities that drive multi-organ injury. In pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH), CKM manifests as systemic metabolic derangements, right ventricular dysfunction, and renal compromise. Renal dysfunction strongly predicts mortality in PAH, yet current therapies provide little renal benefit. Moreover, the mechanisms driving PAH nephropathy remain poorly understood, limiting our ability to effectively treat PAH-CKM.
METHODS: Single-nucleus RNAsequencing identified cell-type-specific transcriptional alterations in autopsy-derived kidneys from control (n=5) and PAH (n=4) patients. Mitochondrial, cytoplasmic, and phosphoproteomic analyses profiled proteomic alterations. AlphaFold 3/ChimeraX modeling defined the predicted structural consequences of altered protein phosphorylation. Histological analysis assessed renal fibrosis, glomerular structure, immune cell infiltration, and nephron segment density.
RESULTS: 3 of 4 PAH patients exhibited renal impairment, with a cohort mean estimated glomerular filtration rate of 61±36 mL/min/1.73m². snRNA-seq identified a distinct cellular architecture in PAH kidneys, marked by an increase in thick ascending limb, proximal tubule, and immune cell nuclei and depletion of collecting duct nuclei. Transcriptional profiling demonstrated proximal tubule and thick ascending limb cells both upregulated fatty acid oxidation, ferroptosis, and cuproptosis pathways. PAH lymphocytes displayed heightened T-cell receptor signaling, natural killer cell-mediated cytotoxicity, and Th17 differentiation. Histological analyses demonstrated increased perivascular fibrosis, glomerular T-cell infiltration, and a reduction in glomerular basement membrane density in PAH kidneys. Mitochondrial and cytoplasmic proteomics revealed broad metabolic dysfunction characterized by impaired β-oxidation, TCA cycle activity, amino acid metabolism, transsulfuration, and urea cycle pathways. Phosphoproteomics predicted increased GSK3β, STK, and casein kinase activity in PAH kidneys. Finally, using the totality of our data, we nominated multiple druggable targets that could be evaluated to counteract PAH nephropathy.
CONCLUSIONS: Integrated multi-omic profiling demonstrates PAH nephropathy is defined by glomerular structural remodeling, proximal nephron ferroptotic and cuproptotic signaling, amino acid metabolic dysregulation, and innate and adaptive leukocyte activation. Future studies intervening on these pathways could lead to the development of novel therapeutics to augment renal function in PAH.
CLINICAL PERSPECTIVE: What Is New?: Single-nucleus RNA sequencing and histological analyses defined nephron remodeling and increased leukocyte infiltration as key cellular features of the PAH kidney.Integrated transcriptomic and proteomic analyses nominated metabolic derangements including ferroptosis, cuproptosis, disrupted fatty acid and amino acid metabolism, and impaired TCA cycle activity as candidate drivers of PAH nephropathy. What Are the Clinical Implications?: Discrete cellular and metabolic mechanisms may contribute to PAH-CKM syndrome, which suggests renal dysfunction is not solely due to abnormal hemodynamics.Dysregulated programmed cell death pathways, immune signaling, amino acid metabolism, and kinase activity may be potentially druggable targets that could be engaged to counteract PAH-CKM.

**What Is New?**
- 在人体PAH尸检肾脏中首次定义了一种独立的细胞构筑改变：thick ascending limb、proximal tubule与免疫细胞核比例升高而collecting duct核耗竭，提示PAH肾病并非单纯血流动力学后果。
- 首次将proximal tubule与thick ascending limb的ferroptosis、cuproptosis及fatty acid oxidation通路上调与β-oxidation、TCA cycle、氨基酸代谢、transsulfuration、urea cycle的蛋白质组紊乱整合为同一机制节点，并预测GSK3β、STK与casein kinase活性升高。
- 通过AlphaFold 3/ChimeraX对磷酸化改变进行结构后果建模，把磷酸化蛋白质组信号推进到可解释的结构层面，并据此在人体多组学数据中提名可成药靶点。

**研究设计**
- 人体尸检肾脏横断面比较：PAH n=4 对 control n=5
- snRNA-seq 解析细胞类型特异转录改变与核比例变化
- mitochondrial、cytoplasmic、phosphoproteomics 三重蛋白质组分析
- AlphaFold 3/ChimeraX 对磷酸化改变做结构后果建模
- 组织学评估肾纤维化、肾小球结构、免疫浸润与nephron segment density
- 基于全部数据提名候选可成药靶点（无干预验证）

**主要发现**
- 4例PAH中3例存在肾功能受损，队列平均eGFR为61±36 mL/min/1.73m²。
- PAH肾脏呈thick ascending limb、proximal tubule与免疫细胞核增多、collecting duct核减少的细胞构筑重构。
- Proximal tubule与thick ascending limb细胞共同上调fatty acid oxidation、ferroptosis与cuproptosis通路；PAH淋巴细胞显示T-cell receptor signaling、NK细胞介导的细胞毒作用与Th17分化增强。
- 组织学见perivascular fibrosis增加、肾小球T细胞浸润以及glomerular basement membrane密度下降。
- 蛋白质组提示β-oxidation、TCA cycle、氨基酸代谢、transsulfuration与urea cycle广泛受损，磷酸化蛋白质组预测GSK3β、STK与casein kinase活性升高，并据此提名多个可成药靶点。

**为什么入选** 以人体PAH尸检肾脏为样本，将snRNA-seq与mitochondrial/cytoplasmic/phosphoproteomics及组织学整合，把PAH-CKM中的肾脏损伤从“血流动力学附属”重新定义为具有特定细胞状态、死亡通路与激酶信号的独立机制实体；虽无rescue实验，但靶点提名具备后续干预研究的可操作入口。

**局限**
- 样本量极小（PAH n=4，control n=5），且为尸检横断面组织，无法区分因果与伴随现象。
- 全部结论基于关联性多组学与结构预测，未在细胞或动物模型中做功能验证、敲低/回补或药物干预实验，提名靶点均未验证。
- 为bioRxiv预印本，同行评议状态未知；作者单位与基金信息未提供。

**可借鉴之处**
- 多组学叠层设计可借鉴：以snRNA-seq锁定细胞类型与状态，再以mitochondrial/cytoplasmic/phosphoproteomics在同一组织上补齐代谢与信号维度，最后用组织学做空间与结构印证，形成“细胞—通路—结构”闭环。
- 磷酸化蛋白质组结合AlphaFold 3/ChimeraX的结构预测思路，可用于从激酶活性推断（GSK3β、STK、casein kinase）走向靶点可成药性判断，适用于任何以激酶信号为核心的器官损伤研究。
- 免疫-实质/基质互作分析框架（glomerular T-cell infiltration、Th17 differentiation、NK-mediated cytotoxicity 与perivascular fibrosis并存）可直接迁移至肺血管重构与免疫-成纤维细胞crosstalk研究，用于检验免疫活化是否早于或驱动基质沉积。
