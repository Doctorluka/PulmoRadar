原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Prematurity affects approximately 10% of live births worldwide and contributes to substantial short and long-term morbidity, particularly respiratory disease. Approximately 35% of infants born before 32 weeks gestation are diagnosed with bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD). Many preterm infants regardless of BPD diagnosis also develop respiratory problems in both childhood and adulthood, more recently termed as prematurity-associated lung disease (PLD). As survival of preterm infants continues to improve, the lifetime burden of PLD and related health conditions remains high. Optimizing lung growth, minimizing the impact of comorbidities, and improving quality of life are ongoing challenges. Individuals born preterm are also at higher risk for being diagnosed with early-onset chronic obstructive pulmonary disease in adulthood. Despite these public health implications, outpatient management is often variable, and PLD remains underrecognized and heterogeneous across clinical settings. In this expert consensus statement from the BPD Collaborative, we summarize key knowledge gaps and provide pragmatic recommendations to help standardize outpatient care for PLD across the lifespan. Recommendations address models of care delivery, approaches to disease monitoring and management, and the evaluation and treatment of common comorbidities. Given limited high-quality evidence in several domains, many recommendations are necessarily based on expert opinion. Our objectives are to highlight priorities for future research, promote continuity between pediatric and adult care, and support development of evidence-based strategies to reduce underdiagnosis and undertreatment and to improve long-term outcomes for individuals born preterm.

  • 胎龄 <32 周出生的婴儿中约 35% 被诊断为 bronchopulmonary dysplasia (BPD)；但无论是否诊断 BPD，许多早产儿在儿童期和成年期仍会出现呼吸问题，即 PLD。

Distinct Morphological Types of Small Airway Obstructions in Smokers with Emphysema and End-Stage Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease.

杂志American journal of respiratory and critical care medicine
IF: 21.7 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2025 Dec
10.1164/rccm.202410-2101OC
作者Vincent Geudens · Ghislaine Gayan-Ramirez
机构Laboratory of Respiratory Diseases and Thoracic Surgery (BREATHE), Department of Chronic Diseases and Metabolism, Catholic University of Leuven, Leuven, Belgium.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 首次在充气整肺样本中以 tissue micro-CT 与 histopathology 交叉配准的方式，把吸烟者和终末期 COPD 的 terminal bronchiole 阻塞从笼统的「小气道阻塞」细分为 occlusion、web、collapse 三种可区分的形态学亚型，并证实其组织学本质为 mucus plugs。
  • 明确了阻塞亚型的构成比随疾病阶段演变：吸烟伴肺气肿者以 webs 和 occlusions 为主，而终末期 COPD 同时存在全部三种亚型，提示亚型谱本身即可作为疾病进展的结构标志。
  • 揭示同一肺内存在区室异质性并据此提出机制转变：无肺气肿区域 nonaerated TB 更多，而轻度肺气肿区域以结构破坏为主，说明「阻塞主导」向「破坏主导」的转变由肺气肿的存在而非单纯吸烟暴露所决定。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Rationale: The precise nature of small airway obstructions in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) remains poorly understood, especially at early disease stages. Objectives: This study aimed to characterize small airway obstructions and numbers up to the terminal bronchioles (TBs) in smokers with limited emphysema and end-stage COPD. We hypothesized that obstruction subtypes would differ in morphology, nature, and number from early to end-stage COPD. Methods: Whole lungs were inflated and processed from seven control donors (control: declined for extrapulmonary reasons); from eight donors with a history of smoking, of whom three had <5% emphysema (smokers with no emphysema) and five had >5% emphysema (smokers with emphysema); and from eight patients with end-stage COPD. Micro-computed tomography of tissue was used to assess number of TBs, aerated TBs, and number and type of obstructions and was cross-correlated with histopathology. Measurements and Main Results: Obstructions were mainly present in smokers with emphysema and patients with COPD, resulting in less aerated TBs. On the basis of emphysema extent, more nonaerated TBs were present in regions with no emphysema than in regions with mild emphysema; however, destruction was more prominent in mild emphysema. Multiple types of obstructions were identified, comprising occlusions, webs, and collapses. In smokers with emphysema, obstructions primarily comprised webs and occlusions, whereas all obstruction types were present in COPD. On histopathology, obstructions were identified as mucus plugs. Conclusions: Multiple types of obstruction characterized as mucus plugs were identified in smokers with emphysema and patients with end-stage COPD. Their morphology, nature, and number evolved from smokers with emphysema to end-stage COPD. A shift from obstruction-dominant dysfunction to destruction-dominant pathology was found in smokers on the basis of emphysema presence.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • 横断面人体组织形态学研究：充气整肺样本 + tissue micro-CT + 组织病理学交叉关联。
  • 三组对照：7 例 control donors、8 例有吸烟史者（3 例 <5% 肺气肿、5 例 >5% 肺气肿）、8 例 end-stage COPD。
  • 主要测量：TB 总数、aerated TB 数、阻塞数量与亚型分类，并按肺气肿范围做区域分层比较。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • 阻塞主要见于吸烟伴肺气肿者和 COPD 患者，并伴随可通气 terminal bronchiole 减少。
  • 按肺气肿范围分层：无肺气肿区域比轻度肺气肿区域存在更多 nonaerated TB，但结构破坏在轻度肺气肿区域更显著。
  • 鉴定出多种阻塞形态，包括 occlusions、webs 和 collapses；吸烟伴肺气肿者以 webs 与 occlusions 为主，终末期 COPD 则三种亚型并存。
  • 组织病理学上这些阻塞被确认为 mucus plugs。
  • 基于肺气肿存在与否，吸烟者呈现从 obstruction-dominant 功能障碍向 destruction-dominant 病理改变的转变。

AI 解读 · 为什么入选

在人体整肺样本中建立小气道阻塞的形态学分类学，并用 micro-CT 与组织学双重验证其本质为黏液栓，兼具结构与机制信息。；提出阻塞亚型谱随吸烟相关肺气肿到终末期 COPD 演变，以及「阻塞主导→破坏主导」的转变假说，为小气道疾病的阶段化研究提供可直接检验的框架。；发表于 AJRCCM，样本虽小但为稀缺的人体整肺多模态组织学数据。

AI 解读 · 局限

  • 仅有摘要可用，缺少阻塞定量、统计方法与随访信息，无法评估效应量与偏倚控制。
  • 样本量极小（control 7 例、smokers 8 例且进一步分为 3/5 两亚组、COPD 8 例），结论为描述性形态学观察，因果与阶段演变推断有待更大队列验证。
  • 所附 full text 片段实为另一篇 SPECT/CT 通气异质性研究（Frontiers in Physiology），与本篇不匹配，故未用于评估。

AI 解读 · 可借鉴之处

  • 整肺充气 + 组织 micro-CT 与 histopathology 逐级交叉验证的设计，可将影像上可计数的小气道病变与具体组织学实体（此处为 mucus plug）一一对应，适合移植到小气道重塑、肺泡-小气道交界区乃至肺血管重塑的定量形态学研究。
  • 把结局变量从「阻塞计数」升级为「形态学亚型分类 + 通气 TB 比例」，可为动物模型或人组织样本提供更分层的表型描述框架，避免把异质性病变合并为单一终点。
  • 以 terminal bronchiole 为计数单位、以 aerated TB 比例作为结构与功能的桥接指标，可用于评估任何以远端气道/肺泡损伤为核心的干预或基因修饰模型。

TLR7 promotes smoke-induced experimental lung damage through the activity of mast cell tryptase.

杂志Nature communications
IF: 18.1 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2023 Nov 14
10.4049/jimmunol.164.11.5549
作者Gang Liu* · Tatt Jhong Haw* · Malcolm R Starkey* · Philip M Hansbro
机构Centre for Inflammation, Centenary Institute, and Faculty of Science, University of Technology Sydney, Camperdown, New South Wales, Australia.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 首次通过TLR7缺陷小鼠和TLR7激动剂imiquimod吸入模型证明TLR7是烟雾诱导肺气肿和COPD的充分且必要的因果驱动因子，而不仅是既往认为的表达升高标志物。
  • 揭示TLR7下游由肥大细胞类胰蛋白酶（mMCP-6）介导：imiquimod诱导的肺气肿在mMCP-6缺陷小鼠或肥大细胞稳定剂cromolyn处理下减轻，闭合了TLR7→肥大细胞→类胰蛋白酶的机制链。
  • 抗TLR7单克隆抗体治疗可抑制烟雾诱导的肺气肿、实验性COPD及肺肥大细胞积聚，并在人COPD肺组织和既有数据集中验证TLR7+肥大细胞增多且与疾病严重度相关，提供了严格的在体验证和人类关联证据。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Toll-like receptor 7 (TLR7) is known for eliciting immunity against single-stranded RNA viruses, and is increased in both human and cigarette smoke (CS)-induced, experimental chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). Here we show that the severity of CS-induced emphysema and COPD is reduced in TLR7-deficient mice, while inhalation of imiquimod, a TLR7-agonist, induces emphysema without CS exposure. This imiquimod-induced emphysema is reduced in mice deficient in mast cell protease-6, or when wild-type mice are treated with the mast cell stabilizer, cromolyn. Furthermore, therapeutic treatment with anti-TLR7 monoclonal antibody suppresses CS-induced emphysema, experimental COPD and accumulation of pulmonary mast cells in mice. Lastly, TLR7 mRNA is increased in pre-existing datasets from patients with COPD, while TLR7+ mast cells are increased in COPD lungs and associated with severity of COPD. Our results thus support roles for TLR7 in mediating emphysema and COPD through mast cell activity, and may implicate TLR7 as a potential therapeutic target.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • 使用TLR7缺陷小鼠和野生型小鼠暴露于香烟烟雾（CS）建立实验性COPD/肺气肿模型。
  • 通过吸入TLR7激动剂imiquimod在不暴露CS的情况下诱导肺气肿。
  • 在mMCP-6缺陷小鼠或cromolyn处理的小鼠中评估imiquimod诱导的肺气肿。
  • 治疗性给予抗TLR7单克隆抗体，评估对CS诱导的肺气肿、COPD和肺肥大细胞积聚的影响。
  • 分析既有COPD患者数据集和COPD肺组织中的TLR7 mRNA及TLR7+肥大细胞。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • TLR7缺陷显著减轻CS诱导的肺气肿和COPD严重程度。
  • 单独吸入imiquimod即可在无CS暴露下诱导肺气肿。
  • imiquimod诱导的肺气肿在mMCP-6缺陷小鼠中减轻，或在WT小鼠经cromolyn处理后减轻。
  • 治疗性抗TLR7 mAb抑制CS诱导的肺气肿、实验性COPD和肺肥大细胞积聚。
  • COPD患者数据集中TLR7 mRNA升高，COPD肺中TLR7+肥大细胞增多且与疾病严重度相关。

AI 解读 · 为什么入选

该研究通过基因敲除、激动剂诱导、下游效应分子缺陷、药理稳定剂和抗体阻断多条证据链，建立了TLR7→肥大细胞类胰蛋白酶在烟雾诱导肺气肿中的因果机制，并辅以人类COPD数据集和肺组织验证，发表于Nature Communications。

AI 解读 · 局限

  • 仅获取到摘要，全文未提供，具体信号通路、肥大细胞亚群及细胞来源细节不详。
  • 提供的全文片段来自另一篇无关论文（H. influenzae与中性粒细胞性哮喘），不能用于评估本文方法学。

AI 解读 · 可借鉴之处

  • 可借鉴TLR7激动剂（imiquimod）吸入联合基因缺陷小鼠（TLR7-/-、mMCP-6-/-）和药理学阻断（cromolyn、抗TLR7 mAb）的rescue策略，用于解析其他固有免疫受体在肺重塑中的作用。
  • TLR7+肥大细胞作为连接固有免疫与结构性肺损伤的节点，提示在肺泡生物学、免疫-基质细胞crosstalk或肺血管重塑研究中可关注肥大细胞蛋白酶对基质细胞和内皮细胞的影响。
  • 利用既有COPD转录组数据集和患者肺组织进行受体/细胞类型关联分析，可作为机制发现后向人类转化的验证路径。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Pulmonary lymphatic vessels are essential for interstitial fluid balance and macromolecular clearance, yet the endothelial mechanisms regulating lymphatic vessel function during chronic fibroproliferative lung injury remain undefined. Here, we demonstrate that the lineage-defining transcription factor ERG in lymphatic endothelial cells (LECs) governs junctional plasticity and fluid drainage in pulmonary fibrosis. In human idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) lungs, lymphatic ERG expression is markedly downregulated. Lineage-specific inducible deletion of Erg in murine LECs (Erg-CKO) unexpectedly conferred robust protection against bleomycin-induced pulmonary fibrosis, significantly dampening acute inflammation, reducing edema, and preserving pulmonary compliance. Mechanistically, loss of ERG enhanced lymphatic drainage capacity in vivo. Transcriptomic profiling of isolated LECs revealed that ERG deficiency activates actomyosin contractile pathways and selectively upregulates Endothelin Receptor Type B ( Ednrb ), driving junctional remodeling from continuous zippers into discontinuous button-like configurations that facilitate interstitial fluid entry. Pharmacological blockade of EDNRB with BQ-788 abolished junctional reorganization, eliminated the enhanced lymphatic drainage, and completely reversed the anti-fibrotic protection in Erg-CKO mice. Together, our findings identify an endothelial ERG-EDNRB regulatory axis that controls lymphatic junctional architecture and demonstrate that promoting lymphatic clearance via endothelial junctional remodeling represents a viable therapeutic strategy for fibrotic vascular remodeling.

Clade B serpins promote direct basal-to-goblet cell differentiation in airways of patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Goblet cell hyperplasia (GCH) is a hallmark of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) and contributes to morbidity and mortality. We investigated the cellular and molecular origins of GCH in COPD using single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq), spatial transcriptomics, and in vitro models. We identified "basal-to-goblet transitional cells" (BGTC) which are transcriptionally and physically located between basal and goblet cells and are characterized by expression of clade B serpins. In vitro studies indicate that inflammatory cytokines, including IL-1β, stimulate basal-to-goblet differentiation through a SERPINB3/4 + intermediate and SERPINB3 overexpression induces differentiation of airway basal cells into IL1B -producing inflammatory goblet cells. In addition, we identified a subset of SERPINB4 + "goblet-variant basal cells" from lungs of COPD patients that express goblet cell and inflammatory genes ex vivo. Together, these results indicate clade B serpins contribute to altered epithelial differentiation and inflammatory signaling in COPD and support therapeutic targeting of clade B serpins to reduce GCH.
