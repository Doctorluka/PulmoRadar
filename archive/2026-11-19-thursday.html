Cocrystals for sequestering and separating large PAHs.

杂志Science advances
IF: 11.7 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2026 Nov 13
10.1126/sciadv.aei5388
作者María G Vasquez-Ríos · Leonard R MacGillivray
机构Département de Chimie, Université de Sherbrooke; Sherbrooke, Québec, Canada.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 作者把由硼酸和线性烯烃衍生的boroxine organic assemblies（BOAs）作为first-generation constitutional dynamic library（CDL），用于以共晶形式包结和分离大型PAHs，拓展了BOA从静态组装体到动态主体库的用途。
  • 揭示PAH的电子性质、形状和尺寸可诱导BOAs在host-guest组装中的构型与构象异构，提出‘shrink-wrap’过程并证明PAH在CDL组装中起模板作用。
  • CDL实现至多三种不同PAHs的分离，并得到电子性质可调、且可在同一固相内让两种不同BOAs的烯烃发生[2+2] photocycloaddition的共晶。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Highly adaptive boroxine organic assemblies (BOAs) derived from a boronic acid and a linear alkene function as a first-generation constitutional dynamic library (CDL) to sequester and separate large polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) in form of cocrystals. Configurational and conformational isomerism of the organoboroxines in host-guest assemblies is induced by electronic properties, shape and size of the PAHs through a "shrink-wrap" process/mechanism, which reveals a templating role of the PAH during the CDL assembly. The CDL was applied to separate up to three different PAHs. The cocrystals exhibit tunable electronic properties. The CDL afforded a cocrystal wherein alkenes of two different BOAs undergo [2 + 2] photocycloaddition in the same solid.

  • 比较PAH电子性质、形状和尺寸对‘shrink-wrap’组装的诱导作用。
  • 测试对多种PAHs的分离能力、共晶电子性质及固态[2+2] photocycloaddition。

  • ‘shrink-wrap’机制将PAH的电子性质、形状与尺寸同主体异构化联系起来。
  • 该CDL可分离至多三种不同PAHs。
  • 共晶的电子性质可调。
  • 在同一共晶固相中，两种不同BOAs的烯烃可发生[2+2] photocycloaddition。

02 · 三年内旗舰系统研究 仅摘要

CCR2-Dependent Myeloid Cell Recruitment Links Post-MI Heart Failure to Pulmonary Hypertension.

杂志Circulation research
IF: 18.0 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2026 Oct 26
10.1161/CIRCRESAHA.126.329553
作者Jonathan L Gillan · Jana Grune
机构Department of Cardiothoracic and Vascular Surgery, Deutsches Herzzentrum der Charité (DHZC), Berlin (J.L.G., M.M.K., A.W., T.C.F.-H., N.H., C.K., J.G.).

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 首次以一个可干预的因果节点串联起“MI→HF→PH-LHD”：CCR2 依赖的单核细胞募集被证明是连接左心疾病与肺血管病变的必需环节，而不仅是伴随现象。
  • 新定义了干预时间窗的概念——在 MI 后第 10 天（HFrEF 已确立）才开始给予 CCR2 拮抗剂 RS102895，仍可减轻肺血管重塑与右心室压力/肥厚，从而把肺部表型与左心功能本身解耦。
  • 在人类 PH-LHD 与 PH-LHD 大鼠肺组织中都验证到 VCAM1、SELE 等炎症黏附分子表达升高，为“血管炎症激活—髓系募集”轴提供了跨物种的人体组织层面佐证。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

BACKGROUND: Pulmonary hypertension due to left heart disease (PH-LHD) is a common complication of myocardial infarction (MI) and heart failure (HF). Myeloid inflammation is a major hallmark of both the local cardiac and systemic post-MI response, with resident and recruited monocyte-derived macrophages orchestrating each step of the ensuing inflammatory cascade, from clearance of dead cardiomyocytes to scar tissue formation. Similarly, myeloid inflammation is a key feature of the PH lung, driving remodeling of small pulmonary vessels. We thus hypothesized that MI-induced systemic myeloid inflammation contributes directly to the development of PH-LHD.

METHODS: We assessed expression of inflammatory adhesion molecules, as markers of vascular inflammatory activation and myeloid cell recruitment pathways, in patients with pulmonary arterial hypertension, PH-LHD, as well as in a PH-LHD rat model. We then induced MI and subsequent HF in 8- to 10-week-old female C57BL/6J mice via permanent ligation of the left anterior descending coronary artery. HF mice were then treated with a CCR2 (C-C chemokine receptor 2) antagonist to block monocyte recruitment and the development of PH-LHD was assessed at 6 weeks post-MI by a combination of invasive hemodynamics, flow cytometry, echocardiography, and lung histology.

RESULTS: Expression of key inflammatory adhesion molecules, including VCAM1 and SELE, was increased in PH-LHD lungs from humans and rats. Following early post-MI development of HF with reduced ejection fraction, mice exhibited increased numbers of circulating monocytes, lung monocytes, and lung interstitial macrophages, as well as PH-LHD by 6 weeks, characterized by right ventricular hypertrophy, elevated right ventricular pressures, and lung vascular remodeling of small-to-medium-sized pulmonary vessels. Administration of the selective CCR2 antagonist RS102895 from day 10 post-MI to reduce monocyte tissue recruitment attenuated lung vascular remodeling, right ventricular systolic and diastolic pressures, right ventricular hypertrophy, and lung adhesion molecule expression at 6 weeks, compared with vehicle-treated controls, without improving the underlying left-sided HF.

CONCLUSIONS: This proof-of-concept study demonstrates a causal role for CCR2-dependent myeloid cell recruitment in the progression from MI-induced HF to secondary pulmonary vascular disease.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • 人类 PH-LHD 与 PAH 肺组织 + PH-LHD 大鼠模型：检测炎症黏附分子（VCAM1、SELE）表达。
  • 8–10 周龄雌性 C57BL/6J 小鼠永久结扎 LAD 诱导 MI 与 HFrEF。
  • MI 后第 10 天起给予选择性 CCR2 拮抗剂 RS102895，对照组给 vehicle，观察至 MI 后 6 周。
  • 终点组合：有创血流动力学、flow cytometry、echocardiography、肺组织学（肺小—中等血管重塑）。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • 人类及大鼠 PH-LHD 肺组织中 VCAM1、SELE 等关键炎症黏附分子表达升高，提示肺血管炎症激活与髓系募集通路被打开。
  • 小鼠 MI 后早期出现 HFrEF，至 6 周时循环单核细胞、肺单核细胞及肺 interstitial macrophages 数量增多，并出现 RV 肥厚、右心室压升高与肺小—中等血管重塑等 PH-LHD 表型。
  • 自 MI 后第 10 天起用 RS102895 阻断 CCR2，可减轻肺血管重塑、降低右心室收缩压与舒张压、减轻 RV 肥厚，并降低肺组织黏附分子表达。
  • 上述获益并未伴随左侧 HF 的改善，说明该干预作用于“HF→肺血管病变”这一下游环节，而非通过逆转左心功能实现。

AI 解读 · 为什么入选

机制链条完整且因果性强（CCR2 依赖髓系募集为节点，药理学阻断可 rescue 肺血管与右心表型），并配有 human PH-LHD 组织与大鼠模型的跨物种证据；采用延迟给药设计实现“肺血管表型—左心功能”解耦，对肺血管重塑与免疫—血管 crosstalk 研究具直接方法学参考价值，期刊为 Circulation Research。

  • 仅获摘要，无 OA 全文；实验细节（如 CCR2 拮抗剂剂量/给药途径、样本量、统计学与盲法、单核细胞亚群定义、是否做 CCR2 或髓系细胞特异性遗传学验证）不详，故仅能按摘要层面评价。
  • 概念验证性研究：单一模型（雌性小鼠永久 LAD 结扎）、单一药理学工具（RS102895），“CCR2 依赖性”缺少细胞特异性 lineage tracing/conditional deletion 的直接证据，CCR2 下游效应机制未阐明。

  • “先造左心疾病、再在第 10 天起给药”的延迟干预设计，可用于区分某靶点是作用于肺血管重塑本身，还是仅仅继发于左心功能改善；评价时需并行左心与右心/肺血管两套终点（invasive hemodynamics + echocardiography + 肺组织学）。
  • 用 flow cytometry 同步刻画循环单核细胞、肺单核细胞与肺 interstitial macrophages 的三层次髓系区室，是研究 immune–stromal/血管 crosstalk 时可复用的分层读数逻辑；若延伸到成纤维细胞或肺泡生物学，可加做肺成纤维细胞活化与肺泡屏障指标的配对检测。
  • 以黏附分子（VCAM1、SELE）作为“血管炎症激活状态”的可测替代读数，便于在 human 组织与动物模型间做 translational 对齐，也便于评估其他抗炎/抗募集策略是否真正触及肺血管内皮激活环节。

CD146-HIF-1α hypoxic reprogramming drives vascular remodeling and pulmonary arterial hypertension.

杂志Nature communications
IF: 18.1 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2019 Aug 07
10.1016/j.ddmod.2011.04.001
作者Yongting Luo# · Xiyun Yan#
机构Beijing Advanced Innovation Center for Food Nutrition and Human Health, China Agricultural University, Yuanmingyuan West Road 2, 100193, Beijing, China. luo_yongting@163.com.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 将 CD146-HIF-1α 的相互强化定义为肺血管重塑和 PAH 的关键决定节点，而非仅作为缺氧下游的单基因变化。
  • 在 SMC 中特异性敲除 Cd146 可打断该正反馈并减轻慢性缺氧小鼠的肺血管重塑，提供了细胞类型特异的体内因果证据。
  • 抗 CD146 抗体能在两种啮齿类模型中缓解已建立的 PH 并改善心功能，把该轴从机制节点推进到可干预的 anti-remodeling 靶点。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) is a vascular remodeling disease of cardiopulmonary units. No cure is currently available due to an incomplete understanding of vascular remodeling. Here we identify CD146-hypoxia-inducible transcription factor 1 alpha (HIF-1α) cross-regulation as a key determinant in vascular remodeling and PAH pathogenesis. CD146 is markedly upregulated in pulmonary artery smooth muscle cells (PASMCs/SMCs) and in proportion to disease severity. CD146 expression and HIF-1α transcriptional program reinforce each other to physiologically enable PASMCs to adopt a more synthetic phenotype. Disruption of CD146-HIF-1α cross-talk by genetic ablation of Cd146 in SMCs mitigates pulmonary vascular remodeling in chronic hypoxic mice. Strikingly, targeting of this axis with anti-CD146 antibodies alleviates established pulmonary hypertension (PH) and enhances cardiac function in two rodent models. This study provides mechanistic insights into hypoxic reprogramming that permits vascular remodeling, and thus provides proof of concept for anti-remodeling therapy for PAH through direct modulation of CD146-HIF-1α cross-regulation.

  • 机制假说：CD146 与 HIF-1α 转录程序相互强化，推动 PASMC 合成表型。
  • 体内因果：SMC 特异性 Cd146 基因敲除，在慢性缺氧小鼠中评估肺血管重塑。
  • 治疗验证：anti-CD146 抗体干预两种啮齿类 PH 模型，评估已建立 PH 与心功能。
  • 表达关联：CD146 在 PASMCs 中上调并与疾病严重程度成比例（摘要层面）。

Mucosal vaccination with hemagglutinin stalk provides broad protection against influenza virus subtypes in mice.

杂志The Journal of clinical investigation
IF: 13.3 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2026 Nov 16
10.1038/s41598-018-26538-7
作者Weizhong Li · Xiaoping Zhu
机构Division of Immunology, Virginia-Maryland College of Veterinary Medicine, University of Maryland, College Park, Maryland, USA.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 本研究构建了融合单体 IgG Fc 的 prefusion 修饰 HA Stalk 抗原，将保守 stalk 抗原与 FcRn 介导的上皮转运结合起来，形成不同于传统株特异性疫苗的鼻内广谱疫苗设计。
  • 研究把 FcRn 跨呼吸道上皮转运与黏膜疫苗免疫原性连接起来，提出 Stalk-Fc 可借助 FcRn 穿过呼吸道上皮屏障并驱动呼吸道局部与系统性抗体应答。
  • 在活体小鼠中，鼻内 Stalk-Fc 免疫对 H1N1、H5N1、H6N2 和 H9N2 多亚型感染提供保护，并伴随鼻腔/BAL/血中持久 IgA、IgG、肺组织 resident memory T cells 及骨髓 long-lived plasma cells，构成较严格的体内广谱保护验证。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Influenza viruses continually evolve through antigenic drift and shift, spreading via respiratory droplets and aerosols. Developing a universal nasal vaccine is essential for preventing seasonal and pandemic infections by inducing long-lasting immunity in the respiratory tract. The neonatal Fc receptor (FcRn) transfers IgG Abs across the epithelial barrier. This study investigates whether the i.n. delivery of the conserved Stalk antigen of HA, utilizing the FcRn, can stimulate protective immune responses against various influenza virus subtypes. We engineered a monomeric IgG Fc fused to a modified HA Stalk in its prefusion form. Mice i.n. immunized with Stalk-Fc exhibited significant protection against infections from H1N1, H5N1, H6N2, and H9N2 compared with groups of control mice. The immunized mice produced long-lasting HA Stalk-specific IgA and IgG Abs in nasal washes, bronchoalveolar lavage, and blood, along with tissue-resident memory T cells in the lungs and long-lived plasma cells in the bone marrow. This led to notably lower viral replication in the nasal turbinate and lungs. The Stalk-specific Abs demonstrated strong cross-reactivity against different influenza subtypes through neutralization, Ab- or complement-dependent cellular cytotoxicity, or phagocytosis. These results indicate that FcRn efficiently transports HA Stalk-Fc across the respiratory epithelial barrier, supporting the development of a universal influenza nasal vaccine.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • 构建单体 IgG Fc 与 prefusion 修饰 HA Stalk 融合的 Stalk-Fc 抗原。
  • 小鼠鼻内免疫，并以 H1N1、H5N1、H6N2、H9N2 进行感染挑战。

Evidence from three taxonomically distinct species for a non-AhR mechanism of developmental neurotoxicity of an environmentally derived mixture of polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons.

杂志bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology
文章2026 Aug 18
10.1016/j.cotox.2023.100392
作者Samantha E Phelps · Nishad Jayasundara
机构Nicholas School of the Environment, Duke University, Durham, North Carolina, USA.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 首次以同一环境沉积物提取的复杂PAH混合物REPSE（而非单一化合物或简单混合物）在三种进化上不同的物种中平行解析发育神经毒性机制，把混合物毒理从“成分描述”推进到“跨物种机制比较”。
  • 利用对CYP1A诱导具有天然耐受性的Republic Creosoting killifish种群，以及PAH不激活AhR的线虫作为正交体系，把PAH神经毒性从经典AhR激活机制中部分“解绑”，提出保守的非AhR致病机制。
  • 在亚致畸、仍可诱导CYP1A的浓度下即观察到神经行为改变与多巴胺能神经元损伤，并在线虫中把机制指向神经元氧化还原状态（roGFP）与能量可用性（ATP:ADP比值）这一新的候选节点。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Typical environmental exposures to the toxic class of chemicals known as polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) involve complex mixtures; however, relatively few mechanistic toxicity studies have evaluated them as environmental mixtures, instead focusing on individual compounds or simple mixtures. In this study, we first derived Republic Sediment Extract (REPSE), a complex PAH mixture extracted from sediment at the Republic Creosoting site of the Elizabeth River in Norfolk, Virginia. After characterizing the PAH contents of REPSE, we evaluated its mechanisms of developmental neurotoxicity in three evolutionarily distinct taxa, leveraging the unique strengths of Atlantic killifish, zebrafish, and Caenorhabditis elegans as model species, with a focus on the Aryl hydrocarbon Receptor (AhR) pathway. Embryonic REPSE exposure caused induction of CYP1A in both fish species at sub-teratogenic concentrations, consistent with activation of the canonical AhR pathway. These sub-teratogenic exposures nevertheless induced neurotoxicity across both fish species, altering neurobehavioral phenotypes in fish, and induced dopaminergic neuronal damage in worms, again at non-teratogenic concentrations. To determine whether these effects were linked to canonical AhR response pathways, we examined killifish offspring from the pollution-adapted Republic Creosoting population, which exhibited characteristic recalcitrance to CYP1A induction, but remained susceptible to the neurobehavioral effects of REPSE. The induction of neuronal damage in worms provides orthogonal evidence for a non-AhR mechanism, because C. elegans AhR is not transcriptionally activated by PAHs as in vertebrates. Further probing of potential mechanisms underlying REPSE-induced neurotoxicity in worms revealed altered neuronal redox status (roGFP) and energy availability (ATP:ADP ratio). Collectively, our multispecies approach reveals conserved mechanisms of PAH mixture neurotoxicity, including effects that extend beyond canonical AhR signaling.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • 从Virginia Elizabeth River的Republic Creosoting站点沉积物制备并化学表征复杂PAH混合物REPSE。
  • 在killifish、zebrafish与C. elegans三个进化上不同的物种中进行胚胎期REPSE暴露。
  • 以CYP1A诱导作为经典AhR通路激活读数，并据此匹配亚致畸暴露浓度。
  • 用对CYP1A诱导耐受的污染适应killifish种群与PAH不激活AhR的线虫，进行通路依赖性拆解。
  • 读出包括鱼类神经行为表型、线虫多巴胺能神经元损伤、roGFP氧化还原状态与ATP:ADP比值。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • REPSE在亚致畸浓度下即可诱导两种鱼胚胎的CYP1A表达，符合经典AhR通路激活。
  • 在同一亚致畸浓度下，两种鱼出现神经行为表型改变，线虫出现多巴胺能神经元损伤。
  • 来自Republic Creosoting污染适应种群的killifish子代虽对CYP1A诱导耐受，却仍对REPSE的神经行为效应易感。
  • 线虫AhR不被PAH以脊椎动物方式转录激活，其神经元损伤构成独立于经典AhR机制的正交证据。
  • 线虫中REPSE暴露伴随神经元氧化还原状态改变（roGFP）与ATP:ADP比值变化，提示能量/氧化还原失衡参与其中。

AI 解读 · 为什么入选

以真实环境复杂混合物为暴露源，在三个分类学上相距远的物种中做平行验证，并用天然低应答种群与无经典AhR激活的线虫完成“受体依赖 vs 非依赖”的机制拆解，机制链从混合物表征延伸到行为与神经元表型；属bioRxiv预印本，非肺脏研究，但AhR/PAH外源物应答与跨物种验证逻辑具跨领域参考价值。

AI 解读 · 可借鉴之处

  • “污染适应种群”可视为天然的低应答/功能对照，用于拆解受体依赖与非依赖效应，比单纯药理抑制更贴近真实生理与进化背景，适合迁移到其他受体通路的因果拆解。
  • 三物种平行、以亚致畸浓度为窗口、以行为与特定神经元群形态为读出的设计，可作为复杂环境混合物机制研究的验证框架；跨物种一致性可显著提高因果推断强度。
  • 线虫roGFP氧化还原与ATP:ADP在体读出，提供了“神经元代谢-氧化应激”层面的功能表型，可移植到线粒体应激或外源物暴露的神经/细胞损伤研究。
  • 对肺脏与外源物应答研究而言，本研究的提示是间接的：在把PAH暴露效应归因于AhR之前，需设置受体非依赖的对照与读出。
