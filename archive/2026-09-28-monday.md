### 1. One-year progression of CT emphysema subtypes predict lung function decline: SPIROMICS.

American journal of respiratory and critical care medicine | 2026 Sep 25 | Emilia A Hermann, R Graham Barr
IF 2025：21.7 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Medicine, Columbia University Irving Medical Center.
- 领域：Department of Medicine, Columbia University Irving Medical Center；据隶属与摘要推测从事 COPD 影像流行病学与定量 CT 表型研究。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42788667/
- DOI：10.1093/ajrccm/aamag497

**Abstract**

RATIONALE: Intermediate endpoints that predict lung function decline are needed to improve the efficiency of clinical trials in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD).
OBJECTIVES: To test if one-year progression of quantitative computed tomography (QCT) measures predicts subsequent FEV₁ decline.
METHODS: SPIROMICS recruited individuals with ≥ 20 pack-years smoking history with or without COPD and nonsmoking controls who underwent four annual and a later fifth visit. We analyzed chest CT scans at visits one and two for machine-learned CT emphysema subtypes (combined bronchitic-apical emphysema [CBaE], diffuse emphysema) and standard QCT measures of emphysema (percent emphysema-950HU, 15th percentile HU, volume-adjusted lung density), airways, functional small airways, hyperinflation, parenchymal texture and vasculature. Linear mixed-effects models evaluated associations between one-year QCT measure changes and FEV₁ decline from visits two through five, adjusting for demographics, site, enrollment group, baseline QCT value, and time-varying anthropometrics and smoking.
MEASUREMENTS AND MAIN RESULTS: Among 880 participants (43% mild/moderate COPD, 17% severe COPD, 29% smoking controls, 11% nonsmoking controls), FEV₁ declined -30.4mL/year (95% CI: -34.5, -26.3). A standard deviation (SD) one-year increase in CBaE and diffuse emphysema predicted an accelerated decline in FEV1 of - 4.2 mL/year (95% CI: -8.3, -0.2) and -6.2 mL/year (95% CI: -10.7, -1.8), respectively. Changes in standard QCT emphysema measures also predicted change in FEV1 decline, but only CBaE and diffuse emphysema remained independently predictive of accelerated FEV1 decline when all were considered together.
CONCLUSIONS: One-year progression of CT emphysema subtypes predicted accelerated FEV₁ decline, supporting their potential use as intermediate endpoints in COPD trials.

**What Is New?**
- 首次把「1 年内的 CT 变化量」而非单一时点 CT 表型作为预测因子，证明 CBaE 与 diffuse emphysema 的 1 年进展可前瞻性预测 visits 2–5 的 FEV₁ 加速下降（每 SD 分别 -4.2 与 -6.2 mL/year）。
- 在同时纳入 percent emphysema-950HU、15th percentile HU、volume-adjusted lung density、airways、functional small airways、hyperinflation、parenchymal texture 与 vasculature 等标准 QCT 指标后，只有两种 machine-learned emphysema subtype 仍独立预测 FEV₁ 下降，说明亚型分层携带了传统密度指标之外的独立信息。
- 在一个设计严格的多中心纵向队列（880 人，含 mild/moderate COPD 43%、severe COPD 17%、smoking controls 29%、nonsmoking controls 11%，四次年度访视加第五次访视）中，以线性混合效应模型校正基线 QCT 值、时变人体测量与吸烟后仍成立，为中间终点提供了人群级验证。

**研究设计**
- SPIROMICS 多中心前瞻性队列，入组 ≥20 pack-years 吸烟者（伴或不伴 COPD）及非吸烟对照
- visits 1 与 2 的胸部 CT 用于 machine-learned CT emphysema subtypes（CBaE、diffuse emphysema）及标准 QCT 指标
- 结局为 visits 2 至 5 的 FEV₁ 下降斜率，采用 linear mixed-effects models
- 校正人口学、中心、入组组、基线 QCT 值、时变人体测量与吸烟

**主要发现**
- 880 名参与者总体 FEV₁ 下降速率为 -30.4 mL/year（95% CI -34.5, -26.3）。
- CBaE 每增加 1 SD（1 年变化）对应 FEV₁ 额外下降 -4.2 mL/year（95% CI -8.3, -0.2）；diffuse emphysema 每增加 1 SD 对应 -6.2 mL/year（95% CI -10.7, -1.8）。
- 标准 QCT emphysema 指标（percent emphysema-950HU、15th percentile HU、volume-adjusted lung density）的 1 年变化同样与 FEV₁ 下降相关。
- 当所有 QCT 指标同时进入模型时，仅 CBaE 与 diffuse emphysema 仍独立预测 FEV₁ 加速下降。
- 结论认为 1 年 CT emphysema subtype 进展具备作为 COPD 临床试验中间终点的潜力。

**为什么入选** 以大型多中心纵向队列（SPIROMICS）和 machine-learned 影像亚型，回答「何种中间终点能预测肺功能下降」这一试验设计问题；方法学上把纵向变化量与多指标竞争性调整结合，并在 AJRCCM 发表，对肺结构-功能轨迹研究有直接参考价值。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要，无 OA 全文可核对；模型具体设定、敏感性分析、缺失数据处理与亚型分割的可重复性均不详。
- 观察性关联，不能推断 CT 亚型进展与 FEV₁ 下降之间的因果关系或共同上游机制。
- 摘要未报告非 FEV₁ 终点（如 FVC、生活质量、急性加重）以及亚型分类在不同 CT 协议/人群中的泛化性。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可借鉴「delta 表型」思路：把纵向影像或组学指标的 1 年变化量而非基线值作为暴露，用于预测后续功能/结构轨迹，这一设计可迁移到肺泡或肺血管重塑的纵向队列分析。
- 多指标互相调整（mutual adjustment / 竞争性预测）的逻辑值得复用：把临床常规指标与新分层指标放在同一模型，检验新指标是否提供独立信息，可避免把共线性的替代指标误判为独立机制节点。
- machine-learned CT subtype 可作为人群表型锚点，与 fibroblast、immune-stromal crosstalk 或 alveolar 生物学样本（如 BAL、 explant）对接，检验特定细胞状态是否富集于 CBaE 或 diffuse emphysema 轨迹；本研究未涉及任何细胞或分子层面实验。

### 2. Alveolar macrophages inhibit emphysematous pathology via expression of carbonic anhydrase 4.

Cell reports | 2026 May 26 | John J Ponessa, Mark C Siracusa
IF 2025：7.5 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：年内旗舰系统研究
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Center for Immunity and Inflammation, Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey, Newark, NJ, USA; Department of Medicine, Rutgers, The State University of New Jersey, Newark, NJ, USA; NemaGen Discoveries, Princeton, NJ, USA.
- 领域：肺部免疫与肺泡巨噬细胞生物学，聚焦 COPD/emphysema 中 Car4 的保护机制。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42085183/
- DOI：10.1016/j.celrep.2026.117315

**Abstract**

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) including emphysema is the fourth leading cause of death worldwide. Therapies to treat COPD remain limited and fail to prevent disease progression. Alveolar macrophages (AMs) reside in the alveoli of the lung and are ideally positioned to encounter inhaled particles and pathogens. Despite exposure to these stimuli, AMs exhibit specialized phenotypes that restrict inflammation to prevent lung damage. Here, we demonstrate that AMs express high levels of the surface-bound enzyme carbonic anhydrase 4 (Car4). Deletion of Car4 on AMs results in increased susceptibility to emphysematous pathology, and therapeutic treatment with Car4 is sufficient to prevent emphysema-like disease. Consistent with murine studies, reduced levels of human Car4 (CA4) distinguish COPD patients with emphysema from those without. Mechanistically, murine and human Car4 directly inhibit neutrophil elastase, which promotes destruction of alveolar walls and drives emphysema. Collectively, these studies reveal the existence of a lung-specific elastase inhibitor that protects the elastin-containing walls of the alveoli and provides important therapeutic insight into a disease that affects 300 million individuals globally.

**What Is New?**
- 首次发现肺泡巨噬细胞高表达表面酶 Car4，并证明其作为肺特异性 elastase 抑制剂的保护性功能，而非仅限制炎症。
- 在 AM 上缺失 Car4 足以增加 emphysema 易感性，而外源 Car4 治疗可预防 emphysema 样疾病，确立了 Car4 为因果性保护节点。
- 机制上直接证明 murine 和 human Car4 抑制 neutrophil elastase，且 human COPD 伴 emphysema 者 CA4 水平降低，提供了跨物种验证。

**研究设计**
- 基于小鼠模型的 AM 上 Car4 缺失
- 外源 Car4 治疗性干预
- human COPD 患者样本中 CA4 水平比较
- murine 和 human Car4 对 neutrophil elastase 的直接抑制实验

**主要发现**
- 肺泡巨噬细胞（AMs）高表达表面 Car4。
- AM 上 Car4 缺失导致小鼠对 emphysematous pathology 易感性增加。
- Car4 治疗足以预防 emphysema 样疾病。
- human COPD 伴 emphysema 患者 CA4 水平降低，可区分有无 emphysema。
- Murine 和 human Car4 直接抑制 neutrophil elastase，后者促进肺泡壁破坏。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整：AM 表面 Car4 → 抑制 neutrophil elastase → 保护含 elastin 的肺泡壁；具备基因缺失、治疗性 rescue 和 human 样本验证，且发表于 Cell Reports。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无 OA 全文，无法评估具体实验细节、样本量和统计方法。
- human 数据为关联性，CA4 降低与 emphysema 的因果关系尚不明确。
- Car4 治疗的具体递送方式、剂量和长期安全性未知。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可借鉴 AM 特异性基因缺失结合治疗性回补（如外源 Car4）的策略，验证肺泡保护因子的因果作用，类似设计可用于 fibroblast/immune-stromal crosstalk 研究。
- 利用 neutrophil elastase 活性检测和 elastin 降解模型，可解析 Car4 与蛋白酶-抗蛋白酶平衡在肺气肿中的作用，并延伸至肺泡壁重塑和 pulmonary vascular remodeling 情境。
- 在 human COPD 样本中按 emphysema 表型分层检测 CA4，结合 scRNA-seq 识别 Car4-high AM 状态，可为肺泡免疫-基质交互研究提供可转化切入点。

### 3. Basophils trigger emphysema development in a murine model of COPD through IL-4-mediated generation of MMP-12-producing macrophages.

Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences of the United States of America | 2018 Dec 18 | Sho Shibata, Hajime Karasuyama
IF 2025：9.4 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：领域经典
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Immune Regulation, Graduate School of Medical and Dental Sciences, Tokyo Medical and Dental University (TMDU), Tokyo 113-8510, Japan.; Department of Respiratory Medicine, Graduate School of Medical and Dental Sciences, Tokyo Medical and Dental University (TMDU), Tokyo 113-8510, Japan.
- 领域：第一作者主要研究领域为呼吸病学与免疫调控交叉，聚焦 COPD/肺气肿中的免疫细胞机制（据 Tokyo Medical and Dental University 免疫调控与呼吸内科 affiliation 及摘要推断）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30510003/
- DOI：10.1073/pnas.1813927115

**Abstract**

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) is a leading cause of morbidity and mortality worldwide. It has generally been considered a non-Th2-type lung disorder, characterized by progressive airflow limitation with inflammation and emphysema, but its cellular and molecular mechanism remains ill defined, compared with that of asthma characterized by reversible airway obstruction. Here we show a previously unappreciated role for basophils at the initiation phase of emphysema formation in an elastase-induced murine model of COPD in that basophils represent less than 1% of lung-infiltrating cells. Intranasal elastase instillation elicited the recruitment of monocytes to the lung, followed by differentiation into interstitial macrophages (IMs) but rarely alveolar macrophages (AMs). Matrix metalloproteinase-12 (MMP-12) contributing to emphysema formation was highly expressed by IMs rather than AMs, in contrast to the prevailing assumption. Experiments using a series of genetically engineered mice suggested that basophil-derived IL-4, a Th2 cytokine, acted on lung-infiltrating monocytes to promote their differentiation into MMP-12-producing IMs that resulted in the destruction of alveolar walls and led to emphysema development. Indeed, mice deficient for IL-4 only in basophils failed to generate pathogenic MMP-12-producing IMs and hence develop emphysema. Thus, the basophil-derived IL-4/monocyte-derived IM/MMP-12 axis plays a crucial role in emphysema formation and therefore may be a potential target to slow down emphysema progression at the initiation phase of COPD.

**What Is New?**
- 在 elastase-induced murine COPD 模型中，将不足1%肺浸润细胞的 basophils 认定为 emphysema 启动阶段的关键细胞，改变了既往将 COPD 视为非 Th2 型、主要由中性粒细胞/巨噬细胞驱动的认识。
- 明确 basophil-derived IL-4 作用于肺浸润单核细胞，促使其分化为 MMP-12-producing interstitial macrophages（IMs），形成 basophil→IL-4→monocyte→IM→MMP-12→alveolar wall destruction 的因果机制链。
- 通过 basophil-specific IL-4 缺陷小鼠证明该轴的必要性：缺乏 basophil 来源 IL-4 时不能产生致病性 MMP-12-producing IMs，也不发生 emphysema，提供了细胞来源特异的 in vivo 遗传验证。

**研究设计**
- elastase 诱导小鼠 COPD/emphysema 模型。
- 检测肺浸润免疫细胞及单核细胞向 IMs 与 AMs 的分化。
- 比较 IMs 与 AMs 的 MMP-12 表达。
- 使用一系列 genetically engineered mice，包括 basophil-specific IL-4 缺陷小鼠，进行功能验证。

**主要发现**
- Intranasal elastase 刺激后，单核细胞被募集到肺，随后主要分化为 interstitial macrophages（IMs），而很少分化为 alveolar macrophages（AMs）。
- MMP-12 主要高表达于 IMs，而非 AMs，与此前认为 AMs 是主要来源的假设相反。
- basophil-derived IL-4 作用于肺浸润单核细胞，促进其向 MMP-12-producing IMs 分化。
- MMP-12-producing IMs 导致肺泡壁破坏和 emphysema 形成。
- basophil-specific IL-4 缺陷小鼠不能产生致病性 MMP-12-producing IMs，也不发生 emphysema。

**为什么入选** 该文在 PNAS 报道完整因果机制链，用 basophil-specific IL-4 缺失做严格 in vivo 遗传验证，并挑战 AMs 是 MMP-12 主要来源的既有假设；同时提示低丰度 basophils 在 COPD 启动期的重要作用。

**局限**
- 仅基于 PubMed 摘要评估；未获取本文全文，无法评价样本量、统计方法、具体基因工程策略及人类组织验证。
- 结论来自 elastase-induced murine COPD 模型，能否外推到香烟烟雾模型或人类 COPD/emphysema 尚不明确。
- 提供的 optional full text 片段为另一篇关于 MMP-12 与视网膜血管新生的 PLOS ONE 论文，与本文无关，未用于评价。

**可借鉴之处**
- 稀少免疫细胞功能验证：即使 basophils 占肺浸润细胞<1%，仍可通过遗传工程小鼠做细胞特异性细胞因子缺失来确立因果，适用于研究 immune-stromal crosstalk 中低丰度免疫细胞的作用。
- 巨噬细胞亚群分辨：monocyte→IM 与 AM 的功能分工提示，在肺泡/间质巨噬细胞研究中应区分细胞来源和 niche，可用 lineage tracing、骨髓嵌合、scRNA-seq 等验证蛋白酶或效应分子的真正来源。
- 机制轴可迁移：IL-4-巨噬细胞-MMP-12 轴和蛋白酶介导基质降解的概念，可与肺气肿、肺泡壁破坏及 pulmonary vascular remodeling 中的基质重塑研究交叉，但需在相应模型中验证。

**Abstract**

Early immune processes in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) are poorly defined. Here, we integrate single-cell transcriptomics of lung digests and bronchoalveolar-lavaged cells (n = 108 patients), subcellular-resolution tissue spatial transcriptomics, and single-cell phospho-CyTOF of blood cells to define myeloid states associated with early stages of fibrosis. Two distinct myeloid gene transcriptional programs are increased in IPF compared to non-diseased controls-type I interferon-activated and fibrotic remodeling programs. At cell, organ, and patient levels, cell types with increased type I interferon-activated program (FABP4hi alveolar macrophages, classical monocytes, interstitial macrophages, and/or non-classical monocytes) are associated with architecturally better-preserved lung tissue, the alveolar barrier, and less fibrotic lung, or less severe disease. Circulating monocytes exhibit heightened type I interferon response that inversely correlate with severity of clinical disease. Our findings show that myeloid cells with increased type I interferon-activated gene programs are associated with less fibrotic stages of IPF.

**What Is New?**
- 本文在 IPF 中整合人源单细胞、空间和磷酸流式数据，分离出 type I interferon-activated 与 fibrotic remodeling 两类髓系转录程序，将既往笼统的髓系活化细化为可区分的疾病相关状态。
- 新定义了一组高 type I interferon-activated 程序的髓系细胞状态，包括 FABP4hi alveolar macrophages、classical monocytes、interstitial macrophages 和/或 non-classical monocytes，并显示其与肺组织结构保留、肺泡屏障完整及更少纤维化相关。
- 在循环单核细胞中通过 phospho-CyTOF 验证 type I interferon 反应增强且与临床严重度负相关，建立了从肺组织/肺泡腔到外周血再到疾病严重度的跨层面关联。

**研究设计**
- 人肺组织消化液与 BAL 细胞 scRNA-seq（n=108 例患者）
- 亚细胞分辨率组织空间转录组
- 外周血细胞 phospho-CyTOF
- IPF 与非病对照比较，并在细胞、器官、患者层面关联髓系状态与纤维化程度/临床严重度

**主要发现**
- IPF 相比非病对照，两类髓系基因转录程序增高：type I interferon-activated 与 fibrotic remodeling。
- 高 type I interferon 程序的细胞包括 FABP4hi alveolar macrophages、classical monocytes、interstitial macrophages 和/或 non-classical monocytes。
- 在细胞、器官和患者层面，这些高 type I interferon 髓系细胞与架构更好保留的肺组织、肺泡屏障和较少纤维化或较轻疾病相关。
- 循环单核细胞显示增强的 type I interferon 反应，且与临床疾病严重度呈负相关。
- 总体提示 type I interferon 活化的髓系状态与 IPF 较轻纤维化阶段相关。

**为什么入选** 人源多模态数据（n=108）覆盖肺组织、肺泡腔和外周血，聚焦 IPF 早期免疫与髓系状态；发现 type I interferon 活化髓系程序与较轻结构/临床严重度负相关，对肺泡巨噬细胞、免疫-基质 crosstalk 与肺纤维化阶段分层有参考价值；发表于 Cell Reports。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要，缺少全文方法细节，无法判断因果、混杂校正和细胞互作分析等具体设计。

- 空间转录组、phospho-CyTOF 的具体分析和验证队列细节不详，样本可能以特定阶段 IPF 为主。

- 可借鉴其多模态设计：肺消化液 scRNA-seq + BAL 细胞 scRNA-seq + 亚细胞空间转录组 + 血 phospho-CyTOF，用于解析免疫-基质 crosstalk 与肺泡微环境。

- 按纤维化阶段/组织架构保留程度分层，可帮助识别早期保护性免疫程序，适合推广到其他慢性肺疾病的免疫-间质互作研究。

**Abstract**

UNLABELLED: Fibroblasts maintain the extracellular matrix (ECM) to support tissue homeostasis and wound healing. In fibrotic diseases, fibroblasts are a primary driver of disease progression through excess collagen secretion and enhanced contractility. Replacing native tissue with a collagen rich fibrotic scar leads to a decline in tissue function. Recent research into idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) has identified fibroblast subpopulations that may be primed for the hyper-activation that leads to increased progression of fibrotic disease. Understanding the contribution of these subpopulations to disease progression requires integrating experimental and computational techniques to understand their dynamic contributions to tissue phenotype. Herein, we introduce a framework for modeling subpopulations using a multiscale mechanistic computational model to understand differences within subpopulations, at the intracellular level and how these differences contribute to cell-and tissue-level pathology. We build and validate this framework using two well-defined subpopulations of fibroblasts in IPF. The subpopulations are defined by the presence or absence of Thy-1, a cell-surface protein that regulates fibroblast mechanosensing. We first developed a logic-based network model of a fibroblast. We then applied this model to identify sub-networks that regulate myofibroblast marker expression in the two subpopulations. Coupling this with an agent-based model (ABM) of the lung microenvironment, we observed how different rules regulating cell fate decisions in each subpopulation affected collagen content. Computational image outputs were analyzed with the open-source biological image analysis software QuPath to quantify how changes in subpopulation dynamics change model-predicted foci characteristics such as size and collagen density. We find that the ability for Thy-1 + fibroblasts to transition to Thy-1 - fibroblasts significantly increases total collagen content, as well as influences fibrotic foci characteristics. Overall, we present a combined experimental and computational framework for studying how dynamic changes in fibroblast subpopulations lead to tissue-level disease phenotypes.
AUTHOR SUMMARY: In wound healing fibroblasts are responsible for rebuilding the scaffolding, called the extracellular matrix (ECM), of the damaged tissue to aid in regeneration. In fibrosis, the normal wound healing processes are hijacked leading to overproduction of ECM proteins, such as collagen, by fibroblasts leading to fibrosis. In fibrotic diseases with no known cause, such as idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF), subpopulations of fibroblasts have been identified as possible drivers of disease. Herein, we use a multiscale computational model that represents intracellular, cellular, and tissue level signaling to study how the presence or absence of a single protein on a fibroblast's surface, Thy-1, affects the formation of fibrotic scar in IPF. Thy-1 regulates how a fibroblast senses the stiffness of the microenvironment. The absence of Thy-1 impacted intracellular signaling and when combined across many cells led to fibrosis at the tissue level. Additionally, if normal Thy-1 + fibroblasts could dynamically lose Thy-1 expression the amount of collagen they produced correlated with that in end stage IPF lungs. This model presents a framework for studying different fibroblast subpopulations using multiscale computational modeling to understand how changes in a single protein in one cell can, over an entire population, affect the dynamics of disease progression.
