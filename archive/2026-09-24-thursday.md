### 1. Sotatercept Improves Right Heart Mechanics Despite Reduced Myocardial Contractility in Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension: A Prospective Exercise Hemodynamic Study.

Circulation | 2026 Sep 22 | Yogesh N V Reddy, Barry A Borlaug
IF 2025：41.3 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：The Department of Cardiovascular Medicine, Mayo Clinic, Rochester, MN (Y.N.V.R., S.K., W.R.M., T.H., R.P.F., B.A.B.).
- 领域：Mayo Clinic 心血管内科，聚焦肺动脉高压与右心-肺动脉耦合的静息及运动血流动力学评估。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42770225/
- DOI：10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.126.081334

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: The right ventricular (RV) response to pressure overload is a crucial determinant of outcomes in pulmonary arterial (PA) hypertension. Sotatercept improves clinical outcomes, but its effects on the right ventricle have been inconsistent. We evaluated the effects of sotatercept on load-independent measures of RV contractility, RV-PA coupling, and right heart deformation at rest and exertion in patients with PA hypertension.
METHODS: In this prospective trial among participants with PA hypertension recruited between July 2024 and May 2025, we performed simultaneous echocardiography and invasive pressure measurements during rest and exercise to evaluate changes in RV contractility (end-systolic elastance [Ees]), afterload (PA elastance [Ea]), RV-PA coupling (ratio of arterial Ea to Ees), and right heart deformation, including RV and right atrial strain, before and after 24 weeks of sotatercept using mixed models.
RESULTS: Among 30 participants (n=21; 70% female; mean age, 49.3 years [SD 13.5]), sotatercept decreased PA Ea. These changes were associated with reduced RV contractility, PA pulsatility index, and the maximal rate of RV pressure increase over time (dP/dtmax; P<0.001 for all). The decrease in Ees was associated with a decrease in PA Ea (r=+0.70 [95% CI, +0.44 to +0.85]; P<0.0001) with preserved RV-PA coupling (mean difference, +0.08 [95% CI, -0.11 to +0.26]; P=0.42). Although systolic indices measured at the base of the right ventricle (tricuspid annular plane systolic excursion and systolic velocity of the lateral tricuspid annulus) decreased after sotatercept use, there was an increase in RV free wall-dependent systolic indices, including resting and exercise RV free wall strain (mean difference, +6.2% [95% CI, +4.5 to +7.8]; P<0.0001) and fractional area change (P=0.0001). Beyond the right ventricle, there was also improved right atrial filling and contractile performance across rest and exercise (P<0.0001 for all).
CONCLUSIONS: Sotatercept decreases RV contractility in proportion to reduced afterload, while preserving the RV-PA coupling relationship. Despite decreased RV contractility, global measures of right heart performance, such as RV free wall strain and RA strain, improve, alongside redistribution of RV deformation from annular motion to free wall shortening. These data suggest that sotatercept causes an energetically favorable reduction in resting RV contractility to match the lower afterload, which does not appear physiologically detrimental when considering the concurrent improvement in RV free wall contractile mechanics and exertional right heart reserve.
REGISTRATION: URL: https://www.clinicaltrials.gov; Unique identifier: NCT06409026.

**What Is New?**
- 首次在前瞻性静息+运动血流动力学研究中，用 load-independent 指标（Ees、PA Ea、Ea/Ees）证明 sotatercept 引起的 RV 收缩力下降与后负荷降低严格成比例（r=+0.70），属于能量学上更有利的匹配而非心肌抑制。
- 新定义了一种 sotatercept 治疗后的右心形变再分布表型：TAPSE 与 lateral tricuspid annular S' 下降，而 RV free wall strain 与 FAC 反而升高，提示整体泵功能的改善被瓣环运动指标的下降所掩盖。
- 把 sotatercept 的右心获益从 RV 扩展到右房，首次显示 RA 充盈与收缩性能在静息和运动负荷下同步改善，构成 RV-PA 轴之外的新机制环节。

**研究设计**
- 前瞻性单中心试验（Mayo Clinic），2024-07 至 2025-05 招募，NCT06409026。
- 30 例 PAH 患者，sotatercept 治疗 24 周，治疗前后自身对照。
- 静息与运动状态下同步 echocardiography 与侵入性压力测量。
- 主要读出：RV contractility（Ees）、afterload（PA Ea）、RV-PA coupling（Ea/Ees）、RV 与 RA strain。
- 统计方法：mixed models 分析治疗前后变化。

**主要发现**
- sotatercept 显著降低 PA Ea，同时伴随 RV 收缩力（Ees）、PA pulsatility index 与 dP/dtmax 下降（均 P<0.001）。
- Ees 的下降与 PA Ea 的下降相关（r=+0.70，95% CI +0.44~+0.85，P<0.0001），而 RV-PA coupling 比值无显著变化（平均差 +0.08，95% CI -0.11~+0.26，P=0.42）。
- 基部来源的收缩指标 TAPSE 与 lateral tricuspid annular S' 下降，但 RV free wall 依赖的指标升高，包括静息与运动 RV free wall strain（平均差 +6.2%，95% CI +4.5~+7.8，P<0.0001）与 fractional area change（P=0.0001）。
- 右房充盈与收缩性能在静息与运动状态下均改善（均 P<0.0001）。
- 作者解读为 sotatercept 造成静息 RV 收缩力按后负荷下调的能量学有利变化，结合 RV 游离壁收缩力学与运动右心储备的改善，不具生理学危害。

**为什么入选** ['虽是临床研究，但用 load-independent 生理学指标（Ees/Ea）闭合了「降后负荷药物是否伤害 RV」这一长期争议的机制链。', '同步侵入性压力+超声+运动负荷的设计在 PAH 领域较为严格，且设置了治疗前后自身对照。', '提出 RV 形变再分布与 RA 功能改善两个可延伸的机制观察点，对右心重构与 RV-PA coupling 研究有方法学价值。', '发表于 Circulation。']

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无 OA 全文，缺少入组流程图、运动方案细节、缺失数据处理与敏感性分析信息。
- 摘要中样本量表述不一致（先写 30 例，后写 n=21），实际分析人群与随访完整性无法从摘要确认。
- 单臂治疗前后设计、单中心、样本量小，无对照或随机化，无法排除时间效应、基础治疗调整与安慰剂/回归均值影响；结论中的「能量学有利」为机制推测，未直接测量心肌能量学或做因果验证。

**可借鉴之处**
- 「侵入性压力 + 同步超声 + 运动负荷」的负荷不依赖评估框架（Ees/Ea、dP/dtmax、PA pulsatility index）可用于检验任何以降后负荷为目标的肺血管干预对 RV-PA coupling 的净效应，避免把收缩力下降误判为药物毒性。
- 区域性形变指标的取舍逻辑值得借鉴：以 RV free wall strain 与 FAC 反映心肌本身的收缩力学，以 TAPSE/S' 反映瓣环位移，否则单一 TAPSE 读数会给出方向相反的结论。
- RA strain 与运动右心储备可作为「右心-右房轴」的功能读出，供研究 immune-stromal crosstalk、间质纤维化或肺泡/肺血管重构所致右心适应不良时作为终点参考。

### 2. Mechanosensitive Stanniocalcin-1 Suppresses Pulmonary Artery Smooth Muscle Cell Proliferation and Attenuates Experimental Pulmonary Hypertension.

Circulation research | 2026 Sep 09 | Mariko Kogami, Utako Yokoyama
IF 2025：18.0 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：年内旗舰系统研究
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Physiology (M.K., Y.K., S.I., K.U., H.I., Y.H., U.Y.), Tokyo Medical University, Japan.; Department of Respiratory Medicine (M.K., S.A.), Tokyo Medical University, Japan.
- 领域：第一作者隶属东京医科大学生理学系与呼吸内科，主要方向为肺循环机械力生物学与肺动脉高压中平滑肌细胞表型的机制研究。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42713657/
- DOI：10.1161/CIRCRESAHA.125.327869

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Idiopathic pulmonary arterial hypertension (IPAH) is driven by progressive vascular remodeling, particularly smooth muscle cell (SMC) proliferation. Current combination vasodilator therapies have markedly improved outcomes; however, prognosis remains poor in subgroups such as patients with respiratory comorbidities. Elevation of intravascular hydrostatic pressure is a hallmark of IPAH, yet its direct role in pulmonary artery SMCs remains unclear. We aimed to identify pressure-responsive mediators using a newly developed hydrostatic pressurization system to model hypertensive hemodynamics.
METHODS: Pulmonary artery SMCs from 4 patients with IPAH were exposed to high hydrostatic pressure (70/40 mm Hg, 60 bpm). Transcriptomic profiling identified differentially expressed genes, which were validated by quantitative polymerase chain reaction. Functional studies included PIEZO1 (piezo type mechanosensitive ion channel component 1) modulation, rhSTC1 (recombinant human stanniocalcin-1) treatment, bromodeoxyuridine incorporation, and Western blotting for cell-cycle regulators. Chronic hypoxia-induced pulmonary hypertension was assessed in wild-type and Stc1-/- mice by hemodynamic and histological analyses, with or without intratracheal rhSTC1 administration.
RESULTS: RNA sequencing revealed STC1 to be a pressure-induced gene in IPAH SMCs. PIEZO1 activation upregulated STC1, whereas knockdown blunted this response. STC1 was upregulated in IPAH lungs, while rhSTC1 reduced pulmonary arterial SMC proliferation and increased p-p53, p21, and p27 expression. Stc1-/- mice under hypoxia exhibited significantly higher right ventricular systolic pressure and greater pulmonary arterial medial thickness than wild-type mice. CD68-positive macrophages were increased in Stc1-/- mice under normoxia and further elevated with hypoxia. Intratracheal administration of rhSTC1 attenuated PAH in wild-type and Stc1-/- mice.
CONCLUSIONS: Elevated hydrostatic pressure drives STC1 expression via PIEZO1, suggesting an adaptive but insufficient protective response in IPAH. Modulation of STC1 (stanniocalcin-1) may represent a potential therapeutic approach.

**What Is New?**
- 首次利用新开发的静水压加压系统（70/40 mm Hg、60 bpm）对 4 例 IPAH 患者来源的肺动脉 SMC 进行转录组筛选，将 STC1 明确为压力诱导基因，而非此前仅作为钙磷代谢相关分泌因子被认识。
- 确立了 PIEZO1→STC1 的机械转导因果轴：PIEZO1 激活可上调 STC1、敲低则削弱该反应，把机械感受离子通道与下游分泌型保护因子连成一条新机制链。
- 通过 Stc1-/- 小鼠慢性缺氧模型（RVSP 更高、肺血管中膜更厚）与气管内 rhSTC1 在体 rescue，在遗传缺失与补充回补两端同时验证了 STC1 对实验性 PAH 的因果保护作用。
- 提出 IPAH 中 STC1 上调是一种“适应性但不足”的内源保护反应，为把压力本身而非仅血管收缩作为治疗靶点提供了概念依据。

**研究设计**
- 人源 IPAH 肺动脉 SMC（n=4）接受新型静水压加压（70/40 mm Hg、60 bpm）后行转录组测序，差异基因经 qPCR 验证
- PIEZO1 药理学激活与敲低实验，检验其对 STC1 表达的调控
- rhSTC1 处理 + BrdU 掺入 + Western blot 检测细胞周期调控因子（p-p53、p21、p27）
- 野生型与 Stc1-/- 小鼠慢性缺氧 PAH 模型，部分给予气管内 rhSTC1
- 在体评估右心室收缩压、肺血管中膜厚度组织学及 CD68 阳性巨噬细胞浸润

**主要发现**
- 高静水压使 IPAH 来源肺动脉 SMC 中 STC1 表达上调，IPAH 患者肺组织中 STC1 亦升高。
- PIEZO1 激活可上调 STC1，PIEZO1 敲低则削弱压力诱导的 STC1 反应。
- rhSTC1 抑制肺动脉 SMC 增殖，并升高 p-p53、p21、p27 等细胞周期负调控蛋白。
- Stc1-/- 小鼠在慢性缺氧下右心室收缩压更高、肺小动脉中膜更厚。
- Stc1-/- 小鼠常氧下 CD68 阳性巨噬细胞已增多，缺氧后进一步升高；气管内给予 rhSTC1 可减轻野生型与 Stc1-/- 小鼠的 PAH。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整且闭合（血流机械力→PIEZO1→STC1→p53/p21/p27→平滑肌增殖抑制），同时具备人源 IPAH 细胞、基因敲除小鼠遗传学证据与在体重组蛋白 rescue 三重验证，并提出了“压力驱动的适应性保护不足”这一新的病理生理视角；发表于 Circulation Research。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要、无 OA 全文，加压系统的具体参数细节、统计方法与样本处理流程无法评估。
- 人源验证基于 4 例 IPAH 患者来源 SMC，缺少非 IPAH 或健康对照的直接比较，样本量有限。
- 慢性缺氧模型不能完全再现 IPAH 的遗传与血流动力学特征，PIEZO1 上游及 STC1 下游受体的作用细节尚不明确。

**可借鉴之处**
- 静水压加压培养结合转录组筛选的范式，可迁移到其他机械敏感细胞（如肺成纤维细胞、内皮细胞、血管周细胞）的力学生物学研究，用于寻找压力应答的候选调控节点。
- “基因敲除小鼠加重表型 + 气管内/局部重组蛋白回补”这一双向因果验证设计，是判定某个内源因子为保护性还是致病性的可靠模板，可用于其他血管重塑或肺泡损伤模型。
- STC1 经 p-p53、p21、p27 抑制细胞周期这一读出逻辑，提示在肺动脉平滑肌增殖研究中可把细胞周期负调控轴作为机制落点；同时 Stc1-/- 小鼠中 CD68+ 巨噬细胞在常氧即升高、缺氧后进一步增加，值得作为免疫-基质 crosstalk 的切入点进一步探查。

### 3. Deficiency of the Deubiquitinase UCHL1 Attenuates Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension.

Circulation | 2024 Jul 23 | Haiyang Tang*, Akash Gupta*, Ankit A Desai
IF 2025：41.3 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：领域经典
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者单位：State Key Laboratory of Respiratory Disease, National Clinical Research Center for Respiratory Disease, Guangzhou Institute of Respiratory Health, Department of Clinical Laboratory, The First Affiliated Hospital of Guangzhou Medical University, Guangzhou, Guangdong, China (H.T., C.B., S.L.).; Department of Medicine and Arizona Health Sciences Center, Department of Cellular and Molecular Medicine, College of Medicine-Tucson, University of Arizona (A.G., G.G., N.A.W.).
- 通讯作者单位：Krannert Cardiovascular Research Center (S.A.M., S.S., Y. Shi, S.R.N., S.C.-Y., R.D., A.A.D.), Indiana University, Indianapolis.
- 第一作者领域：第一作者来自广州医科大学附属第一医院/广州呼吸健康研究院，并合作 University of Arizona，研究领域可推断为呼吸疾病尤其是肺动脉高压的血管生物学与临床实验室研究。
- 通讯作者领域：通讯作者来自 Indiana University Krannert Cardiovascular Research Center，研究领域可推断为心血管/肺血管疾病机制，尤其肺动脉高压与泛素-蛋白酶体/去泛素化调控。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38695173/
- DOI：10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.123.065304

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: The ubiquitin-proteasome system regulates protein degradation and the development of pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH), but knowledge about the role of deubiquitinating enzymes in this process is limited. UCHL1 (ubiquitin carboxyl-terminal hydrolase 1), a deubiquitinase, has been shown to reduce AKT1 (AKT serine/threonine kinase 1) degradation, resulting in higher levels. Given that AKT1 is pathological in pulmonary hypertension, we hypothesized that UCHL1 deficiency attenuates PAH development by means of reductions in AKT1.
METHODS: Tissues from animal pulmonary hypertension models as well as human pulmonary artery endothelial cells from patients with PAH exhibited increased vascular UCHL1 staining and protein expression. Exposure to LDN57444, a UCHL1-specific inhibitor, reduced human pulmonary artery endothelial cell and smooth muscle cell proliferation. Across 3 preclinical PAH models, LDN57444-exposed animals, Uchl1 knockout rats (Uchl1-/-), and conditional Uchl1 knockout mice (Tie2Cre-Uchl1fl/fl) demonstrated reduced right ventricular hypertrophy, right ventricular systolic pressures, and obliterative vascular remodeling. Lungs and pulmonary artery endothelial cells isolated from Uchl1-/- animals exhibited reduced total and activated Akt with increased ubiquitinated Akt levels. UCHL1-silenced human pulmonary artery endothelial cells displayed reduced lysine(K)63-linked and increased K48-linked AKT1 levels.
RESULTS: Supporting experimental data, we found that rs9321, a variant in a GC-enriched region of the UCHL1 gene, is associated with reduced methylation (n=5133), increased UCHL1 gene expression in lungs (n=815), and reduced cardiac index in patients (n=796). In addition, Gadd45α (an established demethylating gene) knockout mice (Gadd45α-/-) exhibited reduced lung vascular UCHL1 and AKT1 expression along with attenuated hypoxic pulmonary hypertension.
CONCLUSIONS: Our findings suggest that UCHL1 deficiency results in PAH attenuation by means of reduced AKT1, highlighting a novel therapeutic pathway in PAH.

**What Is New?**
- 本文将 deubiquitinase UCHL1 定义为 PAH 中此前未被充分认识的促病调节节点：其缺失或药理抑制在多种临床前模型中减轻 RVH、RVSP 和闭塞性血管重塑。
- 本文闭合 UCHL1-AKT1 去泛素化机制链：UCHL1 沉默使 human PAEC 中 K63-linked AKT1 降低、K48-linked AKT1 升高，Uchl1-/- 动物肺和 PAEC 中 total/activated Akt 降低而 ubiquitinated Akt 升高。
- 本文提供遗传、药理与人类遗传变异三重验证：Uchl1-/- 大鼠、Tie2Cre-Uchl1fl/fl 小鼠和 LDN57444 结果一致，且 UCHL1 rs9321 与肺 UCHL1 表达、甲基化及患者 cardiac index 关联。

**研究设计**
- 人类 PAH 组织/PAEC 与动物 PH 模型检测 UCHL1 表达
- 使用 UCHL1 抑制剂 LDN57444、Uchl1-/- 大鼠和 Tie2Cre-Uchl1fl/fl 小鼠进行 loss-of-function
- 在 3 种临床前 PAH 模型评估 RVH、RVSP 和闭塞性血管重塑
- 检测 Akt 总/活化/泛素化水平及 K63/K48-linked AKT1
- 人群 rs9321 与甲基化、肺 UCHL1 表达、cardiac index 关联；Gadd45α-/- 小鼠验证 hypoxic PH

**主要发现**
- 动物 PH 模型和 PAH 患者 human PAEC 中血管 UCHL1 staining 和蛋白表达增加。
- LDN57444 抑制 human PAEC 和 SMC 增殖；在 3 种 PAH 模型中，LDN57444、Uchl1-/- 大鼠和 Tie2Cre-Uchl1fl/fl 小鼠均降低 RVH、RVSP 和闭塞性血管重塑。
- Uchl1-/- 动物肺和 PAEC 中 total/activated Akt 降低、ubiquitinated Akt 升高；UCHL1-silenced human PAEC 中 K63-linked AKT1 减少、K48-linked AKT1 增加。
- UCHL1 rs9321 位于 GC-enriched 区域，与 reduced methylation（n=5133）、increased lung UCHL1 expression（n=815）和 reduced cardiac index（n=796）相关。
- Gadd45α-/- 小鼠肺血管 UCHL1 和 AKT1 表达降低，并减轻 hypoxic pulmonary hypertension。

**为什么入选** 本文用去泛素化酶 UCHL1 连接蛋白稳态与 PAH，形成 UCHL1-AKT1 机制轴；药理、全身 KO、内皮条件 KO 多模型一致，并补充人类 variant-甲基化-表达-表型关联，机制链和转化验证兼具，发表于 Circulation。

**局限**
- 仅基于 abstract，未获取 OA 全文，无法核对具体样本量、统计方法、实验条件和完整 rescue 数据。
- UCHL1 是否直接结合并去泛素化 AKT1 的生化证据（如催化突变、binding assay）在 abstract 中未展开。
- LDN57444 的脱靶效应、细胞类型特异性，以及 rs9321 人群关联的因果性，abstract 未能充分说明。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可将 deubiquitinase-substrate 轴作为肺血管重塑研究节点，结合 K63/K48 linkage-specific 泛素化检测判断靶蛋白稳定性，而不只测总蛋白水平。
- 内皮特异性条件敲除（Tie2Cre-Uchl1fl/fl）联合全身 KO、药理抑制和多模型验证，是可复用的 loss-of-function 三角设计，适合 alveolar 或 immune-stromal crosstalk 研究。
- 人群 variant（rs9321）与甲基化、组织表达及表型关联可作为机制锚点；若结合 scRNA-seq 或 co-culture，可进一步定位 UCHL1 高表达细胞状态及其与 fibroblast/immune 细胞的互作。

**Abstract**

UNLABELLED: Chronic thromboembolic pulmonary hypertension (CTEPH) is a life-threatening pulmonary vascular disease, characterized by persistent thrombotic obstruction and progressive pulmonary vascular remodeling, yet the molecular mechanisms linking persistent thrombosis to vascular remodeling remain incompletely understood. Clinical studies have reported elevated plasma von Willebrand factor (VWF) levels and reduced ADAMTS13 in patients with CTEPH, but whether VWF/ADAMTS13 dysregulation contributes directly to disease pathogenesis remains unclear. Here, using newly established rat models of CTEPH, we identify a causative role for dysregulation of the VWF-ADAMTS13 axis in chronic thromboembolic progression. CTEPH rats developed persistent, unresolved VWF- and fibrin-rich thrombi accompanied by markedly increased endothelial VWF deposition. In contrast, ADAMTS13 expression and activity were significantly reduced in CTEPH rats. Consistent with these findings, genetic Adamts13 deficiency further exacerbates pulmonary microvascular thrombosis and accelerated early mortality following disease induction. Mechanistically, ultra-large (UL)-VWF accumulated on the pulmonary endothelial surface, promoting robust platelet recruitment under shear. This platelet-VWF interaction stimulated the release of platelet-derived pro-remodeling mediators, including TGF-β1 and PDGF-BB. Genetic ablation of Vwf markedly reduced in situ microvascular thrombosis within pulmonary arterioles, attenuated pulmonary arterial remodeling, and improved pulmonary hemodynamics. Moreover, treatment with recombinant ADAMTS13 reduced endothelial UL-VWF accumulation, suppressed platelet activation, and effectively prevented thrombosis and platelet-driven pro-remodeling signaling in CTEPH rats. Collectively, these findings identify dysregulation of the VWF-ADAMTS13 axis as a key driver of pulmonary thrombosis and vascular remodeling in CTEPH and support therapeutic targeting of this pathway as a potential disease-modifying strategy.
KEY POINTS: VWF-ADAMTS13 dysregulation promotes persistent pulmonary thrombosis and platelet-driven arterial remodeling within pulmonary arterioles.Recombinant ADAMTS13 treatment or VWF ablation abrogates pulmonary arterial thrombosis and halts vascular remodeling in CTEPH.

**Abstract**

Influenza viruses circulate in humans, causing a substantial burden on global health. Investigation of influenza-host interactions could identify host factors that regulate influenza pathogenicity. Sphingosine 1-phosphate (S1P) is a bioactive lipid mediator and regulates crucial cellular processes. S1P lyase (SPL), an enzyme that mediates S1P degradation, was shown to display anti-influenza activity in a cell culture system. Here, we constructed a mouse model to demonstrate the antiviral function of SPL in respiratory epithelial cells during influenza in vivo. Deletion of SPL from lung epithelial cells exacerbated influenza-induced weight loss and mortality. Influenza virus began to propagate more effectively in the absence of SPL at the innate immune stage. Increased virus titers were sustained during influenza and associated with enhanced accumulation of multiple immune cell types in the lungs. Single-cell RNA sequencing was conducted to further define the function of SPL in lung epithelial cells. SPL deletion increased the proportion of alveolar type 1 (AT1) cells compared to alveolar type 2 (AT2) cells with alteration of the related signaling pathways, suggesting a role of SPL in AT1/AT2 programming. Importantly, host innate defense pathways were changed in SPL-deficient lung epithelial cells upon infection, which corroborates the antiviral function of SPL. This study elucidates the host protective function of SPL in lung epithelial cells during influenza and provides gene signature profiles critical for SPL-mediated alleviation of influenza pathogenicity. The findings may contribute to development of host-directed therapeutics to better control influenza.
