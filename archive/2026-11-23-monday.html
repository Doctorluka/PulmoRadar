Atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease risk prediction in individuals with and without impaired spirometry.

杂志Chest
IF: 9.6 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2026 Nov 18
10.1016/j.chest.2026.03.049
作者Suurya Krishnan · Jean Bourbeau
机构Respiratory Epidemiology and Clinical Research Unit, Centre for Outcomes Research and Evaluation, Research Institute of the McGill University Health Centre and McGill University, Montreal, QC, Canada.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 该研究在7个美国人群队列、26523名参与者中首次系统量化了PCE对10年MACE的判别力在spirometry受损者中明显低于正常者（AUC 0.740 vs 0.792），尽管前者MACE发生率约高一倍。
  • 研究明确显示，在PCE变量基础上加入dyspnea与impaired spirometry、PRISm/气流受限分级或FEV1%predicted/FEV1/FVC样条，仅使AUC提高0.002–0.003，提示常规spirometry表型对PCE的增量预测价值有限。
  • 在spirometry受损亚组中，仅“连续FEV1%predicted + FEV1/FVC + dyspnea”模型较PCE变量有统计学显著但幅度很小的AUC提升（0.750 vs 0.746），而校准度、敏感度、特异度及总体表现均无差异。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

BACKGROUND: Cardiovascular disease risk scores likely underestimate risk in individuals with impaired spirometry (airflow obstruction and preserved ratio impaired spirometry (PRISm)).

RESEARCH QUESTION: How do risk scores perform between individuals with impaired and normal spirometry, and can they be improved by adding spirometry and dyspnea?

STUDY DESIGN AND METHODS: Ten-year incidence of major cardiovascular events (MACE) ie., myocardial infarction, stroke or death from coronary heart disease, from 7 US population-based cohorts was used for this analysis. The pooled cohorts' equations' (PCE) ability to predict MACE was compared between normal and impaired spirometry. Cox-models adding spirometry, dyspnea to PCE variables were evaluated for improvement in MACE prediction.

RESULTS: Among 26523 participants, those with impaired (N=7547) compared with normal (N=18932) spirometry had a higher incidence of MACE (14% vs 7%), hazard ratio 95%CI 1.25[1.17,1.34] adjusted for PCE variables. Discrimination using area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC)95%CI of the PCE for MACE was 0.792[0.780,0.803] vs 0.740[0.724,0.755] in normal vs impaired spirometry. Discrimination of MACE improved slightly by adding to the PCE: dyspnea plus 1)impaired spirometry 0.792[0.783,0.800], 2)PRISm, mild, moderate+ airflow obstruction 0.792[0.783,0.801], 3)FEV1%predicted, FEV1/FVC splines 0.793[0.784,0.802], compared with PCE variables 0.790[0.781,0.798]. The differences in AUC[95%CI] were: 1)0.002[0.001,0.003]p=0.002, 2)0.002[0.001,0.004]p=0.002, 3)0.003[0.002,0.005]p=0.0005 respectively. Among participants with impaired spirometry, only the model that added FEV1%predicted, FEV1/FVC continuous and dyspnea to the PCE showed improvement in AUC[95%CI], 0.750[0.735,0.765] vs 0.746[0.730,0.761] for PCE variables, difference of 0.004[0.001,0.008]p=0.008. There were no differences among models in calibration, sensitivity, specificity, overall performance, and after bootstrapping 1000 samples.

INTERPRETATION: Performance of the PCE was better among individuals with normal vs impaired spirometry, despite the latter having an excess burden of MACE. Adding spirometry and dyspnea to PCE variables showed a small statistically significant improvement in MACE prediction, which may not be meaningful clinically, so other risk factors should be considered.

  • PCE对MACE的AUC在正常spirometry者为0.792（0.780–0.803），在受损者为0.740（0.724–0.755）。
  • 在全体人群中，加入dyspnea联合spirometry表型使AUC由0.790升至0.792–0.793，绝对增量0.002–0.003。
  • 在spirometry受损亚组中，仅连续FEV1%predicted、FEV1/FVC与dyspnea联合模型使AUC由0.746升至0.750，差值为0.004（0.001–0.008）。
  • 各模型在校准度、敏感度、特异度、总体表现及bootstrap后均无显著差异，作者认为增量预测的临床意义可能有限。

02 · 三年内旗舰系统研究 仅摘要

Prostaglandin I2 receptor activation promotes alveolar regeneration via the JUN/p53 pathway.

杂志American journal of respiratory and critical care medicine
IF: 21.7 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2026 Jul 01
10.1093/ajrccm/aamag116
作者Tingting Yu · Yujun Shen
机构Department of Pharmacology, Tianjin Key Laboratory of Inflammatory Biology, Center for Cardiovascular Diseases, Key Laboratory of Immune Microenvironment and Disease (Ministry of Education), The Province and Ministry Co-sponsored Collaborative Innovation Center for Medical Epigenetics, State Key Laboratory of Experimental Hematology, School of Basic Medical Sciences, Tianjin Medical University, Tianjin, China.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 在全部前列腺素受体中，IP 被 pinpoint 为与 IPF 患者过渡态 AT2 细胞中 AT1 基因富集关联最强的受体，从而把一条此前未被认识的脂质介质受体轴引入肺泡再生领域。
  • 首次闭合了从受体到转录程序的因果链：IP 通过 PKA 抑制 MAP3K5，压制 JNK/JUN 轴，解除 JUN 对 p53 依赖的 AT1 基因表达的抑制，确立 JUN 异常激活是 AT2→AT1 转分化的关键刹车。
  • 以 AT2 细胞特异性条件敲除加 selexipag 药理激活的双向 in vivo 验证，并在 IPF 患者原代 AT2 细胞中复现，使该通路从相关性提升到可干预的因果节点。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

RATIONALE: Transdifferentiation of alveolar type 2 (AT2) epithelial cells into alveolar type 1 (AT1) cells is essential for maintaining lung homeostasis and facilitating repair following injury. However, the molecular mechanisms governing AT2-to-AT1 transdifferentiation remain unclear.

OBJECTIVES: To investigate the role of the prostaglandin I2 receptor (IP) in regulating AT2-to-AT1 transdifferentiation and elucidate the underlying mechanisms.

METHODS: Alveolar organoid cultures and bleomycin- or lipopolysaccharide (LPS)-induced murine lung injury models were used to assess the role of IP in AT2-to-AT1 transdifferentiation and lung repair. Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq), ATAC-seq, and biochemical assays were performed to explore the regulatory signaling pathways downstream of IP in AT2 cells.

MEASUREMENTS AND MAIN RESULTS: Among all prostaglandin receptors, IP exhibited the strongest association with AT1 gene enrichment in transitional AT2 cells from patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF). Pharmacological inhibition or genetic deletion of IP significantly impaired the AT2-to-AT1 transition in organoid cultures. Conditional knockout of IP in AT2 cells exacerbated bleomycin- and LPS-induced lung injury by reducing epithelial regeneration and increasing fibrosis. Mechanistically, IP deficiency led to aberrant JUN activation, which suppressed p53-dependent AT1 gene expression. IP activation promoted PKA-mediated inhibition of MAP3K5, thereby suppressing the JNK/JUN axis and enhancing p53-driven AT2-to-AT1 transdifferentiation. Pharmacological activation of IP with selexipag promoted alveolar epithelial regeneration and reduced lung fibrosis in mice. IP agonist also enhanced AT2-to-AT1 transdifferentiation in primary AT2 cells from patients with IPF.

CONCLUSIONS: IP is a key regulator of alveolar epithelial regeneration. Therapeutic activation of IP may be a promising strategy for promoting lung repair.

  • 肺泡类器官培养体系评估 IP 对 AT2→AT1 转分化的作用
  • 博来霉素与 LPS 两种小鼠肺损伤模型＋AT2 细胞条件性敲除 IP
  • scRNA-seq 与 ATAC-seq 结合生化实验解析下游信号
  • 患者来源 IPF 组织过渡态 AT2 细胞分析与原代 AT2 细胞验证

  • IPF 患者过渡态 AT2 细胞中，IP 在全部前列腺素受体里与 AT1 基因富集相关性最强
  • 药理性抑制或遗传缺失 IP 显著削弱类器官中的 AT2→AT1 转分化
  • AT2 细胞条件敲除 IP 加重博来霉素与 LPS 诱导的肺损伤，表现为上皮再生减少、纤维化增加
  • 机制上 IP 缺失导致 JUN 异常激活并抑制 p53 依赖的 AT1 基因表达，IP 激活则经 PKA 抑制 MAP3K5、压制 JNK/JUN 轴
  • selexipag 激活 IP 促进小鼠肺泡上皮再生并减轻纤维化，且在 IPF 患者原代 AT2 细胞中增强转分化

Regeneration of the lung alveolus by an evolutionarily conserved epithelial progenitor.

杂志Nature
IF: 56.1 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2018 Mar 08
10.1016/j.molcel.2010.05.004
作者William J Zacharias · Edward E Morrisey
机构Department of Medicine, University of Pennsylvania, Philadelphia, Pennsylvania 19104, USA.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 本研究在AT2细胞群内定义了一个Wnt响应性AEP谱系，并将其确立为急性肺损伤后肺泡上皮再生的主要兼性祖细胞，而不仅是稳态维持群体。
  • 通过转录组、表观基因组和功能表型的整合，研究把AEP界定为一种独特的上皮细胞状态，并锁定Wnt与Fgf信号为其特异性响应节点。
  • 研究发现保守表面标志TM4SF1可直接分离人AEP，并在3D organoid中证明其功能，从而把小鼠谱系发现推进到人源功能验证。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Functional tissue regeneration is required for the restoration of normal organ homeostasis after severe injury. Some organs, such as the intestine, harbour active stem cells throughout homeostasis and regeneration; more quiescent organs, such as the lung, often contain facultative progenitor cells that are recruited after injury to participate in regeneration. Here we show that a Wnt-responsive alveolar epithelial progenitor (AEP) lineage within the alveolar type 2 cell population acts as a major facultative progenitor cell in the distal lung. AEPs are a stable lineage during alveolar homeostasis but expand rapidly to regenerate a large proportion of the alveolar epithelium after acute lung injury. AEPs exhibit a distinct transcriptome, epigenome and functional phenotype and respond specifically to Wnt and Fgf signalling. In contrast to other proposed lung progenitor cells, human AEPs can be directly isolated by expression of the conserved cell surface marker TM4SF1, and act as functional human alveolar epithelial progenitor cells in 3D organoids. Our results identify the AEP lineage as an evolutionarily conserved alveolar progenitor that represents a new target for human lung regeneration strategies.

Noradrenergic input from sympathetic nerves to fibroblasts regulates lung fibrosis.

杂志Science translational medicine
IF: 15.8 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2026 Nov 18
10.1126/scitranslmed.adw3631
作者Genta Ishikawa · Erica L Herzog
机构Department of Internal Medicine, Section of Pulmonary, Critical Care, and Sleep Medicine, School of Medicine, Yale University, New Haven, CT 06520, USA.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 首次将交感神经末梢、noradrenaline 与 α-SMA+ 效应成纤维细胞定义为空间和功能耦合的促纤维化单元。
  • 在 α-SMA+ 效应成纤维细胞中治疗性删除 Adra1d 可抑制 bleomycin 肺纤维化，确立 ADRA1D 为该细胞状态的上游因果节点而非仅相关标志物。
  • 通过整体肺透明化成像、IPF/SSc-ILD 人肺组织、人 precision-cut lung slices 与 scRNA-seq 二次分析形成跨物种、跨模型的严格验证链。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Pulmonary fibrosis is a progressive, irreversible condition in which expansion of effector fibroblasts and extracellular matrix obliterates alveolar gas exchange regions of the lung through incompletely understood mechanisms. We found sympathetic nerve fibers in fibrotic alveolar regions enriched for alpha-smooth muscle actin-positive (α-SMA+) effector fibroblasts and noradrenaline by applying optical clearing and whole-lung imaging to the bleomycin mouse model of lung fibrosis and multichannel immunofluorescence to explanted lung tissues from patients with idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) or systemic sclerosis-associated interstitial lung disease (SSc-ILD). Human lung fibroblasts cultured with sympathetic nerves displayed enhanced production of fibrotic markers, and sympathetic nerve-specific deletion of the nerve growth factor receptor neurotrophic receptor tyrosine kinase 1 mitigated bleomycin-induced lung fibrosis in mice. Pharmacologic inhibition of noradrenaline receptors, including α1-adrenoreceptors, mitigated collagen accumulation and pathologic remodeling in bleomycin-challenged mice and in human precision-cut lung slices. Accumulation of α-SMA+ effector fibroblasts expressing α1-adrenoreceptor subtype D (ADRA1D) was observed in fibrotic mouse lungs and in explanted lung tissues from patients with IPF or SSc-ILD. Therapeutic deletion of Adra1d in α-SMA+ effector fibroblasts suppressed fibrosis in the bleomycin model. Secondary analysis of single-cell RNA sequencing datasets from IPF or SSc-ILD lung explants revealed α1-adrenoreceptor antagonist-responsive, disease-specific programs in effector fibroblasts. Together, these studies show that a fibrogenic unit comprising sympathetic nerve-derived noradrenaline and ADRA1D+ effector fibroblasts promotes lung fibrosis in disease-relevant modeling systems. Strategies targeting sympathetic innervation or α1-adrenoreceptors may provide previously unrecognized therapeutic opportunities in IPF, SSc-ILD, and related fibrotic conditions.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • bleomycin 小鼠肺纤维化模型结合 optical clearing 与 whole-lung imaging。
  • IPF 与 SSc-ILD explanted 肺组织多通道免疫荧光。

  • 成纤维细胞异质性区分亚型：炎症性成纤维细胞富集于 SSc-NSIP，CTHRC1⁺ 成纤维细胞富集于 UIP。
  • AB 与病理性成纤维细胞间存在促纤维化信号互作，UIP 中 TGF-β 信号放大。

Single-cell lung eQTL colocalization implicates alveolar epithelial regulatory effects at COPD lung-function loci.

文章2026 Nov 15
10.64898/2026.11.12.26367877
作者Rahel Barbara Eberle · Aabida Saferali
机构未提供

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 首次将多祖先 FEV1/FVC GWAS（n=588,452）与覆盖 38 种肺细胞类型的单细胞 eQTL（n=114）系统共定位，把肺功能位点的效应基因从“位点”推进到“具体细胞类型”，信号主要集中于上皮与免疫细胞群。
  • 在更保守的 shared-effect prior 下，仅 DSP（已知 COPD 效应基因）与 NRP2（候选效应基因）两条共定位信号依然成立，且二者均定位于 alveolar type 2 细胞，为同一细胞区室提供了稳健的双基因证据。
  • 发现多数候选共定位仅在单细胞 eQTL 中可见、在 bulk 肺 eQTL 中检测不到，直接证明单细胞分辨率能找回被组织平均化掩盖的细胞类型特异性调控信号。
  • 通过保守先验、与 bulk eQTL 交叉验证及 posterior probability 阈值（>0.7）三重过滤，把候选共定位从 20 条压缩到 2 条高置信信号，建立了比单纯共定位计数更严格的判定路径。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

BACKGROUND: Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) and lung function are highly heritable, but for most genome-wide association study (GWAS) loci, the effector gene and the lung cell type in which it acts are unknown. Single-cell expression quantitative trait loci (eQTLs) offer a way to resolve the cell types in which regulatory variants act.

RESULTS: We integrated a multi-ancestry GWAS of the ratio of forced expiratory volume in one second to forced vital capacity (FEV1/FVC; Shrine et al., n = 588,452) with single-cell lung eQTLs across 38 cell types (Natri et al., n = 114) by colocalization. We identified 20 candidate colocalizations (posterior probability of a shared causal variant > 0.7) for 13 genes in nine cell types, predominantly epithelial and immune. The two strongest signals localized DSP, an established COPD effector gene, and NRP2, a candidate effector gene, to alveolar type 2 epithelial cells; only these two colocalizations remained supported under a more conservative shared-effect prior. Both signals, together with FAM13A, were also detectable in bulk lung eQTLs, whereas most remaining loci colocalized only in the single-cell analysis.

CONCLUSIONS: Single-cell eQTL colocalization resolves the alveolar epithelium as a cellular context for DSP and nominates NRP2 as a candidate lung function effector gene in the same compartment, highlighting the additional cellular resolution provided by single-cell data. The remaining candidate colocalizations warrant replication.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • 研究类型：计算遗传学整合分析（GWAS × 单细胞 eQTL 共定位），非实验性研究。
  • 暴露/结局数据：多祖先 FEV1/FVC GWAS（Shrine et al., n=588,452）× 单细胞肺 eQTL（Natri et al., n=114，38 种细胞类型）。
  • 统计方法：共定位分析，共享因果变异 posterior probability > 0.7 为初筛阈值，并以更保守的 shared-effect prior 复筛。
  • 对照验证：与 bulk 肺 eQTL 共定位结果比对，区分单细胞特有信号与组织水平信号。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • 共获得 20 条候选共定位（PP>0.7），涉及 13 个基因、9 种细胞类型，细胞分布以 epithelial 与 immune 为主。
  • 最强两条信号分别将 DSP 与 NRP2 定位到 alveolar type 2 细胞；两者是唯一在保守 shared-effect prior 下仍被支持的共定位。
  • DSP、NRP2 与 FAM13A 三条信号在 bulk 肺 eQTL 中同样可检测，说明其稳健性较高且不依赖单细胞数据独有。
  • 其余大部分候选位点仅在单细胞 eQTL 分析中共定位，在 bulk 数据中缺失，凸显细胞分辨率对低丰度细胞信号的必要性。
  • 作者结论认为单细胞 eQTL 共定位可界定 alveolar epithelium 作为 DSP 的细胞学背景，并提名 NRP2 为同一区室的肺功能候选效应基因。

AI 解读 · 为什么入选

机制链完整（GWAS 位点→因果变异→效应基因→细胞类型），采用保守先验与 bulk 对照双重稳健性验证，且直接从人肺单细胞 eQTL 出发、以 alveolar type 2 上皮为落点，对肺泡生物学与上皮-基质/免疫 crosstalk 方向有方法学与靶点双重参考价值；通讯作者为 Saferali Aabida、第一作者 Eberle Rahel，出自 COPD 遗传学与肺单细胞组学背景团队。
