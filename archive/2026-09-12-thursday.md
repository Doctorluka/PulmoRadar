### 1. Vascular smooth muscle cell-derived osteoprotegerin drives pulmonary arterial hypertension via AKT activation and is attenuated by monoclonal antibody therapy.

Cardiovascular research | 2026 Sep 07 | Merve Keles, Allan Lawrie
IF 2025：10.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Faculty of Medicine, National Heart and Lung Institute, Imperial College London, London, UK.
- 领域：Imperial College London 国家心肺研究所，研究方向为肺动脉高压的血管重塑机制与 OPG/TNFRSF11B 等分泌介质的细胞来源与信号通路。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42702807/
- DOI：10.1093/cvr/cvag197

**Abstract**

AIM: Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) is driven by sustained vasoconstriction and structural remodelling of distal pulmonary arteries. We have previously demonstrated that osteoprotegerin (OPG, tnfrsf11b) is upregulated in PAH animal models and patient tissues, with circulating levels being prognostic. OPG knockout mice exhibit an attenuated PAH phenotype, and therapeutic inhibition using a human monoclonal anti-OPG antibody reduces disease severity in multiple rodent models. However, the key cellular source of pathogenic OPG and the mechanism underlying therapeutic blockade remain unknown.
METHODS AND RESULTS: To define the main cellular source of OPG, conditional Tnfrsf11b deletion was generated by crossing OPGfl/fl mice with PGKcretg/+ (global), LysMcretg/+ (myeloid), Myl2cretg/+ (cardiac myocytes), Cdh5cretg/+ (endothelial cells, ECs), smMHCcretg/+ (VSMCs), Col1a2cretg/+ (fibroblasts, FBs). Knockouts were induced by tamoxifen injection, and PAH established using Sugen5416/hypoxia (SuHx) model. Cardiopulmonary phenotyping included echocardiography, cardiac catheterisation, and histological analysis. Mechanistic studies were performed in VSMCs using peptide-based kinase activity profiling (PamStation).PGKcretg/+/OPGfl/fl mice phenocopied the previous results obtained with global OPG knockout (OPG-/-). Only VSMC-specific deletion (smMHCcretg/0/OPGfl/fl) resulted in significantly improved haemodynamic parameters, evidenced by reduced RVSP and pulmonary vascular resistance (PVR), and reduced pulmonary vascular remodelling demonstrating that VSMC-derived OPG is the primary driver of PAH pathogenesis.Kinome profiling of control and IPAH-PASMCs treated with OPG +/- anti-OPG antibody revealed IPAH specific regulation of PI3K-Akt and enrichment of pathways involving autophagy, and longevity regulation linked to cellular stress resistance and growth. Anti-OPG antibody treatment reduced AKT phosphorylation in IPAH PASMCs. Knock-down of AKT1 by siRNA reduced OPG-induced proliferation and migration of human PASMC. These results align with emerging data implicating OPG in cellular senescence and vascular aging.
CONCLUSION: Together, these findings highlight VSMC-derived OPG as a critical mediator of pulmonary vascular remodelling in PAH pathogenesis and suggest that targeting OPG via a therapeutic antibody is mediated through PI3K-Akt signalling.

**What Is New?**
- 首次通过 six-way 细胞特异性条件性敲除（PGK、LysM、Myl2、Cdh5、smMHC、Col1a2）平行比较，明确只有 VSMC 来源的 Tnfrsf11b 缺失才能复现血流动力学改善，从而把致病性 OPG 的细胞来源锁定为 VSMC 而非内皮、髓系或成纤维细胞。
- 首次将 anti-OPG 单抗的治疗效应机制闭合到 IPAH-PASMC 中的 PI3K-Akt 轴，显示抗体处理降低 AKT 磷酸化、siRNA 敲低 AKT1 可抵消 OPG 诱导的增殖与迁移。
- 在 SuHx 模型中建立了 VSMC 特异性 OPG 缺失与 RVSP、PVR 下降及肺血管重塑减轻之间的因果链，而非仅停留于表达相关性。

**研究设计**
- 构建 Tnfrsf11b floxed 小鼠，分别与 PGK、LysM、Myl2、Cdh5、smMHC、Col1a2 Cre 品系杂交，tamoxifen 诱导条件性敲除。
- 以 Sugen5416/hypoxia（SuHx）建立 PAH 模型，进行超声心动图、心导管与组织学心肺表型分析（RVSP、PVR、肺血管重塑）。
- 在人 IPAH-PASMC 中用 OPG ± anti-OPG 抗体处理，行 peptide-based kinase activity profiling（PamStation）解析激酶组。
- 以 AKT1 siRNA 敲低验证 OPG 诱导的 PASMC 增殖与迁移是否依赖 AKT1。

**主要发现**
- PGK-Cre/OPGfl/fl 小鼠表型复现既往全球 OPG 敲除（OPG-/-）结果，验证条件性敲除体系有效。
- 六种细胞特异性敲除中仅 smMHC-Cre/OPGfl/fl（VSMC 来源）显著改善血流动力学，RVSP 与 PVR 下降，肺血管重塑减轻。
- 对照组与 IPAH-PASMC 的激酶组比较显示，OPG 处理引起 IPAH 特异性的 PI3K-Akt 调控，并富集 autophagy 及 longevity regulation 等与细胞应激抵抗和生长相关通路。
- anti-OPG 抗体处理降低 IPAH-PASMC 中 AKT 磷酸化水平。
- AKT1 siRNA 敲低抑制 OPG 诱导的人 PASMC 增殖与迁移，支持 PI3K-Akt 为下游必需节点。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整（细胞来源→分泌介质→激酶通路→靶点敲低验证），采用多 Cre 条件性敲除结合 SuHx 在体模型与患者来源 IPAH-PASMC，兼具 in vivo 因果性与人源细胞验证，并直接关联 anti-OPG 单抗这一治疗策略，机制与转化落点清晰。

**局限**
- 仅基于摘要，缺少全文的方法细节、样本量、统计效力与抗体剂量/给药方案等信息。
- 人源证据主要来自 IPAH-PASMC 体外实验，尚无 VSMC 特异性机制在人肺组织或人源体内模型中的直接验证。
- OPG 作用于 VSMC 的上游受体与 PI3K-Akt 之间的直接分子连接（如 RANK/RANKL 或其他受体）未在摘要中阐明。

**可借鉴之处**
- 细胞来源归因的 cre-lox 平行筛选范式值得借鉴：用同一 floxed 等位基因搭配多个组织特异性 Cre，在单一疾病模型中判定「哪种细胞的配体才是真正驱动者」，可用于成纤维细胞、免疫细胞或内皮来源的介质归因。
- 机制闭合路径可复用：先在患者来源细胞（IPAH-PASMC）做 peptide-based kinome profiling（PamStation）锁定通路，再用中和抗体 + siRNA 双向验证，形成「抗体表型—激酶谱—靶点敲低」的闭环。
- OPG 与 autophagy、longevity regulation、cellular senescence 通路的富集提示分泌型 TNF 受体家族配体可能通过衰老/应激抵抗程序参与肺血管重塑，为免疫-基质 crosstalk 与血管老化交叉研究提供切入点。

### 2. Mechanosensitive Stanniocalcin-1 Suppresses Pulmonary Artery Smooth Muscle Cell Proliferation and Attenuates Experimental Pulmonary Hypertension.

Circulation research | 2026 Sep 09 | Mariko Kogami, Utako Yokoyama
IF 2025：18.0 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Physiology (M.K., Y.K., S.I., K.U., H.I., Y.H., U.Y.), Tokyo Medical University, Japan.; Department of Respiratory Medicine (M.K., S.A.), Tokyo Medical University, Japan.
- 领域：第一作者主要研究领域：肺循环生理与呼吸病学交叉，聚焦肺动脉平滑肌细胞的机械力响应及肺动脉高压机制。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42713657/
- DOI：10.1161/CIRCRESAHA.125.327869

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Idiopathic pulmonary arterial hypertension (IPAH) is driven by progressive vascular remodeling, particularly smooth muscle cell (SMC) proliferation. Current combination vasodilator therapies have markedly improved outcomes; however, prognosis remains poor in subgroups such as patients with respiratory comorbidities. Elevation of intravascular hydrostatic pressure is a hallmark of IPAH, yet its direct role in pulmonary artery SMCs remains unclear. We aimed to identify pressure-responsive mediators using a newly developed hydrostatic pressurization system to model hypertensive hemodynamics.
METHODS: Pulmonary artery SMCs from 4 patients with IPAH were exposed to high hydrostatic pressure (70/40 mm Hg, 60 bpm). Transcriptomic profiling identified differentially expressed genes, which were validated by quantitative polymerase chain reaction. Functional studies included PIEZO1 (piezo type mechanosensitive ion channel component 1) modulation, rhSTC1 (recombinant human stanniocalcin-1) treatment, bromodeoxyuridine incorporation, and Western blotting for cell-cycle regulators. Chronic hypoxia-induced pulmonary hypertension was assessed in wild-type and Stc1-/- mice by hemodynamic and histological analyses, with or without intratracheal rhSTC1 administration.
RESULTS: RNA sequencing revealed STC1 to be a pressure-induced gene in IPAH SMCs. PIEZO1 activation upregulated STC1, whereas knockdown blunted this response. STC1 was upregulated in IPAH lungs, while rhSTC1 reduced pulmonary arterial SMC proliferation and increased p-p53, p21, and p27 expression. Stc1-/- mice under hypoxia exhibited significantly higher right ventricular systolic pressure and greater pulmonary arterial medial thickness than wild-type mice. CD68-positive macrophages were increased in Stc1-/- mice under normoxia and further elevated with hypoxia. Intratracheal administration of rhSTC1 attenuated PAH in wild-type and Stc1-/- mice.
CONCLUSIONS: Elevated hydrostatic pressure drives STC1 expression via PIEZO1, suggesting an adaptive but insufficient protective response in IPAH. Modulation of STC1 (stanniocalcin-1) may represent a potential therapeutic approach.

**What Is New?**
- 本研究利用高静水压搏动系统在IPAH患者来源PASMC中鉴定STC1为压力诱导基因，将肺血管SMC的机械力暴露直接连接到转录适应程序，而非仅关注体液因子或遗传变异。
- 研究定义PIEZO1–STC1为压力感知至STC1表达的机械转导轴：PIEZO1激活上调STC1，而PIEZO1敲低削弱该反应，闭合了上游力学信号与下游STC1诱导之间的机制环节。
- 通过Stc1-/-小鼠缺氧模型和气管内rhSTC1补充实验，研究把STC1从表达变化提升为抑制PASMC增殖、增加p-p53/p21/p27并减轻实验性PH的因果保护节点，同时提示其在IPAH中代偿不足。

**研究设计**
- 人IPAH来源PASMC（n=4）施以高静水压（70/40 mmHg，60 bpm）。
- RNA sequencing筛选差异基因，qPCR验证。
- PIEZO1激活/敲低、rhSTC1处理、BrdU掺入和细胞周期调控蛋白Western blot。
- WT与Stc1-/-小鼠慢性缺氧PH模型，血流动力学与组织学评估。
- 气管内给予rhSTC1，在WT和Stc1-/-小鼠中评估PH表型。

**主要发现**
- RNA sequencing显示STC1是IPAH PASMC中的压力诱导基因。
- PIEZO1激活上调STC1，PIEZO1敲低削弱压力诱导的STC1表达。
- STC1在IPAH肺中上调；rhSTC1降低肺动脉SMC增殖并增加p-p53、p21和p27表达。
- 缺氧下Stc1-/-小鼠较WT有更高RVSP和更重肺动脉中膜增厚；CD68阳性巨噬细胞在常氧下已增多，缺氧后进一步升高。
- 气管内rhSTC1减轻WT和Stc1-/-小鼠的PAH。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整：机械压力–PIEZO1–STC1–细胞周期抑制–PASMC增殖–实验性PH；结合人IPAH细胞、Stc1-/-遗传学、慢性缺氧模型及rhSTC1 rescue，并有CD68巨噬细胞表型；发表于Circulation Research，基础研究价值高。评估基于摘要，无OA全文。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无OA全文，无法评估样本量、统计方法、压力系统细节、PIEZO1干预特异性和完整机制数据。
- 人IPAH PASMC仅4例，体外高静水压系统不能完全模拟IPAH复杂微环境；小鼠模型为慢性缺氧PH，非IPAH。
- STC1下游受体/信号、巨噬细胞增多与SMC增殖的因果关系，以及rhSTC1给药途径的转化相关性尚不明确。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可复用「高静水压搏动培养 + RNA-seq/qPCR + 机械通道（如PIEZO1）药理学或敲低」策略筛选压力响应基因，并用BrdU和p-p53/p21/p27 Western验证增殖与细胞周期机制。
- 可借鉴「Stc1-/- + 慢性缺氧 + 重组蛋白气管内回补」的因果验证框架：先看RVSP和肺血管中膜厚度，再结合CD68巨噬细胞评估免疫–血管壁crosstalk。
- 对人IPAH PASMC、小鼠缺氧PH模型及重组STC1局部给药的组合，可启发肺血管remodeling中机械力–SMC–免疫细胞交互研究，但需在自身模型中验证STC1轴是否适用。

### 3. MicroRNA-224 orchestrates BMP and TGFβ signaling and is a therapeutic target in preclinical pulmonary arterial hypertension.

Science translational medicine | 2026 Sep 02 | Olympia Bikou, Yassine Sassi
IF 2025：15.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai, New York, NY, USA.; Department of Medicine I, LMU University Hospital, Munich, Germany.
- 领域：据其 Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai 与 LMU University Hospital Munich 的隶属关系及本文内容推断，主要研究领域为肺动脉高压等肺血管疾病的 microRNA 与分子机制；摘要未明示，属推断。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42685153/
- DOI：10.1126/scitranslmed.aef6676

**Abstract**

Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) is a progressive pulmonary vascular disease that leads to right heart failure and ultimately death. Therapeutic options targeting the underlying mechanisms of the disease are urgently needed. MicroRNAs (miRs) have emerged as critical regulators of cardiovascular homeostasis and disease. Here, we identified microRNA-224-5p (miR-224) as a regulator of pulmonary vascular remodeling and delineate its mechanism of action in PAH. miR-224 expression was increased in the lungs of patients with PAH and across multiple experimental models of pulmonary hypertension, including mouse, rat, and pig models, as well as in pulmonary arterial smooth muscle cells (PASMCs) isolated from patients with PAH. In vitro, miR-224 overexpression was sufficient to induce PASMC proliferation. In vivo, adeno-associated virus 1 (AAV1)-mediated overexpression of miR-224 exacerbated PAH in mice, whereas intratracheal delivery of aerosolized AAV1-ToughDecoy-miR-224 or a chemically modified antisense oligonucleotide targeting miR-224 (LNA-224) attenuated disease severity in Sugen/Hypoxia (Su/Hx) and monocrotaline models in both mice and rats. Moreover, SMC-specific inhibition of miR-224 via an AAV1 vector expressing ToughDecoy-miR-224 reversed pulmonary vascular remodeling and improved right ventricular function in the Su/Hx mouse model. Mechanistically, miR-224 targeted multiple components of the bone morphogenetic protein (BMP)/transforming growth factor-β (TGFβ) signaling pathway, leading to suppressed BMP/SMAD signaling and enhanced TGFβ-associated responses. Inhibition of miR-224 restored the balance between growth-inhibitory BMP signaling and growth-promoting TGFβ signaling in PASMCs. Collectively, these findings identify miR-224 as a regulator of pulmonary vascular remodeling and highlight miR-224 inhibition as a promising therapeutic strategy for PAH.

**What Is New?**
- 首次将 microRNA-224-5p 定位为肺血管重塑的调控节点，并在 PAH 患者肺组织、患者来源 PASMC 以及 mouse、rat、pig 多种实验模型中一致验证其升高，构成跨物种的人源—动物双重证据链。
- 机制上实现闭环：miR-224 同时靶向 BMP/TGFβ 通路的多个组分，造成 BMP/SMAD 生长抑制信号下调与 TGFβ 促生长反应增强，从而把 PASMC 增殖表型归因于一个可调控的双通路失衡节点，而非单个通路异常。
- 治疗学层面提供新证据：气溶胶化 AAV1-ToughDecoy-miR-224 与化学修饰反义寡核苷酸 LNA-224 在 Su/Hx 与 monocrotaline 两种模型、小鼠与大鼠两个物种中均减轻病情，且 PASMC 特异性的 miR-224 抑制可逆转肺血管重塑并改善右心室功能。

**研究设计**
- 人源样本：PAH 患者肺组织与患者来源 PASMC 中检测 miR-224 表达。
- 多物种表达验证：mouse、rat、pig 多种实验性肺高压模型。
- 体外功能：miR-224 过表达是否足以诱导 PASMC 增殖。
- 在体 gain-of-function：AAV1 介导 miR-224 过表达是否加重小鼠 PAH。
- 在体 loss-of-function 与治疗：气溶胶 AAV1-ToughDecoy-miR-224、LNA-224，以及 SMC 特异性 AAV1-ToughDecoy。
- 模型覆盖：Sugen/Hypoxia 与 monocrotaline，小鼠与大鼠；读出包括疾病严重度、肺血管重塑与右心室功能。
- 机制读出：BMP/SMAD 与 TGFβ 相关反应，及其在 miR-224 抑制后的再平衡。

**主要发现**
- miR-224 在 PAH 患者肺组织、患者来源 PASMC 及 mouse、rat、pig 多种肺高压模型中表达升高。
- 体外 miR-224 过表达足以诱导 PASMC 增殖，提示其具备促增殖的充分性。
- AAV1 介导的 miR-224 过表达加重小鼠 PAH；相反，气溶胶化 AAV1-ToughDecoy-miR-224 或 LNA-224 在 Su/Hx 与 monocrotaline 小鼠及大鼠模型中均减轻疾病严重程度。
- SMC 特异性抑制 miR-224（AAV1-ToughDecoy-miR-224）可逆转肺血管重塑并改善 Su/Hx 小鼠的右心室功能。
- 机制上 miR-224 靶向 BMP/TGFβ 通路多个组分，导致 BMP/SMAD 信号受抑、TGFβ 相关反应增强；抑制 miR-224 可恢复生长抑制性 BMP 信号与促生长性 TGFβ 信号之间的平衡。

**为什么入选** ['具备完整因果链：人组织与患者细胞表达异常 → 体外充分性 → 在体过表达加重 → 在体抑制 rescue。', '跨物种（人、mouse、rat、pig）与跨模型（Su/Hx、monocrotaline）验证，且包含 SMC 特异性干预，机制归因明确。', '机制落在 BMP 与 TGFβ 双通路失衡这一肺血管重塑核心节点上，并给出可吸入递送的治疗性策略，转化属性强。', '发表于 Science Translational Medicine，兼具机制深度与临床前转化证据。']

**局限**
- 本次评估仅基于摘要，无 OA 全文，样本量、统计方法、具体靶基因与 3'UTR/luciferase 验证形式、miR-224 直接靶点的逐条证据均无法核实。
- pig 模型似乎仅用于表达验证，未纳入干预实验；右心室功能改善的结论仅在 Su/Hx 小鼠中展示，范围有限。
- 摘要未提供长期给药的安全性、脱靶效应、AAV1 递送效率与免疫原性数据，也未说明是否评估了内皮、成纤维细胞等其他细胞类型的贡献。

**可借鉴之处**
- miR-224 属于同时作用于 BMP 与 TGFβ 两条通路的“节点型” miRNA，提示在 BMPR2 轴失调相关肺血管疾病中，可从 miRNA 层面寻找上游干预点，而非仅针对单一配体或受体。
- 在体功能验证采用 gain-of-function（AAV1 过表达）与 loss-of-function（ToughDecoy、LNA 反义寡核苷酸）并行，并叠加细胞类型特异性启动子与气溶胶吸入递送，是可复用于其他 miRNA 或小 RNA 研究的完整设计范式。
- 该工作聚焦 PASMC，尚未涉及内皮、成纤维细胞或免疫—基质 crosstalk；若结合 scRNA-seq 与谱系追踪界定 miR-224 的细胞来源与旁分泌效应，可延伸至肺泡重塑与肺纤维化相关方向的研究。

### 1. Monocyte-derived galectin-1hi cells provide innate immune help in the generation of functional memory CD8+ T cells.

Nature immunology | 2026 Aug 31 | Kihong Lim#, Minsoo Kim#
IF 2025：27.7 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Microbiology and Immunology, University of Rochester, Rochester, NY, USA. kihong_lim@urmc.rochester.edu.
- 领域：感染免疫学/黏膜免疫学方向，Department of Microbiology and Immunology, University of Rochester，具体研究主题不详。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42675114/
- DOI：10.1186/s12864-018-4772-0

**Abstract**

After viral infections, tissue-resident memory T cells (TRM cells) are generated and reactivated rapidly upon re-exposure to previously encountered viral pathogens, providing immediate immune effector functions to limit infection at the site of viral entry. Here we found that a subset of newly recruited CCR2+ monocytes differentiated into memory-stage CCR2-tdTomato+ cells and persisted in the lung for more than 4 months after infection with the influenza virus. Selective depletion of the memory-stage CCR2-tdTomato+ cells reduced significantly the formation of lung CD8+ TRM cells and compromised secondary heterosubtypic immune protection. Memory-stage CCR2-tdTomato+ cells colocalized with lung CD8+ TRM cells and secreted galectin-1, which activated CD8+ T cells directly and enhanced transforming growth factor-β sensing. Intranasal administration of recombinant galectin-1 as an adjuvant for the influenza vaccine induced superior memory CD8+ T cell responses. Thus, the presence of a unique memory-like monocyte-derived subset provided crucial signals to establish and maintain functional CD8+ TRM cells in the lung.

RESULTS: In PAH lungs, E2F1 was increased and arterial endothelial cell (AEC) fraction and expanded arterial program scores were elevated. Similarly, Egln1 Tie2Cre lungs showed increased E2F1, induction of arterial remodeling genes, and activation of an E2F target program. Genetic loss of E2f1 reduced right ventricle systolic pressure, right ventricle hypertrophy, vascular remodeling, and distal muscularization in Egln1-driven PH mice model. Bulk RNA-seq showed suppression of E2F, mitotic, epithelial mesenchymal transition, and extracellular matrix-remodeling programs. Single-cell RNA-seq showed reduced AEC accumulation, normalized CAP1/CAP2 distribution, and reduced progression along the CAP1-AEC trajectory. CellChat analysis identified loss of an arterial communication hub, including reduced ECM, VEGF, and Notch signaling when E2F1 is loss. Conversely, E2F1 overexpression in human lung microvascular ECs increased proliferation, activated E2F/cell-cycle and Notch/arterial programs. Pharmacological inhibition of E2F via HLM006474 suppressed endothelial proliferation and attenuated Egln1-driven and MCT-induced PH, including reversal of established MCT-PH.
CONCLUSIONS: E2F1 acts as a disease-relevant transcriptional factor linking endothelial cell-cycle activation to arterial programming, matrix and angiogenic communication programs, and pulmonary vascular remodeling. Genetic or pharmacological E2F inhibition mitigates experimental PH, supporting E2F1 as a therapeutic target in PAH.

**What Is New?**

**Abstract**

UNLABELLED: Upon injury to the distal lung, alveolar type 2 cells (AT2s) must make a discrete switch from surfactant factories to stem cells capable of regeneration, which involves both proliferation and differentiation into oxygen-exchanging alveolar type 1 (AT1) cells. However, the discrete signals and molecular pathways facilitating this fundamental switch in AT2 functionality are uncertain. Here we demonstrate that the bioactive lipid lysophosphatidic acid (LPA), typically associated with driving fibrosis, is an extremely efficient inducer of this state change in comparison to previously implicated signals IL-1β and p53 stabilization. We observed endogenous production and accumulation of LPA in influenza-injured murine lungs, creating a microenvironment that facilitates AT2 progenitor switching. Multiple transcriptomic approaches reveal elevation of Fosl1 and Jun, core members of the Activator Protein 1 (AP-1) transcription factor family, in response to LPA. Using novel genetic models combined with influenza injury, we demonstrate that AP-1 activity in AT2s is necessary for effective alveolar regeneration at both the cellular and physiologic levels. These findings unveil a critical relationship between paracrine LPA and cell-intrinsic AP-1 in facilitating effective lung alveolar regeneration.
HIGHLIGHTS: LPA facilitates progenitor switching in lung regeneration via initiation of a discrete transcriptomic stateLPA promotes AT2 state switching via Jun (AP-1)Impaired AP-1 signaling significantly restricts recovery from influenza infection.

**What Is New?**
- 首次把 bioactive lipid lysophosphatidic acid (LPA) 定位为比 IL-1β 与 p53 stabilization 更高效的 AT2 祖细胞状态切换诱导信号，并证明其在 influenza 损伤小鼠肺内原位生成与累积，从而构成促再生的微环境。
- 新定义了 AT2 细胞内在的 AP-1（核心成员 Jun 与 Fosl1）为肺泡再生的必需因果节点，而非仅仅是损伤后的伴随性转录反应。
- 闭合了「旁分泌 LPA—细胞内在 AP-1—AT2 状态切换—肺泡再生」这一机制链，并在细胞层面与生理（肺功能）层面同时给出必要性证据。

**研究设计**
- 以流感（influenza）损伤小鼠肺为体内再生模型，结合 AT2 祖细胞状态切换读出。
- 检测损伤肺内 LPA 的内源性生成与累积，并在体外比较 LPA、IL-1β、p53 stabilization 诱导状态切换的效率。
- 多种 transcriptomic 手段（含单细胞/转录组层面分析）鉴定 LPA 下游 Fosl1 与 Jun（AP-1）升高。
- 构建新型遗传模型，在 AT2 中干预 AP-1 活性，评估细胞与生理层面的肺泡再生。

**主要发现**
- LPA 在流感损伤小鼠肺内源性产生并累积，形成有利于 AT2 祖细胞切换的微环境。
- 与 IL-1β 和 p53 stabilization 相比，LPA 是极高效的 AT2 由表面活性物质合成细胞向再生干细胞状态切换的诱导剂。
- LPA 处理后多种转录组分析一致显示 AP-1 家族核心成员 Fosl1 与 Jun 上调。
- AT2 中的 AP-1 活性对有效的肺泡再生是必需的，缺失或抑制会显著限制流感感染后的恢复。
- 由此提出 LPA 启动离散转录状态、并经 Jun(AP-1) 促成 AT2 状态切换的机制模型。

**为什么入选** 该预印本给出了一条较完整的再生机制链：从损伤肺内源性脂质信号（LPA）到细胞内在转录因子（AP-1/Jun/Fosl1），再到 AT2 祖细胞状态切换与肺泡再生的细胞与生理双重终点；且以新型遗传模型 + influenza 损伤在体内验证必要性，并将 LPA 与既往候选信号 IL-1β、p53 stabilization 做了效率比较，对肺泡上皮再生与损伤修复研究具有明确的机制参考价值。
