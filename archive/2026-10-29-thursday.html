A GCN1-independent activator of the kinase GCN2.

杂志eLife
IF: 6.4 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院2区 · 新锐2区
文章2026 Oct 27
10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.122.059594
作者JiaYi Zhu* · Giulia Emanuelli* · Stefan J Marciniak
机构Cambridge Institute for Medical Research (CIMR), University of Cambridge, Cambridge, United Kingdom.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 通过in cellulo化学筛选发现三种结构不同、在低微摩尔浓度即可刺激GCN2活性的小分子，扩展了GCN2药理激活剂的结构类型。
  • 鉴定出一个区别于既往GCN2激活剂的分子，可在不依赖GCN1的情况下激活GCN2，从而把GCN2激活与GCN1衔接需求解耦。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Mutations of EIF2AK4, which encodes the eIF2α kinase GCN2, cause a severe inherited form of pulmonary hypertension called pulmonary veno-occlusive disease (PVOD). Some pathogenic variants of GCN2 are amenable to pharmacological reactivation by low concentrations of ATP pocket-binding inhibitors. Kinase inhibition at modestly elevated concentrations limits the clinical utility of these drugs against PVOD. We therefore performed an in cellulo chemical screen for GCN2 activators and identified three structurally distinct compounds with low micromolar stimulatory activities. Unlike previously described GCN2 activators, one of these molecules activated GCN2 independently of GCN1. Modelling supported by structure activity screens suggested it binds within the ATP pocket of GCN2, but unlike existing ligands does not protrude inward into the allosteric pocket or outward into the solvent. This overcomes a key requirement of other GCN2 activators.

Identification of a Novel EBF1-Expressing Disease-Associated Arterial Subpopulation in Pulmonary Arterial Hypertension.

杂志Circulation research
IF: 18.0 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院1区 · 新锐1区
文章2026 Oct 23
10.1161/CIRCRESAHA.125.328069
作者Woosoung Choi* · Dayeon Lee* · Aram Lee* · Eunsik Yun* · Suk-Won Jin
机构Department of Life Sciences (W.C., D.L., G.L., S.J., B.P., M.K., H.C., K.Y., Y.-S.J., J.C., J.P., S.-W.J.), Gwangju Institute of Science and Technology, Republic of Korea.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 在Sugen5416/hypoxia大鼠PH病程4个时间点构建肺内皮单细胞多组学图谱，定义了随阶段演进而出现的EBF1+疾病相关动脉内皮亚群，而非仅描述已知内皮异质性。

  • 在人类PAH单细胞数据和免疫荧光中验证该亚群及EBF1升高，并在造模前和成病后均进行体内Ebf1敲低rescue，提供跨物种、时序和干预层面的严格验证。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

BACKGROUND: Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) is a debilitating disease with a poor prognosis. However, only a limited number of palliative interventions are currently available. To address this unmet need, it is essential to understand the molecular causes of PAH by identifying unique disease-associated populations and their key molecular regulators during disease progression.

METHODS: We constructed a single-cell multiome data set at 4 distinct time points during the pathogenesis of pulmonary hypertension (PH) using endothelial cells isolated from the Sugen5416/hypoxia rat model. We assessed the conservation of novel disease-associated endothelial cells by reanalyzing previously reported single-cell RNA sequencing data from human patients with PAH. We validated the functional importance of EBF1 (early B-cell factor 1) in PAH by performing in silico simulations and an in vivo knockdown mediated by a pulmonary endothelium-targeting associated virus.

RESULTS: We found that PH progression gradually altered the transcriptional and epigenetic landscape of the pulmonary endothelium in a stage-specific manner, leading to the emergence of a novel disease-associated arterial endothelial population expressing EBF1. A similar endothelial subset was present in human patients with PAH. Immunofluorescence revealed that EBF1 expression was elevated in both human and rat lungs during PH progression. In silico simulations suggested that EBF1 is required for the pathogenic state, and the associated virus-mediated in vivo knockdown of Ebf1 ameliorated the pathological hallmarks of PH, including right ventricular systolic pressure and right ventricular hypertrophy, when delivered either before the onset of PH or after its establishment.

CONCLUSIONS: We identified EBF1 as a key transcriptional regulator of a novel disease-associated population in both humans and rats. We further demonstrated that inhibiting EBF1 within the pulmonary endothelium ameliorates the pathological features of PH, suggesting that EBF1 and its transcriptional targets represent a novel and promising therapeutic avenue for PAH.

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

RATIONALE: Immune dysregulation is a pivotal driver of pulmonary hypertension (PH) pathogenesis, yet therapeutic targeting of inflammation has yielded limited clinical success. Although cytosolic RNA-binding proteins are known to regulate immune responses in PH, the specific role of the nuclear m⁶A reader YTHDC1 remains undefined.

OBJECTIVES: To elucidate the function of macrophage YTHDC1 in orchestrating inflammatory vascular remodeling during PH.

METHODS: Expression profiles of YTHDC1 and TP53-induced glycolysis and apoptosis regulator (TIGAR) were evaluated in human and rodent PH specimens. The mechanistic and therapeutic roles of this axis were assessed using myeloid-specific knockout mice and pharmacological inhibitors (YL-5092, 5Z-7-oxozeaenol) across hypoxia-, SU5416/hypoxia-, and monocrotaline-induced PH models.

MEASUREMENTS AND MAIN RESULTS: In patients with idiopathic or connective tissue disease-associated PAH, YTHDC1 and TIGAR expression levels were elevated in pulmonary and circulating macrophages, correlating with disease severity. In murine models, myeloid-specific deletion of Ythdc1 or Tigar conferred profound protection against hypoxia- and SU5416/hypoxia-induced PH. Mechanistically, hypoxia upregulated Ythdc1, which directly bound and stabilized Tigar mRNA. The subsequent accumulation of Tigar protein operated non-enzymatically via a specific domain (residues 152-161) to interact with Tak1, thereby activating the NF-κB cascade, augmenting pro-inflammatory cytokine release, and driving pulmonary artery smooth muscle cell proliferation. Pharmacological inhibition of YTHDC1 (YL-5092) or disruption of the TIGAR-TAK1 interaction (5Z-7-oxozeaenol) effectively attenuated vascular inflammation and mitigated PH progression in diverse rodent models.

CONCLUSIONS: The macrophage YTHDC1/TIGAR/NF-κB signaling axis acts as a critical determinant of inflammation and vascular remodeling in PH, representing a potential biomarker and therapeutic target for this disease.

Distinct regulation of influenza HA head- versus stem-specific B cell responses in HA mRNA-LNP vaccinated and naïve infant African green monkeys.

杂志Mucosal immunology
IF: 7.6 · JCR: Q1 · 中科院2区 · 新锐2区
文章2026 Oct 21
10.1016/j.mucimm.2026.100420
作者Courtney L Page · Martha A Alexander-Miller
机构Department of Microbiology and Immunology, Wake Forest University School of Medicine, Winston-Salem, North Carolina, United States.

AI 解读 · WHAT IS NEW?

  • 首次在新生非人灵长类体内证明，HA mRNA-LNP 免疫后再经呼吸道感染，可在第 7 天肺引流淋巴结中观察到 class-switched、亲和成熟的 ASC 扩增，符合记忆 B 细胞回忆并快速分化为 ASC 的过程。
  • 明确了疫苗带来的亲和力优势在表位层面并不统一：该优势延伸到 HA head 特异性 germinal center B 细胞，却不延伸到 HA stem 结合细胞。
  • 揭示了一个此前未预料到的基线差异：未免疫新生动物的 HA stem 结合 GC B 细胞表观亲和力反而更高，从而解释了 stem 特异性应答不受疫苗增益的原因，将 head 与 stem 应答区分为两条不同的调控路径。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

The generation of memory B cells following influenza virus vaccination and their rapid recruitment into the virus-specific immune response following infection is an essential component of vaccine-induced protection. We have previously reported the capacity of an influenza hemagglutinin (HA) expressing mRNA-LNP vaccine administered to newborn African green monkeys (AGM) to elicit both HA head- and stem-specific antibodies that exhibit a wide array of effector functions. Vaccine-induced responses culminated in enhanced protection compared to non-vaccinated newborn AGM. These results were noteworthy given the lack of an approved influenza vaccine for this age group. Here, we evaluated early antibody and cellular responses following respiratory infection of vaccinated versus naïve animals to gain insights into the events that may be associated with the improved protection in vaccinated newborns. Seven days following challenge, we find an increase in the number of class-switched, affinity matured antibody secreting cells in the lung draining lymph nodes of vaccinated newborns, consistent with recall of memory B cells and rapid differentiation into antibody secreting cells (ASCs). Our data are consistent with a vaccine-mediated affinity advantage in the ASC population. While this benefit extended to HA head-specific germinal center B cells, it was not the case for cells binding HA stem. This was the result of unexpectedly higher apparent affinity of these cells in naïve animals. These data extend our understanding of how vaccination and infection shape activation and differentiation of the head- and stem-specific responses in infants following influenza virus infection.

AI 解读 · 研究设计

  • 新生 African green monkey 分为 HA mRNA-LNP 免疫组与未免疫组，随后进行呼吸道流感病毒感染攻击。
  • 攻击后第 7 天取肺引流淋巴结，分析 class-switched、亲和成熟的 antibody secreting cells（ASC）。
  • 将 HA head 结合与 HA stem 结合的特异性 B 细胞分开，分别评估其 GC B 细胞频数与表观亲和力，并与既往保护数据衔接。

AI 解读 · 主要发现

  • 免疫组新生动物在攻击后第 7 天肺引流淋巴结中 class-switched、亲和成熟 ASC 数量增加。
  • ASC 群体呈现疫苗介导的亲和力优势，符合记忆 B 细胞快速回忆分化。

原文摘要 · ABSTRACT

Pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH) is associated with increased oxidative DNA damage, while haploinsufficiency of BMPR2 and other dosage-sensitive genes contributes directly to disease. Template-strand 8-oxoguanine (8-oxoG) provides a potential transient route to the same loss-of-function outcome through transcriptional mutagenesis without altering genomic DNA. RNA polymerase II can insert adenine opposite the lesion and generate a coding-sense C>A error in the nascent transcript. We asked whether this chemistry could generate premature-stop transcripts in dosage-sensitive PAH genes. Across 17,880 C>A-accessible coding positions in pulmonary vascular genes, 1,192 could generate premature termination codons, including 883 predicted to engage nonsense-mediated decay (NMD). Restricting these sites to expressed autosomal-dominant PAH genes with definitive ClinGen validity and established haploinsufficiency identified 116 NMD-prone positions in BMPR2, KDR, SMAD9, and TBX4. Eight exactly reproduce ClinVar pathogenic or likely pathogenic stop variants, including five in BMPR2. At BMPR2 c.1424, template-strand 8-oxoG and the expert-panel-reviewed genomic c.1424C>A allele is predicted to generate the same UAA-containing transcript. Published oxidized-guanine maps detect signal at seven of the eight coordinates, providing coordinate-level support for oxidative modification at these sites. These findings define a sequence-resolved route through which repairable oxidative DNA damage could transiently reproduce loss-of-function transcripts encoded by pathogenic PAH alleles without permanent genomic mutation.
