### 1. Resveratrol attenuates LPS/CSE-induced macrophage ferroptosis through activation of the Nrf2-ferroportin axis.

International immunopharmacology | 2026 Sep 11 | Zhuozhe Li, Zhihong Chen#, Zhilong Jiang#
IF 2025：4.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Pulmonary and Critical Care Medicine, Zhongshan Hospital, Fudan University, Shanghai, China.
- 领域：中山医院呼吸与危重症医学科，方向为 COPD/AECOPD 的炎症与氧化应激机制（据科室与摘要推断，具体方向不详）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42727368/
- DOI：10.1016/j.intimp.2026.117380

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Resveratrol (Res) has therapeutic effects in a mouse model with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). However, the role of ferroportin (FPN) in the Res-mediated therapeutic effects remains unclear.
PURPOSE: We investigated the role and signaling pathways of FPN in macrophages and a mouse model with acute exacerbation of COPD (AECOPD) after Res treatment.
STUDY DESIGN/METHODS: Res was intraperitoneal (i.p.) injected into AECOPD-like mouse model or added into cultured macrophages prior to LPS/CSE treatment. Macrophages with FPN knockdown and over-expression were established by infection of the cells with lentiviral vector encoding FPN shRNA and cDNA.
RESULTS: Res i.p. suppressed pulmonary infiltration of neutrophils and emphysema in the LPS/CSE-treated mice, in association with upregulated expression of FPN. LPS, CSE and their mixture increased the expression of FPN, TNF-α and IL-6 in macrophages, with increased hemosiderin deposits, reactive oxygen species (ROS) and malondialdehyde (MDA), but the effects were reversed by pre-treatment with Res. In addition, Res activated nuclear factor erythroid 2-related factor 2 (Nrf2) and NAD-dependent protein deacetylase sirtuin-1 (SIRT1), that was reversed by their inhibitors Brusatol and EX527, and subsequently abolished Res-induced upregulation of FPN in the treated cells. Knock-down of FPN partially reduced Res-mediated suppression of macrophage ferroptosis, whereas over-expression of FPN enhanced the effects. Adoptive transfer of FPN over-expressing bone marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs) reduced lung inflammation and injury, in association with reduced lung neutrophil infiltrates, and expression of TNF-α, IL-6 in the treated mice.
CONCLUSION: Res suppressed AECOPD partially through activation of Nrf2/FPN signaling and subsequent suppression of macrophage ferroptosis. FPN would be a potential therapeutic target in the treatment of AECOPD.

**What Is New?**
- 首次把 ferroportin (FPN) 确立为 Res 治疗 AECOPD 的必要下游节点：lentiviral FPN shRNA 敲低部分削弱、FPN cDNA 过表达增强 Res 对巨噬细胞铁死亡的抑制作用，将此前仅属伴随变化的 FPN 表达升级为因果性调控环节。
- 新定义了一条 Nrf2/SIRT1→FPN 的上游调控链：Brusatol 抑制 Nrf2、EX527 抑制 SIRT1 均可消除 Res 诱导的 FPN 上调，从而把 Res 的抗氧化/去乙酰化活性与细胞铁外排机制连成闭合通路。
- 新增体内因果验证：过表达 FPN 的 bone marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs) 过继转移即可在无 Res 条件下复制其减轻肺损伤、中性粒细胞浸润及 TNF-α/IL-6 表达的效应，为 FPN 作为 AECOPD 可干预靶点提供了严格的 in vivo 证据。

**研究设计**
- AECOPD 样小鼠模型（LPS/CSE 刺激）+ Res 腹腔注射（i.p.）干预。
- 体外培养巨噬细胞，以 LPS、CSE 或二者混合物刺激，Res 预处理。
- lentiviral vector 介导 FPN shRNA 敲低与 FPN cDNA 过表达。
- Nrf2 抑制剂 Brusatol、SIRT1 抑制剂 EX527 干预以定位上游。
- 过表达 FPN 的 BMDM 过继转移回小鼠。
- 读出：肺中性粒细胞浸润、肺气肿、FPN/TNF-α/IL-6 表达、hemosiderin 沉积、ROS、MDA。

**主要发现**
- Res i.p. 抑制 LPS/CSE 小鼠肺内中性粒细胞浸润并减轻肺气肿，同时伴随 FPN 表达上调。
- LPS、CSE 及其混合物升高巨噬细胞 FPN、TNF-α、IL-6 表达及 hemosiderin 沉积、ROS、MDA 水平，Res 预处理可逆转上述效应。
- Res 激活 Nrf2 与 SIRT1；Brusatol 和 EX527 可逆转该激活，并随之取消 Res 诱导的 FPN 上调。
- FPN 敲低部分削弱、FPN 过表达增强 Res 对巨噬细胞铁死亡的抑制。
- 过表达 FPN 的 BMDM 过继转移减轻小鼠肺炎症与损伤，伴肺中性粒细胞浸润及 TNF-α、IL-6 表达下降。

**为什么入选** 机制链相对完整（Res→Nrf2/SIRT1→FPN→巨噬细胞铁死亡→肺损伤），同时具备双向遗传学调控与体内过继转移的功能 rescue 验证，指向 AECOPD 的潜在可干预靶点，属于有明确因果层级的动物+细胞联合研究。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要、无 OA 全文，无法核实剂量、时间点、样本量、统计方法，也未给出 GPX4、ACSL4、脂质过氧化等 ferroptosis 分子标志的数据。
- 结论全部来自小鼠模型与体外细胞，未见人肺组织或患者样本验证，向人类 AECOPD 的外推性未知。
- 摘要显示 LPS/CSE 本身即上调 FPN、Res 又进一步上调 FPN，这一方向性看似矛盾，其机制解释（如应激代偿性上调与功能性铁外排的区别）在摘要中未阐明。

**可借鉴之处**
- lentiviral shRNA/cDNA 双向（loss- and gain-of-function）加 BMDM 过继转移的组合，是判定某分子为因果节点而非炎症伴随现象的高性价比实验范式，可直接迁移到肺泡/气道炎症模型中其他候选分子的验证。
- 以 LPS+CSE 双刺激叠加 ferroptosis 读出（hemosiderin 沉积、ROS、MDA），可作为巨噬细胞铁死亡研究的可复用刺激—表型体系；其中 iron handling（FPN 介导的铁外排）与脂质过氧化的耦合逻辑，对肺血管重塑及间质/免疫细胞铁代谢研究同样有参照价值。
- 先用通路抑制剂（Brusatol、EX527）阻断上游再评估下游 FPN，可用于确立多节点通路的层级关系；若读者关注成纤维细胞或免疫—间质 crosstalk，可借鉴同一'上游抑制—下游读出—细胞回输'的三步设计思路。

### 2. Lung-Targeted HGF mRNA Restores Alveolar Structure in Experimental Emphysema.

The European respiratory journal | 2026 May 01 | Dian Chen, Kai Wang
IF 2025：23.8 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：年内旗舰系统研究
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Respiratory and Critical Care Medicine, Peking University Third Hospital, Beijing, China.
- 领域：第一作者隶属于北京大学第三医院呼吸与危重症医学科，方向为临床呼吸病学，尤其 COPD/肺气肿相关研究。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42067211/
- DOI：10.1183/13993003.02323-2025

**Abstract**

BACKGROUND: Emphysema, a major component of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) characterized by progressive alveolar destruction, lacks effective medical therapies. Hepatocyte growth factor (HGF) possesses potent regenerative functions, but its therapeutic potential remains unrealized due to challenges in achieving targeted delivery and sustained lung expression.
METHODS: We first assessed associations between HGF expression and emphysema severity using human datasets, lung tissue, and both elastase-induced and cigarette-smoke-induced murine models. We repurposed a clinical-stage SM102 lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) platform to deliver human HGF mRNA in murine models, evaluating therapeutic efficacy via i.t. instillation in the elastase model. After optimizing nebulization, we assessed efficacy in the cigarette-smoke model. We investigated underlying mechanisms via single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq), which we validated in patient-derived lung organoids.
RESULTS: HGF expression displayed a biphasic pattern across the emphysema spectrum, with upregulation in milder disease states and marked reduction in advanced emphysema. i.t. delivery of HGF mRNA LNPs restored lung function and attenuated alveolar destruction in the elastase model. Nebulized delivery achieved efficient pulmonary distribution and demonstrated comparable therapeutic efficacy in the cigarette-smoke model, including improved lung function, reduced inflammation, and decreased apoptosis. scRNA-seq analysis detected enhanced alveolar type II (AT2) cell proliferation and differentiation in the elastase model and human organoids.
CONCLUSION: This study provides proof-of-concept evidence for a therapeutic strategy for emphysema. Using a clinical-stage LNPs platform, we demonstrate that HGF mRNA therapy is effective via both direct instillation and optimized nebulization, prompting structural and functional recovery by activating endogenous repair pathways in AT2 cells.

**What Is New?**
- 首次将 HGF 定位为肺气肿病程中阶段依赖的内源性修复节点——HGF 表达呈双相模式，轻中度病变上调而晚期显著下降，突破了既往仅将其视为单向保护因子的认识。
- 首次把临床阶段 SM102 lipid nanoparticles 平台用于人 HGF mRNA 的肺靶向递送，并证明优化雾化可达到与气管内滴注（i.t.）相当的结构与功能疗效，为蛋白类再生因子解决了靶向递送与持续肺内表达两大瓶颈。
- 机制上闭合到细胞层级：scRNA-seq 与人源肺 organoids 双重验证显示修复由 alveolar type II (AT2) 细胞增殖与分化增强介导，而非单纯抗炎或抗凋亡效应。
- 在 cigarette-smoke 诱导模型这一更贴近人类 COPD 病因的模型中完成雾化疗效验证，使结论从单一 elastase 破坏模型提升到跨模型的可重复性证据。

**研究设计**
- 人源数据 + 肺组织 + elastase 与 cigarette-smoke 两种小鼠模型，解析 HGF 表达与肺气肿严重度的关系。
- 以临床阶段 SM102 LNP 包裹人 HGF mRNA，elastase 模型经 i.t. 给药剂型验证，cigarette-smoke 模型经雾化给药验证。
- 终点覆盖肺功能、肺泡结构、炎症与凋亡指标。
- scRNA-seq 解析机制，并在 patient-derived lung organoids 中验证 AT2 细胞学表型。

**主要发现**
- 人类数据集、肺组织及两种小鼠模型均显示 HGF 表达随肺气肿严重度呈双相变化：轻症上调、晚期显著降低。
- i.t. 递送 HGF mRNA LNP 在 elastase 模型中恢复肺功能并减轻肺泡结构破坏。
- 雾化递送实现高效肺内分布，在 cigarette-smoke 模型中取得相当疗效，伴肺功能改善、炎症减轻与凋亡下降。
- scRNA-seq 显示处理组 AT2 细胞增殖与分化增强，该表型在人源肺 organoids 中得到验证。
- 总体构成肺气肿 mRNA 再生治疗的 proof-of-concept，说明疗效由激活 AT2 内源性修复通路而非直接抗损伤机制驱动。

**为什么入选** 选题切入点清晰：以"再生因子递送难题"为切口，用临床阶段 LNP 平台 + 双给药路径 + 双疾病模型 + scRNA-seq 与人 organoids 双验证，形成从人源相关性到机制细胞学再到功能 rescue 的较完整链条，是 mRNA 肺靶向递送在肺气肿方向少见的系统性 proof-of-concept。

**局限**
- 仅基于 PubMed abstract，无 OA 全文，具体剂量、LNP 表征、表达持续时间、雾化粒径与肺外分布等关键参数无法核实。
- 人源证据仅到 patient-derived lung organoids，缺少人体或大动物层面的递送与安全性数据，HGF 作为促增殖因子在肺内的长期风险未在摘要中讨论。
- 摘要未说明抗 LNP/HGF 免疫应答、重复给药可行性及真实世界中晚期肺气肿（HGF 已显著下降阶段）的疗效差异。

**可借鉴之处**
- LNP-mRNA 平台 + 雾化给药的组织靶向思路，可迁移到其他丢失型肺泡或肺血管重塑疾病的"补充缺失因子"研究范式，重点在于剂量-分布-持续表达三者的平衡优化。
- scRNA-seq 锁定驱动细胞类型、再由 patient-derived lung organoids 做功能验证的两步闭环，是解析"再生性治疗究竟作用于哪种细胞"的低成本高说服力设计，可复用于 fibroblast、AT2 或免疫-基质 crosstalk 相关课题。
- 用疾病分期分层描述靶点表达（双相模式）而非只做病例-对照比较，可避免"晚期下降即治疗无效"的误判，也提示干预窗口可能位于特定病程阶段。

### 3. SREBP-mediated Signaling Restores Stem Cell Niche Properties in Human Lung Fibroblasts.

American journal of respiratory cell and molecular biology | 2026 Feb 01 | Grégoire Justeau, Laurent Boyer
IF 2025：6.4 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：2区 · 新锐2026：2区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：领域经典
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Institut Mondor de Recherche Biomédicale, Inserm U955, University Paris Est Créteil, Fédération Hospitalo-Universitaire SENCODE, Creteil, France.; Department of Respiratory and Sleep Medicine, Angers University Hospital, Angers, France.
- 领域：呼吸与睡眠医学临床背景，结合 Inserm U955 实验室工作，聚焦气肿相关肺泡损伤与肺成纤维细胞/脂质代谢。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40920982/
- DOI：10.1165/rcmb.2024-0541OC

**Abstract**

Emphysema is characterized by chronic alveolar destruction. Lipofibroblasts (LIFs) are crucial in the stem cell niche surrounding alveolar type II cells and may contribute to alveolar regeneration. We aim to determine whether emphysema is associated with LIF reduction and whether SREBP (sterol regulatory element-binding protein) activation promotes LIF differentiation and fibroblast stem cell niche properties. We quantified LIFs in the lungs of patients with emphysema compared with controls by costaining vimentin and ADFP (adipose differentiation-related protein). Using available datasets, we explored the expression level of lipogenic pathways in mesenchymal cells. Fibroblasts from patients were isolated, and SREBP-mediated signaling was activated with an LXR (liver X receptor) agonist, T0901317, and compared with rosiglitazone, a PPAR-γ (peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor γ) agonist (gene expression and lipidomic analysis). The stem cell niche properties of fibroblasts were evaluated through coculture with the H441 cell line or primary alveolar type II cells in organoid assays. Patients with emphysema had half as many LIFs as controls. T0901317 induced lipogenic differentiation of human lung fibroblasts and increased triglyceride contents and several phosphatidylcholine forms, particularly dipalmitoylphosphatidylcholine (PC32_0), one of the main surfactant components. Rosiglitazone increased only ADFP expression, with minor effects on lipid components. SREBP mediated signaling in fibroblasts and, to a lesser extent, PPAR-γ activation and increased their stem cell niche properties through the increase of organoid numbers. LIFs are decreased in the alveoli of patients with emphysema. Activation of SREBP-mediated signaling promotes lipogenic differentiation of fibroblasts and enhances their stem cell niche properties.

**What Is New?**
- 首次在人肺组织中定量证明气肿患者肺泡区 LIF（vimentin/ADFP 共染）数量约为对照的一半，把“LIF 减少”从动物推测变为人类组织学证据。
- 新定义 SREBP/LXR 轴为人肺成纤维细胞脂质分化与 niche 功能的上游可控节点：T0901317 诱导甘油三酯及多种 phosphatidylcholine（尤其 PC32_0/DPPC）生成，而 PPAR-γ 激动剂 rosiglitazone 仅上调 ADFP、对脂质组成影响轻微，提示两条通路并非等价。
- 通过 H441 或原代 alveolar type II 细胞共培养 organoid 实验，把“脂质分化表型”与“stem cell niche 支持能力”机制性闭合，证明 SREBP 激活提高 organoid 数量而不仅是改变脂质标志物。

**研究设计**
- 人肺组织学：气肿患者 vs 对照，vimentin＋ADFP 共染定量 LIF。
- 公共数据集分析间充质细胞中 lipogenic pathway 表达。
- 患者来源肺成纤维细胞体外处理：T0901317（LXR 激动剂/SREBP 通路）对比 rosiglitazone（PPAR-γ 激动剂）。
- 读出：基因表达＋脂质组学（甘油三酯、phosphatidylcholine 组成）。
- 功能学：与 H441 细胞系或原代 alveolar type II 细胞共培养 organoid assay，计数 organoid 数量。

**主要发现**
- 气肿患者肺内 LIF 数量约为对照的一半。
- T0901317 诱导人肺成纤维细胞脂质分化，升高甘油三酯含量及多种 phosphatidylcholine，尤其 PC32_0（DPPC，主要 surfactant 成分之一）。
- Rosiglitazone 仅上调 ADFP 表达，对脂质组分影响轻微，说明 SREBP 与 PPAR-γ 通路在诱导脂质分化上效力不同。
- SREBP 介导的信号（以及程度较轻的 PPAR-γ 激活）提高成纤维细胞的 stem cell niche 特性，表现为共培养 organoid 数量增加。
- 结论：气肿肺泡 LIF 减少，激活 SREBP 信号可促进成纤维细胞脂质分化并增强其 niche 支持属性。

**为什么入选** 机制链完整且以人类组织为锚点：人肺气肿 LIF 减少 → LXR/SREBP 激动剂驱动脂质分化并改变脂质组 → organoid 共培养验证 niche 功能恢复，并有 PPAR-γ 激动剂作为通路特异性对照；主题直接落在 alveolar biology 与 fibroblast-上皮 niche 交叉。

**局限**
- 仅有摘要，无 OA 全文，实验细节（样本量、统计、细胞来源与传代、organoid 定量方式）无法核实。
- LIF 定量仅依赖 vimentin/ADFP 共染，缺乏谱系追踪或单细胞层面确认该细胞状态身份。
- 未提供气肿模型中的 in vivo 干预/rescue 数据，SREBP 激活能否在体内逆转肺泡破坏尚不清楚。

**可借鉴之处**
- 可复用的对照设计：以 LXR 激动剂 T0901317 与 PPAR-γ 激动剂 rosiglitazone 平行处理同一批人源肺成纤维细胞，用基因表达＋脂质组学区分 SREBP 依赖与非依赖效应，适合迁移到其他 fibroblast 状态重编程问题。
- organoid 共培养（成纤维细胞＋H441 或原代 ATII）可作为定量读出“niche 支持功能”的功能学终点，比单纯 marker 染色更接近因果；适合用于肺泡再生、immune-stromal crosstalk 或 pulmonary vascular remodeling 中基质细胞支持能力的评价。
- 脂质组学（triglyceride、phosphatidylcholine species 如 PC32_0）可作为 lipofibroblast/基质细胞状态的读出维度，与 scRNA-seq 中 lipogenic pathway 打分结合，用于判断成纤维细胞亚状态。

### 1. Markers of microvascular instability predict severity and survival in idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis.

Thorax | 2026 Sep 10 | David J Csordas, John S Kim#, Shayn M Peirce#
IF 2025：9.0 · JCR：Q1 · 中科院2025：1区 · 新锐2026：1区

- 来源：pubmed
- 槽位：近一月高质量
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Department of Biomedical Engineering, University of Virginia School of Engineering and Applied Science, Charlottesville, Virginia, USA.; Robert M. Berne Cardiovascular Research Center, University of Virginia School of Medicine, Charlottesville, Virginia, USA.
- 领域：根据单位与摘要，第一作者主要研究领域为生物医学工程与心血管/微血管生物学，采用蛋白质组学和多组学分析疾病相关微血管标志物。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42722423/
- DOI：10.1136/thorax-2026-225147

**Abstract**

INTRODUCTION: Most research on idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis (IPF) has focused on the interplay among fibroblasts, the immune system and epithelial cells. There is growing evidence that microvascular dysfunction also plays a role in disease progression, but large human translational studies are lacking. In this research, we aim to identify a proteomic signature of microvascular instability and assess the impact of current therapeutics on the microvasculature.
METHODS: Olink proteomic data from patients with IPF were obtained from the Pulmonary Fibrosis Foundation Patient Registry (PFF-PR) (n=914) and an independent validation cohort (n=366). Among the PFF-PR, 640 patients also have whole-blood RNA sequencing data available. A subset of 79 microvascular-associated proteins was curated, and their associations with disease severity and transplant-free survival were examined. An adaptive least absolute shrinkage and selection operator was used to generate a novel microvascular risk score.
RESULTS: Higher plasma levels of five microvascular-associated proteins (SDC1, MMP10, THBS2, HGF and SERPINA5) were associated with lung function and survival in both cohorts. Whole-blood RNA sequencing of patients with microvascular risk revealed enrichment of immune-mediated processes. Patients with higher microvascular risk who were subsequently put on nintedanib in the following year had significantly better 3-year transplant-free survival compared with patients who did not receive antifibrotic intervention (HR 0.56, 95% CI 0.35 to 0.89, p=0.0142).

**可借鉴之处**
- 可借鉴‘上皮内在代谢/线粒体调控因子—细胞状态失衡—分泌组—间质活化’这一分层因果设计：先用条件性诱导敲除建立充分性，再用分泌因子中和在人类离体组织（hPCLS）中做反向验证，适合迁移到肺泡上皮损伤修复与 fibroblast 活化的 crosstalk 研究。

### 2. Adenine nucleotide translocase 2 (ANT2) deficiency reprograms ferroptosis in alveolar progenitor cells to promote emphysema.

bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology | 2026 Jul 13 | Ugonna Mbaekwe, Corrine R Kliment


- 来源：biorxiv
- 槽位：预印本
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者/通讯作者单位：Division of Pulmonary, Allergy and Critical Care Medicine, University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine; Pittsburgh, PA 15213.; Department of Cellular and Molecular Pathology, University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine.
- 领域：肺/过敏与危重症医学及细胞分子病理方向，聚焦肺泡上皮祖细胞与线粒体代谢（据单位与摘要推断）。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42523460/
- DOI：10.1021/ac60228a014

**Abstract**

Stem cell dysfunction and loss of renewal capacity are primary characteristics of tissue aging and decremental regeneration in response to injury. Alveolar type 2 cells (AT2) are key progenitor cells responsible for lung repair and are thought to be dysfunctional in diseases such as chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD). AT2 cells are highly metabolic and rely on mitochondria, but how mitochondrial mechanisms influence their maintenance and cell fate is unclear. This gap is critical as no current therapies target lung repair or mitochondrial function in COPD. Here, we report that adenine nucleotide translocase 2 (ANT2), a key ATP/ADP transporter, is reduced in AT2 cells from COPD lungs, and that ANT2 loss impairs bioenergetics (ATP). We also identify, for the first time, ferroptotic susceptibility as a consequence of ANT2 loss in AT2 cells, leading to impaired self-renewal and progenitor capacity in alveolar organoids. Together, loss of ANT2 and the associated cellular dysfunction resulted in worsened lung damage or emphysema due to cigarette smoke in mice. Therapeutic restoration of ANT2 expression resulted in renewed AT2 stem cell function and prevention of emphysema by reducing oxidative stress and ferroptosis. These findings highlight the importance of ANT2 in metabolic regulation, plasticity, and cell resiliency of AT2 cells in the lung and that ANT2 is a potential target for lung repair.

**What Is New?**
- 首次将线粒体 ATP/ADP 转运体 ANT2 界定为肺泡 type 2 (AT2) 祖细胞维持与自我更新的代谢调控节点，并在人 COPD 肺 AT2 中证实其表达降低。
- 首次把 ANT2 缺失与 ferroptosis 易感性建立因果联系，闭合了「ANT2 缺失→生物能量学（ATP）受损→氧化应激/ferroptosis 易感→AT2 干性与祖细胞功能丧失」这一新机制链。
- 通过在小鼠中治疗性恢复 ANT2 表达实现 in vivo rescue，逆转 AT2 干细胞功能并阻止香烟烟雾诱导的肺气肿，为该代谢节点提供了严格的体内因果验证。

**研究设计**
- 比较人 COPD 肺与对照 AT2 中 ANT2 表达
- 在 AT2 中敲低/缺失 ANT2，检测 ATP 生物能量学、氧化应激与 ferroptosis 易感性
- 以肺泡 organoid 评估 AT2 自我更新与祖细胞/分化能力
- 小鼠香烟烟雾诱导肺气肿模型 + 治疗性恢复 ANT2 的 rescue 实验

**主要发现**
- ANT2 在 COPD 肺 AT2 细胞中降低，其缺失损害 AT2 的生物能量学，表现为 ATP 水平下降。
- ANT2 缺失使 AT2 细胞对 ferroptosis 易感，这是此前未被识别的后果。
- 由此 AT2 的自我更新与祖细胞能力在肺泡 organoid 中受损。
- ANT2 缺失相关细胞功能障碍导致小鼠对香烟烟雾诱导的肺损伤/肺气肿更敏感、病情加重。
- 治疗性恢复 ANT2 表达通过减少氧化应激与 ferroptosis，恢复 AT2 干细胞功能并预防肺气肿。

**为什么入选** 该研究把线粒体代谢节点（ANT2）、细胞命运（ferroptosis）与肺泡祖细胞功能（organoid 自我更新）串联成完整机制链，并在小鼠香烟烟雾肺气肿模型中用 ANT2 恢复实现 in vivo rescue，同时以人 COPD AT2 提供临床相关性证据，是「代谢—细胞可塑性—肺修复」方向的机制型基础研究。

### 3. Epithelial QKI Protects Against Emphysema by Maintaining Mitochondrial Integrity.

bioRxiv : the preprint server for biology | 2026 Jul 10 | Kiyoshi Uemasu, Toru Nyunoya


- 来源：biorxiv
- 槽位：预印本
- 依据：abstract_only
- 第一作者单位：未提供
- 通讯作者单位：未提供
- 第一作者领域：第一作者 Uemasu Kiyoshi，依论文内容推断其研究领域为 COPD/肺气肿的肺泡上皮生物学与线粒体功能，具体单位不详。
- 通讯作者领域：通讯作者单位不详；从论文内容推断其研究领域为 COPD 上皮损伤、线粒体质量控制与肺功能关联。
- 链接：https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42465480/
- DOI：10.64898/2026.07.09.737340

**Abstract**

Single-cell transcriptomic profiling of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) lungs identified QKI, an RNA-binding protein, as a candidate emphysema-associated gene, but its epithelial role in COPD pathobiology remains unclear. We show that QKI expression is reduced in human COPD lungs and that alveolar type 2 epithelial (AT2) cell QKI protein levels correlate strongly with spirometric indices and diffusing capacity (DL CO ). Lung epithelium-specific QKI knockout mice (QKI Δ/Δ ) developed spontaneous airspace enlargement with emphysema-like mechanics, and QKI-deficient AT2 cells showed impaired spheroid colony formation and increased apoptosis. Integrated transcriptomic and proteomic analyses of primary AT2 cells revealed a selective reduction in functional mitochondrial (respiratory-chain and metabolic) protein abundance despite relatively preserved transcript levels, consistent with mitochondrial transcriptome-proteome discordance. QKI loss increased mtDNA abundance and TOMM20 staining but decreased ATP5A, indicating accumulation of structurally increased but functionally dysfunctional mitochondria. In human epithelial cells, CRISPR-mediated QKI deficiency reduced oxidative respiration, increased glycolytic reliance, elevated mitochondrial ROS and membrane potential, and increased apoptosis; these phenotypes were partially rescued by QKI re-expression. These findings identify epithelial QKI as a regulator of mitochondrial integrity and stress tolerance in COPD.

**What Is New?**
- 首次将 RNA-binding protein QKI 从 COPD 单细胞转录组筛出的候选基因，正式确立为肺泡 type 2 上皮（AT2）细胞中驱动肺气肿发生的因果性调控节点，而非仅仅是关联标志物。
- 通过 lung epithelium-specific QKI knockout 小鼠（QKI Δ/Δ）自发出现气腔扩大与 emphysema-like mechanics，建立了上皮 QKI 缺失导致肺气肿表型的严格体内因果链。
- 新定义了 QKI 缺失下的线粒体 transcriptome-proteome discordance 机制：mtDNA 丰度与 TOMM20 升高而 ATP5A 降低，形成“结构增多但功能失能”的线粒体蓄积。

**研究设计**
- 人 COPD 肺组织与 AT2 细胞中 QKI 表达/蛋白水平的测量，并与 spirometric indices 及 DLCO 做相关性分析
- 肺上皮特异性 QKI 敲除小鼠（QKI Δ/Δ）的自发表型观察与肺力学评估
- QKI 缺陷原代 AT2 细胞的 spheroid colony formation 与凋亡检测
- 原代 AT2 细胞整合 transcriptomic 与 proteomic 分析，聚焦线粒体呼吸链与代谢蛋白
- 人上皮细胞 CRISPR 介导 QKI 缺失 + QKI re-expression，评估氧化呼吸、糖酵解依赖、mtROS、膜电位与凋亡

**主要发现**
- 人 COPD 肺中 QKI 表达降低，且 AT2 细胞 QKI 蛋白水平与 spirometric indices 和 diffusing capacity（DLCO）呈强相关。
- 上皮特异性 QKI 敲除小鼠自发出现气腔扩大和类肺气肿力学；QKI 缺陷 AT2 细胞 spheroid 集落形成受损、凋亡增加。
- AT2 细胞整合组学显示 respiratory-chain 与代谢类线粒体功能蛋白选择性减少，而相应转录本水平相对保留，提示 mitochondrial transcriptome-proteome discordance。
- QKI 缺失使 mtDNA 丰度与 TOMM20 染色增加但 ATP5A 减少，表明线粒体在结构上增多而功能上失能。
- 人上皮细胞中 CRISPR 敲除 QKI 降低氧化呼吸、增加糖酵解依赖、升高 mitochondrial ROS 与膜电位并促凋亡，QKI 再表达可部分 rescue 上述表型。

**为什么入选** 机制链较完整（人组织相关性→上皮特异性敲除小鼠体内因果→AT2 线粒体转录组-蛋白组脱耦→人细胞 CRISPR 敲除与 re-expression rescue），把肺气肿的上皮端机制收敛到 RNA-binding protein 对线粒体完整性的调控，且 AT2 与线粒体质量控制视角对肺泡生物学读者有迁移价值；目前为 bioRxiv 预印本。
